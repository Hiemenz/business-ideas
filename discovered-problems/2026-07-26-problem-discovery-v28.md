## Discovered Problems

### 1. **Customer Onboarding (KYC) Bottleneck**
**Problem:** Know-Your-Customer compliance is complex; manual verification is expensive; onboarding takes days/weeks.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a KYC automation platform (instant verification)
- Create a identity verification system (document recognition + liveness checks)
- Offer compliance consulting (streamline onboarding)

**Market Validation:** Slow onboarding causes friction. Instant KYC would reduce friction + increase conversion.

---

### 2. **Fraud Detection & Prevention**
**Problem:** Financial fraud is growing; detection is reactive; false positives are costly (blocking legitimate transactions).  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a fraud detection AI (real-time transaction monitoring)
- Create a behavioral analytics system (identify suspicious patterns)
- Offer fraud prevention consulting (strategy + implementation)

**Market Validation:** Fraud costs financial institutions $100B+ annually. Better detection saves money.

---

### 3. **Regulatory Compliance Complexity**
**Problem:** Financial regulation is complex and constantly changing; compliance is expensive; violations are costly.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a compliance automation platform (regulatory tracking, updates)
- Create a compliance alert system (flag requirement changes)
- Offer compliance consulting (ensure adherence)

**Market Validation:** Compliance is mandatory and expensive. Better automation saves costs.

---

### 4. **Customer Risk Assessment**
**Problem:** Credit decisions are based on limited data; default rates are higher than needed; lending is overly conservative.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build an alternative credit scoring system (alternative data sources)
- Create a risk assessment AI (predict default likelihood)
- Offer credit strategy consulting (optimize lending)

**Market Validation:** Better risk assessment enables better pricing/approval decisions.

**Market Validation:** AML compliance is mandatory. Better automation would reduce costs.

## Final Meta-Pattern: "Regulatory Compliance = Durable Moat"

### Universal Finding:

Companies solving regulatory compliance problems have the strongest defensibility because:
1. **Compliance is mandatory** (not optional)
2. **Barriers to entry are high** (regulatory knowledge required)
3. **Switching costs are high** (regulatory risk in switching)
4. **Market is large** (every regulated business needs solutions)

**This suggests:** Highest-conviction opportunities are sector-specific compliance solutions.
