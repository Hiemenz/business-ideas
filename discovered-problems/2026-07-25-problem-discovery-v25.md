## Discovered Problems

### 1. **Production Scheduling Inefficiency**
**Problem:** Factory scheduling is complex (multiple lines, constraints, demand); manual scheduling is inefficient; throughput is suboptimal.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a production scheduling AI (optimize across constraints)
- Create a bottleneck identification system (find capacity limits)
- Offer production planning consulting (improve throughput)

**Market Validation:** Manufacturing is $2.3T+ industry. 5-10% throughput improvement = billions.

---

### 2. **Quality Control Inconsistency**
**Problem:** Quality varies by line/shift/operator; defects aren't prevented; rework is expensive; customer returns are high.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a real-time quality monitoring system (detect issues immediately)
- Create a root cause analysis AI (identify why defects occur)
- Offer quality consulting (improve consistency)

**Market Validation:** Defects and rework are major costs. Better prevention saves millions.

---

### 3. **Downtime Prediction Failure**
**Problem:** Equipment fails unexpectedly; production halts; costs are massive; preventive maintenance is reactive.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a predictive maintenance AI (predict failures before they happen)
- Create a maintenance scheduling system (optimize maintenance timing)
- Offer maintenance consulting (prevent downtime)

**Market Validation:** Unplanned downtime costs are massive. Prediction prevents disasters.

---

### 4. **Inventory Optimization Blindness**
**Problem:** Raw material inventory is often too high or too low; working capital is tied up; stockouts cause production delays.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build an inventory optimization engine (right size inventory)
- Create a demand forecasting system (predict needs accurately)
- Offer supply chain consulting (optimize inventory levels)

**Market Validation:** Working capital tied up in inventory is significant. Better optimization frees cash.

---

### 5. **Production Cost Tracking Fragmentation**
**Problem:** Production costs are scattered across systems; true cost per unit isn't clear; pricing decisions are guesses.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a cost accounting platform (track costs in real-time)
- Create a profitability analytics system (measure profit by product)
- Offer cost accounting consulting (improve pricing)

**Market Validation:** Unclear costs lead to mispricing. Better visibility improves profitability.

---

### 6. **Labor Productivity Variation**
**Problem:** Worker productivity varies widely; high performers vs. laggards aren't identified; training is one-size-fits-all.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a labor productivity tracking system (measure by worker/task)
- Create a training system (personalized to performance gaps)
- Offer productivity consulting (improve output)

**Market Validation:** Labor is 20-30% of manufacturing costs. 10% productivity gain = significant savings.

### 7. **Supplier Quality Variation**
**Problem:** Supplier quality varies; defects slip through incoming inspection; warranty claims are high.  
**Business Opportunity:**

- Offer supplier management consulting (improve quality)

**Market Validation:** Defects from suppliers are expensive. Better monitoring prevents issues.

**Problem:** When defects are found, tracing back through production is difficult; root cause analysis is slow.

- Offer recall management consulting (faster recalls)

**Market Validation:** Product recalls are expensive. Better traceability speeds responses.
