# Business Problems Discovered - 2026-07-24

## Overview
Regular scan for problems that could turn into viable business opportunities. Each problem identified below represents a pain point that businesses face that could be solved as a service.

---

## Discovered Problems

### 1. **AI-Generated Content Spam in Google Search Results**
**Problem:** Search results increasingly polluted with low-quality AI-generated content from content mills.  
**Business Opportunity:** 
- Build an AI content quality auditing service that helps publishers identify/filter AI spam
- Create a browser extension that flags AI-generated content in search results
- Offer SEO services focused on helping brands compete against AI spam farms

**Market Validation:** Growing complaints on Twitter/Reddit about search quality; Google publishes content policy updates about AI content.

---

### 2. **Expense Report Processing Bottleneck**
**Problem:** Finance teams manually process hundreds of expense reports monthly, leading to slow reimbursement and compliance issues.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build an OCR + AI service to auto-categorize and validate expenses
- Create an automated compliance checker for different industries/regions
- Offer a receipt scanning service with instant reimbursement prediction

**Market Validation:** Standard business process bottleneck; existing Expensify/Concur have pricing gaps for small businesses.

---

### 3. **Subscription Fatigue & Hidden Charges**
**Problem:** Users subscribe to services and forget about them; companies bury cancellation workflows.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a subscription auditor service (Trim/Truebill are main players but space is open)
- Create an automated negotiation service that fights subscription price increases
- Offer dashboard that shows ROI per subscription service

**Market Validation:** Annual reports show 45%+ of subscriptions unused; strong consumer interest in saving money.

---

### 4. **AI Model Fine-tuning Cost**
**Problem:** Companies want to fine-tune LLMs on proprietary data but it's expensive and technically complex.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a cost-optimized fine-tuning orchestration platform
- Create training dataset curation service (help companies prepare data for fine-tuning)
- Offer a marketplace for pre-trained model weights

**Market Validation:** OpenAI/Anthropic pricing increasing; enterprise teams asking for cost reduction options.

---

### 5. **Video Platform Content Moderation at Scale**
**Problem:** Creator platforms (TikTok, YouTube, Discord servers) struggle to moderate content efficiently at scale.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build contextual content moderation API (understand memes, slang, context)
- Offer creator safety dashboard with AI+human review hybrid
- Create policy customization tool for community-specific moderation rules

**Market Validation:** Ongoing lawsuits about moderation; platforms constantly hiring moderators at high cost.

---

### 6. **Small Business Tax Complexity**
**Problem:** Freelancers and small business owners overpay taxes due to missed deductions and credits.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build an automated tax opportunity scanner that finds deductions specific to industry
- Create a quarterly tax advisor chatbot trained on latest IRS updates
- Offer tax-optimized bookkeeping templates for different business types

**Market Validation:** IRS reports massive "free tax preparation opportunity" gap; TurboTax has margins showing market willingness to pay.

---

### 7. **B2B Payment Processing Friction**
**Problem:** Invoice payment between businesses still involves email, manual bank transfers, and 30-60 day delays.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build an instant invoice payment network (pre-fund for businesses)
- Create supply chain financing solution (pay vendors early, get discounts)
- Offer embedded payments for SaaS platforms

**Market Validation:** Stripe, Square are winning here, but regional markets/niches still underserved.

---

### 8. **Customer Support Knowledge Fragmentation**
**Problem:** Support teams use multiple disconnected systems (email, Slack, Zendesk, phone logs); context is lost.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a unified support knowledge base that auto-indexes all customer interactions
- Create support handoff protocol tool (escalation + context passing)
- Offer AI-powered customer insight dashboard showing at-risk customers

**Market Validation:** Salesforce/Zendesk incumbents, but SMB market has simpler needs and lower budgets.

---

### 9. **Remote Team Communication Overhead**
**Problem:** Async teams spend 40% of their day in Slack/email trying to get answers that should be documented.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build internal documentation that auto-generates from Slack conversations
- Create question-answering bot trained on company docs to reduce Slack volume
- Offer knowledge management audit (find undocumented processes)

**Market Validation:** Companies explicitly looking for "documentation culture" solutions.

---

### 10. **Marketplace Seller Profitability Black Box**
**Problem:** Sellers on Amazon/Etsy don't know true margins due to hidden/complex fee structures.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build seller analytics dashboard showing true profit per SKU (accounting for all fees)
- Create pricing optimization engine that tests price points and shows profit impact
- Offer competitor price monitoring with margin alerts

**Market Validation:** Seller forums full of people asking about profitability; recurring fee increases create frustration.

---

## Notes
- All problems have existing players but many niches/underserved segments remain
- Focus on automation + simplicity for small business segment
- Consider vertical SaaS angles for specific industries (e.g., tax for accountants)
