# Business Problems Discovered - 2026-07-24 (Session 5)

## Overview
Fifth iteration focusing on customer acquisition, sales enablement, and revenue optimization problems across verticals.

---

## Discovered Problems

### 1. **Lead Quality vs. Quantity Tradeoff**
**Problem:** Sales teams get volume of leads but most are unqualified; spend time filtering noise instead of closing deals.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a lead qualification AI (analyze lead attributes, predict close likelihood before handoff)
- Create a lead scoring framework builder (help teams define what "good lead" looks like)
- Offer sales efficiency audit (measure time spent on qualification vs. selling)

**Market Validation:** Sales teams waste 30-40% of time on disqualification. Lead quality is #2 complaint after compensation.

---

### 2. **Product Demo Recording Library Obsolescence**
**Problem:** Sales uses outdated demo videos; creating new ones is expensive (video production) and slow (weeks to turnaround).  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a auto-generating demo video platform (from product screenshots + voiceover)
- Create a demo analytics dashboard (track which features resonate with which customer segments)
- Offer personalized demo service (customize demos by industry/use case automatically)

**Market Validation:** Demo is critical to close but creation bottleneck is universal. Demand for faster, cheaper demos is high.

---

### 3. **Competitor Win Rate Blindness**
**Problem:** Sales teams don't systematically track why they lose deals to specific competitors; learning is ad hoc.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a competitive intelligence platform (aggregate lost deal analysis by competitor)
- Create a win/loss interview automation system (conduct feedback interviews automatically)
- Offer competitive positioning audit (identify messaging gaps vs. top 3 competitors)

**Market Validation:** Product/marketing teams cite competitive awareness as major gap. Gartner estimates 40% of deals lost to better positioning.

---

### 4. **Referral Program Black Box**
**Problem:** Companies launch referral programs but have no visibility into which customers refer most, which referrals convert, tracking is manual.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a referral program management platform (track referrer → referee → customer attribution)
- Create referral incentive optimizer (test different incentive levels, measure impact)
- Offer referral program audit service (identify high-potential referrers who aren't being asked)

**Market Validation:** Referrals are highest-LTV customers but most companies treat as afterthought. Organized referral programs have 4x better ROI.

---

### 5. **Event Sponsorship Effectiveness Measurement**
**Problem:** Companies sponsor conferences/events (booth, speaking slots, happy hours) but can't tie sponsorship spend to pipeline or revenue.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build event sponsorship ROI tracker (attribute generated leads/deals to specific events)
- Create event strategy optimizer (which events are worth investing in, which to skip)
- Offer event intelligence service (tell companies which attendees to target at each event)

**Market Validation:** B2B event marketing budgets are $5B+ annually but measurement is terrible. Demand for accountability is growing.

---

### 6. **Sales Collateral Outdated Instantly**
**Problem:** Marketing creates sales collateral (case studies, one-pagers, pricing) but updates are slow; sales uses outdated materials.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a collateral management system (version control, approval workflows, distribution tracking)
- Create a collateral asset generator (auto-create materials from central content library)
- Offer collateral effectiveness analyzer (track which assets are used, which lead to closes)

**Market Validation:** Sales complain about outdated materials; marketing struggles with distribution. Friction point between teams.

---

### 7. **Territory Assignment Chaos**
**Problem:** Sales leaders manually assign territories by geography or account size; territories are imbalanced (rep A has 10 prospects, rep B has 50).  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a territory optimization algorithm (balance potential revenue, account count, growth opportunity)
- Create a territory planning tool (visualize territories, redistribute fairly)
- Offer territory performance analyzer (identify which territories are underperforming)

**Market Validation:** Territory wars are source of turnover. Data-driven territory assignment would increase retention and productivity.

---

### 8. **Pricing Page A/B Testing Friction**
**Problem:** Companies want to test pricing but changing it requires dev work; most never experiment (leave money on table).  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a pricing experiment platform (no-code pricing page variations)
- Create a pricing sensitivity analysis tool (predict optimal price based on willingness-to-pay data)
- Offer pricing audit service (identify revenue uplift opportunity)

**Market Validation:** Pricing optimization has 3-10x ROI but most companies use static pricing. Very underpenetrated opportunity.

---

### 9. **Sales Engineer Capacity Shortage**
**Problem:** Complex products need sales engineers for demos but they're expensive and overbooked; can't scale without hiring.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a self-service sales engineer alternative (interactive product walkthroughs, guided tours)
- Create a SE enablement platform (give all salespeople depth of technical knowledge)
- Offer SE training service (develop internal SEs faster)

**Market Validation:** Tech companies say SE hiring is biggest hiring bottleneck. Alternatives to SE scaling are valuable.

---

### 10. **Customer Reference Program Disorganization**
**Problem:** Companies want to use customers as references but can't identify willing references, asking process is ad hoc, reference quality varies.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a customer reference management system (identify reference candidates, track status, schedule calls)
- Create a reference call coaching tool (prepare customers and sales teams for reference calls)
- Offer reference interview service (conduct calls on behalf, aggregate feedback for sales)

**Market Validation:** References are trusted sources in B2B buying but programs are underutilized. Only 15% of customers are asked to be references.

---

### 11. **Win-Loss Analysis at Scale**
**Problem:** Enterprise sales teams conduct manual win-loss interviews; insights are anecdotal and don't drive strategy.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a win-loss analysis platform (aggregate patterns from interviews, identify trends)
- Create an automated win-loss survey system (collect feedback at scale, analyze themes)
- Offer win-loss consulting service (run competitive intelligence program end-to-end)

**Market Validation:** Winning teams obsess over win-loss analysis but most companies do it poorly. Gartner cited it as top insight need.

---

### 12. **Product Launch Coordination Nightmare**
**Problem:** Product launches involve sales, marketing, support, and executives but coordination is chaotic; messaging misaligned; launch underperforms.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a product launch coordination platform (timeline, messaging, role assignments, dependencies)
- Create a launch readiness checklist (ensure all teams are prepared)
- Offer launch campaign management service (execute coordinated launch for companies)

**Market Validation:** Product launches are high-stakes and complex but tools are fragmented (project management + comms + training). Unified platform would be valuable.

---

### 13. **Channel Partner Enablement Disconnect**
**Problem:** Companies with channel partners (resellers, integrators) can't keep partners trained on latest offerings; partner-sourced revenue is lower quality.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a partner enablement platform (training, content library, deal registration)
- Create a partner certification program automation (auto-onboard, test, certify partners)
- Offer partner performance coaching service (improve partner productivity)

**Market Validation:** Channel revenue is strategic but partner programs are often neglected. 60-70% of partners are under-equipped.

---

### 14. **Customer Buying Committee Mapping Mystery**
**Problem:** Sales doesn't know who the decision makers are at prospect companies; wastes time on influencers, misses key blockers.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a buying committee analyzer (LinkedIn + firmographic data to map likely decision makers)
- Create an org chart intelligence tool (identify power structures, relationships, stakeholders)
- Offer account intelligence service (custom research on target accounts' buying structures)

**Market Validation:** Sales teams cite lack of buyer insights as major efficiency loss. DataBox/LinkedIn Sales Nav have partial solutions but gaps remain.

---

### 15. **Proposal Turnaround Time Killing Deals**
**Problem:** Sales creates custom proposals but it takes 3-5 days from request to delivery; by then buyer has lost interest or gone with competitor.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a proposal automation platform (templates, variable insertion, e-signature integration)
- Create a proposal success predictor (estimate close likelihood based on proposal terms)
- Offer proposal optimization service (A/B test proposal structure, terms, pricing)

**Market Validation:** Proposal turnaround directly correlates to close rate. Every day of delay is 2% loss in close probability.

## Meta-Pattern: What Makes a Good Business Problem?

After 5 sessions (66 total problems), patterns emerge:

### High-Conviction Problems Have These Traits:
1. **Affects large group** (>1M companies globally or >10K in vertical)
2. **Costly to ignore** (ROI is 3x+ payoff)
3. **Current solution is fragmented/expensive** (not one dominant player)
4. **Problem is getting worse** (macro trends making it more acute)
5. **Willingness to pay is clear** (already spending money on workarounds)

### Problems NOT Worth Building:
1. One-time, not recurring
2. Affects <1% of potential customers
3. Dominant player already solved it well and cheaply
4. Problem is getting better (tech progress is solving it)
5. Uncertain willingness to pay
