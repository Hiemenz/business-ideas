## Discovered Problems

### 1. **Fleet Maintenance Scheduling Complexity**
**Problem:** Managing fleet maintenance is complex; unplanned breakdowns halt operations; maintenance costs are high.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a predictive fleet maintenance platform (predict breakdowns)
- Create a maintenance scheduling system (optimize maintenance timing)
- Offer fleet maintenance consulting (reduce downtime and costs)

**Market Validation:** Fleet downtime is costly. Better prediction prevents expensive breakdowns.
