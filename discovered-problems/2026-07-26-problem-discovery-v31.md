## Discovered Problems

### 1. **Clinical Trial Patient Recruitment Bottleneck**
**Problem:** Clinical trials need patients but recruitment is slow and expensive; enrollment misses timelines; trials are delayed.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a patient recruitment platform (identify eligible patients, targeted outreach)
- Create a trial matching engine (connect patients to trials they qualify for)
- Offer recruitment consulting (accelerate enrollment)

**Market Validation:** Patient recruitment is major cost and timeline driver in drug development.

---

### 2. **Regulatory Compliance Documentation Burden**
**Problem:** Pharma has extreme regulatory requirements (FDA, EMA, etc.); documentation is complex; compliance is expensive and time-consuming.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a compliance automation platform (auto-generate required documents)
- Create a regulatory tracking system (monitor changing requirements)
- Offer compliance consulting (ensure adherence, reduce risk)

**Market Validation:** Regulatory compliance is non-negotiable and expensive.

## Final Insight: "Regulation = Moat + Market Opportunity"

### Key Finding:

Highly regulated industries (Healthcare, Finance, Pharma, Energy) have:
- **High entry barriers** (compliance complexity)
- **High defensibility** (switching costs, regulatory lock-in)
- **High margins** (less price competition)
- **Sticky customers** (compliance-driven adoption)

**Companies winning in regulated verticals build $10B+ businesses because defensibility is unmatched.**
