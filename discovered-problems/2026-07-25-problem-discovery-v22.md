# Business Problems Discovered - 2026-07-25 (Session 22)

## Overview
Twenty-second iteration focusing on hospitality, restaurants, hotels, and food service operations challenges.

## Discovered Problems

### 1. **Restaurant Table Management Chaos**
**Problem:** Table availability isn't transparent; seating is manual; wait times are long; revenue is lost.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a table management system (real-time availability, optimization)
- Create a wait list management platform (track customers, notify when ready)
- Offer reservation management consulting (maximize covers, reduce wait)

**Market Validation:** Seating efficiency directly impacts revenue. Better systems would increase covers.

---

### 2. **Food Waste & Inventory Loss**
**Problem:** Restaurants throw away significant food; inventory shrinkage is high; waste is invisible.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build an inventory tracking system (real-time food inventory)
- Create a waste tracking platform (measure, identify patterns)
- Offer waste reduction consulting (reduce costs, improve sustainability)

**Market Validation:** Food costs are 28-35% of revenue. Waste reduction = profit improvement.

---

### 3. **Labor Scheduling Complexity**
**Problem:** Restaurant scheduling is complex (shifts, skill mix, regulations); manual scheduling causes inefficiency; labor costs are high.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a workforce scheduling platform (optimize shifts, reduce labor)
- Create a skill-based assignment system (match skills to positions)
- Offer labor optimization consulting (reduce payroll while maintaining service)

**Market Validation:** Labor is 25-35% of costs. 10% efficiency gain = significant savings.

---

### 4. **Menu Engineering Blindness**
**Problem:** Restaurants don't know which dishes are profitable; menu design is guesswork; pricing is inconsistent.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a menu analytics platform (track sales, profitability by item)
- Create a menu engineering tool (identify high-margin items, optimize menu)
- Offer menu design consulting (maximize profitability)

**Market Validation:** Menu optimization can increase profitability by 10-20%. High-ROI opportunity.

---

### 5. **Customer Experience Measurement Failure**
**Problem:** Restaurants track revenue but not customer satisfaction; repeat customers aren't identified; loyalty is low.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a customer satisfaction tracking system (feedback collection)
- Create a loyalty program platform (reward repeat customers)
- Offer customer experience consulting (improve satisfaction, retention)

**Market Validation:** Customer lifetime value is critical. Better retention would increase profitability.

---

### 6. **Kitchen Operations Inefficiency**
**Problem:** Kitchen workflow is often manual; food prep isn't coordinated; wait times are long; food quality varies.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a kitchen management system (coordinate prep, timing)
- Create an order queue optimization system (minimize wait times)
- Offer kitchen process consulting (improve efficiency)

**Market Validation:** Kitchen efficiency directly impacts customer satisfaction. Better coordination would help.

---

### 7. **Supply Chain Food Safety**
**Problem:** Food safety is critical but tracking is manual; contamination incidents aren't prevented; compliance is reactive.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a food safety tracking system (supplier accountability, compliance)
- Create a traceability system (track food from supplier to plate)
- Offer food safety consulting (compliance + prevention)

**Market Validation:** Food safety incidents are catastrophic. Better tracking would prevent disasters.

---

### 8. **Reservation No-Show Prevention**
**Problem:** Reservation no-shows are common (20-30%); revenue is lost; tables are wasted.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a no-show prediction model (identify likely no-shows)
- Create a reminder & confirmation system (automated confirmations, reminders)
- Offer reservation optimization consulting (reduce no-shows)

**Market Validation:** No-shows directly impact revenue. Better prevention would increase covers.

---

### 9. **Staff Training & Development**
**Problem:** Service quality varies widely; staff training is ad-hoc; turnover is high; customer experience suffers.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a training platform (staff training, skill development)
- Create a performance tracking system (identify top/bottom performers)
- Offer staff development consulting (improve service quality)

**Market Validation:** Service quality drives customer satisfaction. Better training would help.

---

### 10. **Online Ordering Integration Chaos**
**Problem:** Restaurants use multiple platforms (DoorDash, Uber Eats, own app); orders are fragmented; coordination is poor.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build an order aggregation platform (unified order view)
- Create a delivery coordination system (manage multiple platforms)
- Offer marketplace optimization consulting (maximize online sales)

**Market Validation:** Online orders are growing share of revenue. Better integration would help.

### 11. **Beverage Program Optimization**
**Problem:** Beverage profitability is typically 70-80% but programs are poorly managed; upselling is minimal.  
**Business Opportunity:**
- Build a beverage sales tracking system (sales by category, profitability)
- Create an upselling optimization system (when/how to suggest premium drinks)
- Offer beverage program consulting (maximize revenue per customer)

**Market Validation:** Beverage margins are high. Better optimization would increase profitability.

### 12. **Guest Preference Tracking**
**Problem:** Loyal customers come back but preferences aren't remembered; service is generic; personalization is minimal.

### 13. **Inventory Cost Tracking**

**Market Validation:** Shrinkage directly impacts margins. Better tracking would identify problems.
