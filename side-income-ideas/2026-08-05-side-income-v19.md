# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-05 (Round 19)

### 1. Amazon FBA Lost & Damaged Inventory Reimbursement Tracker
Amazon FBA sellers are routinely owed reimbursements for inventory that Amazon loses in its fulfillment network, damages during picking or shipping, or misprocesses during customer returns — but Amazon does not automatically issue these reimbursements. Sellers must manually identify discrepancies by comparing their inventory reports to Amazon's reimbursement history, calculate the claim amounts, and submit reimbursement requests before the 18-month eligibility window expires. This tool imports the seller's FBA inventory reports, reconciliation reports, and reimbursement history via Amazon SP-API, identifies open reimbursement opportunities by type (lost inbound, lost in warehouse, damaged, return discrepancy), calculates the claim value using the seller's COGS or Amazon's reimbursement rate, and generates formatted case submission notes for each open claim. Monetization: $20-35/mo subscription or 10-15% contingency on recovered reimbursements. Distribution: r/FulfillmentByAmazon, Amazon seller Facebook groups, Helium 10 and Jungle Scout user communities. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 2. Google Business Profile Review Response Generator for Local Service Businesses
HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, and other local service businesses need to respond to every Google Business Profile review — both positive (to thank the customer) and negative (to address the complaint professionally) — because response rates affect Google's local ranking signals and public perception. Most business owners don't respond because they don't know what to say or fear saying the wrong thing on a negative review. This tool accepts the review text and star rating, then generates 2-3 professionally worded response options: one empathetic and brief, one detailed and action-oriented, and one that gently asks a negative reviewer to contact the business privately. Monetization: $12-20/mo unlimited use. Distribution: r/smallbusiness, local service business Facebook groups, HomeAdvisor and Angi contractor communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. SaaS Churn Risk Early Warning Dashboard
Small SaaS founders with 50-500 customers want to identify at-risk accounts before they churn — but have no signal beyond looking at invoice status. This lightweight dashboard connects to the product's usage database (via a simple SQL query or API the founder provides), tracks weekly active usage per account, flags accounts whose usage has dropped more than 40% over a 30-day rolling window, and generates a weekly "accounts to call this week" list sorted by usage decline severity and account revenue. Monetization: $30-60/mo per founder. Distribution: Indie Hackers, SaaS founder Slack groups (MicroConf community, Ramen Club), r/SaaS. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Youth Sports League Treasurer Financial Report Generator
Volunteer treasurers of youth sports leagues, rec leagues, and club sports teams collect registration fees and pay expenses across a season but have no easy way to generate a formal financial statement for board meetings, parent transparency, or IRS Form 990-N filing (required for small tax-exempt organizations). This tool accepts a simple income/expense log (or imports from a shared Google Sheet), categorizes transactions into a standard chart of accounts for sports organizations (registration income, equipment, field rental, coaching, tournament fees, insurance), and generates a season-end financial report with a balance sheet, income statement, and fund balance summary formatted for a board meeting or parent newsletter. Monetization: $8-15/mo or $39/season. Distribution: AYSO regional coordinator communities, Little League volunteer administrator forums, youth sports director Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Vacation Rental Competitor Pricing & Occupancy Monitor
Airbnb and VRBO hosts managing a single property or small portfolio want to know when local competitors drop prices, when nearby listings are booked out (indicating demand spikes), and when major local events create pricing opportunities — but have no easy way to track this without manually checking competitor listings. This tool accepts a set of competitor listing URLs, scrapes publicly visible pricing and availability calendars on a weekly schedule, flags price drops or occupancy surges in the user's market, and generates a weekly summary with recommended dates to raise or lower price based on local supply/demand. Monetization: $20-35/mo per host. Distribution: Airbnb host Facebook groups, BiggerPockets short-term rental forums, STR investor communities. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 6. Food Truck Daily Sales & Location Performance Tracker
Food truck operators sell at rotating locations — lunch spots, farmers markets, events, and private catering — and need to know which locations generate the best revenue per hour of operation, which menu items sell out early (indicating under-supply), and which days of the week are worth additional staffing. A daily sales log with location tagging, staffing hours entry, and top-selling item tracking generates a weekly performance ranking: revenue per hour by location, best-selling items by location type, and a rolling 30-day location profitability comparison. Monetization: $10-18/mo. Distribution: food truck operator Facebook groups, National Food Truck Association member communities, street food vendor Discord servers. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
