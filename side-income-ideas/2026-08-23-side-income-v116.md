# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-23 (Round 116)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Independent Bee Swarm Removal & Live Relocation Service Dispatch, Equipment & Rehoming Tracker

An independent beekeeper who takes paid swarm-removal calls — a homeowner with a basketball-sized cluster of bees on a tree branch, a business with a colony that's moved into a wall void — has to triage each call by urgency and accessibility (a low branch is a same-day job; a wall cut-out is a multi-hour, multi-visit project requiring a return trip), track which bee vacuum, suit, and ladder equipment is loaded for the day's route, and keep a record of where every removed colony was ultimately rehomed, since a hobbyist beekeeper who takes a removed colony without a documented handoff has no way to prove where a swarm went if a customer follows up weeks later asking. An operator running a spring-and-summer swarm season currently triages incoming calls, tracks equipment loadout, and logs colony rehoming destinations on a phone notes app and a paper call log, and a same-day removal call that gets missed because it was buried under older messages is a swarm that moves on and a customer who calls a competitor, while a wall cut-out job scheduled without confirming the return-visit equipment (drywall patch materials, comb removal tools) is a second wasted trip.

The tool maintains an incoming-call triage board sorting jobs by urgency and job type (exposed swarm vs. structural cut-out) with a same-day-priority flag, a per-job equipment checklist confirming the bee vacuum, suit, and ladder gear needed is loaded before departure, and a colony rehoming log recording which apiary or fellow beekeeper received each removed colony with the date and location.

Monetization: $12-20/mo per operator. Distribution: r/beekeeping and r/Beekeeping swarm-call threads, state beekeepers association member directories, and local beekeeping club Facebook groups where independent swarm-removal beekeepers already trade triage and rehoming-record tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Ski & Snowboard Tuning Shop Service Ticket, Wax/Stone Inventory & Seasonal-Rush Queue Tracker

An independent ski and snowboard tuning shop takes in equipment for edge sharpening, base grinding, and hot waxing ahead of a customer's next trip, with each pair needing a specific service tier (a quick same-day wax vs. a full stone-grind-and-tune) that determines both the turnaround promised and the wax/stone-grit combination used, while running a rush of intake tickets that spikes hard in the weeks before ski season and again after every holiday weekend. A shop running dozens of pairs through the shop floor at once currently tracks which equipment belongs to which customer, what service tier was promised and by when, and which wax/edge-bevel spec was used per pair on a stack of paper tags stapled to each ski, and a pair returned to the wrong customer because two tags looked identical is an angry call mid-storm, while a rush-season queue tracked only by the order tickets were dropped off is a shop that can't tell a customer picking up "by Friday" whether that promise is actually still realistic.

The tool maintains a per-ticket intake record (customer, equipment, service tier, and promised pickup date) with a rush-season queue view sorted by promised date rather than drop-off order, a wax/edge-bevel spec log per pair for repeat customers who want the same setup every visit, and a stone/wax/base-material inventory tracker with reorder alerts before a Saturday rush depletes stock.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per shop. Distribution: r/skiing and r/snowboarding tuning-shop threads, the Professional Ski Instructors of America (PSIA) regional networks that cross over into shop staff, and local ski-shop-owner Facebook groups where independent tuning shops already trade rush-season queue and inventory tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Mobile Pet Hospice & In-Home Euthanasia Veterinarian Family Scheduling & Aftercare Preference Tracker

An independent mobile veterinarian who provides in-home pet hospice consultations and end-of-life euthanasia appointments has to manage a scheduling calendar unlike an ordinary vet practice's — most calls are same-day or next-day and emotionally urgent, each family has specific aftercare preferences (private cremation with ashes returned, communal cremation, a paw-print keepsake, burial arrangements coordinated with a pet cemetery) that must be confirmed before the appointment so nothing is decided in the moment, and a follow-up sympathy note or check-in call is expected days later. A vet running a solo mobile practice across a wide service radius currently tracks incoming family requests, aftercare preferences, and follow-up timing on a phone notes app and a mental list, and a same-day request that gets missed because it arrived between other appointments is a family left without support at the worst possible moment, while a forgotten aftercare preference — cremation type, ash return timing — is a grieving family who has to make an already painful phone call to clarify something they thought they'd already settled.

The tool maintains a per-family appointment record (pet name, requested date/urgency, service address, and confirmed aftercare preference) with a same-day-request priority flag, an aftercare coordination checklist tracking cremation or burial arrangements through to completion, and a follow-up reminder scheduler for a sympathy note or check-in call at a set interval after the appointment.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per veterinarian. Distribution: the International Association for Animal Hospice and Palliative Care (IAAHPC) member directory, r/veterinarians and r/AskVet mobile-practice threads, and local mobile-vet Facebook groups where independent in-home hospice and euthanasia practitioners already trade scheduling and aftercare-coordination workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Wheelchair & Mobility Equipment Repair Technician Client Equipment Profile & Loaner Tracker

An independent mobile technician who repairs power wheelchairs, scooters, and other mobility equipment in clients' homes has to track each client's specific equipment make, model, and serial number against a growing repair-ticket history (a client's third motor failure in two years is a different conversation than a first-time flat tire), keep a parts-on-order list linked to the specific repair waiting on each part since mobility-equipment parts are frequently backordered for weeks, and manage a small fleet of loaner chairs and scooters lent out while a client's own equipment is in the shop, since a client without a working wheelchair has no independent mobility at all until the repair is done. A technician running a route of dozens of ongoing clients currently tracks equipment profiles, parts-on-order status, and loaner-unit whereabouts on a paper client-card file and a phone's photo roll of serial numbers, and a repair job stalled because nobody followed up on a three-week-old parts order is a client stuck without mobility longer than necessary, while a loaner chair that's been out for months because nobody tracked its return date is a unit the technician can't lend to the next client who needs one.

The tool maintains a per-client equipment profile (make, model, serial number, and repair history) with a repeat-failure flag surfacing patterns worth a bigger conversation, a parts-on-order tracker linking each backordered part to the specific job waiting on it, and a loaner-equipment fleet tracker showing which units are out, to which client, and since when.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per technician. Distribution: the National Registry of Rehabilitation Technology Suppliers (NRRTS) professional network, r/wheelchairs and r/disability technician-referral threads, and independent mobility-equipment-repair Facebook groups where mobile technicians already trade parts-sourcing and loaner-fleet workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Home Inventory Documentation Service Room-by-Room Catalog & Insurance-Update Tracker

An independent home inventory documentation specialist walks a client's home room by room, photographing and video-cataloging furniture, electronics, art, and valuables to produce an insurance-ready inventory report a homeowner can hand to their insurer before a loss ever happens — or lean on immediately after a fire or theft when memory alone can't reconstruct what was lost. A specialist running visits across dozens of client homes has to track which rooms have been documented in a given visit, which high-value items need a separate appraisal or receipt attached to the record for adequate coverage, and which clients are due for a re-documentation visit after a year or two of new purchases, since an inventory that's gone stale relative to a client's actual belongings under-serves the exact claim it was built to support. A specialist currently tracks per-client documentation progress, high-value item flags, and re-visit due dates on a folder of raw video files and a spreadsheet with no systematic reminder system, and a client who bought a houseful of new furniture since their last visit but was never flagged for a re-documentation appointment is a policyholder walking into a claim with an outdated record, while a high-value item photographed without its receipt or appraisal attached is a claim that comes up short on proof of value.

The tool maintains a per-client, per-room documentation-completion tracker showing which rooms are finished, in progress, or not yet started for a given visit, a high-value item flag linking a receipt or appraisal document to each item that needs one, and a re-documentation due-date scheduler recurring on a set interval (commonly annual or biennial) per client.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per specialist. Distribution: r/homeowners and r/insurance inventory-documentation threads, the National Association of Home Inventory Professionals member network, and independent insurance-agent referral relationships (agents who see underinsured claims firsthand refer clients to a documentation specialist as a value-add). Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Independent Running Gait-Analysis & Shoe-Fitting Specialist Client Profile & Re-Fit Interval Tracker

An independent gait-analysis and shoe-fitting specialist — running a fitting studio inside a specialty running store or a mobile pop-up at race expos — records each client's foot measurements, gait-analysis findings (overpronation, neutral stride, arch type), and the specific shoe models that matched well, so a returning client doesn't have to repeat a full analysis every time they need a new pair. A specialist running dozens of fittings a week currently tracks client foot profiles, gait findings, and shoe-model recommendations on paper fitting cards filed by last name, and a repeat client whose old fitting card can't be found is a fitting redone from scratch that a returning customer expected to be faster the second time, while a client who bought shoes six months ago and is due for a mileage-based re-fit (running shoes lose cushioning after a set mileage range) never gets a reminder because nobody is tracking purchase dates against typical shoe lifespan.

The tool maintains a per-client foot-profile and gait-analysis record with photos/notes from the fitting session, a shoe-model purchase history linking each pair sold to the client's profile for faster repeat fittings, and a mileage-based re-fit reminder scheduler flagging clients approaching the typical replacement interval for their shoe type.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per specialist. Distribution: r/running and r/AdvancedRunning gait-analysis threads, the Road Runners Club of America (RRCA) specialty-store network, and independent running-store owner Facebook groups where in-house fitting specialists already trade client-profile and re-fit-reminder workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
