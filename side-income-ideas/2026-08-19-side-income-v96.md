### 1. Freelance Proofreader & Copyeditor Manuscript Pass & Style-Guide Tracker

Independent proofreaders and copyeditors working for indie authors, small presses, and self-publishing clients need to track a per-manuscript project record showing exactly which editing pass a document is on — first read, line edit, or final proof — since an editor juggling 6-12 concurrent manuscripts can't rely on memory to know whose novel is back with the author for revisions versus whose short-story collection is ready for a final proof pass. The tool also needs a per-client style-guide and preference sheet, since publishers specify Chicago vs. AP style, and indie authors often have their own quirks (a preferred spelling of a character's name, a house rule on em-dashes vs. en-dashes) that an editor working across a dozen clients can't hold in their head consistently across a 300-page project. It also needs a turnaround-deadline tracker tied to each client's hard publication or submission date, since a manuscript still in revision two days before a scheduled release is a crisis both the editor and the author need visibility into early rather than discovering at the deadline. Most independent proofreaders and copyeditors currently manage this across a folder of tracked-changes documents and a mental note of which style guide applies to which client, with no structured link between a specific manuscript's current pass and the deadline it's racing against.

The tool maintains a per-manuscript pass tracker (first read, line edit, final proof, delivered), a per-client style-guide/preference sheet, and a turnaround-deadline calendar tied to each project's publication or submission date.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/freelanceWriters and r/editors, the Editorial Freelancers Association (EFA) member directory, and indie-author and self-publishing Facebook groups where authors post looking for editors. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Independent Equine Dental Technician Floating-Interval & Dental-History Tracker

Independent equine dental technicians who float horses' teeth across a client base of barns and boarding stables need to track a per-horse floating-interval record, since the recommended cycle varies by the horse's age and dental history — a young horse or one with a known dental issue may need floating every 6 months while an older horse with a stable mouth may go 12 — and a technician servicing 100-200 horses across a route can't rely on memory to know which horse is due this month versus which one was just done last visit. The tool also needs a per-horse dental-finding log (hooks, points, wave mouth, missing or loose teeth) that a vet or a new barn owner may ask about later, since equine dentistry findings inform broader health and performance decisions the horse's owner relies on the technician to remember accurately visit over visit. It also needs a route view grouping upcoming due horses by barn or stable location, since a technician working a rotating circuit of boarding facilities needs to plan efficient driving loops rather than backtracking across the same county multiple times in a month. Most independent equine dentists currently manage this across a paper index card per horse and a rough memory of which barn is due next, with no structured link between a specific horse's floating history and its next-due date.

The tool maintains a per-horse floating-interval tracker with an auto-calculated next-due date, a per-horse dental-finding log across visits, and a route view grouping due horses by barn/stable for efficient scheduling.

Monetization: $15-25/mo for a solo technician tracking up to 150 horses, scaling to $35/mo for larger rosters. Distribution: the International Association of Equine Dentistry (IAED) member directory, regional equine dentistry certification-program alumni networks, and boarding-stable and horse-owner Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 3. Sports Memorabilia & Trading Card Dealer Inventory, Grading-Submission & Consignment Tracker

Independent sports memorabilia and trading card dealers running a rotating inventory of raw and graded cards, autographs, and game-used items need to track a per-item inventory record showing acquisition cost, current estimated market value, and grading status, since a dealer holding hundreds of items across multiple sports and eras can't rely on memory to know which cards are still raw versus which are back from grading and ready to list. The tool also needs a grading-submission tracker following each batch sent to a grading service (PSA, BGS, SGC) from submission date through expected and actual return, since submission turnaround can run weeks to months and a dealer with capital tied up in a dozen submitted batches needs to know exactly what's still in the grading pipeline versus what's overdue. It also needs a consignment tracker for items sold on behalf of other collectors, recording the consignor's item, agreed commission split, and settlement status once sold, since a dealer running a consignment case at a card show or online needs a clear accounting of what's owed to whom after each sale. Most independent dealers currently manage this across a spreadsheet of item photos and a mental tally of what's out for grading, with no structured link between a specific item's grading-submission status and its current listed value.

The tool maintains a per-item inventory tracker (acquisition cost, estimated value, grading status), a grading-submission tracker by batch and service, and a consignment tracker with commission-split and settlement status.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/sportscards and r/baseballcards, the Professional Sports Authenticator (PSA) collector community and card-show vendor networks, and regional card-show promoter contact lists for direct outreach to dealer-table regulars. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Billiards & Pool Table Service Technician Table-History & Route Tracker

Independent mobile billiards technicians who recover felt, relevel slate, and service pool tables at home and commercial accounts need to track a per-table service record tied to a specific client address, since a technician servicing 80-150 tables across a metro area can't rely on memory to know which table just got a felt recover versus which one is overdue for releveling after settling on an uneven floor. The tool also needs a parts and felt-color inventory sized to scheduled jobs, since felt comes in specific colors and weights a client may have chosen originally and expects matched on a recover, and a technician arriving without the right felt roll or rail-cushion rubber for a scheduled job wastes a trip. It also needs a route view spanning both home accounts and commercial accounts (bars, rec centers, pool halls) that rebook on a recurring service cycle, since commercial tables see heavier use and need more frequent attention than a homeowner's rec-room table. Most independent billiards technicians currently manage this across a paper work-order pad and a rough memory of which table got which felt color, with no structured link between a specific table's service history and its next scheduled visit.

The tool maintains a per-table service-history record (felt recover date, releveling, rail/cushion condition) by client address, a felt-color and parts inventory sized to the week's scheduled jobs, and a route board spanning home and commercial accounts.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the Billiard Congress of America (BCA) affiliated retailer and service-technician network, r/billiards, and local pool-hall and bar-owner referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Custom & Private-Label Candle Maker Batch Log & Wholesale Order Tracker

Independent candle makers producing custom scents and private-label runs for wholesale accounts and craft-fair sales need to track a per-batch production log — wax type, fragrance load percentage, pour date, and cure time — since a wholesale boutique client who reorders a specific scent months later expects the exact same fragrance profile, and a maker running dozens of scent variations can't rely on memory to reproduce a batch precisely from a note scribbled on a sticky pad. The tool also needs a wax, fragrance-oil, and vessel inventory tracked by supplier lot number, since fragrance-oil scent profiles can shift subtly lot to lot and a maker fulfilling a large reorder needs to know whether the fragrance oil on hand matches the lot used in the original batch closely enough, or whether a new lot needs a small formula adjustment noted for next time. It also needs a wholesale-account order and reorder tracker distinct from one-off craft-fair sales, recording each account's standing SKU list, reorder cadence, and outstanding invoice status, since a maker supplying 5-15 boutique accounts needs to know who's due to reorder before they run out on the shelf. Most independent candle makers currently manage this across a paper batch notebook and a spreadsheet of craft-fair sales, with no structured link between a specific batch's exact formula and the wholesale order it needs to match.

The tool maintains a per-batch production log with formula and cure-time detail, a wax/fragrance/vessel inventory tracked by supplier lot, and a wholesale-account order and reorder tracker.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/candlemaking and r/soapmaking crossover communities, the Chandlers Guild and regional candle-maker association Facebook groups, and wholesale gift-market and boutique-buyer trade show exhibitor lists. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Custom Vinyl Decal & Sign-Cutting Shop Proof-Approval & Roll-Inventory Tracker

Independent vinyl decal and sign-cutting shop operators producing custom decals, vehicle lettering, and small business signage need to track a per-order design-proof approval cycle, since almost every custom order — a personalized tumbler decal, a business's vehicle-door lettering, a yard sign — requires the customer to sign off on a digital proof before anything gets cut, and a shop that cuts vinyl before final approval risks wasting material on a design the customer wanted changed. The tool also needs a roll-vinyl and transfer-tape inventory tracked by color and finish, since a shop stocking 30-50 vinyl colors and finishes (matte, gloss, chrome, glitter) needs to know what's on hand before quoting a job that specifies an exact color match, and running out of a commonly used color mid-order stalls production. It also needs a production queue spanning small same-day decal orders alongside larger sign and vehicle-wrap jobs that take days, since a shop owner juggling both order types needs to see which quick jobs can be knocked out between longer projects rather than letting small orders pile up behind a big one. Most independent vinyl/sign shop operators currently manage this across an email inbox of proof approvals and a rough visual check of the vinyl rack, with no structured link between a specific order's proof-approval status and whether it's actually cleared to cut.

The tool maintains a per-order proof-approval tracker gating cutting until the customer signs off, a roll-vinyl/transfer-tape inventory by color and finish, and a production queue spanning quick decal orders and longer sign/wrap jobs.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/vinylcutting and r/signage, the Sign Association of your state/regional sign-trade groups, and Etsy-seller and craft-business Facebook groups where custom decal shop owners already discuss production workflow. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
