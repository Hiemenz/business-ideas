# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 30)

### 1. BJJ & Martial Arts Academy Belt Promotion & Stripe Tracker
Brazilian jiu-jitsu academies and martial arts schools tracking student belt progression have a universal problem: no system that shows a coach, at a glance, which students are overdue for a stripe or belt promotion review. The coach knows in their head that a student has been at blue belt for two years, but can't easily pull up how many months each student has been at their current rank, how regularly they attend (since consistent attendance is the most common promotion criterion), and which students the coach has flagged as "promotion-ready." This tool maintains a per-student rank history (start date at each belt and stripe level, promotion dates logged as they happen, current rank, and attendance pattern from a simple weekly check-in log), and gives the coach a dashboard sorted by time-at-current-rank: which white belts have been at four stripes for 3+ months, which blue belts are approaching the minimum time for purple, which students have consistent attendance. A promotion ceremony export generates a formatted list for the coach's class announcement. Monetization: $15-25/mo per academy. Distribution: r/bjj, BJJ Belt Rank Facebook communities, IBJJF affiliate school networks, Martial Arts on Business (MAB) academy owner communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Real Estate Photographer Shoot Scheduler & Edited Gallery Delivery Tracker
Real estate photographers shooting 5-20 properties per week manage a constant juggle: confirming shoot times with listing agents, tracking which shoots have been edited and which galleries have been delivered, and following up on the (frequent) cases where a gallery is delivered but the agent hasn't confirmed receipt or approved it for MLS upload. This tool maintains a per-shoot record: property address, listing agent contact, shoot date and time, package type (photos only, photos + virtual tour, photos + floor plan), estimated turnaround, editing completion date, gallery delivery link, and agent confirmation status. A weekly dashboard shows: shoots this week, shoots awaiting editing, galleries delivered but unconfirmed, and galleries approved and delivered. Automated delivery email drafts (sent from the photographer's template) reduce the repetitive communication that consumes 30 minutes per day. Monetization: $15-20/mo per photographer account. Distribution: r/RealEstate photographer communities, Real Estate Photography Facebook groups, Canopy MLS and Zillow photographer community forums, PFRE (Photography for Real Estate) community. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Wedding Planner Vendor Payment Schedule & Deposit Tracker
Wedding planners managing 15-40 active weddings per year spend meaningful time tracking vendor payment schedules: every caterer, venue, photographer, florist, and band has a contract with a deposit amount (usually 25-50% at signing) and a balance due date (typically 2-4 weeks before the wedding). Missing a vendor payment deadline triggers late fee clauses and in some cases vendor cancellation rights. This tool maintains a per-wedding vendor payment ledger: each vendor name, total contract amount, deposit amount and paid status, balance amount and due date, and payment method used. A 30-day upcoming payment calendar shows every payment due across all active weddings sorted by due date — the planner's daily morning check. A wedding-level payment summary shows the couple their total vendor commitment and what's been paid vs. outstanding. When a payment is made, the planner logs it with date and reference number. Monetization: $18-28/mo per planner account (unlimited weddings). Distribution: r/weddingplanning, Wedding Planning Facebook groups for professionals, The Knot Pro and WeddingWire Pro planner communities, NACE (National Association for Catering and Events) member communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Indoor Plant Care Service Client Plant Inventory & Visit Log
Independent plant care service providers — "plant nannies" who visit client homes and offices on a regular schedule to water, fertilize, prune, and treat plants — currently manage their client plant records in notes apps or from memory. When a client adds a plant or a plant needs a specific treatment, there's no consistent record. This tool maintains a per-client plant inventory: plant common name, species if known, location in the home or office, pot size and soil type, last watered date, watering frequency, fertilizer schedule, and any ongoing treatments (pest, disease). After each visit, the service provider logs what was done for each plant (watered, fertilized, repotted, pruned, treated) and any notes (new growth observed, signs of distress, client request). The client gets a brief post-visit summary email generated from the visit log. A per-plant care calendar shows the provider which plants at which clients need fertilizing or treatment on the next visit. Monetization: $12-18/mo per provider account (unlimited clients). Distribution: r/plantclinic and r/houseplants plant care enthusiast communities, INPCA (Interior Plantscape Association) member communities, houseplant service provider Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Podcast Editor Client Episode Delivery & Publish Schedule Tracker
Freelance podcast editors managing 5-15 active podcast clients per month track episode delivery through an inbox-and-memory system: recording files come in at inconsistent times, editing takes variable time depending on episode length and complexity, and the client's publish schedule creates a hard deadline the editor must hit. Missing a client's publish date — even by a day — damages the relationship. This tool maintains a per-client episode queue: client name, show name, episode title, raw audio received date, promised delivery date, editing status (received/in-progress/delivered), delivered file link, and client approval status. A weekly workload view shows all episodes in progress across all clients sorted by due date — the editor's prioritization tool. When an episode is delivered, the system generates a delivery email draft with the file link. A per-client earnings tracker (flat fee per episode or hourly rate) calculates monthly revenue from each client. Monetization: $12-18/mo per editor account. Distribution: r/podcasting editor communities, Podcast Movement conference community, podcast editor Facebook groups, Descript and Adobe Audition power user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. Community Garden Plot Assignment & Seasonal Waiting List Manager
Community garden coordinators managing 20-100 garden plots handle plot assignment, plot holder renewals, waitlist management, and plot abandonment removals as an ongoing administrative workload — typically in a shared spreadsheet that multiple board members update, creating version conflicts and data loss. This tool maintains a plot map (plot number, dimensions, location in the garden, current holder name, annual fee status, and any plot-specific notes like raised bed, water access, or tool storage), a waitlist (applicant name, contact, application date, plot size preference, and position in queue), and a seasonal renewal workflow (generating renewal reminder emails to each plot holder with their annual fee amount and due date, tracking payment received, and automatically flagging non-renewed plots for reassignment to the next person on the waitlist). When a plot becomes available, the system generates a waitlist offer email to the next eligible applicant. Coordinator-facing dashboard shows: plots rented vs. available, overdue fee payments, and waitlist length. Monetization: $8-15/mo per garden account or $50-80/year flat. Distribution: American Community Gardening Association (ACGA) coordinator networks, r/vegetablegardening, Nextdoor community group administrator communities, local parks and recreation department community garden program networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
