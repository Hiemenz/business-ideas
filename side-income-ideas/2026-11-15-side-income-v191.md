# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-15 (Round 191)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: an aquarium service tech, a reptile breeder, a collectibles dealer, a pet-waste removal operator, a golf-cart detailer and a mobile windshield-chip repairer.

---

### 1. Aquarium Service Tech Tank, Visit & Supply Tracker

A tech who maintains home and office aquariums has to track each tank's size, livestock, water readings, visit schedule and supplies. Each tank has different needs, and a missed visit can cost fish.

The tool tracks tanks and visit schedules, logs water readings, tracks supplies used and sends a monthly summary to the customer.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per tech. Distribution: aquarist communities, r/Aquariums and local fish-store networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Reptile Breeder Clutch, Hatch & Sales Tracker

A breeder has to track pairings, clutch dates, incubation temperatures, hatch dates, genetics and sales. Pairings and lineage affect value, and buyers want hatch records.

The tool tracks pairings and clutches, records hatch results and genetics, and manages listings and buyer deposits.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per breeder. Distribution: breeder communities, r/reptiles and expo vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Collectibles Dealer Item, Cost & Sale Tracker

A dealer in comics, cards or toys has to track each item's cost, grade, listing price, platform and sale. Fees and shipping vary by platform, and profit per item is easy to lose track of.

The tool logs items with photos and purchase cost, tracks listings by platform, calculates fees and profit and shows slow-moving stock.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per dealer. Distribution: collector communities, r/comicbooks and card-dealer groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Pet-Waste Removal Route, Yard & Recurring-Plan Tracker

A pooper-scooper operator has to track customers by route day, yard notes (gate codes, dog count), visit logs and recurring billing. Gates and dogs change, and missed yards cause complaints.

The tool plans routes by day, stores yard notes, logs completed visits with a photo and bills recurring plans.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per operator. Distribution: pet-service groups, r/entrepreneur and local neighborhood groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Golf-Cart Detailer Job, Package & Repeat-Customer Tracker

A mobile detailer who cleans and customizes golf carts at communities has to track customers, packages (wash, wax, upholstery), add-ons and visit dates. Community managers can send many customers, and a reminder brings repeat work.

The tool tracks jobs and packages, schedules route days by community and sends a repeat reminder at 8 weeks.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per detailer. Distribution: detailing communities, r/Detailing and local community boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Mobile Windshield-Chip Repairer Job, Route & Insurance-Quote Tracker

A mobile repairer who fixes windshield chips has to track jobs, vehicle details, location, price and follow-up. Chips spread quickly, so timing sells the job, and fleet customers give repeat work.

The tool logs jobs with photos, plans daily routes, tracks fleet accounts and sends a reminder to check the repair after 30 days.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per repairer. Distribution: auto-service groups, r/Detailing and local fleet-manager networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
