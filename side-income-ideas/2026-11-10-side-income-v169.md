# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-10 (Round 169)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a local SEO freelancer, a webinar producer, a Notion template seller, a 3D-print on-demand seller, a real-estate drone photographer and an AI voice-agent setup freelancer.

---

### 1. Local SEO Freelancer Client Ranking, Task & Monthly Report Tracker

A freelancer who handles local SEO for plumbers, dentists and lawyers has to track each client's Google Business Profile tasks, citations, reviews requested and keyword rankings. With 8-15 clients, the monthly report is a time sink and the freelancer forgets which tasks were done for whom. Clients stay when they can see results.

The tool keeps a per-client task checklist, a log of ranking and call-count snapshots and a one-click monthly summary.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per freelancer. Distribution: local-SEO communities, r/localseo and agency-owner Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 2. Webinar Producer Event, Registrant & Replay Follow-Up Tracker

A producer who runs webinars for coaches and software companies has to track each event's registration numbers, show-up rate, offer conversions and replay-link follow-ups. With several events a month, which email reminders were sent and what the show-rate was for each is easy to lose. Clients judge the producer by registrants-to-attendees-to-sales.

The tool logs events with registrations, attendance and sales, schedules reminder and replay emails, and compares show rates across events.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per producer. Distribution: webinar and launch-manager groups, Skool agency communities and r/Entrepreneur. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 3. Notion Template Seller Product, Sales & Customer-Update Tracker

A seller of Notion and spreadsheet templates through Gumroad, Etsy and their own site has to track product versions, where each is listed, sales per channel and customer update emails. When a template changes, past buyers should get the new version, and few sellers keep a record of who bought which version. Reviews and refund requests also need a log.

The tool tracks products with versions and listing locations, sales per channel, buyer update lists and a review/refund log.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per seller. Distribution: Notion creator communities, r/Notion and Gumroad seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. 3D-Print On-Demand Seller Order, Filament & Print-Queue Tracker

A hobbyist who sells 3D-printed products on Etsy and a personal shop has to track orders, print times, filament stock and the queue for several printers. A late print delays shipping, and running out of a filament color mid-job wastes hours. Profit per item is also unclear without the cost of filament and machine time.

The tool shows an order queue by printer with estimated finish times, filament stock with a low-stock alert, and per-item cost and margin.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per seller. Distribution: 3D-printing communities, r/3Dprinting and Etsy seller forums. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Real-Estate Drone Photographer Shoot Schedule, Delivery & Invoice Tracker

A drone photographer who shoots listings for real-estate agents has to track bookings, weather reschedules, editing deadlines and delivery. Agents want photos before the listing goes live, so a missed delivery loses the client. Many agents pay late, and invoices from a dozen agents get lost.

The tool tracks shoots with a weather-reschedule flag, an edit-and-deliver countdown to each listing's go-live date, and invoices with an overdue reminder.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per photographer. Distribution: real-estate-media Facebook groups, r/dronephotography and local agent networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. AI Voice-Agent Setup Freelancer Client Script, Call-Log & Change-Request Tracker

A freelancer who builds AI phone receptionists for dentists, salons and home-service companies has to track each client's call script, business-hours rules, call outcomes and change requests. Clients report "it said the wrong price," and the freelancer needs to find that call and update the script. With 6-12 clients, versions and fixes get mixed up.

The tool stores the script version per client, logs sampled call outcomes with problems found, tracks change requests and reports bookings made by the agent.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per freelancer. Distribution: AI-agency communities, r/AI_Agents and automation Skool groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.
