# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 26)

### 1. Lawn Care & Landscaping Weekly Route Sheet Generator
Lawn care and landscaping business owners with 20-60 weekly residential accounts schedule their crews' routes in their heads or in a text thread, spending 30-60 minutes each Sunday rearranging stops to minimize drive time and accommodate customer hold requests or one-time add-on services (mulch delivery, gutter cleaning, fall cleanup). This tool maintains the customer roster with each account's address, service frequency (weekly, biweekly, monthly), and active/hold status, and generates a weekly route sheet: stops sorted geographically to minimize backtrack, any add-on services flagged per stop, hold accounts removed automatically, and a crew-facing PDF that lists stops in order with the service notes for each. Monetization: $15-25/mo per business. Distribution: lawn care owner Facebook groups, GreenIndustryPros community, r/lawncare (owner side), Jobber and Housecall Pro user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Youth Baseball & Softball Pitch Count and Innings Tracker
Youth baseball leagues — Little League, Pony Baseball, USSSA — enforce pitch count limits that restrict how many pitches a player can throw per game and mandate rest days before a pitcher can throw again based on the total pitches thrown. Coaches track this with a paper scorecard or memory during a game, frequently losing track mid-game or discovering after the fact that a pitcher exceeded their limit. This tool provides a live game-scoring interface: enter the pitcher's name, start each pitch sequence with a tap, and the app tracks cumulative pitch count for the game, displays the pitcher's remaining pitch budget under their age group's limit, and alerts when the pitcher is within 10 pitches of the daily limit or has entered a rest-day zone. After the game, the pitch count log for each player is saved to their season history so the coach can see who is available to pitch on which days of the tournament. Monetization: $8-15/mo per team or $25-40/season flat. Distribution: Little League team parent Facebook groups, USSSA baseball coach communities, GameChanger user communities, youth sports parent forums. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Beauty Salon & Barbershop No-Show Deposit Request Generator
Salons and barbershops that take appointments for color services, extensions, or long bookings lose $80-200 each time a client no-shows or cancels within 24 hours without paying a deposit. Implementing a deposit policy requires telling existing clients about it — a conversation many owners avoid because they're unsure how to phrase it without sounding accusatory. This tool generates a personalized, professional deposit request message for a specific client (selecting the tone — warm, firm, or direct — and the service type) and a policy announcement message for the owner's full client list explaining the new deposit requirement and how to pay. Also generates a terms-of-service one-pager the owner can text or email to clients at booking confirmation. Monetization: $10-18/mo subscription or $25 one-time purchase. Distribution: salon owner Facebook groups, r/hairstylist, Vagaro and Square Appointments user communities, barbershop owner communities. Time to first dollar: 1 week.

### 4. Freelance Web Developer Client Site Maintenance Log
Web developers who maintain client websites on a retainer basis — applying plugin updates, monitoring uptime, renewing SSL certificates, running security scans, and making content edits — need to document what work was done each month to justify their retainer fee and protect themselves when a client disputes what services were delivered. Most track this in a notes app or don't track it at all. This tool maintains a per-client site maintenance log: update entries by date (plugin updated, backup run, SSL renewed, content change made, support ticket resolved), generates a monthly maintenance summary PDF for the client email update ("Here's what we did on your site this month"), and tracks upcoming renewals (domain registration, hosting renewal, SSL certificate) with alerts at 60 and 30 days. Monetization: $12-20/mo per developer account (unlimited client sites). Distribution: r/webdev, Indie Hackers freelancer communities, WP Engine and Flywheel partner communities, Duda and Squarespace designer communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Community Theater Audition Sign-Up & Casting Grid Tool
Community theater directors managing auditions for a production with 15-40 roles and 40-100 auditionees use paper sign-in sheets and a manually maintained casting grid that becomes chaotic as callbacks are added and role decisions are revised. This tool provides a digital audition sign-up page (shareable link where auditionees enter their name, contact, age range, which roles they're auditioning for, and any scheduling conflicts for the rehearsal calendar), generates the director's audition day schedule (time slots for each auditionee based on which roles they're read for), and a casting grid view (all roles down one axis, all auditionees across the other, with a scoring or notation field per cell for the director's callback and casting decisions). When casting decisions are finalized, generates an acceptance/callback/thank-you email draft for each category. Monetization: $10-20 per production or $30-50/year for theater companies running multiple shows. Distribution: American Association of Community Theatre (AACT) member networks, r/communitytheater, theater director Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. 4-H Club Member Project Record Book Helper
4-H members ages 8-18 are required to maintain annual project record books documenting their project work, goals, activities, expenses, income, and what they learned — submitted at county fairs and used for competition judging and scholarship applications. Most members find the record book format confusing and procrastinate until the week before the fair, submitting incomplete books that underrepresent their actual project work. This tool walks the member (or their parent) through structured weekly or monthly project log entries throughout the year: activities completed, skills learned, items purchased or materials used, items sold or produced, and reflection notes. At fair time, it compiles the entries into a formatted project record book ready to print and submit, pre-organized to match the county's standard record book sections. Monetization: $5-10/year per member (sold to 4-H families directly or licensed to county 4-H programs). Distribution: 4-H parent Facebook groups, county 4-H coordinator networks, National 4-H Council community resources. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
