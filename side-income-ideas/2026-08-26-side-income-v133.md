# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-26 (Round 133)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Luggage & Travel Bag Repair Specialist Intake, Parts-Match & Warranty Tracker

An independent luggage repair specialist fixing broken zippers, snapped handles, and torn wheels on suitcases and travel bags has to match every repair to the exact hardware a specific bag brand and model calls for, since a zipper pull or a wheel assembly that looks close enough often isn't interchangeable across brands, and a repair started before the right replacement part is confirmed in stock is a bag torn apart on the bench with no way to finish the job that day. A repairer also has to track whether a bag is still under the manufacturer's own warranty, since sending a customer's Rimowa or Away suitcase back to the brand for a free warranty repair instead of billing for the work out of pocket is the difference between a happy customer and a wasted labor cost the shop eats. A specialist running a rotating queue of drop-off repairs across different brands and damage types currently tracks intake notes and parts-order status on a claim ticket taped to each bag and a supplier order pad, and a bag reassembled with the wrong wheel because nobody checked the model number twice, or a warranty-eligible repair billed to the customer because nobody thought to check the purchase date, is a refund and a customer who takes their next broken suitcase somewhere else.

The tool maintains a per-bag intake record capturing brand, model, and damage type with a hardware-parts-match reference and stock-on-hand flag before a repair is started; a manufacturer-warranty checker flagging any bag still inside its brand's warranty window before billing the customer directly; and a repair-queue board sequencing drop-offs against parts availability so nothing sits half-disassembled waiting on a backordered zipper pull. Monetization: $12-19/mo per repairer. Distribution: r/backpacking and luggage-repair-adjacent travel-gear Facebook groups, the Airline Baggage Handlers' referral networks that funnel damaged-bag claims to local repair shops, and shoe/leather-repair trade association directories where independent luggage repairers often cross-list. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. CNC Machining & Custom Precision Fabrication Job Shop Quote-to-Ship, Tooling & Material-Cert Tracker

An independent one- or two-person CNC machine shop taking on custom precision parts for local manufacturers and product designers has to quote every job against the exact material, tolerance, and finish spec a customer's drawing calls for, since a part machined to a standard tolerance when the drawing actually called for a tighter spec is scrap stock and a wasted material cost the shop can't bill for. A shop also has to track which specific tool — a particular end mill, a specific insert — each job's program calls for, and whether that tool's remaining life can actually finish the run, since a tool that wears out mid-batch on a multi-part order is a batch of parts with a visible finish defect discovered only during final inspection. Many customers, especially in aerospace-adjacent or medical-device work, also require a material certification (a mill cert or heat-treat cert) traceable to the specific stock lot a part was cut from, and a shipped part with no traceable cert on file is a shipment the customer's own quality department will reject on receipt regardless of how well the part actually measures. A shop owner running several jobs at once across a mix of one-off prototypes and repeat production runs currently tracks job specs, tool life, and material certs on a shop-floor traveler sheet and a filing cabinet of supplier paperwork, with no systematic cross-check confirming a specific job's tolerance, tooling, and material cert are all actually ready before that part ships.

The tool maintains a per-job quote-to-ship traveler capturing material, tolerance, and finish spec that follows the part through every operation; a tool-life tracker counting remaining usable life against each job's required run length with a swap alert before a batch is put at risk; and a material-certification log tying each shipped part back to its specific stock lot's mill or heat-treat cert. Monetization: $19-29/mo per shop. Distribution: r/machining and r/CNC practitioner communities, the National Tooling and Machining Association (NTMA) regional chapter network, and local manufacturer supplier-day trade shows where independent job shops already trade tolerance-tracking and traceability tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Production Pottery Studio & Ceramics Kiln Operator Batch-Firing, Glaze-Recipe & Wholesale-Account Tracker

An independent production potter throwing dinnerware and wholesale ceramics has to track every piece through its full firing schedule — bisque fire, glaze application, final glaze fire — since a piece pulled from the kiln at the wrong stage or fired on the wrong cone temperature for its specific clay body can crack, warp, or come out with a glaze that never properly matured. A potter running a rotating stock of glaze recipes also has to track each glaze's exact formula and firing-cone requirement, since two glazes that look similar in the bucket can require different cone temperatures, and a shelf of mismatched pieces loaded into the same firing because nobody double-checked which glaze needed which cone is a kiln load of ruined work discovered only after it's already cooled. A potter fulfilling standing wholesale orders to local shops and restaurants alongside studio retail sales currently tracks firing schedules and glaze recipes on a whiteboard by the kiln and wholesale orders on a notepad, and a batch fired at the wrong cone because the glaze log was out of date, or a restaurant reorder missed because nobody was tracking that account's usual pace, is a kiln's worth of wasted clay and fuel or a lost wholesale account in a business that runs on both technical firing precision and reliable restock timing.

The tool maintains a per-batch firing-schedule tracker moving each piece through bisque and glaze fire stages with the correct cone temperature flagged per glaze; a glaze-recipe library recording each formula's ingredients and required firing cone, cross-checked against what's loaded into each kiln run; and a wholesale-account tracker showing each shop's standing order size and typical reorder cadence so a restock never gets missed. Monetization: $15-25/mo per studio. Distribution: r/Pottery and production-potter Facebook groups, the National Council on Education for the Ceramic Arts (NCECA) member network and its annual conference, and local craft-fair and wholesale-gift-show vendor associations where independent potters already trade firing-schedule and wholesale-account tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Bounce House & Inflatable Party Rental Operator Booking, Blower-Fleet & Setup-Site Tracker

An independent bounce house and inflatable rental operator has to match every booking to a specific unit in the fleet — a standard bounce house, a combo unit with a slide, an obstacle course — since a unit promised for a Saturday birthday party that's actually still packed wet from Friday's rental is a truck arriving at a client's driveway with nothing to set up. An operator also has to assess each booking's setup site before the truck rolls, since a unit that needs a flat 20-by-20 grass pad and standard electrical access can't go into a driveway with an overhead power line too close or a yard with no outlet within the blower's cord length, and a site issue discovered only at delivery is a canceled party and a refund. Every blower motor in the fleet also has to be tracked for wear and maintenance, since a blower that cuts out mid-party deflates the inflatable in front of a yard full of kids and is the fastest way to lose a rental deposit dispute. An operator running a full weekend of back-to-back deliveries across a scattered service area currently tracks unit availability, site requirements, and blower maintenance on a garage whiteboard and a driver's memory, and a double-booked unit or a site that couldn't actually support the delivery, discovered only when the truck is already there, is a client who won't rebook in a referral-driven local business.

The tool maintains a per-unit booking calendar cross-checked against cleaning/dry-out turnaround time between rentals; a site-assessment checklist capturing setup-space dimensions, ground surface, and electrical-access requirements collected at booking time; and a blower-fleet maintenance tracker flagging any motor due for service before its next scheduled delivery. Monetization: $19-29/mo per operator. Distribution: r/EventPlanning and bounce-house/inflatable-rental operator Facebook groups, The Bash and GigSalad vendor community boards where inflatable rental operators already list their services, and regional party-rental trade association meetups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Sauna & Cold Plunge Rental Operator Booking, Water-Turnover & Temperature-Log Tracker

An independent operator running a mobile wood-fired or electric sauna trailer alongside cold-plunge tubs has to track each unit's water and temperature status before every booking, since a cold plunge that hasn't had its water turned over and chilled back down between back-to-back sessions is an unusable tub the next client discovers only after they've already shown up, and a sauna that hasn't reached its target temperature by the scheduled start time is a booking that starts late and runs short. An operator also has to track each plunge tub's filtration and sanitation cycle — a shared cold-plunge tub used by multiple clients in a single day needs its water quality checked and treated between uses, since a tub that goes a full booking day without a water-quality check is a liability the operator doesn't want tied to a client's skin reaction or infection complaint. A mobile operator running a full week of site visits across gyms, private events, and residential bookings currently tracks unit temperature readiness and water-turnover timing on a driver's phone notes and memory, and a sauna that wasn't hot enough at delivery or a plunge tub whose water quality nobody checked between three back-to-back bookings, is a bad review in a wellness business that depends entirely on the experience matching what was promised.

The tool maintains a per-unit booking calendar with a pre-arrival temperature-readiness countdown for each sauna and target-chill countdown for each cold plunge; a water-turnover and filtration log tracking time-since-last-treatment for every plunge tub in the fleet; and a route-scheduling board sequencing the week's mobile deliveries against each unit's setup and teardown time. Monetization: $19-29/mo per operator. Distribution: r/coldplunge and r/sauna wellness-entrepreneur communities, mobile-wellness and cold-plunge-operator Facebook groups, and local gym/wellness-studio partnership networks where independent mobile sauna and cold-plunge operators already trade booking and water-quality tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. In-Home Pet Euthanasia & Veterinary Hospice Provider Case-Scheduling, Medication & Aftercare Tracker

An independent mobile veterinarian providing in-home pet euthanasia and hospice care has to schedule each visit against a family's specific timeline while also tracking a hospice patient's ongoing pain-management and medication schedule between visits, since a hospice case that's supposed to get a comfort-medication check every few days that slips through the cracks is a pet the family sees suffering with no way for the vet to know until the next scheduled visit. A provider also has to track each euthanasia appointment's controlled-substance dosage against the animal's specific weight and condition, since dosing calculations have to be right every single time in a procedure a grieving family is present for, and a mobile practice carrying its own limited controlled-substance stock has to reconcile what's been administered against what's on hand after every visit. Every case also carries its own aftercare arrangement — private cremation with ashes returned, communal cremation, or a family's own burial plans — and a mixed-up aftercare request, discovered only when the wrong urn comes back or ashes go to the wrong family, is a devastating mistake in a business built entirely on trust during someone's worst day. A solo mobile provider running a full week of scheduled hospice check-ins and end-of-life appointments across a service area currently tracks medication schedules, dosage calculations, and aftercare requests on a paper case file and a phone calendar, with no systematic link between a hospice patient's medication due-date and the aftercare paperwork tied to that same family's eventual appointment.

The tool maintains a per-patient hospice medication and comfort-check schedule with a due-date reminder between visits; a controlled-substance dosage calculator and inventory-reconciliation log tied to each euthanasia appointment; and an aftercare-request tracker recording each family's cremation or burial preference through to confirmed delivery. Monetization: $25-39/mo per provider. Distribution: the International Association for Animal Hospice and Palliative Care (IAAHPC) member directory, r/VetTech and mobile-veterinary-practice Facebook groups, and local veterinary referral networks where in-home hospice and euthanasia providers already receive case referrals from general-practice clinics. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
