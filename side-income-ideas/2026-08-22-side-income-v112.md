# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-22 (Round 112)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Print-on-Demand Shop Operator Design Catalog & Multi-Platform Order Tracker

An independent print-on-demand seller running a storefront across Etsy, Printful/Printify-fulfilled Shopify, and Amazon Merch simultaneously manages a growing catalog of designs, each mapped to a different set of product types (t-shirts, mugs, tumblers, posters) and a different mockup image per platform, while sales trickle in from three or four channels at once with different fulfillment partners and different payout schedules. A seller running 150-400 live designs across multiple storefronts currently tracks which design is listed on which platform, which mockup file goes with which product variant, and which designs are actually profitable after each platform's fulfillment cost and referral fee on a spreadsheet that falls out of date the moment a new design goes live, and a design pulled for a trademark complaint on one platform but left live on another, or a best-selling design whose per-unit margin nobody recalculated after a fulfillment-cost increase, quietly bleeds profit every seller assumes is there.

The tool maintains a design catalog (source file, product-type mappings, and per-platform listing status) with a per-platform mockup and listing-copy library, a margin calculator computing true profit per design after each platform's print cost, shipping, and referral fee, and a best/worst-seller ranking view refreshed from imported sales-order CSVs across all connected storefronts.

Monetization: $19-35/mo per seller account. Distribution: r/printondemand and the Merch Informer/Placeit power-user communities, plus print-on-demand seller Facebook groups where operators already trade margin-tracking and multi-platform listing workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Petting Zoo & Pony Ride Rental Operator Event Booking & Animal Health-Record Tracker

A mobile petting zoo and pony ride operator books events — birthday parties, school fairs, corporate picnics, church festivals — that each require a specific mix of animals (a pony for rides, plus goats, rabbits, and a mini donkey for the petting pen), a trailer loaded with the right portable fencing and feed, and, since these are live animals appearing at a public event, a current health certificate and vaccination/Coggins-test record per animal that the venue or county fair often requires to see before setup. An operator running a roster of a dozen animals across fifteen to twenty weekend events during peak spring-to-fall season currently tracks which animals are booked for which event, each animal's current vaccination and health-certificate expiration, and a rest/rotation schedule so no single animal works every weekend, on a paper booking calendar and a folder of vet records, and an animal booked to an event past its health-certificate expiration is exactly the gap a county fair coordinator or a concerned parent is quick to ask about, while an over-worked animal with no rest rotation is a welfare problem that costs the business its reputation.

The tool maintains a per-animal profile (species, temperament notes, and vaccination/health-certificate/Coggins-test expiration dates) with an event-booking calendar assigning specific animals to specific events and flagging any animal booked past a document's expiration, a rest-rotation view showing each animal's bookings over the trailing 30 days, and an equipment/trailer-loadout checklist per event type.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per operator. Distribution: mobile petting zoo and pony ride operator Facebook groups and the American Petting Zoo Association's informal operator network, where independent operators already trade booking and animal-rotation workflow tips ahead of spring event season. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks (timed ahead of spring/summer event season).

### 3. Professional Tarot Reader & Astrologer Session Booking & Client Reading-History Tracker

An independent tarot reader or astrologer building a client base through in-person readings, video-call sessions, and recurring monthly subscribers needs to remember what came up in a specific client's last reading — which cards or transits were significant, what the client was asking about, and what follow-up the reader promised to check on next time — since a returning client who has to re-explain their situation from scratch because the reader kept no notes is a client who doesn't rebook. A reader running twenty to forty active clients across one-off sessions and recurring monthly subscribers currently tracks session notes in a personal journal or scattered notes app with no structured link between a specific client and their reading history, and a client's birth-chart data (for astrology clients, exact birth date, time, and location, which must be re-entered correctly every time a new chart element is calculated) sits buried in an old text thread, making even a five-minute chart lookup a scavenger hunt before a paying session starts.

The tool maintains a per-client profile (birth data for astrology clients, reading preferences, and a running session-history log noting date, spread or transit focus, key cards or placements discussed, and any follow-up promised) with a booking calendar for one-off and recurring sessions, and a subscription/package-credit tracker for clients on a recurring monthly reading plan.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per reader. Distribution: r/tarotreaders and r/astrology practitioner threads, the Tarot Professionals Facebook group, and independent metaphysical-practitioner directories where readers already trade client-notes and booking workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Office Plantscaping & Interior Plant Maintenance Service Client Site & Care-Visit Tracker

An interior plantscaping company that leases and maintains live plants inside office lobbies, restaurant dining rooms, and medical waiting rooms runs a recurring biweekly or monthly maintenance route across dozens of client sites, each with its own plant inventory (a six-foot fiddle-leaf fig by the reception desk, a row of pothos along a window ledge) that needs watering, pruning, fertilizing, and periodic pest-treatment on a plant-specific schedule, plus a rotating stock of leased plants that get swapped out when one declines beyond saving. An operator maintaining forty to eighty client sites currently tracks each site's plant inventory, the specific care needs and last-service date per plant, and which plants are due for a swap-out on a paper route sheet and a mental inventory of the warehouse's backup stock, and a plant that quietly declines between visits because nobody flagged it for extra attention is a client site that starts to look neglected — exactly the kind of visible failure that gets a maintenance contract canceled at renewal.

The tool maintains a per-site plant inventory (species, location within the site, and container/leasing terms) with a per-plant care-history log noting watering, pruning, fertilizing, and pest-treatment dates, a route-scheduling board sequencing biweekly/monthly visits across all client sites, and a swap-out queue flagging plants whose condition notes indicate they're due for replacement from warehouse stock.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per plantscaping company account (unlimited client sites). Distribution: the Associated Landscape Contractors of America's interior plantscape division and regional interior-plantscaping Facebook groups, where small operators already trade route-scheduling and plant-health workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Henna & Face Painting Artist Booking, Design Library & Event Kit Tracker

An independent henna and face painting artist works weekend bridal parties, birthday parties, and community festivals back-to-back, each event needing a specific design consultation beforehand (a bride's requested henna pattern coverage and placement, or a festival's expected volume of walk-up face-painting requests), a portfolio of prior designs to show new clients during booking, and a fully stocked kit of henna cones or face paints in the specific color palette a client requested, since running out of a specific paint color mid-event with a line of kids still waiting is a refund and a bad review. An artist juggling eight to fifteen bookings a month across bridal, birthday, and festival gigs currently tracks each client's design request and event details in text-message threads and a phone photo album with no structured link between a booking and the specific design discussed, and a henna artist who shows up to a bridal event without confirming the exact coverage (hands only versus hands-and-forearms) discussed at booking is a bride whose expectations weren't met on her wedding day.

The tool maintains a booking calendar with per-event design-consultation notes (requested pattern, coverage area, or color palette) linked to a searchable portfolio of past design photos organized by style and occasion, a supply/kit inventory tracker flagging low henna-cone or paint-color stock before the next big event, and a deposit/balance payment tracker per booking.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per artist. Distribution: r/henna and r/facepainting practitioner threads, the World Henna Exchange community, and local bridal-vendor Facebook groups where independent henna and face-painting artists already trade booking and portfolio workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Custom Resin Art & Epoxy Tumbler Maker Commission & Cure-Time Batch Tracker

An independent resin artist making custom epoxy tumblers, river tables, and coasters to order runs each commission through a multi-stage cure schedule — a base coat that must fully cure before a design layer is poured, a design layer that needs its own cure window before a clear topcoat seals it, with cure times running anywhere from eight hours to several days depending on the resin brand and ambient humidity — while juggling ten to twenty concurrent commissions at different cure stages on a shelving rack, each with its own client-specified color palette, glitter or additive mix, and personalization text or image. A maker running a full rack of commissions at once currently tracks which piece is at which cure stage, which color/additive formula was mixed for which client's order, and when each piece is finally safe to move to the next pour on a sticky-note system taped to each cup, and a piece moved to its next layer before fully curing is a ruined commission and a client who ordered a custom gift for an event date that's now impossible to hit.

The tool maintains a commission queue by cure stage (base coat, design layer, topcoat, cured/ready-to-ship) with a per-piece cure-timer countdown computed from the specific resin product's cure-time spec and the shop's ambient temperature, a per-client color/additive formula and personalization-spec log for repeat and matching-set orders, and a batch material-cost tracker reconciling resin, pigment, and glitter usage against each commission's price.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per maker. Distribution: r/ResinCasting and r/epoxy, the Let's Resin and ArtResin brand user communities, and craft-fair vendor Facebook groups where independent resin artists already trade cure-schedule and commission-tracking workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
