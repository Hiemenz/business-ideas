# Side Income Ideas - 2026-09-01 (Round 159)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow trackers for six operator types not yet covered in this repo: an independent chess coach, a self-service laundromat owner, a valet parking company operator, an independent airport shuttle operator, a cat cafe operator, and a board game cafe operator.

---

### 1. Independent Chess Coach Student-Rating, Lesson-Plan & Tournament-Prep Tracker

An independent chess coach teaching private lessons and small group classes to scholastic and adult students has to track each student's current rating (USCF or FIDE) alongside a personalized curriculum plan — opening repertoire, tactical themes, endgame technique — since a coach running 15-30 students across staggered skill levels can't rely on memory for which student just finished a rook-endgame unit versus which one is still working through basic tactics, and a lesson that repeats material a student already covered is a session the parent or adult student feels was wasted. A coach also has to track each student's upcoming tournament schedule and tailor recent lessons toward that student's specific opening repertoire and time-control needs in the weeks before a rated event, since a student who shows up to a tournament having drilled the wrong opening because the coach lost track of the event date is a preventable result. Because coaches are often paid per lesson package (a 10-lesson block, a monthly retainer) rather than per single session, a coach also needs a simple way to track remaining lessons in each student's package and flag when a renewal conversation is due before the student's block runs out unnoticed.

The tool maintains a per-student profile (current rating, curriculum progress, and opening repertoire) with a lesson-plan log noting what was covered each session; a tournament-prep tracker flagging upcoming rated events per student with a countdown to tailor recent lessons around; and a lesson-package balance tracker showing remaining sessions per student with a renewal-due alert.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per coach. Distribution: the US Chess Federation (USCF) coach and club directory, r/chess coaching threads, and local scholastic chess league Facebook groups where independent coaches already trade curriculum and tournament-prep tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Self-Service Laundromat Owner Machine-Uptime, Revenue-Reconciliation & Vendor-Service Tracker

An independent laundromat owner running a coin- or card-operated facility has to track which specific washers and dryers are currently out of service against the facility's total machine count, since a broken dryer that sits unrepaired for a week is lost revenue on every single load that would have run through it, and an owner who only discovers a machine has been down for days during a walk-through has no way to know how much revenue that outage actually cost. An owner also has to reconcile daily coin-box or card-system revenue per machine against expected utilization, since a machine whose take has quietly dropped — a card reader malfunctioning, a coin mechanism jamming on certain denominations — is a problem an owner running an unattended facility has no way to catch without a machine-by-machine revenue baseline to compare against. Because most laundromats run equipment under a lease-to-own or per-machine service contract with a distributor (Speed Queen, Dexter, Wascomat dealers commonly service equipment this way), an owner also needs to track each machine's service-contract terms and next scheduled maintenance visit, since a machine that breaks down between contracted service visits with no clear record of what's covered is a billing dispute with the vendor the owner has no documentation to win.

The tool maintains a per-machine status board (in-service, out-of-service, awaiting parts) with downtime duration and estimated lost-revenue calculation; a daily revenue-reconciliation log per machine flagging any unit whose take has dropped meaningfully below its baseline; and a vendor service-contract tracker per machine with scheduled-maintenance and warranty-coverage status.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per facility. Distribution: the Coin Laundry Association (CLA) member directory and annual trade show, and r/laundromat and independent-laundromat-owner Facebook groups where owners already trade machine-uptime and revenue-reconciliation tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Valet Parking Company Ticket-Reconciliation, Claim-Damage & Staffing Tracker

An independent valet parking company contracting with restaurants, hotels, and event venues has to reconcile every ticket issued against every vehicle actually retrieved at the end of a shift, since a valet operation running several simultaneous events each with dozens to hundreds of cars has no room for a ticket that doesn't match a key at closeout — a mismatch discovered only when an angry customer is standing at the podium waiting for a car nobody can locate. A company also has to document each vehicle's pre-parking condition with a quick walk-around note or photo, since a damage claim filed days after a guest's car was valeted needs a contemporaneous record showing whether a scratch or dent existed before the company's attendant ever touched the vehicle, and a company with no pre-parking condition record has no defense against a claim it didn't cause. Because valet companies staff a rotating pool of part-time attendants across multiple simultaneous venue contracts on any given weekend night, an operator also needs a per-shift staffing assignment board matching certified, insured attendants to specific venue contracts, since dispatching an attendant to a venue without current coverage under the company's garage-keeper's liability insurance is a gap that surfaces only after an incident.

The tool maintains a per-shift ticket-and-key reconciliation log matching every issued ticket to a retrieved vehicle at closeout; a per-vehicle pre-parking condition note with an optional photo attached to the ticket number; and a per-shift staffing board assigning attendants to venue contracts with insurance-coverage status per attendant.

Monetization: $29-49/mo per company. Distribution: the National Parking Association's valet-operator resources and regional hospitality-association event-vendor directories where restaurants and hotels source valet contractors. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Airport Shuttle Operator Manifest, Flight-Delay & Multi-Stop Route Tracker

An independent airport shuttle operator running a small fleet of vans on scheduled or on-demand routes between a city and its airport has to track each day's passenger manifest against actual flight arrival and departure times, since a shared-ride shuttle timed to a passenger's flight that's delayed or moved up has to adjust the pickup window without stranding other passengers already booked on that same run, and an operator with no live link between a booking and its underlying flight status finds out about a schedule change only when the passenger calls confused about where the van is. An operator also has to plan multi-stop routes efficiently — a single shuttle run commonly picks up or drops off several passengers at different hotels or neighborhoods before or after the airport leg — since a poorly sequenced stop order adds real minutes to every passenger's trip and a driver improvising the route on the fly risks missing someone's flight window entirely. Because shuttle vehicles run daily, high-mileage routes, an operator also needs a per-vehicle maintenance and inspection tracker, since a van that breaks down mid-route on the way to a passenger's flight is a missed-flight liability the operator has no way to explain away without a maintenance record showing the vehicle was current on its scheduled service.

The tool maintains a daily passenger manifest linked to each booking's flight number with a live delay/schedule-change flag; a multi-stop route sequencer optimizing pickup and drop-off order against each passenger's flight time; and a per-vehicle maintenance and inspection-due tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per operator, scaling to $49/mo for a multi-van fleet. Distribution: the National Limousine Association's ground-transportation operator resources and r/rideshare and airport-shuttle-operator Facebook groups where independent operators already trade flight-tracking and route-sequencing tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Cat Cafe Operator Colony Health, Adoption-Pipeline & Session-Booking Tracker

An independent cat cafe operator — hosting a resident colony of adoptable cats in a dedicated lounge space alongside a food-and-beverage counter, typically in partnership with a local shelter or rescue — has to track each cat's individual health record (vaccination status, spay/neuter status, any medication schedule) and behavioral notes, since a cafe running a rotating colony of 10-20 cats can't rely on memory for which cat is due for a vet visit versus which one has a known food allergy staff need to flag to visiting guests, and a cat whose vaccination lapses unnoticed is a health-code and shelter-partnership risk the cafe can't afford. An operator also has to manage a timed-session booking system limiting how many guests are in the cat lounge at once (most cafes cap lounge occupancy for the cats' wellbeing and to meet local health-code separation between the food-service area and the animal space), since overbooking a session past the lounge's cat-to-guest ratio stresses the animals and is exactly the kind of visible problem that shows up in bad reviews. Because the entire point of the model is adoption, an operator also needs an adoption-pipeline tracker recording which cats are currently available, which have pending applications with the partner shelter, and which have been adopted and need to be replaced in the rotation, since a cafe that can't tell a guest asking about a specific cat whether it's still available is missing the exact moment that turns a cafe visit into an adoption.

The tool maintains a per-cat health and behavioral record with vaccination and vet-visit due dates; a timed-session booking calendar enforcing the lounge's maximum cat-to-guest ratio per slot; and an adoption-pipeline board tracking each cat's status from available through pending application to adopted, with a rotation-refresh alert.

Monetization: $29-49/mo per cafe. Distribution: the Cat Cafe Owners' informal national network and Facebook group, and partner-shelter/rescue coordinator relationships where cat cafes already source their resident colonies. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 6. Board Game Cafe Operator Library Checkout, Table-Reservation & Cover-Charge Tracker

An independent board game cafe operator running a library of several hundred to a thousand-plus board games alongside table seating and a food-and-beverage menu has to track which specific games are currently checked out to which table, since a library that size with no checkout system means staff spend the busiest hours of a Friday night hunting for a missing game box or fielding a complaint that a game is missing pieces nobody flagged after the last use. An operator also has to manage table reservations against a cover-charge or time-block model (many board game cafes charge a per-person cover for unlimited game access during a seated time block, distinct from a straight food-and-beverage tab), since double-booking a table across overlapping reservations or losing track of which party's cover charge was actually collected at a busy walk-in counter is revenue leakage an operator running a thin margin on food service can't absorb. Because a board game library needs regular upkeep, an operator also needs a per-game condition and completeness tracker flagging when a game is due for a piece-count check or has been reported damaged, since a cafe's whole value proposition collapses if guests keep pulling boxes off the shelf only to find missing pieces mid-game.

The tool maintains a per-game checkout log tied to a specific table and return-condition check; a table-reservation and cover-charge tracker enforcing the time-block model and flagging any table whose cover hasn't been collected; and a per-game condition/completeness tracker with a piece-count-check due date.

Monetization: $29-49/mo per cafe. Distribution: the Board Game Cafe Owners Facebook group and r/boardgames business-owner threads, and regional tabletop-industry trade show (Origins, PAX Unplugged) exhibitor networks where cafe owners already trade library-management and reservation-workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
