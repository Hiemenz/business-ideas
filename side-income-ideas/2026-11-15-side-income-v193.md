# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-15 (Round 193)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a grill-cleaning service, a stand-up comedian, a freelance video editor, a trivia host, a garage-sale organizer and a mural painter.

---

### 1. Grill-Cleaning Service Customer, Visit & Seasonal Tracker

A person who deep-cleans barbecue grills at customers' homes has to track each grill type, visit date, price and the next due date. Spring is the rush, and a yearly reminder drives repeat work.

The tool logs grill and job details with before and after photos, schedules routes and sends a spring reminder to past customers.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per cleaner. Distribution: home-service groups, r/BBQ and local neighborhood boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to spring.

### 2. Stand-Up Comedian Gig, Set & Booking Tracker

A working comedian has to track gigs, venues, pay, set lists, tested jokes and booker contacts. Bookers remember who was good, so follow-up matters, and travel costs eat into pay.

The tool logs gigs with pay and costs, tracks set lists and joke performance, stores booker contacts and reminds follow-up.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per comedian. Distribution: open-mic communities, r/standupcomedy and local comedy-club networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Freelance Video Editor Project, Revision & Invoice Tracker

A freelance video editor has to track projects, footage received, revision rounds, deadlines and payments. Clients ask for endless revisions, so a record of rounds supports a fair extra charge.

The tool tracks projects and deadlines, counts revision rounds, flags overage and tracks invoices.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per editor. Distribution: editor communities, r/VideoEditing and freelance groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Trivia Host Venue, Question & Booking Tracker

A trivia host who runs weekly pub nights has to track venues, pay, question sets used, team counts and results. Reusing questions at a venue upsets regulars, and attendance by venue shows which gigs to keep.

The tool tracks venues and pay, logs question sets used per venue, records attendance and flags slow nights.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per host. Distribution: trivia-host communities, r/trivia and local bar-owner groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Garage-Sale Organizer Client, Item & Sales Tracker

A person who runs garage and estate sales for others has to track clients, items, pricing, sale-day sales and the client's cut. Photos and lists attract buyers, and leftover disposition needs tracking.

The tool logs items and prices, records sale-day totals, calculates the commission split and tracks leftovers.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per organizer. Distribution: estate-sale communities, r/EstateSales and local senior-services networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Mural Painter Commission, Wall & Weather Tracker

A mural painter has to track commissions, wall measurements, approved sketches, paint cost and weather windows. Outdoor walls need dry weather, and clients change colors late.

The tool tracks commissions with sketch approvals, estimates paint by square footage, schedules weather holds and tracks deposits.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per painter. Distribution: mural-artist communities, r/muralart and local business-district groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
