# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-29 (Round 147)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo's prior 146 rounds, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Independent Landman Lease Acquisition, Division-Order & Curative-Title Tracker

An independent landman working oil and gas lease acquisition and title curative projects for multiple operator and mineral-acquisition clients at once has to track each tract's chain-of-title research status, outstanding curative requirements (missing heirship affidavits, unreleased old leases, unresolved probate), and lease terms (primary term expiration, held-by-production status, bonus and royalty rate) across dozens of tracts spread over several counties, since a landman who loses track of a lease's primary-term expiration date can cost a client the entire mineral position if it lapses unnoticed. A landman also has to reconcile division-order interest calculations — net revenue interest and working interest percentages per tract, often split across multiple heirs or co-owners — against what an operator's division order actually reflects before signing off, since a division order signed with the wrong decimal interest either shorts a client's royalty check for months or has to be corrected after payments already went out wrong.

The tool maintains a per-tract title curative checklist (chain-of-title status, outstanding curative items, courthouse document references) tied to county recorder records; a lease-term tracker flagging primary-term expiration and held-by-production status per tract; and a division-order interest reconciliation log comparing a landman's own calculated decimal interest against what each operator's division order shows before it's signed. Monetization: $30-50/mo per landman. Distribution: the American Association of Professional Landmen (AAPL) member directory and regional landman association chapters. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Freelance Motion Graphics Designer & 2D/3D Animator Client Project, Revision-Round & Render-Delivery Tracker

An independent motion graphics designer producing animated explainer videos, title sequences, and brand animations for multiple agency and direct-brand clients at once has to track each project's asset requirements (brand style guide, source files, voiceover or music licensing status), the specific revision-round count included in the contracted scope, and the exact output format and codec each client's delivery platform requires (broadcast-spec ProRes, a web-optimized H.264 export, a layered After Effects project file for an in-house team to continue editing), since delivering the wrong format or missing a client's specific export spec means a redo cycle that burns unpaid hours. A designer also has to track which render is the actual final approved version across a project that's gone through several rounds of client feedback, since a client requesting "the version from two rounds ago with this one note added" is a common request that's easy to get wrong without a clear version history.

The tool maintains a per-project asset and licensing checklist (style guide, source files, music/voiceover rights status); a revision-round counter tracking rounds used against what the contract includes, with a flag before an out-of-scope revision is quietly absorbed for free; a render-version history linking each delivered cut to the specific client feedback that produced it; and an output-format/codec checklist per client's delivery platform. Monetization: $20-35/mo per designer. Distribution: the Motion Design community on Dribbble and School of Motion's alumni network, plus r/motiongraphics. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Freelance Technical Writer & Documentation Specialist Client Project, Doc-Version & SME-Review Tracker

An independent technical writer producing user manuals, API documentation, and internal knowledge-base articles for multiple SaaS and hardware-company clients at once has to track each documentation project's subject-matter-expert (SME) interview schedule, the specific product version each doc set describes (since a hardware or software product often ships several versions in parallel and a doc written against the wrong version misleads every reader), and which SME has signed off on which section, since publishing a technical document without the SME's final review is exactly the kind of accuracy gap that erodes a technical writer's credibility with an engineering-heavy client. A writer juggling several concurrent client contracts also has to track each project's deliverable format (a DITA-structured knowledge base, a PDF user manual, a Confluence-hosted internal wiki) and delivery deadline, since a client's product-launch date often has zero flexibility on when the documentation has to be ready.

The tool maintains a per-project SME interview schedule and outstanding-question log; a document-version tracker tying each doc set to the specific product version it describes; a section-by-section SME sign-off tracker before any document is marked publish-ready; and a deliverable-format and deadline calendar spanning every concurrent client project. Monetization: $20-30/mo per writer. Distribution: the Society for Technical Communication (STC) member directory and independent technical-writer LinkedIn and Slack communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Independent Consulting Forester Timber Cruise, Client Tract & Harvest-Contract Tracker

An independent consulting forester who advises private landowners on timber management — cruising and marking timber, appraising stand value, and administering a harvest sale on the landowner's behalf without operating the logging equipment themselves — has to track each client tract's most recent cruise data (species composition, board-foot or cord volume estimates by stand), the harvest contract terms negotiated with a logging contractor (per-unit stumpage price, cutting boundaries, completion deadline), and a running compliance check against the sale contract as harvest proceeds, since a forester who doesn't track actual volume removed against the contracted volume can't catch a logger who's cut beyond the marked boundary or under-scaled the harvest before the landowner is shorted on payment. A consulting forester juggling a dozen or more concurrent client tracts across a multi-county service area also has to track each tract's reforestation or best-management-practice follow-up commitments made as part of the harvest agreement, since a landowner relying on the forester's judgment expects that follow-up to actually happen on schedule, not fall through once the harvest revenue has been collected.

The tool maintains a per-tract cruise-data record (species composition, volume estimates by stand) carried forward across visits; a harvest-contract tracker with stumpage price, cutting boundaries, and completion deadline per sale; a harvested-volume reconciliation log comparing scaled harvest volume against the contracted estimate; and a reforestation/BMP follow-up commitment tracker per tract. Monetization: $25-40/mo per forester. Distribution: the Society of American Foresters (SAF) member directory and state consulting-forester registration rosters (several states, including Georgia, maintain a public registered-consulting-forester list). Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Crop Scout & Certified Crop Adviser (CCA) Field Scouting Log, Pest-Threshold & Recommendation Tracker

An independent crop scout or Certified Crop Adviser who walks fields for multiple grower clients during the growing season has to track each field's scouting history — pest and disease pressure observed by date and location within the field, weed density, and stand health — against established economic-injury-level thresholds for that crop and pest, since a recommendation to spray (or not spray) has real cost consequences for the grower and has to be defensible against the actual scouting data if a grower ever questions the call. A CCA also has to track every pesticide-application recommendation made per field, since restricted-use-pesticide recommendations tied to a grower's own state pesticide-applicator license require a documented recordkeeping trail, and a CCA advising a dozen or more grower clients across hundreds of fields during peak season needs that trail organized per field and per date, not reconstructed from memory after the season ends.

The tool maintains a per-field scouting log (pest/disease pressure, weed density, stand health by date and GPS-tagged location within the field) charted against crop-specific economic-injury-level thresholds; a recommendation log tying each spray/no-spray call to the scouting data that justified it; a restricted-use-pesticide recommendation recordkeeping trail per field and grower; and a CCA continuing-education-unit (CEU) tracker for the American Society of Agronomy's CCA program recertification cycle. Monetization: $25-40/mo per scout/adviser. Distribution: the American Society of Agronomy's Certified Crop Adviser program regional chapter network and state Certified Crop Adviser board member directories. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Independent Corporate Trainer & Instructional Designer Course-Build, Client Contract & Delivery-Schedule Tracker

An independent corporate trainer and instructional designer building custom onboarding, compliance, or skills-training courses for multiple corporate clients at once has to track each course-build project against its statement-of-work milestones (needs analysis, storyboard approval, content draft, SME review, final build), since a client paying for a custom course expects visible progress against agreed milestones rather than a single deliverable that shows up at the deadline with no interim checkpoints. A trainer also has to track each finished course's packaging format — SCORM or xAPI for a client's own learning management system, a standalone video series, or a live-facilitation guide — and the specific LMS platform quirks of each client (upload size limits, tracking-data requirements, browser compatibility), since a course package that fails to import cleanly into a client's LMS on delivery day is an embarrassing, billable-hours-eating fire drill. A trainer running several concurrent client engagements also needs a simple view of which delivery dates are approaching across every project, since a missed course-launch date directly disrupts a client's own new-hire cohort or compliance-deadline schedule.

The tool maintains a per-client course-build milestone tracker (needs analysis, storyboard, content draft, SME review, final build) tied to the project's statement of work; a packaging-format checklist per client (SCORM/xAPI version, LMS platform requirements, file-size limits); and a cross-client delivery-schedule calendar flagging every approaching course-launch date. Monetization: $20-35/mo per trainer/instructional designer. Distribution: the Association for Talent Development (ATD) member directory and independent instructional-designer LinkedIn groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
