# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-16 (Round 199)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a magician, a local ghost-tour guide, a murder-mystery party host, a choir director, a circus performer and an indie author selling at book festivals.

---

### 1. Magician Show, Booking & Prop Tracker

A magician who performs at parties and corporate events has to track bookings, show types, deposits, travel and prop condition. Repeat clients and referrals bring most bookings, and each venue has different needs.

The tool tracks bookings with deposits, logs show type per client, schedules prop checks and sends a yearly rebooking message.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per magician. Distribution: magician communities, r/Magic and local event-planner groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Ghost-Tour Guide Tour, Group & Script Tracker

A guide running local ghost or history tours has to track tour dates, group sizes, private bookings, script updates and tips. Weather cancels tours, and private groups pay more than public ones.

The tool tracks tours and bookings, handles weather rescheduling, stores script versions and shows income per tour.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per guide. Distribution: tour-guide communities, r/travel and local tourism boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Murder-Mystery Party Host Script, Cast & Booking Tracker

A host who runs murder-mystery parties has to track scripts, character assignments, guest counts, props and bookings. Each party uses a different script, and repeat clients should not get the same one twice.

The tool tracks bookings and scripts used per client, assigns characters, lists props and sends a pre-party packet.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per host. Distribution: party-host Facebook groups, r/murdermystery and local event networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Choir Director Rehearsal, Roster & Concert Tracker

A paid choir director has to track singers, voice parts, attendance, repertoire and concert dates. Some singers drop out before concerts, and music needs to be ordered ahead of rehearsals.

The tool tracks the roster and voice parts, logs attendance, manages the repertoire and sends concert-countdown reminders.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per director. Distribution: choral-director communities, r/choir and church-music networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Circus Performer Act, Booking & Rider Tracker

A circus or variety performer (aerial, fire, juggling) has to track acts, booking fees, equipment and venue requirements such as ceiling height. Each venue needs a different setup, and equipment needs inspection records.

The tool tracks bookings and fees, stores venue requirements per act, schedules equipment checks and sends a pre-show checklist.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per performer. Distribution: circus-arts communities, r/circus and event-entertainment agencies. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Book-Festival Indie Author Event, Stock & Sales Tracker

A self-published author who sells at book festivals and signings has to track events, fees, stock brought and sales. Some events pay off and others do not, and restocking decisions depend on past sales.

The tool tracks event fees and sales, plans stock per event, shows profit per event and keeps a reader email list.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per author. Distribution: indie-author communities, r/selfpublishing and local festival groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
