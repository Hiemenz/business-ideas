# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-01 (Round 13)

### 1. Online Reseller Inventory & Net Profit Tracker
Resellers who source items from thrift stores, garage sales, and retail clearance to sell on eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, and Amazon need to track cost basis per item, platform-specific selling fees, shipping costs, and net profit per sale — all currently managed across spreadsheets that don't sync to actual sales data. This app connects to selling platform APIs to pull completed sales, matches each sale to an inventory item with the original sourcing cost entered at acquisition, and calculates true net profit after all fees and shipping, with a dashboard showing profit by category, platform, and sourcing location. Monetization: $12-22/mo subscription. Distribution: r/Flipping, r/Ebay, reseller Facebook groups, reseller YouTube creator audiences (large and engaged). Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Independent Mortgage Loan Officer Pipeline & Commission Forecast
Independent MLOs sponsored through mortgage broker shops manage 15-40 active loan files simultaneously, each at a different pipeline stage, and have no simple way to estimate their pending income (loan amount × basis points) across their current book of business. This app tracks each active loan with stage (pre-approval → processing → underwriting → CTC → funded), expected close date, loan amount, and agreed basis point compensation, rolling up into a 30/60/90-day commission forecast that helps the MLO manage cash flow through long close cycles. Monetization: $18-30/mo per MLO. Distribution: mortgage broker-sponsored MLO Facebook groups, r/loanoriginators, NAMB (National Association of Mortgage Brokers) community. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Craft Fair Booth Application Tracker & Revenue-Per-Event Log
Makers, artists, and crafters apply to dozens of juried craft fairs, farmers markets, and maker markets annually — each with different application deadlines, jury fees, booth fees, table sizes, and attendance characteristics — with no systematic way to track application status or compare actual revenue earned across past events. This app tracks every event applied to (deadline, booth fee, jury fee, status, distance, event dates) alongside post-event revenue and expense entries, so the maker can rank events by profitability and prioritize applications for next year's calendar. Monetization: $8-14/mo or $39/yr. Distribution: r/crafts, Etsy seller community forums, craft vendor Facebook groups, handmade seller newsletter communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Pickleball Club Court Reservation & Round-Robin Organizer
Recreational pickleball clubs and park district facilities manage court availability for reserved play and drop-in sessions using paper sign-up sheets and group texts, with no way to auto-rotate players through round-robin sessions fairly or notify members when courts open up. This app lets club admins set court availability windows, allows members to reserve courts or join a drop-in queue, and auto-generates round-robin match rotations for open play sessions based on who checked in, tracking wins/losses per player over time. Monetization: $25-45/mo per club or facility. Distribution: USA Pickleball Association affiliated club directory, pickleball Facebook groups (very large and active), pickleball instructor and ambassador networks. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 5. RV Rental Host Fleet & Calendar Management App
Individuals who own 1-3 RVs listed on RVshare or Outdoorsy need to manage maintenance schedules across multiple vehicles, track rental calendar windows (including platform sync gaps that cause double-bookings), store guest check-in instructions and pre-trip inspection checklists per unit, and organize income and expense records by vehicle for Schedule C tax reporting. This app centralizes all units in one dashboard with iCal sync to rental platform calendars, maintenance interval reminders by mileage or months, and a per-trip income/expense log. Monetization: $22-38/mo. Distribution: RVshare and Outdoorsy host Facebook communities, r/RVshare, RV rental host YouTube channels. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 6. Dance Studio Recital Costume Order & Fee Collection Manager
Dance studio owners running annual recitals coordinate 100-400 costume orders across multiple dance numbers — each with a different costume style, vendor, and size chart — collecting fees from parents while tracking which students have paid, which costumes have arrived, and which need to be distributed before dress rehearsal. This app lets studio owners set up each recital number with its costume details and price, assign enrolled students to each number, track individual costume fees collected and outstanding, and mark costumes as received and distributed per student. Monetization: $25-45/mo per studio or per-recital flat fee. Distribution: Dance Studio Owners Association community, dance studio owner Facebook groups, studio management software adjacent communities (MINDBODY, Jackrabbit Dance user groups). Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.
