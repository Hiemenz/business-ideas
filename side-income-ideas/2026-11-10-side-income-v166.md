# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-10 (Round 166)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a UGC creator, a Skool/online community manager, a newsletter sponsorship seller, a YouTube thumbnail designer, a freelance proposal writer and a funnel/landing-page builder.

---

### 1. UGC Creator Brand Deal, Deliverable & Usage-Rights Payment Tracker

A UGC creator who films product videos for brands has to track each deal: product shipped, filming deadline, number of revisions, the agreed fee and when it is paid. A creator with 8-15 brands at once forgets which deal still owes a paid usage extension or a whitelisting fee, and that money is easy to leave on the table. Late product shipments also delay filming and push payment dates.

The tool logs deals with status (product received, filming, submitted, approved, paid), deadline countdowns, revision counts and a usage-extension/renewal reminder. A monthly income view shows revenue per brand.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per creator. Distribution: UGC creator communities on Facebook and Discord, r/UGCcreators and TikTok creator circles. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Online Community Manager Member-Onboarding, Engagement & Retention Tracker

A freelance manager who runs a Skool or Circle community for a coach has to welcome new members, post prompts, flag silent members and report on retention. With 3-5 communities, it is easy to lose track of which members never posted and are about to cancel. The client wants a monthly report showing the community is healthy and the manager needs numbers to prove it.

The tool tracks new members and their first-week onboarding steps, members inactive for 14+ days with a nudge checklist, and monthly retention and engagement summaries per client.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per manager. Distribution: Skool and Circle community-manager groups, r/communitymanagers and LinkedIn creator-economy groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 3. Newsletter Sponsorship Seller Slot, Advertiser & Invoice Tracker

A freelance sponsorship seller who sells ad slots for small newsletters on commission has to track which issues have open slots, which advertisers were pitched, the agreed rate and whether a sponsor's creative has arrived. An unfilled slot is lost income that never returns, and an unpaid invoice can drag on for months.

The tool shows an issue-by-issue slot calendar with fill status, a pitch log per advertiser, creative-received flags and an invoice/payment tracker with commission calculations.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per seller. Distribution: newsletter-operator communities, r/Newsletters and Twitter/X creator circles. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 4. YouTube Thumbnail Designer Client Order, Revision & CTR Tracker

A freelance thumbnail designer handling 10-25 thumbnails a week for several channels has to track briefs, drafts, revision rounds and delivery deadlines. Channels also want to know which designs raised click-through rate, and the designer who can show that has a better case for a rate increase. Without records, revisions pile up unpaid.

The tool logs orders with brief, stage and due date, caps revisions with a re-quote prompt, and lets the designer record each thumbnail's CTR to show per-client results.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per designer. Distribution: designer Discords, r/Thumbnails and YouTube-creator freelance groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Freelance Proposal Writer Bid, Win-Rate & Follow-Up Tracker

A freelancer who writes proposals on Upwork or by cold outreach has to track every bid: job link, rate quoted, response, follow-up date and outcome. With 20+ bids a week, there is no way to see which proposal template or which job type wins, and winning more at a higher rate is the fastest way to raise income.

The tool logs bids and outcomes, schedules follow-ups, and reports win rate by template, platform and rate band.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per freelancer. Distribution: r/freelance, Upwork forums and freelancer Discords. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Funnel & Landing-Page Builder Freelancer Project, Asset & Revision Tracker

A freelancer who builds sales funnels and landing pages in ClickFunnels, GoHighLevel or Webflow for coaches has to track assets received (copy, images, logos, domain access), build stages and revision rounds. Missing client assets are the main cause of delays, so the freelancer needs a record of what was requested and when. Handing over logins and domains also needs a clear checklist.

The tool tracks per-project asset requests with received/missing status and reminder emails, build stages with deadlines, revision caps, and a handover checklist.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per freelancer. Distribution: GoHighLevel and ClickFunnels builder communities, r/webdev freelance threads and Skool agency groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.
