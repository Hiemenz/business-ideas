# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-16 (Round 196)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a running coach, a boot-camp trainer, a solo wedding musician, a hockey skills coach, a billiards instructor and a youth-league referee.

---

### 1. Running Coach Athlete, Plan & Race Tracker

An online or local running coach has to track each runner's goal race, weekly plan, completed runs, injuries and fees. Plans change when a runner misses a week, and race dates set the schedule.

The tool tracks athletes and goal races, adjusts weekly plans, logs completed workouts and sends a check-in when a runner goes quiet.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per coach. Distribution: running communities, r/AdvancedRunning and local run clubs. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Boot-Camp Trainer Class, Member & Weather Tracker

An outdoor boot-camp trainer has to track class schedules, members, attendance, packages and weather cancellations. Rain days need rescheduling and a message to everyone.

The tool tracks members and packages, takes attendance, sends weather cancel or move texts and shows retention by month.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per trainer. Distribution: fitness-trainer groups, local park-community boards and r/personaltrainer. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Solo Wedding Musician Gig, Setlist & Deposit Tracker

A solo guitarist, pianist or violinist for weddings has to track bookings, ceremony and cocktail-hour sets, song requests, deposits and travel. Couples send song lists late, and dates cluster on weekends.

The tool tracks bookings with deposits, stores song requests per couple, builds setlists and sends a day-before checklist.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per musician. Distribution: musician communities, wedding-vendor groups and r/WeddingPlanning. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Hockey Skills Coach Player, Session & Ice-Time Tracker

A private hockey skills coach has to track players, sessions, skills worked on, ice-time rentals and fees. Ice time is booked ahead and costs money whether students show up or not.

The tool tracks players and session notes, manages ice-time bookings, logs no-shows and tracks package balances.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per coach. Distribution: hockey-coach groups, r/hockey and local rink boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Billiards Instructor Student, Drill & Lesson Tracker

A pool instructor teaching private lessons has to track students' skill levels, drills assigned, lesson packages and table time. Students want homework and proof of progress.

The tool tracks students and drills, counts package lessons, logs table costs and sends a practice plan after each lesson.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per instructor. Distribution: billiards communities, r/billiards and local pool-hall networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Youth-League Referee Game, Assignment & Pay Tracker

A part-time referee has to track game assignments, fees, travel distance, availability and pay dates. Assignors send games at short notice, and pay arrives late.

The tool tracks assigned games and pay, flags conflicts, logs mileage and shows expected earnings by month.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per referee. Distribution: referee communities, r/Referees and local league boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
