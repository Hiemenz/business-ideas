# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 39)

### 1. Youth Sports Photographer Image Gallery Delivery & Print Order Tracker
Youth sports photographers who shoot league and tournament photos for baseball, soccer, basketball, and football teams deliver individual and team portrait galleries, action photo galleries, and print packages to parents — managing this across a Pixieset or Zenfolio gallery link sent via team email, with print orders arriving through the gallery's shopping cart and fulfillment handled by a print lab. When a league coordinator asks "how many families from the U6 soccer division have placed orders?" or "the Redbirds coach says they never got their team photo link," the photographer has no consolidated view. This tool maintains a per-session record (event name, sport, age division, shoot date, school or league name, expected team or family count, gallery delivery date, and gallery link), a per-team delivery log (for each team or group within the session: gallery link sent date, coach or coordinator contact confirmed, and number of downloads/orders from the lab report if available), a print order tracker (families who have ordered: order date, order total, print lab order number, and fulfillment status — shipped/picked up), and a re-engagement prompt calendar (7 and 14 days after gallery delivery, a list of families or teams with no order activity generated for the photographer to follow up via the league coordinator). At season end: revenue summary by league and by sport. Monetization: $10-18/mo per photographer account. Distribution: Professional Photographers of America (PPA) member communities, r/sportsphotoraphy, youth sports photography Facebook groups, Sports Photo Trader and BallerTV photography community forums. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Freelance Makeup Artist Bridal & Event Client Service Tracker
Freelance makeup artists who specialize in bridal and event makeup manage a complex day-of logistics puzzle: each wedding or event has a specific timeline — how many people in the bridal party, what services each person is getting (full face vs. touch-up vs. airbrush), what time each person needs to be done, and how the artist's kit needs to be prepped for the specific looks requested. This currently lives in a text thread with the bride and a notes app. This tool maintains a per-event record (event name, client name, event date, event time, venue or location, and call time for the artist), a service roster (for each person in the party: name, services requested — bridal makeup/bridesmaid makeup/trial/airbrush/hair if offered, service time in minutes, and desired start time in the day-of schedule), a day-of timeline builder (auto-generating a recommended service order and time slots based on the roster and services, working backward from the event time with artist setup time included), a client consultation note (trial appointment observations — skin type, any sensitivities or allergies, product preferences noted, reference photos agreed on), and a kit prep checklist (the specific products flagged for this event based on the consultation notes — foundation shade matched in the trial, airbrush equipment if applicable, any specialty items). Pre-event: the artist reviews the timeline and kit checklist the night before. Post-event: invoice summary with services rendered per person. Monetization: $10-15/mo per artist account. Distribution: r/MakeupArtists, Makeup Artists Guild community forums, Behind the Chair and American Salon alternative community groups, bridal hair and makeup Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Vacation Rental Host Cleaning Crew Scheduling & Turnover Checklist Tracker
Vacation rental hosts managing 2-10 properties on Airbnb and VRBO coordinate cleaning crew turnovers between every guest checkout and check-in — often with same-day turnovers where the cleaning window is as short as 3-5 hours between a 10am checkout and a 3pm check-in. Managing the turnover schedule across multiple properties, notifying the cleaning crew about each turnover's timing, and confirming the property is guest-ready before check-in is coordinated through text messages, a shared calendar, and the host's anxiety. This tool pulls or accepts booking dates from the host (guest checkout date/time and next guest check-in date/time per property) and generates a turnover calendar showing each cleaning window for each property over the next 30 days — flagging any same-day turnovers, back-to-back turnovers, or unusually short windows. For each turnover: a standardized property turnover checklist (room-by-room tasks: linen change, towel restocking, consumable inventory check — toilet paper, soap, coffee — appliance reset, and any special touches like welcome snack restocking), a cleaner assignment log (which crew member is assigned each turnover, with their contact info), and a completion confirmation (the cleaner marks the checklist done and uploads a photo of the property ready for check-in). Host-facing: a dashboard showing all upcoming turnovers and which ones have been confirmed completed. Monetization: $10-18/mo per host account (1-3 properties) or $25/mo for 4-10 properties. Distribution: r/airbnb hosts, BiggerPockets STR community, Airbnb host Facebook groups, STR Cribs and Short-Term Rental University community forums. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Local Food Truck Owner Event Booking Calendar & Prep List Generator
Food truck operators who take catering bookings for weddings, corporate lunches, festivals, and private events manage their booking calendar in their phone, with event confirmations scattered across email and Instagram DMs. When a corporate client asks if the truck is available for a 200-person lunch on a specific Thursday, the owner has to mentally reconstruct their schedule to answer. This tool maintains a per-event booking record (event name, client name and contact, event date and time, event location, expected guest count, agreed menu selection or service style — full menu vs. limited menu vs. prix-fixe, agreed service fee, deposit collected, and balance due), an availability calendar that shows all booked dates at a glance so the owner can answer availability questions in seconds, a prep list generator (for each event, based on the menu selection and guest count: an ingredient prep quantity calculation — if the event orders 200 servings of a taco menu, the tool calculates the approximate quantities of each ingredient to prep, based on portion estimates the owner configures), and a payment tracking log (deposit received date and amount, balance due date, and balance received confirmation). A weekly view shows all upcoming events for the next 14 days with their event details and any outstanding payments. Monetization: $12-18/mo per truck account. Distribution: r/foodtrucks, Food Truck Nation and Street Food Federation community forums, Square and Toast food truck POS alternative communities, food truck owner Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Independent Bookstore Author Event & Book Club Calendar Manager
Independent bookstore owners who host author signings, readings, launch parties, and ongoing book club meetings coordinate these events across a shared calendar, email confirmations with publishers' and authors' publicists, and a social media promotion schedule that often gets started too late. This tool maintains a per-event record (event type — signing/reading/launch party/book club, author name, book title, event date and time, expected attendance cap if any, publisher contact coordinating the event, ARC and finished copy inventory ordered for the event, social media promotion schedule, and any room setup or AV requirements), an event calendar view (showing all upcoming events in the next 60 days with their promotion deadlines — "social post needed 2 weeks out," "event email to mailing list due 10 days out"), an author inventory tracker (for each event: copies ordered vs. copies received, any pre-event pre-orders from customers to set aside, and post-event remaining inventory), a book club series manager (for ongoing book clubs: the club's meeting schedule, current selection, next selection, RSVP count for each meeting, and any discussion guide or author Q&A material on file), and a post-event summary (attendance actual vs. expected, copies sold at the event, any media coverage, and notes for future events with this author or this publisher). Monetization: $12-20/mo per bookstore account. Distribution: American Booksellers Association (ABA) member network, r/bookstores, SIBA (Southern Independent Booksellers Alliance) community forums, independent bookstore owner Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Community College Adjunct Instructor Course Prep & Student Grade Tracker
Community college adjunct instructors who teach 2-4 sections per semester across multiple campuses manage course syllabi, assignment due dates, student attendance, and grade calculations in a mix of the college's LMS (which is often slow and clunky), a personal spreadsheet, and their own notes. When a student emails asking about their current grade, it requires opening the LMS, navigating to the gradebook, and calculating a weighted average manually. This tool maintains a per-section record (course name and section number, campus, semester, meeting days and times, and enrollment count), a course assignment calendar (each assignment or exam name, point value, due date, and the weight of each category in the final grade calculation), a student roster with grade tracker (for each student: attendance log, score on each assignment/exam, running weighted average, and any grade exception notes — late work policy applied, make-up exam taken), a grade distribution dashboard (how many students are currently in each grade band — A/B/C/D/F — for each section, as a weekly temperature check), and a late-submission and incomplete tracker (students with outstanding work: what's missing, the extension granted if any, and the deadline). At semester end: the tool exports a final grade list in the format the college's registrar requires. Monetization: $5-8/mo per instructor account or $25/semester. Distribution: r/adjunct, Adjunct Faculty Alliance community forums, National Coalition for Part-Time Faculty groups, Chegg and Pearson instructor community forums. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
