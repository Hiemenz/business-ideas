# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-20 (Session 102)

## Who this is for

A 29-year-old with software engineering and data analytics skills building simple, focused operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — practical day-to-day workflow trackers (scheduling, client records, inventory, billing, project pipelines), not compliance software. Each tool targets a narrow, specific operator type with a data model built around that operator's actual daily workflow.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series

- Solo-buildable, narrow-scope tools — a single core tracker, not a general-purpose CRM.
- Realistic monthly pricing per account, shown explicitly.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.
- Named distribution channel — a specific subreddit, trade association, or online community.
- **No repeats.** This session's six operator types — independent drywall hangers/tapers, independent glaziers, independent pool & spa builders, independent newborn care specialists/night nurses, independent aging life care professionals/geriatric care managers, and independent wildfire defensible-space/fuels-mitigation contractors — are distinct from every prior session: drywall hanging and taping has never appeared as its own operator type (distinct from the Independent Handyman idea already in this repo, which tracks small mixed-trade jobs across plumbing, carpentry, and occasional drywall patches as one line item among many — a drywall hanger/taper's core problem instead is a per-job board-count and mud-coat-stage tracker following a multi-day hang/tape/coat/sand sequence that a generalist handyman never runs at that scale, plus a multi-crew scheduling problem across several simultaneous new-construction and remodel jobs, a board-and-coat-sequencing problem no handyman or general remodeling tracker in this repo holds); glazing and window/glass installation has never appeared (distinct from the Custom Window Treatment & Blinds Installer idea, which tracks fabric and hardware measurements for soft treatments, and from Mobile Auto Glass Repair, which tracks vehicle glass — a glazier's core problem instead is a per-job glass measurement and special-order lead-time tracker, since architectural glass is custom-cut to opening dimensions and often has a multi-week fabrication lead time from the glass shop that neither auto glass nor window-treatment tracking involves, a measurement-to-special-order problem no other installer tracker in this repo holds); pool and spa new-construction building has never appeared (distinct from the Residential Pool Cleaning & Maintenance Route Operator idea already in this repo, which runs a recurring weekly chemical-service route with zero construction or subcontractor-coordination component — a pool builder's core problem instead is a multi-week excavation-to-plaster construction-stage tracker coordinating excavator, gunite/shotcrete crew, tile setter, and plasterer subcontractors in sequence across several simultaneous builds, a construction-sequencing-and-subcontractor problem no pool maintenance tracker in this repo touches); ## Ideas

### 1. Independent Drywall Hanger & Taper Job Bid, Board-Count & Crew-Schedule Tracker

Independent drywall hanging and taping crews bid jobs by square footage and board count, then run each job through a multi-day hang, tape, coat (typically three mud coats with drying time between each), and sand sequence — and a small operator running two or three crews across several simultaneous new-construction and remodel jobs can't hold every job's current stage, remaining coat count, and drying-time-driven next-available-date in memory without either sending a crew to a job that isn't dry enough to coat yet or leaving a finished job's final coat sitting unscheduled for days.

The tool maintains a per-job bid record (square footage, board count, and mud/tape material estimate against actual usage), a coat-stage tracker showing each active job's current stage and earliest-next-coat date based on drying time, and a multi-crew schedule view assigning crews to jobs by which stage each site is actually ready for today.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/Drywall and the Awci (Association of the Wall and Ceiling Industry) member directory, plus local drywall subcontractor Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Independent Glazier & Architectural Glass Installer Measurement & Special-Order Tracker

Independent glaziers installing storefront glass, custom shower enclosures, mirrors, and replacement window glass need to track precise opening measurements per job, since architectural glass is cut to exact dimensions at a glass fabrication shop and a measurement error discovered only at install means re-ordering a custom piece with another multi-week fabrication lead time — a delay that can leave a commercial storefront boarded up or a bathroom remodel stalled for weeks. The tool also needs a special-order lead-time tracker per glass piece, since different glass types (tempered, laminated, tinted, low-E) carry different fabrication timelines from the glazier's supplier, and a glazier juggling a dozen active orders can't track which pieces are due back from the fabricator this week versus which are still three weeks out without a structured system.

The tool maintains a per-job opening measurement record with photo documentation, a special-order tracker per glass piece (type, dimensions, fabricator, and expected-ready date), and an install-scheduling view that only shows a job as ready to schedule once its glass order is confirmed received.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/Glazing and the National Glass Association (NGA) member directory, plus local glass shop and glazier trade Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Independent Pool & Spa Builder Excavation-to-Plaster Construction-Stage Tracker

Independent pool and spa builders running new in-ground pool construction manage a multi-week sequence — excavation, steel/rebar, gunite or shotcrete shell, plumbing and electrical rough-in, tile and coping, decking, and final plaster — coordinating a different subcontractor crew at each stage across several simultaneous builds, and a builder running 4-8 active pools at once can't rely on memory to know which site is waiting on the gunite crew this week versus which is ready for the tile setter, or when a scheduling gap between subcontractors is quietly adding weeks to a client's promised completion date. The tool also needs a subcontractor and inspection-checkpoint tracker, since most jurisdictions require a signed-off inspection at several construction stages (pre-gunite steel inspection, pre-plaster plumbing/electrical inspection) before the next stage can legally proceed, and a build that skips ahead of an unscheduled inspection risks a costly rework order from the county inspector.

The tool maintains a per-project construction-stage tracker with each stage's subcontractor and target date, an inspection-checkpoint gate that flags a stage as blocked until its required inspection is confirmed passed, and a multi-project dashboard showing every active build's current stage at a glance.

Monetization: $29-45/mo per account. Distribution: r/PoolBuilders and the Pool & Hot Tub Alliance (PHTA) member directory, plus regional pool builder trade association chapters. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Newborn Care Specialist & Overnight Night Nurse Multi-Family Shift & Sleep-Log Tracker

Independent newborn care specialists and overnight night nurses typically work 3-5 overnight shifts a week split across two or three different families at once, each with its own newborn's feeding schedule, sleep-training approach, and handoff notes for the parents or day caregiver — and an NCS juggling multiple concurrent families can't rely on memory to keep each baby's current feeding interval, wake windows, and parent-specific preferences straight from one overnight shift to the next without risking a mixed-up handoff that undermines a family's trust in a highly personal, high-stakes caregiving relationship. The tool also needs a shift-availability calendar showing which overnight slots are booked across all active families simultaneously, since a night nurse building a full multi-family schedule has to avoid double-booking one night while leaving gaps on others, and a per-family sleep-log and feeding-pattern tracker that produces a clean handoff summary for the parents each morning.

The tool maintains a multi-family overnight shift calendar, a per-baby sleep-log and feeding-pattern tracker with a shareable morning handoff summary, and a certification/CPR-renewal tracker since most families require current infant CPR certification before booking.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: the International Nanny Association (INA) newborn care specialist community and Newborn Care Solutions' NCS directory, plus regional postpartum doula and NCS Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Aging Life Care Professional & Geriatric Care Manager Client Assessment & Care-Plan Tracker

Independent aging life care professionals (geriatric care managers) coordinate an aging client's care across physicians, home health aides, family members, and facility staff over months or years — not a single transactional event like a move or a placement search — and a care manager juggling 15-25 active clients needs a standing per-client assessment and care plan documenting current health status, medication list, provider contacts, and family decision-makers, since a care plan that isn't kept current is the first thing a family or an incoming physician asks for when a client's situation changes suddenly. The tool also needs a family-communication log, since aging life care professionals are frequently the single point of contact updating out-of-town adult children on a parent's status, and a missed or forgotten update is exactly what erodes a family's confidence in an ongoing care-management relationship they're often paying a premium for.

The tool maintains a per-client assessment and care-plan document (health status, medications, provider directory, and decision-makers) with a revision history, a family-communication log tracking every update sent and to whom, and a visit/check-in scheduler across the care manager's full active caseload.

Monetization: $29-45/mo per account. Distribution: the Aging Life Care Association (ALCA) member directory and local chapter meetings, plus elder-law attorney and geriatric care referral networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Independent Wildfire Defensible-Space & Fuels-Mitigation Contractor Zone Assessment & Compliance-Photo Tracker

Independent wildfire defensible-space and fuels-mitigation contractors assess a property against the zone-based vegetation-clearance standards most wildfire-prone states now codify (typically a 0-5ft ember-resistant zone, a 5-30ft lean-and-clean zone, and a 30-100ft reduced-fuel zone), then clear and thin vegetation to meet each zone's requirement — and a contractor running a full seasonal book of properties needs a per-property zone assessment with a before/after photo log, since homeowners routinely need documented proof of completed defensible-space work for insurance underwriting or renewal, and a county wildfire-code compliance inspection specifically checks against exactly this kind of zone-by-zone documentation. The tool also needs a weather-dependent crew scheduling view, since fuels-mitigation work (particularly any pile-burning or chainsaw work in dry conditions) is frequently halted by red-flag warnings or local burn bans, and a contractor with a full multi-week seasonal backlog needs to see which scheduled jobs are weather-blocked today versus which can proceed.

The tool maintains a per-property zone assessment with before/after photo documentation by zone, an insurance/county-compliance report generator rolling the photo log into a submittable format, and a crew schedule that flags burn-ban or red-flag-warning days affecting that day's scheduled jobs.

Monetization: $25-39/mo per account. Distribution: r/wildfire and the National Association of Wildfire Mitigation Contractors, plus local Firewise USA community coordinator networks that regularly refer homeowners to vetted mitigation contractors. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
