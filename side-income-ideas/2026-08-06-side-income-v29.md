# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 29)

### 1. Escape Room Game Master Hint Tracking & Puzzle Analytics
Escape room operators who run 5-20 game rooms track hint frequency and room completion rates in a notebook or whiteboard, losing the data after each week. Without aggregate records, they can't answer: which puzzle generates the most hints, which room has an outlier difficulty spike, which 15-minute segment sees the most group give-ups. This tool logs each session: room played, group size, booking type (private vs. public), completion (yes/no), total time if completed, and a quick per-puzzle hint tally the game master fills in on a tablet during the session. After a month, the owner gets: completion rate by room and group size, average hints per puzzle ranked by most-hinted, a time-on-puzzle distribution showing where groups stall, and a weekly trend line for each room's performance. Puzzle adjustments become data-driven rather than gut-feel. Monetization: $20-30/mo per escape room venue. Distribution: Escape Room Owners Facebook group (the largest concentrated operator community), Room Escape Artist industry community, r/EscapeRooms operator discussions. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Sailing School & US Sailing Certification Logbook Tracker
Sailing instructors and community sailing programs tracking student progress toward US Sailing Basic Keelboat, Basic Cruising, or Bareboat Cruising certifications rely on paper logbooks and spreadsheet notes to document student competency sign-offs by skill area. When a student transfers to a different instructor or rejoins after a season gap, the progress record is often missing or inconsistent. This tool maintains a per-student competency checklist keyed to US Sailing certification requirements (each certification's required skills drawn from the US Sailing published standards), lets the instructor check off skills demonstrated and certified, records lesson dates and conditions (boat type, wind strength, location), and generates a printable student logbook page the student takes to their next session or a different instructor. Program coordinators see a cohort dashboard: how many students are within one session of certification, which skill areas most students are missing. Monetization: $15-25/mo per program or sailing school account. Distribution: US Sailing Club directory, Community Sailing nonprofit administrator networks, sailing school owner Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. STR Co-Host Earnings Split & Monthly Settlement Generator
Airbnb and VRBO property management co-hosts who manage short-term rentals on behalf of property owners have one recurring admin headache: calculating the co-host's management fee and producing a clean monthly settlement statement for the owner. Gross reservation revenue minus platform fees, minus cleaning fees (sometimes passed through, sometimes shared, often complicated by last-minute booking cleanings that cost more), minus owner-supplied consumable reimbursements, times the co-host's percentage — then the co-host deducts their own expenses and produces a statement. This tool takes monthly payout data (imported from a CSV download or manual entry per booking), applies the configured fee split (percentage of net, percentage of gross, flat fee plus percentage — all supported), handles pass-through items by category, and generates a settlement statement PDF per property per month: gross revenue, each deduction itemized, the co-host's fee, and the net owner remittance — plus a year-to-date summary. Owners get professional documentation; co-hosts stop spending 30-60 minutes per property per month on spreadsheet math. Monetization: $12-20/mo per co-host account (up to 10 properties); $5/mo per additional property above 10. Distribution: STR co-host Facebook groups, Airbnb Host Community forums, Hospitable and Lodgify user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Freelance Illustrator Project Brief & Revision Round Tracker
Freelance illustrators working with clients on book covers, brand mascots, editorial illustration, and surface pattern design spend as much time managing project communication as drawing: tracking what the brief says, what was approved at each stage, how many revision rounds remain under the contract, and which files were delivered at the end. Scope creep (unlimited revisions, late-stage concept changes) is the biggest margin killer. This tool maintains a per-project record: client name, project type, contracted deliverables, revision rounds included (by contract), reference images provided, and the full brief in structured fields. Each revision round is logged with date, what changes were requested, and what was delivered in response. A revision round counter shows the client and illustrator how many rounds remain. Final delivery is logged with file names and format, generating a delivery confirmation email draft. The illustrator has a complete paper trail if a client disputes what was agreed or delivered. Monetization: $12-18/mo per illustrator account (unlimited projects). Distribution: r/learnart and r/freelance illustrator communities, Society of Illustrators communities, ProCreate and Clip Studio Power User groups, illustration-focused Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Self-Managed Apartment Complex Maintenance Request Log & Vendor Resolution Tracker
Small landlords and property managers who self-manage 5-40-unit apartment complexes track tenant maintenance requests through a mix of text messages, voicemails, and email threads — making it nearly impossible to prove a work order was addressed within the legally required timeframe (most states require landlords to address habitability-related repairs within 24-72 hours). This tool gives tenants a simple web form or SMS number to submit maintenance requests; logs each request with timestamp, unit number, category (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, appliance, pest, other), and description; lets the landlord assign it to a vendor, log the vendor's scheduled visit date, and mark it resolved with completion notes. The landlord gets a per-unit maintenance history that doubles as a habitability compliance record — if a tenant later claims a repair was ignored, the landlord has timestamped documented evidence of request receipt, vendor assignment, and completion. An open-request aging report flags any request older than 48 hours for plumbing and electrical issues. Monetization: $15-25/mo per property manager account (up to 25 units); $3/mo per additional unit. Distribution: r/Landlord, BiggerPockets small-portfolio landlord communities, NARPM (National Association of Residential Property Managers) communities for self-managing landlords. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Local Print Newsletter Layout & Mailing List Manager
Community organizations, neighborhood associations, local businesses, and hobby clubs that distribute a monthly print newsletter — folded, mailed, or distributed at events — spend 3-6 hours per issue importing content into a Word or Google Doc template, adjusting column widths when content overflows, managing who gets a physical copy (the mailing list), and printing individual address labels. This tool provides a fixed-template newsletter layout builder (2 or 3 column, standard 8.5×11 folded format, masthead + content + back panel with mailing area) where the editor pastes content by section, the tool paginates it into the template, and the output is a print-ready PDF with proper margins and bleed. A mailing list manager tracks physical addresses, flags any returned mail as invalid, and prints a sheet of Avery-compatible mailing labels (30-per-page) sorted by ZIP code for first-class presort discounts. Monetization: $10-15/mo per organization account (unlimited issues). Distribution: Nextdoor neighborhood administrator communities, local civic organization administrator networks (Kiwanis, Rotary club secretaries), community gardening and neighborhood improvement association Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
