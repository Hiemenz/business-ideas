# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-10 (Round 167)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a voice-over artist, an online course student-success coach, a freelance photo retoucher, a resume and LinkedIn profile writer, a mobile dog trainer and a cohort-course instructor.

---

### 1. Voice-Over Artist Audition, Booking & Usage-Fee Tracker

A freelance voice-over artist sends many auditions each week through marketplaces and agents, and has to track which were sent, which got callbacks and which jobs are booked. They also track each job's agreed usage term (for example 1 year of broadcast use) so they can invoice a renewal when it ends. Without a record, renewal fees are missed and the artist does not know which audition types land work.

The tool logs auditions and outcomes, tracks booked jobs with delivery deadlines and invoice status, and fires renewal reminders when a usage term ends.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per artist. Distribution: voice-over communities on Facebook and Discord, r/voiceacting and VO coaching groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Online Course Student-Success Coach Check-In & Completion Tracker

A coach hired by a course creator to raise completion has to track each student's progress, last login and check-in history. A coach with 100-300 students cannot see who stalled in module 2 and would respond to a message. Creators judge the coach by completion and refund rates.

The tool imports student progress, flags students stalled for 7+ days, schedules check-in messages and reports completion rate and refund-request rate per cohort.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per coach. Distribution: course-creator communities, Skool groups and r/OnlineCourses. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 3. Freelance Photo Retoucher Batch, Turnaround & Client-Preference Tracker

A retoucher handling batches of wedding, portrait or e-commerce photos has to track the number of images per batch, due date, and each client's style notes ("keep skin texture, no teeth whitening"). With several photographers as clients, forgetting preferences forces rework, and a missed batch deadline costs the client relationship.

The tool tracks batches with image counts and deadlines, a style sheet per client, revision counts and a billing view by image or by batch.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per retoucher. Distribution: photographer and retoucher Facebook groups, r/photoshop and editing-service communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Resume & LinkedIn Profile Writer Client Intake, Draft & Outcome Tracker

A freelance resume writer has to track each client's intake answers, target roles, draft versions and revision requests. A writer taking 15-25 clients a month also wants outcome data (interviews landed after the new resume), which they can turn into testimonials and higher prices. Follow-up messages for upsells (cover letter, LinkedIn rewrite) are often forgotten.

The tool stores intake and drafts per client, tracks revisions, prompts a 30-day outcome check-in and logs upsell offers and results.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per writer. Distribution: career-coach and resume-writer groups on Facebook and LinkedIn, r/resumes and r/careerguidance. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Dog Trainer Client Session, Homework & Package Tracker

A mobile dog trainer who sells 4-8 session packages has to track each dog's training goals, homework assigned and progress between sessions. A trainer with 20-30 active dogs forgets which owner was assigned which exercise. Package balance tracking also prompts renewal conversations before the last session.

The tool keeps a per-dog goal and session-notes record, sends homework summaries to owners after each session, and tracks package balance with a renewal prompt.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per trainer. Distribution: dog-trainer Facebook groups, r/dogtraining trainer threads and local pet-business networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Cohort-Course Instructor Enrollment, Payment-Plan & Attendance Tracker

An instructor who runs live cohort courses (for example a 6-week bootcamp) has to track enrollment, installment payments and session attendance. Students on payment plans quietly miss installments, and absentees need recordings and a catch-up note. The instructor wants to know which cohort members are likely to drop out.

The tool tracks enrollments and payment-plan status with a missed-installment flag, session attendance with a catch-up checklist and a cohort-level completion view.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per instructor. Distribution: Maven and Skool instructor communities, indie course-creator Twitter/X circles. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.
