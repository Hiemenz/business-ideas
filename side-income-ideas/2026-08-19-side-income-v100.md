# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-19 (Session 100)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- Simple, fast-to-build workflow trackers — no regulatory compliance angle.
- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.

### 1. Independent Gunsmith Work-Order, Firearm-Intake & Parts Tracker

Independent gunsmiths performing custom builds, rebarreling, trigger work, and refinishing for individual customers need to track a per-firearm work-order log recording intake condition, requested work, and current bench status (torn down, parts on order, reassembled, test-fired), since a gunsmith juggling 15-25 customer firearms on the bench at once can't rely on memory to know which rifle is waiting on a specific part versus which one is ready for pickup. The tool also needs a chain-of-custody log tied to each firearm's serial number, since a gunsmith handling customer-owned firearms needs a clear, timestamped record of when each one came in and when it left, both for the customer's peace of mind and the gunsmith's own liability protection. It also needs a parts and gunsmithing-supply inventory tracked by caliber, part type, and supplier, since a rebarrel or custom build often stalls waiting on a specific part and a gunsmith needs to know what's on hand before quoting a turnaround time. Most independent gunsmiths currently manage this across a paper tag tied to each firearm and a mental list of what parts are on order, with no structured link between a specific firearm's work-order status and the serial-number chain-of-custody record protecting both parties.

The tool maintains a per-firearm work-order tracker (intake, in-progress, parts-pending, ready) tied to serial number, a chain-of-custody log timestamping intake and pickup, and a parts/supply inventory by caliber and part type.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/gunsmithing and r/gunsmith, the American Gunsmithing Association (AGA) member directory, and local gun-shop and range referral relationships (shops routinely refer custom work they don't do in-house). Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 2. Independent Custom Knife Maker & Bladesmith Commission & Steel-Batch Tracker

Independent bladesmiths and custom knife makers forging and finishing knives for individual commission clients need to track a per-commission stage tracker — design approved, forged, heat-treated, ground, handle/sheath fitted, ready for delivery — since a single custom knife moves through several distinct shop stages over weeks, and a smith juggling 8-15 concurrent commissions can't rely on memory to know which blade is still waiting on heat-treat versus which one just needs a handle fitted. The tool also needs a steel-batch and heat-treat-log inventory tracked by steel type and batch/heat number, since a blade's hardness, edge retention, and any warranty claim trace back to exactly which steel batch and heat-treat cycle it went through, and a smith who can't match a finished blade back to its batch record has no way to diagnose a customer's warranty complaint. It also needs a materials inventory for handle stock (wood, G10, bone) and sheath leather, since custom orders often specify exact handle materials that need to be on hand before a commission can be quoted a delivery date. Most independent bladesmiths currently manage this across a shop whiteboard and a notebook of heat-treat notes, with no structured link between a specific finished blade and the steel batch it came from.

The tool maintains a per-commission stage tracker (design, forge, heat-treat, grind, handle/sheath, ready), a steel-batch/heat-treat log linking each blade to its batch number, and a handle-material and sheath-leather inventory.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/bladesmith and r/knifemaking, the American Bladesmith Society (ABS) member directory, and knife-show and cutlery-guild referral networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Mobile Boat Detailing & Hull-Cleaning Diver Route & Vessel-History Tracker

Independent mobile boat detailers and hull-cleaning divers who wash, wax, and dive-clean hulls at marina slips need to track a per-vessel service-history record noting hull material, antifouling-paint type, and last-cleaning date, since a diver servicing 40-80 boats across several marinas can't rely on memory to know which hull is due for its monthly warm-water cleaning versus which one just got serviced two weeks ago. The tool also needs a seasonal fouling-rate route board organized by slip location, since warm, brackish, or high-traffic marina water fouls a hull far faster than a cold-water or low-traffic slip, and a diver without a structured route ends up guessing which boats actually need this week's visit. It also needs a topside detailing package log (wash, wax, gelcoat polish) separate from the underwater hull-cleaning record, since many clients bundle both services and a diver needs to track which was actually completed on a given visit. Most independent boat detailers currently manage this across a paper marina route sheet and a rough memory of which hull was cleaned when, with no structured link between a specific vessel's fouling rate and its actual next-service-due date.

The tool maintains a per-vessel service-history record (hull material, antifouling type, last-cleaning date) tied to a marina slip, a seasonal fouling-rate route board, and a topside detailing package log.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/boating and r/sailing marina-services threads, the Association of Diving Contractors International (ADCI) small-operator network, and marina and yacht-club referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Independent Sailmaker & Marine Canvas/Rigging Repair Order & Material Tracker

Independent sailmakers and marine canvas fabricators building and repairing sails, bimini tops, dodgers, and running rigging for individual boat owners need to track a per-order pattern-and-material tracker recording sail or canvas dimensions, cloth weight, and hardware specs against a specific boat's rig, since a mainsail recut or a custom bimini top requires exact measurements tied to that one boat and a sailmaker juggling 10-20 concurrent orders can't rely on memory to know which order's pattern is finalized versus which is still waiting on a customer's rig measurements. The tool also needs a UV-resistant thread, sailcloth, and rigging-hardware inventory tracked by type and quantity, since a canvas or sail order stalls waiting on a specific cloth weight or a stainless hardware piece and a sailmaker needs to know what's on hand before quoting a delivery date. It also needs a seasonal rush-queue view, since spring commissioning season generates a flood of canvas and rigging orders all wanting delivery before boats splash for the season. Most independent sailmakers currently manage this across a paper pattern file and a rough inventory count, with no structured link between a specific boat's rig specifications and the order's actual material and hardware needs.

The tool maintains a per-order pattern/material tracker tied to boat rig specifications, a sailcloth/thread/hardware inventory by type and quantity, and a seasonal spring-commissioning rush queue.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/sailing and r/sailmaking, the American Boat Builders and Repairers Association (ABBRA) member network, and marina and yacht-club rigger referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Hot Tub & Spa Service Technician Water-Chemistry & Route Tracker

Independent hot tub and spa service technicians maintaining free-standing residential and light-commercial spas need to track a per-unit water-chemistry log recording bromine/pH readings and filter-change dates on a much tighter cycle than a swimming pool route, since spas run warmer and recirculate faster than pools, throwing chemistry out of balance within days rather than weeks, and a technician servicing 60-100 units across a service area can't rely on memory to know which spa's chemistry was just balanced versus which one is overdue. The tool also needs a cover and jet-pump condition log per unit, since a worn cover or a failing jet pump is a common service call a technician needs to flag and quote separately from a routine chemistry visit. It also needs a route board organized by neighborhood and service-day cycle, since spa maintenance runs on a tighter weekly-to-biweekly cadence than a pool route and a technician without a structured schedule ends up missing a unit's chemistry window. Most independent spa technicians currently manage this across a paper service ticket and a rough memory of which spa's chemistry runs hot, with no structured link between a specific unit's chemistry history and its actual next-service-due date.

The tool maintains a per-unit water-chemistry log (bromine/pH readings, filter-change dates) tied to a client address, a cover/jet-pump condition log, and a tight-cycle route board by neighborhood.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/hottub and local spa-dealer referral relationships (dealers routinely refer service work they don't handle in-house), and the Pool & Hot Tub Alliance (PHTA) member directory. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Independent Custom Soap & Bath Product Maker Batch-Log & Wholesale Order Tracker

Independent soap makers and bath-product crafters producing cold-process soap, bath bombs, and body butters for retail and wholesale sale need to track a per-batch production log recording the recipe, production date, and cure-completion date, since cold-process soap requires a mandatory 4-6 week cure before it's ready to sell and a maker running 10-20 batches a month can't rely on memory to know which batch is fully cured and retail-ready versus which is still three weeks out. The tool also needs a raw-material inventory tracked by oil, lye, and fragrance-oil lot number, since batch-to-batch consistency and any allergen or scent-related customer complaint trace back to exactly which fragrance-oil lot went into a specific batch. It also needs a wholesale and farmers-market order tracker separating cured, sellable inventory from still-curing batches, since a maker taking wholesale orders from local shops needs to commit only to inventory that will actually be cured by the delivery date. Most independent soap makers currently manage this across a curing-shelf label system and a notebook of recipe notes, with no structured link between a specific batch's cure-completion date and what's actually available to sell or ship.

The tool maintains a per-batch production log (recipe, production date, cure-completion date) with lot-numbered raw-material tracking, and a wholesale/farmers-market order tracker separating cured from still-curing inventory.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per account. Distribution: r/soapmaking and the Handcrafted Soap & Cosmetic Guild (HSCG) member directory, plus farmers-market vendor associations and Etsy wholesale seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
