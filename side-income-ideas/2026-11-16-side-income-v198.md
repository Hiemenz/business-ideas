# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-16 (Round 198)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a network cabling installer, a phone-screen repairer, a home-theater calibrator, a mobile printer repair tech, a powersports mechanic and a trailer-graphics installer.

---

### 1. Network Cabling Installer Job, Run & Materials Tracker

A low-voltage installer who runs cable for offices and homes has to track jobs, cable runs, drops, materials and test results. Quotes depend on counts, and customers ask for run maps later.

The tool tracks jobs with drop counts and maps, estimates materials, stores test results and tracks deposits.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per installer. Distribution: low-voltage and IT communities, r/networking and local contractor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Phone-Screen Repairer Ticket, Parts & Warranty Tracker

A phone repairer has to track tickets, device models, parts in stock, repair status and a short warranty on parts. Part costs vary, and returns need a record of the original repair.

The tool tracks tickets and parts, shows stock by model, logs repair warranty dates and texts ready-for-pickup.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per repairer. Distribution: repair-shop communities, r/mobilerepair and local small-business groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Home-Theater Calibrator Client, Room & Settings Tracker

A calibrator who tunes TVs and audio in homes has to track each client's equipment, room notes, settings applied and follow-up visits. Clients change gear and ask for a re-tune.

The tool stores equipment and settings per client, tracks visits and invoices and reminds about a yearly re-calibration.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per calibrator. Distribution: home-theater communities, r/hometheater and local integrator networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Printer-Repair Tech Customer, Machine & Service Tracker

A tech who services office printers has to track each customer's machines, service history, parts and contract dates. Repeat faults on the same model point to a part replacement or a new machine sale.

The tool tracks machines and service history, flags repeat faults, schedules visits and tracks contract renewals.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per tech. Distribution: IT and print-service groups, r/sysadmin and local small-business networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Powersports Mechanic Unit, Job & Season-Prep Tracker

A mechanic who services ATVs, snowmobiles and jet skis has to track units, jobs, parts and seasonal prep. Demand spikes before each season, and storage services need tracking.

The tool tracks units and jobs, manages storage dates, sends a pre-season service reminder and shows revenue by season.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per mechanic. Distribution: powersports communities, r/snowmobiling and local dealer networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to the season.

### 6. Trailer-Graphics Installer Quote, Design & Install Tracker

A person who installs vinyl graphics on trailers and trucks has to track quotes, proofs, vehicle measurements and install dates. Clients approve designs in several rounds, and a fleet can mean many vehicles.

The tool tracks quotes and proof rounds, stores vehicle measurements, schedules installs and tracks fleet batches.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per installer. Distribution: vehicle-wrap communities, r/vinyl and local fleet-owner groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
