# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-10 (Round 168)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: an automation freelancer, a Meta ads freelancer, a YouTube channel manager, a pickleball coach, a mobile bartender and a LinkedIn lead-generation operator.

---

### 1. Automation Freelancer (Zapier/Make) Client Workflow, Maintenance & Retainer Tracker

A freelancer who builds automations in Zapier, Make or n8n has to track every client's live workflows, the apps each one touches, and credentials or connection owners. When a connected app changes, a workflow breaks, and the freelancer needs to know which clients are affected. Retainer clients expect monitoring and monthly updates that the freelancer must record.

The tool lists workflows per client with status and connected apps, logs failures and fixes, and tracks retainer hours against what is billed.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per freelancer. Distribution: Zapier and Make expert communities, r/nocode and automation-agency Skool groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 2. Meta Ads Freelancer Client Spend, Result & Report Tracker

A freelancer who runs Facebook and Instagram ads for several small businesses has to track each account's budget, cost per lead and creative tests. Clients want a weekly report, and writing it by hand eats hours. The freelancer also needs to know which creatives are fatiguing before results drop.

The tool logs weekly spend, leads and cost per lead per client, tracks creative tests with a winner/loser tag, and produces a client-ready summary.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per freelancer. Distribution: media-buyer communities, r/FacebookAds and agency-owner Skool groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 3. YouTube Channel Manager Upload Pipeline, Title Test & Creator Report Tracker

A freelance channel manager who handles uploads, titles and scheduling for creators has to track each video's stage (script, edit, thumbnail, scheduled), the title and thumbnail variants tested and performance after 7 and 30 days. Managers handling 3-6 channels cannot remember which packaging tests won, and creators ask for proof of results.

The tool shows a per-channel pipeline board, records title and thumbnail tests with results, and creates a monthly creator report.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per manager. Distribution: YouTube-creator Discords, r/NewTubers and channel-management Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 4. Pickleball & Racquet Coach Student Skill, Lesson-Package & Court Booking Tracker

A private pickleball or tennis coach has to track each student's skills, drills assigned and progress, plus lesson packages and court bookings. A coach with 25-40 students forgets what each worked on last week and which court is booked when. Package renewals are lost when a student's last lesson passes unnoticed.

The tool keeps per-student skill notes and drills, tracks package balances with a renewal prompt and shows a weekly court and lesson calendar.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per coach. Distribution: pickleball and tennis coach Facebook groups, r/Pickleball and local club networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Bartender Event, Headcount & Supply Tracker

A mobile bartender who staffs private events and weddings has to track each event's date, headcount, drink menu, supplies and deposit status. For each event they must estimate quantities from guest count and duration, and an error either wastes money or runs out of ice or limes mid-party. Several events in a weekend make it easy to mix up supply lists.

The tool tracks events with deposits and balances, generates a supply list from headcount, menu and duration, and shows the weekend's events and packing list in one view.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per bartender. Distribution: bartender and event-staffing Facebook groups, r/bartenders and wedding-vendor networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. LinkedIn Lead-Generation Operator Outreach, Reply & Booked-Call Tracker

An operator who runs LinkedIn outreach for B2B clients tracks connection requests, accepted connections, replies and booked calls per client campaign. With 4-8 clients, they need to see reply rate per message variant and a weekly number to show the client. Clients pay per booked call or on retainer, so booked-call attribution matters.

The tool logs outreach activity per campaign, records message variants with reply rates, tracks booked calls with a client-confirmed status and reports weekly results.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per operator. Distribution: LinkedIn outreach communities, r/B2BSales and agency-owner Skool groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.
