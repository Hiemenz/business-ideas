# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-09 (Session 47)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- Simple, fast-to-build workflow trackers — no regulatory compliance angle.
- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.
- **No repeats.** This session's six operator types — independent tax preparers, window & gutter cleaning operators, mobile carpet & upholstery cleaning technicians, dog boarding & daycare facility operators, private-duty senior home care aides, and freelance resume writers/career coaches — are distinct from every prior session: tax preparers have only ever appeared as an incidental reference inside a "Remote Worker Home Office Tax Deduction Tracker" built for the self-employed taxpayer, never as the preparer's own client-facing practice tracker; window and gutter cleaning has only appeared as a one-line add-on service inside a lawn care route generator, never as its own dedicated route/property tool; carpet and upholstery cleaning has never appeared (the closest prior idea, Independent Upholsterer, covers reupholstering furniture in fabric, an entirely different trade from steam-cleaning existing carpet and upholstery in place); dog boarding/daycare has never appeared (Pet Sitter & Dog Walker and Mobile Pet Groomer cover mobile visit-based services, not an overnight boarding facility's kennel assignment and medication-schedule problem); private-duty senior home care aides have never appeared in any prior session; and freelance resume writers/career coaches have never appeared in any prior session.

### 1. Independent Tax Preparer Client Document & Filing Status Tracker
Solo tax preparers and small prep shops running 100-400 individual and small-business returns each season need to track which client documents have actually arrived (W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, prior-year returns, mortgage interest statements) against a checklist of what each client's return requires, because chasing down the last missing form by email and text across dozens of open returns during the January-April crunch is where preparers lose track of who's ready to file and who's still waiting on a document. Most solo preparers currently manage this with a shared spreadsheet or a stack of manila folders that doesn't show, at a glance, which of this week's 40 open returns are actually complete.

The tool maintains a per-client return record (entity type, prior-year return on file, and a document checklist auto-generated from the client's known income sources), a document intake log marking each required form as received/outstanding with a client-facing "what's still missing" summary that can be sent with one click, and a filing status board (documents pending, in preparation, client review, e-filed, accepted) so a preparer can answer "where does my return stand" without digging through a folder.

Monetization: $20-35/mo per preparer account, with a seasonal pricing option (higher during Jan-Apr, paused off-season). Distribution: the National Association of Tax Professionals (NATP) and National Society of Accountants (NSA) member communities, r/taxpros, and local independent tax preparer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Window & Gutter Cleaning Service Route & Property Tracker
Solo window and gutter cleaning operators service dozens of recurring residential and commercial accounts on staggered schedules — quarterly window cleaning here, biannual gutter cleaning there, seasonal storm-related add-ons everywhere — and need to know which property has how many windows (interior/exterior, screens, hard-to-reach second story), which gutters need guards versus open cleaning, and when each account is actually due next. Most operators currently track this from memory or a paper route book, which breaks down once the account list passes 60-80 properties and a customer calls asking why their gutters weren't cleaned before the fall leaf-drop.

The tool maintains a property profile per client (window count and type by floor, gutter linear footage, guard presence, and access notes like locked gates or dog-on-property warnings), a service-due calendar computing each account's next visit from its recurring frequency with an overdue flag for anything past its window, and a route-ordered job sheet grouping the day's stops geographically with before/after photo capture for dispute-proofing and marketing.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the International Window Cleaning Association (IWCA) member directory, r/windowcleaning, and local window/gutter cleaning business Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Mobile Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning Technician Client Property & Service History Tracker
Independent carpet and upholstery cleaning technicians running a truck-mount or portable extraction rig need to remember each property's carpet fiber type and square footage, which rooms have had prior stain treatment or pet-odor issues, and what solution and dwell time worked last visit — because using the wrong pretreatment on a delicate fiber or forgetting a client's recurring pet-stain problem means a redo visit that eats the day's margin. Most solo techs currently track this on a paper invoice pad that doesn't travel between jobs and gets tossed after the season.

The tool maintains a per-property service record (room-by-room square footage, fiber/upholstery material, and any known stain or odor history), a visit log (date, solution and pretreatment used, problem areas addressed, and photo documentation of stubborn spots before and after), and a rebooking reminder calculated from each client's typical cleaning interval so recurring residential and commercial accounts (offices, rental turnovers) get scheduled before carpets visibly need it again.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC) member network, r/CarpetCleaning, and local carpet cleaning business Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Dog Boarding & Daycare Facility Operator Kennel Assignment & Feeding/Medication Tracker
Small independent dog boarding and daycare facility operators running 15-40 kennels or daycare spots at a time need to track which dog is in which run, feeding schedules and diet restrictions, medication dosing times, and any behavioral or compatibility notes (which dogs can't be group-played together) — because a missed medication dose or an accidental play-group mixing of incompatible dogs is a liability incident, not just an oversight. Most small facilities currently run this off a dry-erase board in the kennel area that doesn't survive a shift change or a busy holiday boarding week.

The tool maintains a per-dog boarding/daycare profile (owner contact, vaccination records on file, feeding instructions, medication schedule with dosing times, and behavioral/compatibility flags), a daily kennel/run assignment board showing which dog occupies which space with check-in/check-out dates, and a medication and feeding checklist per shift that staff mark off in real time so nothing gets missed across a full house of boarders.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per facility account. Distribution: the Pet Care Services Association (PCSA/Pet Boarding & Daycare Association) member directory, r/dogboarding, and local kennel/daycare owner Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 5. Private-Duty Senior Home Care Aide/Companion Client Care Log & Family Communication Tracker
Independent, non-medical senior home care aides and companions working directly for families (outside a home care agency) need to track each client's daily routine — medication reminders, meals, mobility assistance needs, and any changes in condition noticed during a visit — and communicate that back to family members who aren't present, because a family paying for private in-home care wants visibility into how their parent's day actually went, and an aide juggling several clients across the week loses track of routine details without a structured record. Most independent aides currently text family members informally or keep handwritten notes that don't create a consistent record over time.

The tool maintains a per-client care profile (daily routine, medication reminder schedule, mobility/dietary notes, and emergency contact information), a visit log (date, tasks completed, meals and medication reminders given, and any observed changes worth flagging to family), and a family-facing visit summary that's automatically generated and shareable after each visit so relatives stay informed without a phone call.

Monetization: $18-28/mo per aide account. Distribution: private-duty caregiver Facebook groups, Care.com and CareLinx independent caregiver communities, and r/homehealthaide. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Freelance Resume Writer & Career Coach Client Pipeline & Document Version Tracker
Independent resume writers and career coaches working with 15-30 active clients at a time need to track each client's job search goals, which resume/cover letter version is the current one after several rounds of revisions, and where each client stands in a multi-session coaching package (intro call, first draft, revision rounds, LinkedIn optimization, mock interview) — because losing track of which document version a client last received, or forgetting a client is three sessions into a five-session package, undermines the professional polish clients are paying for. Most independent coaches currently manage this across scattered email threads and a folder of dated Word documents with no structure connecting sessions to deliverables.

The tool maintains a client profile (target role/industry, package purchased, and sessions remaining with auto-decrementing balance), a document version log tracking every resume/cover letter/LinkedIn draft sent with version notes so the current approved version is always one click away, and a session log (date, topics covered, action items assigned, and next-session focus) so a client's next call picks up exactly where the last one left off.

Monetization: $18-28/mo per coach account. Distribution: the National Resume Writers' Association (NRWA) and Professional Association of Resume Writers & Career Coaches (PARWCC) member directories, plus r/careerguidance coach-facing threads and career coach Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
