# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-13 (Round 183)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a beekeeper selling honey, a cut-flower farmer, a saddle and tack repairer, a record-store online seller, a voice-over actor and a 3D-print farm operator.

---

### 1. Beekeeper Hive, Harvest & Honey-Sales Tracker

A hobby-to-small-business beekeeper has to track hives by inspection date, queen age, treatments, honey yield per hive and jar sales to markets and shops. Yield per hive shows which colonies earn their keep, and jar inventory by flavor decides what to bottle next.

The tool logs inspections, records harvest weight per hive, tracks bottled stock and sales by channel and shows profit per hive.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per beekeeper. Distribution: beekeeping clubs, r/Beekeeping and farmers-market vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Cut-Flower Farmer Bed, Bouquet & Subscription Tracker

A small flower farmer has to track beds by variety and succession planting, harvest counts, bouquet subscriptions and wedding orders. A missed succession means a gap in stems, and wedding orders need exact stem counts by date.

The tool schedules succession plantings backward from order dates, tracks stems harvested versus committed and shows revenue per bed.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per farmer. Distribution: flower-farmer communities, r/flowers growers threads and farmers-market groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Saddle and Tack Repairer Job, Part & Pickup Tracker

A leatherworker who repairs saddles, bridles and harnesses has to track each item's owner, damage, quote, hardware ordered and promised return date. Riders need tack back before shows, and a quote approval trail avoids disputes.

The tool logs intake with photos, tracks repair stages and parts, records quote approvals and texts when the item is ready.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per repairer. Distribution: equestrian Facebook groups, r/Leathercraft and local feed-store boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Record-Store Online Seller Crate, Grade & Listing Tracker

A seller of used vinyl and CDs online has to track each batch's purchase cost, condition grades, listing status and sale price. With thousands of items, it's easy to buy a duplicate or underprice a rare pressing.

The tool tracks purchase lots, stores grades and photos, flags duplicates before buying and shows profit per lot.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per seller. Distribution: record-collector communities, r/VinylCollectors and Discogs seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Voice-Over Actor Audition, Booking & Usage Tracker

A freelance voice actor auditions for many jobs and has to track each audition, the client, rate quoted, session date, delivery format and usage period. Reuse of a recording beyond its agreed term is lost income if not noted.

The tool logs auditions and win rate, tracks bookings with rates and delivery deadlines and reminds the actor when a usage term ends so they can quote a renewal.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per actor. Distribution: voice-actor communities, r/VoiceActing and audition-platform groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. 3D-Print Farm Job, Filament & Machine-Time Tracker

A person running 5-20 printers for custom orders has to track each job's file, filament used, machine hours, failure rate and price. Failed prints and idle machines quietly eat margin, and filament stock runs out mid-job.

The tool queues jobs by machine, tracks filament spools and failures, computes cost per job and flags low stock before the next order.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per operator. Distribution: 3D-printing communities, r/3Dprinting business threads and Etsy maker groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
