# Side Income Ideas - 2026-09-01 (Round 162)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow trackers for six operator types not yet covered in this repo: an independent guitar instructor, a wedding & event live-band booking coordinator, a trading card & collectibles grading submission service, a mobile chainsaw & outdoor power equipment blade-sharpening route operator, a drone roof & property inspection service, and a rare & used book dealer.

---

### 1. Independent Guitar Instructor Student Repertoire, Technique & Lesson-Package Tracker

An independent guitar instructor teaching private lessons across acoustic, electric, and bass students at wildly different skill levels has to track each student's current technique focus (fingerpicking, barre chords, scales, music theory) and repertoire list, since an instructor running 20-40 students across a full weekly schedule can't rely on memory for which student just nailed a specific chord transition versus which one is still working through open chords, and assigning a song that repeats an already-mastered technique or skips a foundational one is a lesson the student and parent feel didn't move the needle. An instructor building a student toward a recital, a school talent show, or simply a specific song request also has to track progress against that goal with a target date, since a student who shows up underprepared because the instructor lost track of the date is a preventable letdown. Because instructors are commonly paid per lesson package (a 10-lesson block, a monthly subscription) rather than per single session, the instructor also needs a simple way to track each student's remaining lessons in their current package and flag when a renewal conversation is due before the block quietly runs out.

The tool maintains a per-student profile (technique focus, chord/scale progress, and current repertoire) with a lesson-note log per session; a goal tracker linking a student to an upcoming recital, show, or specific song target with a countdown; and a lesson-package balance tracker flagging renewal-due students before their block runs out.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per instructor. Distribution: the National Guitar Workshop and Music Teachers National Association (MTNA) member networks, r/Guitar and r/musicteachers instructor threads, and independent-guitar-teacher Facebook groups where instructors already trade curriculum-pacing and recital-prep tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Wedding & Event Live-Band Booking Coordinator Lineup, Setlist & Deposit Tracker

An independent musician who books and leads a rotating lineup of sidemen for wedding and corporate-event gigs has to track which specific musicians are confirmed for each booking, since a wedding band pulling from a pool of 15-25 freelance players across drums, bass, keys, horns, and vocals can't rely on a group text to confirm who's actually locked in for a given Saturday, and a no-show discovered the morning of a client's wedding is a reputation-ending failure with no time to fix it. The coordinator also has to build and adjust a per-event setlist and run-of-show (first dance, parent dances, dinner music, dance-set order) tailored to that specific couple's requests, since a band that shows up without the couple's confirmed must-play and do-not-play list is a client complaint the coordinator hears about for months afterward. Because most events require a signed contract and a deposit before the date is locked, and a final balance due on the event date, the coordinator also needs a simple way to track which bookings are fully deposited versus still owing, since chasing down a missing final payment after the band has already loaded out is a much harder collection than catching it before the gig.

The tool maintains a per-event lineup roster confirming which musician is booked for which instrument/role with a confirmation-status flag per player; a per-event setlist and run-of-show builder tied to the couple's or client's specific requests; and a deposit-and-final-payment tracker flagging any event approaching its date with an outstanding balance.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per bandleader/coordinator. Distribution: The Knot and WeddingWire vendor-forum threads, the American Federation of Musicians (AFM) local-chapter networks, and independent-wedding-band Facebook groups where bandleaders already trade lineup-reliability and setlist-planning tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Trading Card & Collectibles Grading Submission Service Batch, Turnaround & Client-Card Tracker

An independent submission service that pools client cards into bulk grading orders sent to PSA, BGS, or CGC — cheaper per-card than an individual submitter's own direct order — has to track which specific cards belong to which client within a single mixed submission batch, since a batch of 60-150 cards sent to a grading company and returned weeks later as a stack of graded slabs has no built-in way to remember that card #37 in the batch belongs to a specific client unless the service tracked it going in, and returning the wrong card to the wrong client is an expensive, trust-destroying mistake with cards that can be worth hundreds or thousands of dollars each. The service also has to track each submission batch's declared value, service tier, and expected turnaround against the grading company's own published (and frequently changing) turnaround times, since a client asking "where's my card" needs a specific answer tied to the actual submission date and tier rather than a guess, and a batch that quietly exceeds its expected turnaround without the service noticing is a client relationship that erodes one unanswered question at a time. Because clients pay both a per-card grading fee and the service's own handling markup, the service also needs a simple way to track which clients have paid their invoice for a completed, returned batch versus which cards are sitting in inventory awaiting payment before they can be shipped back.

The tool maintains a per-batch submission tracker linking every card to its owning client with condition notes and declared value; a turnaround tracker per batch computing expected return date against the grading company's current published tier times with an overdue flag; and a per-client invoice-and-payment-status tracker gating a graded card's return shipment until payment is confirmed.

Monetization: $19-35/mo per submission service, scaling with monthly batch volume. Distribution: r/PSAcard and r/sportscards submission-service threads, the Trading Card Dealers Association member network, and independent-grading-service Facebook and Discord groups where operators already trade turnaround-time updates and batch-tracking tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Chainsaw & Outdoor Power Equipment Blade-Sharpening Route Operator Ticket, Turnaround & Route Tracker

An independent mobile sharpening operator who runs a route servicing chainsaw chains, mower blades, and other outdoor power equipment blades for landscaping companies, tree services, and homeowners has to track which specific blades belong to which customer when several jobs' worth of chains and blades are collected in a single stop, since a route stop at a landscaping company that hands over a bin of a dozen mixed mower blades and chainsaw chains has no built-in way to know which sharpened blade goes back on which specific mower or saw unless the operator tagged it at pickup, and a blade returned to the wrong machine is a customer complaint about cut quality that traces back to a mismatch, not the sharpening itself. The operator also has to track each customer's service frequency and next-due date, since a landscaping crew running mowers daily needs blades sharpened on a tighter cycle than a homeowner's occasional-use chainsaw, and a route operator relying on memory for who's due this week loses recurring revenue to a competitor who calls first. Because the operator often sharpens on-site from a mobile grinding rig rather than a drop-off shop, they also need a simple day's-route planner sequencing stops geographically against each customer's due date, so a day servicing ten stops doesn't turn into a wasted trip because a customer's blades weren't actually staged and ready when the truck arrived.

The tool maintains a per-customer blade/chain inventory with a tag-and-match system linking each sharpened item back to its owning machine; a service-frequency tracker computing each customer's next-due date from their actual usage pattern; and a daily route planner sequencing stops geographically against who's currently due.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per operator. Distribution: r/Landscaping and r/chainsaw route-service threads, the Outdoor Power Equipment and Engine Service Association (OPEESA) dealer network, and independent-mobile-sharpening Facebook groups where operators already trade route-density and pricing tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Drone Roof & Property Inspection Service Job, Deliverable & Client Report Tracker

An independent drone operator who flies roof and property inspections for insurance adjusters, roofing contractors, and real estate agents has to track each job's specific deliverable requirements, since an insurance-adjuster client typically needs a full-roof orthomosaic image set with damage annotations while a roofing-contractor client just wants close-up shingle photos of specific flagged areas, and an operator delivering the wrong format to the wrong client type is a redo that burns the flight time and editing hours twice. The operator also has to track flight conditions and airspace status per job site, since a property near an airport or inside controlled airspace may need a same-day FAA LAANC authorization pulled before the flight, and showing up to a job site without having checked airspace status is a wasted trip when the flight can't legally happen that day. Because most jobs are billed per-property with a same-day or next-day turnaround expectation, the operator also needs a simple way to track which completed jobs have had their photo/video deliverable actually sent to the client and invoiced, since a backlog of flown-but-undelivered jobs is revenue sitting uncollected while the next day's bookings pile up.

The tool maintains a per-job deliverable-requirements checklist matched to client type (insurance, roofing, real estate) with a photo/video package template per type; a per-site airspace and LAANC-authorization status check ahead of each scheduled flight; and a job-completion and invoicing tracker flagging any flown job whose deliverable hasn't been sent or invoiced.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per operator, scaling to $49/mo for a multi-pilot operation. Distribution: r/drones commercial-services threads, local Realtor MLS vendor-referral networks, and independent-drone-pilot Facebook groups serving insurance and roofing clients where operators already trade LAANC and deliverable-turnaround tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Rare & Used Book Dealer Inventory, Condition-Grading & Multi-Channel Listing Tracker

An independent rare and used book dealer sourcing inventory from estate sales, library sales, and individual sellers has to track each book's specific condition grade (a used-book trade uses its own standardized scale — Fine, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor — plus dust-jacket condition noted separately from the book itself) and any first-edition or signed-copy details that materially change its value, since a dealer running an inventory of hundreds to thousands of individual titles can't rely on memory for which specific copy of a common title is actually a valuable first printing versus a later, much less valuable edition, and misgrading a book's condition on a listing is the single fastest way to generate a return and a negative review from a buyer expecting exactly what was described. A dealer also commonly lists the same inventory across several channels at once — AbeBooks, eBay, a personal storefront — since no single marketplace reaches every category of buyer, and a book sold on one channel that isn't immediately marked unavailable on the others is a double-sale the dealer has to apologize for and refund. Because sourcing trips (an estate sale, a library book sale) often mean acquiring dozens of books in a single afternoon for a lump sum, the dealer also needs a simple way to track per-lot acquisition cost against what each individual book in that lot eventually sells for, since a dealer with no per-lot cost basis has no real way to know whether a specific sourcing trip was actually profitable.

The tool maintains a per-book inventory record (condition grade, edition/printing details, and acquisition lot) with a cross-channel listing-status sync flagging any book still showing available on a channel after it's sold elsewhere; a per-lot acquisition-cost tracker reconciling lot cost against individual sale prices as books sell; and a sourcing-trip profitability summary per acquisition event.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per dealer. Distribution: the Antiquarian Booksellers' Association of America (ABAA) member network, r/Booksellers and r/usedbooks dealer threads, and independent-bookseller Facebook groups where dealers already trade condition-grading standards and cross-channel listing tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
