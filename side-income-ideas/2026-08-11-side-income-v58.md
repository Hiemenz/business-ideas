# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-11 (Session 58)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- Simple, fast-to-build workflow trackers — no regulatory compliance angle.
- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.
- **No repeats.** This session's six operator types — estate sale liquidators, drone/aerial real estate photographers & videographers, costume designers & cosplay commission makers, irrigation & sprinkler system repair technicians, landscape lighting installers, and pet photographers — are distinct from every prior session: estate sale liquidation has never appeared (distinct from the auctioneer idea in an earlier main-income session, a regulated licensee running a bid-call sale under state consignor trust account rules, and from the furniture flipper & refinisher idea in an earlier session, which sources, owns, restores, and resells individual pieces on its own account — an estate sale liquidator instead catalogs and prices an entire client household's inventory on a commission-split consignment basis and runs a single on-site multi-day sale, an item-cataloging and sale-day logistics problem neither an auction license nor a flipper's acquisition-and-restoration-margin tracker addresses); drone and aerial real estate photography/videography has never appeared (distinct from the wedding & event videographer idea in an earlier session, which tracks raw-footage backup redundancy, edit rounds, and music sync-licensing for a single multi-week wedding edit, and from the freelance photographer client gallery delivery idea referenced in an earlier session, a still-image workflow — a real estate drone shooter instead runs a high-volume, 24-48-hour-turnaround shoot-and-deliver cycle per listing with drone-specific flight/equipment logistics, an entirely different client base, deliverable cadence, and turnaround pressure than either a wedding edit or a generic photo gallery); costume design and cosplay commission work has never appeared (distinct from the independent tailor & alterations shop idea in an earlier session, which hems and repairs a client's existing garments on a same-week ticket, and from the furniture flipper's acquisition-and-resale pattern — a costume commission is instead built from scratch to a client's original design reference against a fixed convention or event deadline, with its own pattern-piece and materials-sourcing tracking no alterations ticket or resale-margin tracker addresses); irrigation and sprinkler system repair has never appeared (distinct from the driveway sealcoating & asphalt maintenance route operator idea in an earlier session, which tracks a multi-year resealing interval and weather-blocked scheduling for asphalt, and from every landscaping/lawn care idea already in this repo, which manages turf maintenance, not a below-ground zone/valve piping system — irrigation instead centers on a per-property zone and valve map plus a twice-yearly spring-activation/fall-winterization scheduling cycle no turf or asphalt tracker touches); landscape lighting installation has never appeared (distinct from the home theater & AV installer idea in an earlier session, which wires indoor audio-visual and automation systems, and from the holiday light & seasonal exterior decor installer idea in an earlier session, which installs and tears down temporary seasonal lighting on a shared compressed calendar-driven window — landscape lighting is instead a permanent low-voltage exterior electrical installation with its own per-fixture wiring/transformer-load map and only periodic, not seasonal-teardown, maintenance visits); and pet photography has never appeared (distinct from the wedding & event videographer idea and the freelance photographer gallery delivery idea referenced above — a pet photography session runs on-location at a client's home or a dog park with unpredictable subject-handling and session-length variability, plus a print/canvas product-fulfillment component neither a wedding video workflow nor a generic photo-gallery tracker addresses).

### 1. Estate Sale Liquidator Inventory, Pricing & Consignment Split Tracker

Independent estate sale liquidators running on-site sales of an entire client household's belongings need to track thousands of individual items across dozens of categories (furniture, collectibles, tools, kitchenware, jewelry) with a set price per item, because a liquidator cataloging a full house before a compressed 2-3 day sale weekend can't rely on memory for which item was priced at what, and losing track of the agreed commission-split percentage on a specific high-value item creates exactly the kind of dispute with a grieving or relocating family that damages a referral-driven reputation fastest. Most independent liquidators currently manage this across a stack of handwritten price tags and a notebook tally with no structured link between what sold, what it sold for, and what the client is actually owed.

The tool maintains a per-sale inventory catalog (item description, category, photo, and asking price), a sale-day tracker recording which items sold and at what final price (including markdown rounds as the sale weekend progresses), and a client settlement report computing the agreed commission split against the total proceeds so a payout statement can be generated the moment the sale closes.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per liquidator account. Distribution: r/EstateSales and r/Antiques threads, the American Society of Estate Liquidators (ASEL) member directory, and local estate sale company Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Drone & Aerial Real Estate Photographer/Videographer Listing Shoot & Delivery Tracker

Independent drone and aerial real estate photographers shooting multiple listings a week for agents and brokers need to track each listing's shoot details (property address, requested media package — stills, video walkthrough, twilight shots — and agent contact), a media library organized per listing so raw footage doesn't get mixed up across a busy Saturday of back-to-back shoots, and a strict delivery deadline since agents typically need edited media within 24-48 hours to get a new listing live, because missing a same-week delivery window on a hot listing costs the shooter that agent's repeat business immediately. Most independent real estate drone shooters currently manage this across a phone photo app and a text-message thread per agent with no structured link between a shoot's raw footage and its delivery deadline.

The tool maintains a per-listing shoot record (address, requested media package, shoot date, and agent contact), a media library per listing linking raw files to their edited/delivered versions, and a delivery tracker (promised turnaround, revision requests, and delivered status) flagging any listing approaching its 24-48-hour delivery window before it slips.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per photographer account. Distribution: r/realestate and r/drones threads, the Professional Aerial Photographers Association (PAPA) member directory, and local real estate photographer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Costume Designer & Cosplay Commission Maker Build-Stage & Materials Tracker

Independent costume designers and cosplay makers building custom commissions from a client's reference design need to track each commission's exact spec (character reference images, measurements, fabric and material list, and armor/prop components), its current build stage (pattern drafted, fabric cut, sewn, painted/weathered, ready to ship), and a hard convention or event deadline the piece must be finished by, because a maker juggling 5-10 commissions at once across a con season can't rely on memory for which piece is waiting on a specific foam or fabric order versus ready for final fitting, and missing a convention deadline is a non-negotiable failure with no partial-credit outcome for the client. Most independent makers currently manage this across a Pinterest reference board and a paper measurement sheet with no structured link between a commission's build stage and its remaining time to deadline.

The tool maintains a per-commission spec sheet (reference images, measurements, and materials list with cost), a build-stage board (drafted, cut, sewn, painted, finished) so shop status is visible at a glance, and a deadline tracker computing days remaining to each commission's convention or event date so the highest-urgency builds surface first across overlapping projects.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per maker account. Distribution: r/costubing and r/cosplay threads, local cosplay meetup and costume-maker Facebook groups, and convention artist-alley vendor networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Irrigation & Sprinkler System Repair Technician Zone Map, Seasonal Turn-On & Repair Ticket Tracker

Independent irrigation and sprinkler system technicians servicing residential and commercial properties need to track each property's zone and valve layout (zone count, valve locations, controller model, and head type per zone), the twice-yearly seasonal scheduling cycle — spring activation and leak-check, fall blowout and winterization — that applies to the technician's entire client base at once, and per-visit repair tickets (broken head, valve failure, controller reprogram), because a technician running 100+ properties can't rely on memory for which zone map belongs to which address, and showing up to winterize a system without knowing the zone count means guessing at compressor blow-out time and risking a missed zone that freezes and cracks a pipe over winter. Most independent technicians currently manage this on a paper zone-map sketch per property with no systematic way to see which properties are due for spring activation or fall winterization this week.

The tool maintains a per-property zone map (zone count, valve locations, controller model, and head type), a seasonal scheduling board (due for spring activation, due for fall winterization) surfacing the whole client base's compressed seasonal workload at a glance, and a per-visit repair ticket log (issue found, parts used, and resolution) building a service history so a future callback starts from the system's actual repair record.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per technician account. Distribution: r/irrigation and r/landscaping threads, the Irrigation Association (IA) certified-professional directory, and local irrigation/lawn-sprinkler contractor Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Landscape Lighting Installer Fixture Inventory, Wiring Map & Maintenance Visit Tracker

Independent landscape lighting installers designing and wiring permanent low-voltage exterior lighting systems need to track each property's fixture inventory (fixture type, wattage, and placement location by zone), the wiring run and transformer-load map (which fixtures run off which transformer and cable run, and the transformer's remaining load capacity for future additions), and periodic maintenance visits (bulb/LED module replacement, timer/photocell adjustment, fixture aiming), because an installer with dozens of properties can't rely on memory for a specific yard's transformer load when a client calls asking to add three more fixtures, and returning to a property months later without the original wiring map means re-tracing buried low-voltage cable runs from scratch. Most independent installers currently manage this across a folder of install photos with no structured link between a property's transformer capacity and what's already wired to it.

The tool maintains a per-property fixture inventory and wiring/transformer-load map (fixture list by zone, cable runs, and remaining transformer capacity), a maintenance visit log (bulb/module replacements, adjustments made, and next-service-due estimate), and a client add-on quoting reference showing at a glance whether a property's existing transformer has capacity for additional fixtures before a quote is given.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per installer account. Distribution: r/landscaping and r/lighting threads, the Association of Outdoor Lighting Professionals (AOLP) member directory, and local landscape lighting installer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Pet Photographer Session, Product Order & Delivery Tracker

Independent pet photographers shooting on-location sessions at clients' homes, dog parks, and studios need to track each session's booking details (pet name, breed/temperament notes, location, and session length booked), the shoot's product order (prints, canvases, digital gallery package) since pet photography income leans heavily on physical product sales rather than the session fee alone, and gallery delivery status, because a photographer booking multiple sessions a week can't rely on memory for which dog is reactive around other animals or needs extra warm-up time before shooting starts, and losing track of a client's canvas order size or print quantity mid-fulfillment creates exactly the kind of mistake that turns a happy session into a refund request. Most independent pet photographers currently manage this across a booking app note field and a separate print-lab order spreadsheet with no structured link between a session and what the client actually ordered afterward.

The tool maintains a per-session booking record (pet name, breed/temperament notes, location, and session length), a product order tracker (prints, canvases, digital package, sizes and quantities, and order status through the print lab), and a gallery delivery tracker (proofing gallery sent, client selections made, final delivery date) so no client is left wondering where their photos are.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per photographer account. Distribution: r/petphotography and r/DogTraining threads, the Professional Pet Photographers of America (PPPA) member directory, and local pet photographer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
