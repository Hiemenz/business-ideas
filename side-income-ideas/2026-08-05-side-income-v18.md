# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-05 (Round 18)

### 1. HOA Violation Notice Response Letter Generator
Homeowners who receive HOA violation notices — overgrown lawn, unapproved paint color, parking violation, holiday decoration policy — need to respond in writing within a short window to dispute the violation, request a hearing, or explain extenuating circumstances. Most homeowners don't know the correct tone, required references to CC&Rs, or how to formally request a hearing, and submit vague responses that carry no weight. This tool accepts the violation notice text, the specific CC&R section cited, and the homeowner's explanation, then generates a professionally formatted response letter that references the correct governing document language, asserts the homeowner's procedural rights, and requests specific relief. Monetization: $8-15 per letter or $12/mo subscription for repeat users. Distribution: r/HOA, HOA homeowner Facebook groups, and housing attorney referral networks. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Freelancer & Solopreneur Quarterly Estimated Tax Calculator
Self-employed freelancers and solopreneurs must pay IRS quarterly estimated taxes (Form 1040-ES) by April 15, June 15, September 15, and January 15 — but most calculate their quarterly payment wrong (or skip it) because they don't track YTD net income, don't know the self-employment tax component, and don't understand the safe harbor rules. This tool accepts YTD gross income, estimated business expenses, and prior year tax liability, then calculates the recommended quarterly estimated payment using both the current-year method and the safe harbor method, outputs the payment voucher amounts for each remaining quarter, and sends a calendar reminder email before each IRS deadline. Monetization: $9/mo or $29/yr. Distribution: r/freelance, r/selfemployed, freelancer Facebook groups, Indie Hackers, and Wave/FreshBooks community integrations. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Wedding Vendor Contract Key Clause Highlighter
Engaged couples sign contracts with photographers, caterers, venues, DJs, and florists without reading the fine print — then get surprised by cancellation penalties, overtime charges, venue exclusivity requirements, or force majeure clauses that leave them with no recourse if a vendor cancels. This tool accepts a PDF upload of a vendor contract, extracts and highlights the 8-12 highest-risk clause categories (cancellation policy, overtime fees, deposit non-refund terms, vendor substitution rights, weather/force majeure, tip expectations, and parking/vendor meal requirements), and outputs a one-page plain-English summary with a risk flag for any unusually aggressive clause compared to industry standards. Monetization: $12-20 per contract review. Distribution: r/weddingplanning, The Knot and WeddingWire community forums, bridal Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Multi-Unit Rental Property Utility Cost Allocation Calculator
Small landlords who own duplexes, triplexes, or small apartment buildings on single utility meters face a recurring problem: fairly splitting shared utility costs (electric, gas, water) between units. Manual splits based on fixed percentages create disputes when occupancy or usage changes. This tool accepts the utility bill total, each unit's square footage, occupancy count, and any sub-meter readings available, then calculates the fair allocation per unit using configurable methodologies (equal split, square footage proportion, occupancy proportion, or sub-meter ratio), generates a monthly utility statement for each tenant, and tracks year-over-year usage trends by unit. Monetization: $10-18/mo per landlord account. Distribution: BiggerPockets landlord forums, r/landlord, small landlord Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 5. Etsy & Handmade Shop Profit Margin Calculator and Pricing Optimizer
Etsy sellers routinely underprice their handmade products by forgetting to account for materials cost escalation, Etsy's transaction fees (6.5% + listing fees + payment processing), shipping packaging costs, and their own labor time. This tool accepts item-level inputs — materials cost, labor hours at a target hourly rate, packaging cost, and shipping weight — and outputs the true cost of goods, the minimum break-even price at various Etsy fee scenarios, and a recommended list price that achieves a target gross margin. It also flags items where the current listing price is below break-even based on the inputs. Monetization: $8-14/mo subscription or free tier limited to 5 items. Distribution: r/EtsySellers, Etsy seller Facebook groups, handmade business podcast communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. Remote Worker Home Office Tax Deduction Tracker
Self-employed remote workers who take the home office deduction (Form 8829 for Schedule C filers) must document their home office square footage, the home's total square footage, and their actual annual expenses (mortgage interest or rent, utilities, homeowner's insurance, repairs, and depreciation) to calculate the allowable deduction. Most self-employed workers either skip the deduction entirely (afraid of the paperwork) or take it incorrectly (forgetting to prorate by the business-use percentage and apply the gross income limitation). This tool collects the square footage, the business-use percentage, and monthly expense entries throughout the year, calculates the deductible amount under both the regular method and the simplified method ($5/sq ft), flags which method produces the larger deduction, and exports a Form 8829-ready summary for the user's tax preparer. Monetization: $7-12/mo or $24/yr. Distribution: r/freelance, r/selfemployed, remote work Slack communities, Keeper Tax and Hurdlr user communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
