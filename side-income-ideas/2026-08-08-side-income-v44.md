# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-08 (Session 44)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

---

### 1. Mobile Phone & Device Repair Technician Ticket & Parts Inventory Tracker
Independent mobile phone/tablet/laptop repair technicians who travel to customers or run a small storefront juggle repair tickets across dozens of device models simultaneously — a cracked screen here, a battery swap there, a water-damage diagnostic somewhere else — each needing the right part (screen, battery, charging port) pulled from a limited parts inventory, plus a warranty period on the repair itself. Most solo techs currently track this on a whiteboard or a generic notes app, which breaks down fast when three repairs are in progress at once and a customer calls asking "is my phone ready?"

The tool maintains a repair ticket per device (customer, device make/model/IMEI or serial, issue description, diagnostic notes, and status — diagnosed, awaiting parts, in repair, ready for pickup), a parts inventory by SKU with low-stock alerts tied to which device models are most commonly serviced, and a warranty tracker per completed repair (repair date, part used, warranty expiration) so a customer's return visit can be instantly checked against warranty coverage.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/phonerepair and r/CellPhoneRepair communities, the Cellular Telecommunications Industry Association (CTIA) independent repair shop forums, and local repair shop Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Independent Upholsterer Job & Fabric Yardage Tracker
Solo upholsterers reupholstering furniture — a client's antique armchair, a set of dining chairs, a boat seat — need to track each piece's exact measurements, fabric yardage required and ordered, foam/batting specs, and a multi-stage job timeline (strip old fabric, repair frame, cut and sew new fabric, final assembly) that can stretch over several weeks per piece. Currently most track this on paper tags stapled to the furniture itself, which get lost or illegible by the time a multi-week job is finished, and fabric orders get mismatched to the wrong client's piece when several jobs are in progress.

The tool maintains a job record per furniture piece (client, piece type, measurements, fabric selected with yardage calculation, foam/batting requirements, and photos before/during/after), a job-stage tracker showing where each piece sits in the strip-repair-cut-sew-assemble pipeline, and a fabric order log matching every yardage order to its specific job so nothing gets cross-delivered to the wrong piece.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the Upholstery Education Alliance and Association of Interior Design Trade Professionals member communities, plus r/Upholstery and local upholsterer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 3. Mobile Pressure Washing & Exterior Cleaning Service Route & Quote Tracker
Solo pressure washing operators bid jobs by surface type and square footage — a driveway, a two-story siding job, a commercial parking lot — each with different chemical/detergent needs (algae treatment, oil-stain pretreatment) and a route that has to sequence efficiently across a service area to avoid wasted drive time between jobs. Most currently quote from memory or a rough per-job mental average, leading to underbid jobs once water and chemical costs are tallied, and route planning happens ad hoc each morning with no view of which neighborhoods have open time slots.

The tool maintains a client property record (surface types, square footage, last service date, and any recurring service frequency for repeat commercial accounts), a quote calculator that computes a bid from surface type/square footage/chemical treatment needed against the operator's own cost-per-gallon and time-per-square-foot history, and a route-ordered job calendar grouping the day's bookings by geographic cluster with before/after photo capture per job for portfolio and dispute-proofing.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the Power Washers of North America (PWNA) member directory, r/PowerWashingPorn and r/pressurewashing communities, and local pressure washing business Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Picture Framer Custom Order & Molding Inventory Tracker
Solo custom picture framing shops handle a steady stream of one-off orders — a client's artwork, a diploma, a jersey shadow box — each with its own molding profile, mat color and dimensions, glazing type (regular, UV, museum glass), and a promised pickup date, while the shop's molding chop stock and mat board inventory need to be tracked so a popular profile doesn't run out mid-order. Most independent framers currently track orders on paper work tickets clipped to the artwork, which works until a busy holiday season hits and a client's irreplaceable photo or heirloom item gets misplaced or its specs get misremembered.

The tool maintains a custom order record per piece (customer, artwork dimensions, molding profile selected, mat specs, glazing type, promised pickup date, and payment status), a molding and mat board inventory tracker with reorder alerts by profile/color so a popular molding never runs out mid-season, and a searchable order history so a repeat customer's prior framing specs (a matching set of family photos, for instance) can be pulled up instantly.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the Professional Picture Framers Association (PPFA) member directory and local frame shop Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Knife & Tool Sharpening Service Route & Customer Edge-History Tracker
Mobile knife and tool sharpening operators running a regular circuit of farmers markets, restaurants, and residential routes need to track which specific knives or tools belong to which customer (a restaurant's chef knife set, a landscaper's mower blades and pruning shears), what edge angle and grind each item was last sharpened to, and a route schedule that groups stops by day-of-week circuit so a customer's next visit lands predictably. Currently this is tracked, if at all, in a paper ticket book that doesn't travel well between market stalls and doesn't flag which restaurant accounts are overdue for their next visit.

The tool maintains a customer item log (owner, item type, edge angle/grind history, and date of last sharpening), a route circuit calendar (farmers market days, restaurant accounts on a recurring schedule, residential one-off stops) with an overdue-for-service flag for recurring commercial accounts, and a per-stop job ticket generator listing exactly which items are expected so nothing gets left behind at the wrong stall or kitchen.

Monetization: $12-18/mo per account. Distribution: the American Bladesmith Society and Sharpening Business communities on Facebook, plus r/sharpening and local farmers market vendor networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. RV & Camper Mobile Repair Technician Service History & Parts Tracker
Mobile RV and camper repair technicians who travel to campgrounds and storage lots to service motorhomes, travel trailers, and fifth wheels need each unit's specific systems on hand before arrival — appliance make/model (fridge, water heater, furnace), slide-out mechanism type, roof material, and prior repair history — because showing up without the right part or gasket wastes a service call that may be an hour's drive from the next job. This is currently tracked, if at all, in a phone notes app or a paper folder per customer that doesn't hold up across a full day of campground-to-campground service calls.

The tool maintains a per-RV service record (owner, unit make/model/VIN, appliance and system specs, and a repair visit log with date, work performed, and parts used), a parts and consumables inventory carried in the service van with low-stock alerts by SKU, and a route-ordered appointment calendar grouping the day's campground and storage-lot stops by geographic cluster to minimize drive time between service calls.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the RV Technician Association (RVTAA) member directory, r/RVtechs and r/rvrepair communities, and local RV/camper repair Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
