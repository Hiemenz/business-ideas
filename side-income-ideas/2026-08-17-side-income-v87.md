### 1. Independent Contingency Recruiter Candidate Pipeline & Placement-Fee Guarantee Tracker

Independent contingency recruiters who place candidates with multiple hiring companies need to track a multi-client candidate pipeline showing exactly which candidate is being submitted to which company at which interview stage, since a recruiter running 15-25 active candidates across a dozen client companies at once can't rely on memory to know which submission is awaiting a first-round callback versus which one just received an offer, and submitting the same candidate to two competing clients without disclosure is a reputation-ending mistake in a small industry. The tool also needs a placement-fee and guarantee-period tracker, because a contingency fee is only fully earned once a placed candidate stays past the client's guarantee window — commonly 90 days — after which a replacement search or partial refund obligation kicks in, and a recruiter juggling a dozen recent placements loses real income tracking guarantee-window expirations by memory. It also needs a client-exclusivity and split-fee tracker for deals worked jointly with another recruiter or agency, since split-fee arrangements require a documented percentage agreement before the placement closes to avoid a payment dispute after the fact. Most independent recruiters currently manage this across a personal spreadsheet and a string of email threads, with no structured link between a specific candidate's submission stage and the guarantee clock on that client's placement.

The tool maintains a multi-client candidate pipeline, a placement-fee/guarantee-period tracker, and a client-exclusivity/split-fee tracker.

Monetization: $25-39/mo per recruiter account. Distribution: r/recruiting and r/humanresources threads, the NPAworldwide and Top Echelon independent-recruiter networks, and the American Staffing Association member directory. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. End-of-Life Doula Client Care-Plan, Vigil & Legacy-Project Tracker

Independent end-of-life (death) doulas supporting dying clients and their families need to track a per-client care-plan record capturing the client's stated wishes, which advance directives are on file, and their vigil-support preferences, since a doula supporting several active clients at once can't rely on memory to know whose plan calls for music and quiet presence versus whose family wants active spiritual ritual during the final vigil, and honoring the wrong preference during an active death vigil is not a mistake that can be corrected. The tool also needs a multi-family-member communication log, because several family members typically need coordinated updates during an active vigil and a doula managing overlapping clients needs a clear record of who's been contacted and what's been shared with whom. It also needs a legacy-project tracker for letters, recordings, or life-review work completed with the client before death, since these projects often unfold over several sessions and a doula running multiple concurrent clients needs to know exactly which project is finished, in progress, or still to be started before time runs out. Most independent death doulas currently manage this across handwritten notes and memory, with no structured link between a specific client's stated wishes and what the family has actually been told.

The tool maintains a per-client care-plan/vigil-preference record, a multi-family-member communication log, and a legacy-project tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per doula account. Distribution: r/hospice and end-of-life-care caregiver forums, the National End-of-Life Doula Alliance (NEDA) member directory, and regional death-doula training-program alumni groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Freelance Flight Instructor Student Logbook, Endorsement & Checkride-Readiness Tracker

Independent flight instructors (CFIs) who teach across several flight schools and rented aircraft rather than working for a single school need to track a per-student logbook-hours and endorsement tracker confirming exactly which FAA-required flight hours and instructor endorsements each student has logged toward their certificate, since a freelance CFI running 8-12 students at once across different training stages can't rely on memory to know which student still needs a solo cross-country endorsement versus which one is cleared for their checkride, and signing off a student for a stage they haven't actually met the hour requirements for is a certificate-jeopardizing mistake for the instructor. The tool also needs a multi-school aircraft-availability and lesson-scheduling tracker, because a freelance CFI books time in different schools' rented aircraft rather than a single fleet, and double-booking a lesson against an aircraft that's already reserved elsewhere wastes a training day for both instructor and student. It also needs a checkride-readiness tracker flagging which students are cleared for which practical-test stage based on their actual logged hours and completed endorsements. Most independent CFIs currently manage this across a paper logbook review and a mental sense of each student's progress, with no structured link between a specific student's actual logged hours and the endorsement they're about to receive.

The tool maintains a per-student logbook-hours/endorsement tracker, a multi-school aircraft-availability/scheduling tracker, and a checkride-readiness tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per instructor account. Distribution: r/flying and r/flightinstructors threads, AOPA's flight instructor resources and regional CFI forums, and local flight-school freelance-instructor Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent AV & Event Production Freelancer Call-Time, Equipment & Invoice Tracker

Independent stagehands, sound techs, and lighting operators who freelance across multiple production companies and venues need to track a multi-promoter gig-booking calendar, since a freelance crew member picking up call times from several different production companies, venues, and event promoters in a busy season can't rely on memory to know which production booked them for Friday's load-in versus which one still needs Saturday confirmed, and a double-booked call time burns a relationship with whichever promoter loses the crew member. The tool also needs an equipment-checklist tracker confirming personal gear — a mixer, cabling, a wireless pack, adapters — is packed and tested before each load-in, since showing up to a gig missing a critical cable or with an untested wireless unit is a show-stopping problem that reflects directly on the freelancer's reputation. It also needs a per-gig invoice tracker, because freelance crew typically bill a flat day rate or an hourly call-time rate per production rather than a retainer, and a crew member working a dozen gigs a month loses real income to a call time they forget to invoice. Most independent AV freelancers currently manage this across a group text with production coordinators and a gear bag packed from memory, with no structured link between a specific gig's call time and the equipment checklist for that show.

The tool maintains a multi-promoter gig-booking calendar, an equipment-checklist tracker, and a per-gig invoice tracker.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per freelancer account. Distribution: r/livesound and r/eventproduction threads, ProductionHUB's freelance crew directory, and local IATSE freelance call-board networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Freelance Recipe Developer & Food Content Creator Recipe-Testing & Brand-Deliverable Tracker

Independent recipe developers and food content creators who produce sponsored recipes, photos, and posts for brand partners need to track a recipe-testing log recording each version's ingredient ratios, method tweaks, and tester feedback across multiple trial batches, since a developer running 4-6 concurrent recipe development projects can't rely on memory to know which version of a recipe was the final approved one versus an earlier draft that still needs another round of testing. The tool also needs a sponsored-content deliverable pipeline tracking which brand partner is owed which recipe, photo set, and post by which publish date, because a content creator juggling several brand deals in a month loses a client relationship the moment a deliverable slips past its contracted date. It also needs a usage-rights tracker recording how long each brand licensed a given recipe or photo and for which platforms, since brand licensing terms typically run for a fixed window — commonly 6 or 12 months — and a creator who loses track of an expired license risks a brand continuing to use content past its paid term. Most independent recipe developers currently manage this across a recipe-testing notebook and a scattered folder of brand contracts, with no structured link between a specific recipe's final tested version and the brand deliverable it was developed for.

The tool maintains a recipe-testing log, a sponsored-content deliverable pipeline, and a usage-rights tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per creator account. Distribution: r/foodblogging and r/FoodPhotography threads, the International Association of Culinary Professionals (IACP) member directory, and food-blogger brand-partnership Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Family Child Care (Home Daycare) Provider Attendance, Meal-Count & Subsidy Billing Tracker

Independent family child care providers running a small in-home daycare need to track a daily attendance and meal-count log for every enrolled child, since a provider caring for 6-8 children at once needs an accurate, contemporaneous record of who was present and which meals were served to support a USDA Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) reimbursement claim, and a claim built from memory at month-end instead of a same-day log is exactly the kind of unsupported record a CACFP sponsoring organization's review flags. The tool also needs a per-child enrollment and tuition/subsidy-billing tracker, because some families pay privately on their own schedule while others are covered through a state child-care-subsidy program with its own separate payment calendar and authorized-hours limit, and a provider running a mixed private-pay and subsidy roster needs a clear view of which child's payment is coming from which source and whether the subsidy authorization is still current. It also needs an immunization and emergency-contact record per enrolled child, since a provider caring for a full roster can't rely on memory to know whose records are up to date or who to call first in an emergency. Most family child care providers currently manage this across a paper sign-in sheet and a manila folder per family, with no structured link between a specific day's meal count and the reimbursement claim it's meant to support.

The tool maintains a daily attendance/meal-count log, a per-child enrollment and tuition/subsidy-billing tracker, and an immunization/emergency-contact record.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per provider account. Distribution: r/daycare and family-child-care provider Facebook groups, the National Association for Family Child Care (NAFCC) member directory, and local CACFP sponsoring-organization provider networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
