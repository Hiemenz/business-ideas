# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-02 (Round 16)

### 1. Municipal Permit Status Tracker for Contractors
Contractors pulling building, electrical, or plumbing permits across multiple active job sites need to know when each permit is approved, when inspections are scheduled, and when the permit is finaled — information buried across different city portal logins that they check manually. This app lets contractors add permit numbers and jurisdictions, then scrapes or polls the relevant municipal permit portals on a schedule and sends a push notification or email when a permit status changes. Monetization: $18-35/mo per contractor. Distribution: NARI (National Association of the Remodeling Industry) member forums, general contractor Facebook groups, construction permit software review communities. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 2. Aerial Photography Job Board & Client Matching for FAA Part 107 Drone Pilots
Licensed FAA Part 107 commercial drone pilots frequently struggle to find consistent paying clients — real estate photographers, land surveyors, insurance adjusters, and cell tower inspection firms all hire drone pilots but post opportunities across scattered job boards or through personal referrals. This niche job board aggregates drone pilot gig postings, lets pilots build a searchable profile with their Part 107 certificate number, equipment list, and specialty (real estate, infrastructure, agricultural mapping), and sends email alerts when new jobs matching their profile are posted within their radius. Monetization: $12-20/mo pilot subscription or $50-150 per job posting from the client side. Distribution: r/drones, Part 107 pilot Facebook groups, DJI drone operator communities, drone photography Discord servers. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 3. AI Meeting Transcript Action Item Extractor for Notion
Teams using Notion as their project hub after meetings with Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams still manually copy action items from AI-generated meeting transcripts into their Notion task databases — a tedious copy-paste workflow that gets skipped when people are busy. This tool connects to a transcript source (uploaded file or Otter.ai / Fireflies.ai export), extracts action items, owners, and due dates using a structured LLM prompt, and creates Notion database entries with the correct owner, due date, and meeting reference properties in one click. Monetization: $10-18/mo per user or team seat. Distribution: Notion template marketplace, r/Notion, Notion community forums, productivity YouTube creator affiliate partnerships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Short-Term Rental Host Expense & Depreciation Tracker for Schedule E
Airbnb and VRBO hosts who own their rental property and file Schedule E must track rental income, operating expenses (cleaning, supplies, platform fees, repairs), and depreciation — and correctly allocate personal-use vs. rental-use percentages for mixed-use properties. This app imports Airbnb and VRBO payout reports, categorizes expenses by deductible type (maintenance, utilities, professional services, depreciation), calculates the rental-use percentage based on booked nights vs. total available nights, and exports a Schedule E summary formatted for the host's CPA or tax software. Monetization: $15-25/mo or $49/yr. Distribution: Airbnb host Facebook groups, BiggerPockets short-term rental forums, STR investor YouTube communities, CPA referral partnerships. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Equipment Dealer Pre-Owned Inventory Aging Dashboard
Small equipment dealers (skid steers, forklifts, trailers, landscaping equipment) carry used inventory that ages on their lot, losing value the longer it sits — but they track this in a spreadsheet or dealer management system that doesn't surface which units have been sitting 60, 90, or 120+ days and what price reductions are warranted. This dashboard imports or manually accepts inventory entries with purchase date, acquisition cost, list price, and days-on-lot, then surfaces aging alerts, calculates the implied ROI at each price tier, and generates a markdown-ready report the dealer can post to Facebook Marketplace or Equipment Trader for aged units. Monetization: $30-55/mo per dealer location. Distribution: equipment dealer Facebook groups, AED (Associated Equipment Distributors) member communications, Equipment Trader dealer community forums. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 6. Motorsport Lap Time & Setup Change Log for Amateur Track Day Drivers
Amateur track day and HPDE (High Performance Driver Education) participants drive their cars on circuit and want to know whether a tire pressure change, suspension adjustment, or brake bias shift improved their lap times — but they track this in notes on their phone with no structured connection between setup changes and lap time results. This app logs each session's setup parameters (tire pressures, damper settings, alignment changes), records lap times manually or via import from a GPS logger (Harry's LapTimer, AiM, Racelogic), and presents a session comparison chart showing how setup changes correlated with lap time deltas over multiple track days at each circuit. Monetization: $8-15/mo or $49/yr. Distribution: r/HPDE, r/trackdays, Porsche/BMW/Corvette track day owner forums, SCCA Time Trial community groups, track day organization event email lists. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
