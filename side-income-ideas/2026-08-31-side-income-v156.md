# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-31 (Round 156)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — not compliance tools, just practical day-to-day workflow trackers for six operator types not yet covered in this repo: an independent ATM route operator, an EV charger installation contractor, a wheelchair ramp and home-accessibility installer, a geothermal heat pump loop installer, a skylight and roof-window installer, and an independent amateur boxing and combat-sports coach.

---

### 1. Independent ATM Route Operator Cash-Float, Surcharge & Host-Location Tracker

An independent ATM route operator who owns and services a fleet of machines placed in convenience stores, bars, laundromats, and small retail locations has to track exactly how much cash is loaded in each machine against how much has been dispensed, since a machine that runs out of cash on a busy Friday night is lost surcharge revenue and an angry host-location owner fielding customer complaints the operator never even sees until the next scheduled refill. An operator running 15-40 machines across a metro area also has to reconcile each machine's surcharge revenue against the processor's settlement report and the specific revenue-split percentage negotiated with that host location, since host agreements vary — some locations get a flat monthly rent, others a per-transaction kickback — and an operator who can't produce an accurate split at month's end loses the host relationship to a competing ATM company on the spot. A vault-cash shortage discovered only when a machine goes dark, or a host location that quietly starts disputing its surcharge split because the operator has no clean per-machine ledger, are the two failures that end this business fastest.

The tool maintains a per-machine cash-load and dispensed-balance tracker with a low-cash refill alert calculated from that location's historical withdrawal pace; a surcharge and revenue-split reconciliation log matching processor settlement reports against each host location's specific agreement; and a host-location contract tracker recording lease terms, renewal dates, and service-call history per machine.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per operator account. Distribution: the National ATM Council (NAC) member directory, r/ATM and small-business-owner threads where independent ATM route operators trade host-negotiation and cash-logistics tips, and regional ATM route brokerage Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. EV Charger Installation Contractor Job-Spec, Panel-Load & Rebate-Paperwork Tracker

An independent electrician or contractor installing Level 2 home chargers and small commercial charging stations has to calculate each site's existing electrical panel capacity against the new charger's amperage draw before ever pulling a permit, since discovering mid-install that a homeowner's 100-amp panel can't support a 50-amp charger circuit without a costly panel upgrade is a quote that blows up in front of the client, and a contractor running 6-10 concurrent installs across residential driveways and small commercial lots can't rely on memory for which site's load calculation already cleared versus which one is still waiting on a panel-upgrade decision. A contractor also has to track the specific charger model, network/app activation status, and firmware version installed per site, since a homeowner calling three months later about a charger that won't connect to its app needs the installer to pull up exactly which unit and account it was registered under rather than starting from scratch, and separately has to manage utility rebate and tax-credit paperwork — many utilities and states require the installer to submit specific documentation before a homeowner's rebate is approved, and a contractor who lets that paperwork lapse past a filing window costs the client money and their own reputation.

The tool maintains a per-job electrical load-calculation worksheet (panel capacity, existing circuit load, and required charger amperage) gating whether a panel upgrade is needed before scheduling; a per-charger install record (model, network activation status, firmware version, and warranty start date); and a utility-rebate and tax-credit paperwork tracker with submission deadlines per program.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per contractor. Distribution: the Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Training Program (EVITP) certified-installer network and r/electricians and r/EVcharging threads where independent contractors already trade panel-load-calculation and rebate-paperwork workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Wheelchair Ramp & Home-Accessibility Installer Site-Survey & Rental-Fleet Tracker

An independent installer who builds and installs modular aluminum wheelchair ramps, grab bars, and other home-accessibility modifications for aging-in-place clients has to calculate each site's exact ramp length from the porch or entry rise using the standard 1:12 slope ratio, since a ramp built too steep fails the client outright and a ramp overbuilt for a rise that didn't need it wastes material the installer eats the cost of, and an installer running several jobs a week across scattered residential addresses can't rely on memory for which site's rise measurement already determined the exact ramp-section count needed. A large share of this business runs on rented rather than sold ramp systems — a client recovering from surgery needs a ramp for eight weeks, not permanently — so an installer also has to track which modular ramp sections are currently out on a rental job versus sitting in inventory ready for the next site, since a ramp section still counted as "in stock" when it's actually installed at a client's home three towns over means quoting a new job with equipment that doesn't exist. A rental client also needs a scheduled pickup and removal date, since a ramp left installed weeks past its rental term with no billing or removal follow-up is money the installer never collects.

The tool maintains a per-site survey record (rise measurement, calculated ramp length and section count, and site-access notes) driving an accurate quote and material pull; a modular-ramp-component inventory tracker distinguishing sections currently installed at a rental job from those available for the next one; and a rental-term tracker with scheduled pickup/removal dates and renewal billing for ongoing rentals.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per installer. Distribution: the National Ramp Alliance dealer network, r/HomeImprovement aging-in-place threads, and Area Agency on Aging and durable-medical-equipment referral partnerships where independent ramp installers already source client referrals. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Geothermal Heat Pump Loop Installer Loop-Field Design & Commissioning Tracker

An independent geothermal (ground-source) heat pump installer has to design each job's loop field — the number and depth of vertical boreholes, or the trench footage for a horizontal loop, sized to the specific home's heating and cooling load and the property's available land — before a drilling subcontractor or trenching crew is ever scheduled, since an undersized loop field means a system that can't hit its rated efficiency and a callback the installer has to explain to a client who paid a premium expecting lower utility bills. An installer running several concurrent jobs across different drillers and property types also has to track each loop's pressure-test and purge/flush results before the system is buried and backfilled, since a leak or an air pocket in a loop that's already covered with dirt is a multi-thousand-dollar excavation to fix rather than a five-minute recheck, and a job where the pressure-test log wasn't properly documented before backfill is a liability dispute waiting to happen if the system underperforms later. Most independent geothermal installers currently manage loop-field sizing calculations, drilling-subcontractor scheduling, and pressure-test sign-offs across a paper design worksheet and a driller's separate invoice, with no structured link between a specific loop's design spec and its confirmed pre-backfill test results.

The tool maintains a per-job loop-field design worksheet (borehole count/depth or trench footage, calculated from the property's heating/cooling load) generating the drilling or trenching subcontractor's work order; a pressure-test and purge/flush log per loop requiring a passed result before the backfill-approval flag is set; and a rebate/tax-credit documentation tracker for the federal and utility geothermal incentive programs clients rely on to offset the system's cost.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per installer. Distribution: the International Ground Source Heat Pump Association (IGSHPA) certified-installer directory and r/geothermal and r/HVAC threads where independent geothermal contractors already trade loop-sizing and commissioning workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Skylight & Roof-Window Installer Measurement, Flashing & Leak-Warranty Tracker

An independent skylight installer has to measure each roof opening's exact rafter spacing, roof pitch, and roofing-material type before ordering a unit, since a skylight is fabricated or selected to fit a specific rough-opening size and roof pitch, and an installer who orders the wrong flashing kit for a client's roofing material — asphalt shingle, tile, and metal roofs each need a different flashing-kit profile — ends up back at the supplier while a hole sits open in the client's roof. An installer running several jobs a week also has to track each installed unit's flashing-kit type and installation date against the manufacturer's leak warranty, since a callback about a leak six months later needs the installer to know instantly whether it's a workmanship issue on their own installation or a manufacturer defect covered under warranty, and a company with no per-unit flashing record ends up re-diagnosing the roof from scratch on every callback. Most independent skylight installers currently manage roof measurements, flashing-kit specs, and warranty callback history on a paper measure sheet and a supplier invoice folder, and a mismatched flashing kit discovered mid-install, or a warranty callback with no record of what flashing system was originally installed, turns a routine job into a costly, trust-eroding return trip.

The tool maintains a per-job roof-opening measurement sheet (rafter spacing, pitch, and roofing-material type) matched to the correct skylight unit and flashing-kit spec before ordering; a per-installation warranty tracker recording flashing-kit type, install date, and manufacturer warranty term; and a leak-callback log linking any post-install issue back to the original installation record so a callback starts with data instead of a re-inspection from scratch.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per installer. Distribution: the Velux and Fakro certified-installer partner networks, r/roofing and r/HomeImprovement skylight-installation threads, and regional roofing-contractor referral partnerships where independent skylight installers already source subcontracted work. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Independent Amateur Boxing & Combat-Sports Coach Fight-Prep, Sparring-Log & Weigh-In Tracker

An independent boxing or combat-sports coach training amateur fighters for sanctioned bouts has to track each fighter's training-camp plan against a fixed fight date — the weeks-out periodization of conditioning, technical work, and taper — because a coach running 8-15 fighters across staggered fight schedules can't rely on memory for which fighter is three weeks from a bout versus which one just started camp after a layoff, and a fighter who peaks at the wrong point in camp because the coach lost track of the timeline is a performance failure that reflects directly on the coach's own reputation with the gym and the sanctioning body. A coach also has to log every sparring session's details — sparring partner, rounds, and any notable head contact or injury flagged — since sparring safety records matter both for the fighter's own health tracking and because a sanctioning body or commission may ask about a fighter's recent sparring activity before clearing a bout, and needs a weigh-in and weight-cut tracker monitoring a fighter's weight trend against their contracted fight-night weight class, because a fighter who misses weight the morning of a weigh-in can lose the bout entirely before ever stepping in the ring. Most independent coaches currently manage camp timelines, sparring logs, and weight-cut tracking across a gym whiteboard and a personal notebook, with no structured link between a fighter's fight date and the training milestones that have to land before it.

The tool maintains a per-fighter training-camp timeline counting down to a fixed fight date with periodized milestone checkpoints; a sparring-session log recording partner, rounds, and any flagged head contact or injury per fighter; and a weigh-in and weight-cut tracker charting weight trend against the fighter's contracted weight class as fight day approaches.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per coach. Distribution: the USA Boxing certified-coach directory and regional gym networks, plus r/amateur_boxing and r/MMA coaching threads where independent trainers already trade fight-camp and weight-cut workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
