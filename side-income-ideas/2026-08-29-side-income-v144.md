# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-29 (Round 144)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — scheduling, client records, and workflow trackers, not compliance software. Each idea below covers a self-employed operator type not already addressed in this repo's prior 143 rounds.

---

### 1. Independent Real Estate Transaction Coordinator Contract-to-Close Deadline & Document Tracker

Transaction coordinators (TCs) manage contract-to-close paperwork for multiple real estate agents and teams at once — tracking inspection-objection deadlines, appraisal-contingency dates, disclosure signatures, earnest-money receipt confirmation, and closing dates across a dozen or more concurrent files, each running its own state-standard contingency-deadline calculus from the contract date. Missing a single deadline, like an inspection-objection window, can cost a client their earnest money or the whole deal, yet most TCs currently run this off spreadsheets, sticky notes, and whatever document-only transaction management tool the brokerage provides, which doesn't calculate or surface the actual deadline math.

The tool maintains a per-agent client roster, per-transaction contingency deadlines auto-calculated from the contract date plus state-standard periods, a document checklist per file (inspection report received, appraisal ordered, title commitment received, disclosures signed), a commission/fee tracker per transaction, and a single master calendar view spanning every active file a TC is juggling — typically 15-30 at once.

Monetization: $40-80/mo per TC seat, or $15-25 per active file for TCs who bill clients per-transaction rather than by retainer. Distribution: state-level "Transaction Coordinator" Facebook and networking groups, and REALTOR-association TC-certification course graduate rosters. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Mobile Phlebotomist Multi-Client Route & Specimen Chain-of-Custody Tracker

Mobile phlebotomists doing at-home and workplace blood draws for lab networks (Labcorp/Quest patient service center overflow), life-insurance paramedical exam companies, and direct clients typically juggle several concurrent paying contracts at different per-draw rates, a route schedule spanning a wide territory, and specimen chain-of-custody requirements — draw time, courier pickup time, delivery confirmation, transport-temperature window — that labs audit against. Missing a transport-time window can void a specimen entirely, costing the phlebotomist both the draw fee and an unpaid redraw trip.

The tool maintains a per-client contract rate sheet, a territory-optimized appointment/route schedule, a specimen chain-of-custody log per patient draw (draw time, courier handoff, delivery confirmation), and a monthly earnings reconciliation rolling up pay across the multiple paying contractors that mobile phlebotomy work almost always involves.

Monetization: $20-35/mo flat per phlebotomist. Distribution: American Society of Phlebotomy Technicians (ASPT) member directory, mobile-phlebotomy Facebook and Reddit communities, and independent-contractor networks run by paramedical exam companies (APPS, ExamOne). Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent Patent & Trademark Docketing Specialist Multi-Client Deadline Tracker

Solo and small IP law firms, along with independent inventors, increasingly hire freelance patent docketing specialists to track USPTO and international/PCT filing deadlines — office-action response windows, extension-of-time fee schedules, maintenance-fee due dates at the 3.5/7.5/11.5-year marks, and priority-claim deadlines — where a single missed date can permanently abandon a client's application. Freelance docketers today juggle each client firm's own docketing system (or nothing at all, for solo inventors) with no unified cross-client view of their own workload.

The tool maintains a per-client, per-matter docket with automatic USPTO deadline calculations, a correspondence/document log per matter, and a single cross-client master deadline calendar so the freelancer never double-books a filing-deadline-heavy week across multiple firms.

Monetization: $30-60/mo per active client firm — freelance docketers typically serve 3-8 small firms concurrently. Distribution: American Intellectual Property Law Association (AIPLA) and Patent Information Users Group (PIUG) job boards, and freelance-docketer LinkedIn groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 4. Independent Diagnostic Medical Sonographer Multi-Facility Shift & Credential Tracker

Per-diem and mobile sonographers pick up shifts across multiple imaging centers, hospitals, and mobile-imaging companies covering echo, OB, vascular, and abdominal studies, each with a different scheduling portal, pay rate, and facility-specific credentialing/orientation requirement. Tracking which facility requires which annual competency check, which shifts are confirmed versus tentative, and reconciling pay across four to six different paying facilities each month is currently handled ad hoc with no central view.

The tool maintains per-facility credential and orientation status with expiration reminders, a shift schedule spanning every facility in one calendar, per-facility pay rates with monthly earnings reconciliation, and an exam-volume log by modality useful for ARDMS continuing-education and specialty-credential documentation.

Monetization: $25-40/mo. Distribution: Society of Diagnostic Medical Sonography (SDMS) per-diem and travel-sonographer forums and Facebook groups, and mobile-ultrasound staffing agency contractor networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Permanent Makeup Artist Color-Map & Touch-Up Cycle Tracker

Permanent makeup and microblading artists manage a per-client color-formulation record — pigment brand, lot number, dilution ratio used — across a multi-visit touch-up cycle (initial session, 6-8 week touch-up, annual color refresh), along with numbing-agent and pigment-lot inventory and expiration tracking. Most currently keep this on paper client cards or in a generic booking app that captures the appointment but not the color-formula or lot data needed for a consistent touch-up months later or for liability documentation if a client has a reaction.

The tool maintains a per-client color-map and pigment-lot record, touch-up-cycle reminder scheduling across the initial-to-touch-up-to-annual-refresh sequence, a before/after photo log tied to each session, and pigment/numbing-agent lot inventory with expiration tracking.

Monetization: $20-35/mo. Distribution: permanent-makeup trainer certification alumni networks (PhiAcademy, LiveTint graduate groups) and permanent-makeup-artist professional Facebook and Instagram groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Freelance CPR/First Aid/BLS Instructor Certification & Client Renewal Tracker

Independent AHA- or Red Cross-authorized CPR, First Aid, and BLS instructors teach for multiple clients at once — daycare centers, corporate HR departments, healthcare facilities needing staff recertification, and open-enrollment public classes — and must track each student's certification-card issuance and 2-year expiration date, class roster and attendance, and their own instructor-authorization renewal (training-center affiliation, instructor-card expiration). Today this is split across the training center's own portal, which doesn't track the instructor's independent client roster, and personal spreadsheets.

The tool maintains a class roster and attendance log per session, a student certification-card issuance log with 2-year expiration reminders (a built-in recurring-revenue driver, since instructors can proactively re-book students before their card lapses), a client-organization contract/recurring-training tracker (for example, "Daycare X needs 8 staff recertified every October"), and the instructor's own authorization-renewal deadline.

Monetization: $20-30/mo. Distribution: American Heart Association Training Center instructor networks and independent CPR-instructor Facebook groups such as "CPR Instructors Network." Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
