# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-14 (Round 185)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a mobile knife sharpener, a home sourdough baker, a stained-glass artist, a closet organizer, a kids' party entertainer and an audiobook narrator.

---

### 1. Mobile Knife Sharpener Route, Customer & Reminder Tracker

A person who sharpens knives at farmers markets and customers' homes or restaurants has to track each customer's items, drop-off or visit dates and return date. Knives dull again in 6-12 months, so a reminder is the main source of repeat business. Restaurant accounts need a regular route.

The tool logs items and prices per customer, schedules route stops, sends a "time for a touch-up" reminder and shows revenue per stop.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per sharpener. Distribution: knife-sharpening communities, r/knives and farmers-market vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Home Sourdough Baker Order, Bake-Day & Pickup Tracker

A home baker who sells loaves through weekly pre-orders has to track each customer's order, flavor, quantity, pickup slot and payment. Dough runs on a schedule, and over-baking or no-shows waste money. A waitlist helps on busy weeks.

The tool opens and closes weekly orders, totals the bake list and ingredient needs, assigns pickup slots and tracks margin per loaf.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per baker. Distribution: home-baker Facebook groups, r/Sourdough and neighborhood boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Stained-Glass Artist Commission, Piece & Materials Tracker

An artist who makes stained-glass panels and suncatchers has to track each commission's design, glass and materials, deposits, progress photos and delivery. Pieces take weeks, and custom sizes change the price. Craft-fair stock also needs counting.

The tool tracks commissions with design approvals, logs materials cost per piece, sends progress photos and shows profit per piece.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per artist. Distribution: stained-glass communities, r/StainedGlass and craft-fair vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Closet Organizer Client, Space & Follow-Up Tracker

A freelancer who organizes closets and garages has to track each client's spaces, before photos, hours, supplies bought and follow-up visit. Clients often need a maintenance visit months later, and quotes depend on the size of the space.

The tool logs spaces with photos and hours, tracks supplies and mileage, generates quotes from past jobs and sends a follow-up reminder.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per organizer. Distribution: professional-organizer groups, r/organization and local home-service boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Kids' Party Entertainer Booking, Package & Deposit Tracker

A magician, balloon artist or face painter has to track each party's date, child's age, package, deposit and travel time. Weekends fill fast, and a double-booking is costly. Parents often rebook for the next birthday, so a yearly reminder helps.

The tool books by date and time with travel buffers, collects deposits, stores each child's age and favorites and sends a birthday-rebooking reminder.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per entertainer. Distribution: party-entertainer Facebook groups, local parent groups and r/entertainment business threads. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Audiobook Narrator Title, Session & Delivery Tracker

A freelance narrator has to track each title's word count, per-finished-hour rate, recording sessions, edits and delivery deadline. Hours recorded versus hours finished decide the real hourly pay, and rights details need a note.

The tool tracks titles with finished-hour estimates, logs session time and re-records, shows effective hourly pay and keeps a delivery calendar.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per narrator. Distribution: voice-acting and audiobook communities, r/VoiceActing and narrator Discords. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
