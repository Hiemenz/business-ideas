# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 27)

### 1. Roofing Contractor Insurance Supplement Checklist Generator
Roofing contractors and public adjusters working storm damage insurance claims frequently leave money on the table because the insurance adjuster's initial estimate omits standard line items that are legitimately reimbursable: drip edge replacement, ice and water shield in code-required zones, starter strip shingles, ridge cap, step and counter flashing, pipe boot replacement, permit fees, dump fees, and the O&P (overhead and profit) markup. Writing a complete supplement request requires knowing which line items are routinely missed and phrasing them in Xactimate language the insurance company's desk adjuster will process without pushback. This tool accepts inputs (roof type, square footage, shingle type, damage type — hail vs. wind, state/locality) and generates a formatted supplement checklist: each commonly missed line item with the Xactimate line code, a brief justification note, and a local code citation where applicable. Roofers take it to the insurance company knowing they haven't left anything behind. Monetization: $18-30/mo per contractor, unlimited claims. Distribution: roofing contractor Facebook groups, r/Roofing, NRCA (National Roofing Contractors Association) member communities, Xactimate user communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. CrossFit & Functional Fitness Gym Benchmark WOD Results Tracker
Affiliate gyms that use benchmark workouts (Fran, Helen, Murph, Fight Gone Bad, Grace, and coach-defined in-house benchmarks) to track athlete progress over time currently manage results through whiteboard photos, a Google Sheet, or SugarWOD — but rarely produce the kind of historical comparison report that shows an athlete their progress across multiple benchmark attempts over years. This tool maintains a per-athlete benchmark history: workout name, date, result (time, rounds, or reps), scaled or RX, and any notes. A coach-facing dashboard shows the last 3 results for every athlete on any benchmark, identifies athletes who haven't attempted a given benchmark in 90+ days, and generates a "PR board" snapshot for posting in the gym. Athlete-facing view: a progress chart for their benchmarks over time. Monetization: $20-35/mo per gym. Distribution: Two-Brain Business affiliate coaching community, CrossFit affiliate owner Facebook groups, r/crossfit, WOD Management Software community forums. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Private Chef Weekly Meal Plan & Shopping List Generator
Private chefs and personal chefs who cook for client households on a weekly basis spend 30-60 minutes per client per week planning a personalized menu and building a consolidated shopping list across multiple recipes. This tool maintains a client profile (dietary restrictions, preferences, household size, cuisine preferences, foods to avoid) and a recipe library the chef builds over time, and generates a weekly meal plan for each client (5-7 dinners, optionally lunches) from the recipe library with one-click adjustments for servings. From the approved meal plan, it generates a consolidated shopping list organized by grocery store section — produce, proteins, pantry, dairy — with quantities consolidated across all recipes for the week. Monetization: $15-25/mo per chef account (unlimited clients). Distribution: personal chef association communities (USPCA — United States Personal Chef Association), r/Chef, private chef Facebook groups, culinary freelancer communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. HOA Annual Meeting Notice & Proxy Ballot Generator
HOA and condo association boards must send a formal annual meeting notice — typically 10-30 days before the meeting per the association's bylaws and state HOA law — that includes the meeting date/time/location, a proposed agenda, candidate information for any board election, and a proxy ballot allowing owners who can't attend to vote. Most small HOAs (20-100 units, self-managed by volunteer board members) produce this notice in Word from scratch each year, sometimes violating their own bylaws' notice format requirements without realizing it. This tool accepts inputs (association name, meeting date and time, location, board positions open for election, candidate names, and any items requiring a vote) and generates a compliant meeting notice packet: cover notice letter with proper advance date confirmation, the meeting agenda, candidate bio section, and a proxy ballot with write-in line — formatted for print or PDF email distribution. Optionally generates the follow-up meeting minutes template pre-populated with the agenda items. Monetization: $10-15/meeting or $25-35/year flat per association. Distribution: HOA board member Facebook groups, r/HOA, community association management software communities (AppFolio, CINC, Buildium HOA users). Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Dog Trainer Client Session Note & Homework Protocol Generator
Private dog trainers and behavior consultants who work with clients one-on-one need to send written session notes and a homework protocol after each session — what was covered, the exercises to practice at home, the criteria for each exercise, and what to do if the dog struggles. Writing these from scratch after a full day of sessions takes 20-40 minutes per client. This tool lets the trainer quickly select the exercises covered from their standard training exercise library (which they build once), add session-specific observations, and generate a formatted client session summary PDF: a brief session overview, an exercise-by-exercise homework guide with criteria and troubleshooting tips, and next session goals. Clients get professional, consistent documentation; trainers send it in 5 minutes instead of 40. Monetization: $12-20/mo per trainer account (unlimited clients). Distribution: r/dogtraining, Association of Professional Dog Trainers (APDT) community, Karen Pryor Academy alumni groups, dog trainer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. Etsy Seller Listing Health Audit Tool
Active Etsy sellers with 50-500 listings rarely audit their listings systematically for search visibility gaps: unused tag slots (Etsy allows 13 tags; most sellers use 8-10), titles that don't front-load the primary keyword, missing section assignments, listings with only 1-3 photos (Etsy allows 10), and listings in the wrong category. Fixing these gaps improves search ranking without any ad spend. This tool connects to the seller's Etsy shop via Etsy's API (or accepts a CSV export of listing data), analyzes each listing across 6 dimensions: tag count (13 of 13 used?), title keyword placement, photo count (10 of 10 used?), section assignment, category accuracy, and listing description length — and generates a prioritized audit report: a per-listing score, the specific gaps found, and a fix priority ranking so the seller knows where to start. Monetization: $12-20/mo or a one-time $15 audit report. Distribution: r/Etsy, Etsy seller Facebook groups, Crochet Business and Handmade Seller communities, Marmalead and eRank user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
