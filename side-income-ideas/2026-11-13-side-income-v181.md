# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-13 (Round 181)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a car-parts dismantler selling online, a knitting pattern designer, a watch repairer, a ski and snowboard tuner, a hand-lettering sign painter and a golf-club repairer.

---

### 1. Car-Parts Dismantler Vehicle, Part & Sale Tracker

A person who buys wrecked cars and sells the parts online has to track each vehicle's purchase cost, the parts pulled, listing status and sale price. With several cars in the yard, it is hard to know which parts are listed, which sold and which vehicle has already paid for itself. Part location in storage also matters.

The tool tracks vehicles with purchase cost, a parts list with storage location, listing and sale status and shows profit per vehicle.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per dismantler. Distribution: parts-reseller Facebook groups, r/MechanicAdvice and eBay Motors seller communities. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Knitting Pattern Designer Pattern, Sales & Test-Knitter Tracker

A designer who sells knitting patterns on Etsy and Ravelry has to track each pattern's status (draft, test knit, tech edit, published), test knitters assigned, sales per platform and customer questions. A designer with 20+ patterns cannot see which sell, and test-knit deadlines slip.

The tool tracks patterns by stage, assigns test knitters with deadlines, shows sales by platform and pattern and logs customer questions for pattern updates.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per designer. Distribution: knitting-designer communities, r/knitting and Ravelry designer groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Watch Repairer Job, Parts & Customer-Communication Tracker

An independent watch repairer has to track each watch's brand, issue, quote, parts ordered, work status and return date. Customers keep expensive timepieces with the repairer, so a clear record of what was received and its condition protects both sides. Quotes and approvals for extra work need a trail.

The tool logs intake with photos and condition notes, tracks the repair stage and parts status, records quote approvals and sends pickup updates.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per repairer. Distribution: watchmaker communities, r/Watchmaking and local jeweler networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Ski and Snowboard Tuner Order, Tag & Pickup Tracker

A seasonal tuner who waxes and sharpens skis and snowboards from a shop or garage has to track each order's tag, service level, drop-off and pickup times and payment. A busy weekend before a trip brings a rush, and a mixed-up pair of skis is a bad moment. Off-season storage services need tracking too.

The tool issues tag numbers, tracks the queue by promised pickup time, records service level and price and sends ready-for-pickup texts.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per tuner. Distribution: ski-shop and tuner Facebook groups, r/skiing and local mountain-community boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to the season.

### 5. Hand-Lettering Sign Painter Commission, Mockup & Install Tracker

A sign painter who letters storefronts, trucks and wall murals has to track each commission's mockup rounds, approved design, paint and materials, install date and weather windows. Painting outdoors depends on weather, and clients change colors late. Quotes depend on size and complexity.

The tool tracks commissions with mockups and approvals, logs materials cost, schedules installs with weather holds and tracks deposits and balances.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per painter. Distribution: sign-painting communities, r/signpainting and small-business Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Golf-Club Repairer Order, Spec & Turnaround Tracker

A person who regrips, re-shafts and adjusts golf clubs has to track each customer's club specs (length, lie angle, grip size), the work ordered, parts used and turnaround. Spec records let a customer reorder the same fit, and tournament dates create hard deadlines. Parts costs vary per job.

The tool stores a spec sheet per customer, tracks orders with parts and cost, shows turnaround countdown and suggests a regrip reminder after 12 months.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per repairer. Distribution: golf-club-builder communities, r/golf equipment threads and local golf-shop networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
