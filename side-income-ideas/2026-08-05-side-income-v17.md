# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-05 (Round 17)

### 1. Field Service Technician Dispatch Route Optimizer
Small HVAC, plumbing, and electrical service companies manually assign technicians to daily job orders — dispatchers eyeball a list and assign without calculating drive time or geographic clustering. This lightweight tool imports the day's job addresses from a spreadsheet or simple CRM export, runs them through a routing API (Google Maps Distance Matrix or OSRM), and outputs an optimized stop sequence for each technician that minimizes total drive time — delivered as a shareable link or printable PDF the dispatcher can hand the tech at the start of the day. Monetization: $25-40/mo per dispatcher seat. Distribution: ACCA (Air Conditioning Contractors of America) member communities, plumbing and electrical contractor Facebook groups, service company owner forums (Contractor Talk). Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 2. Restaurant Menu Engineering Profitability Analyzer
Restaurants need to know which menu items are high-margin and high-volume (stars), high-margin but slow-moving (puzzles), and low-margin drags (dogs) — the classic menu engineering matrix — but most operators have no systematic way to run the analysis against their actual POS data. This tool accepts a CSV export from any POS system (Toast, Square, Clover) with item name, count sold, and cost of goods, then outputs the menu engineering matrix with margin-per-item calculations, a recommended action for each item (promote, reprice, reposition, or remove), and a one-page summary the owner can take to their chef. Monetization: $20-35/mo or $49 one-time report. Distribution: restaurant owner Facebook groups, r/restaurantowners, Toast and Square app marketplace communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Move-In / Move-Out Rental Inspection Report Generator
Landlords conducting move-in and move-out property inspections need timestamped, room-by-room photo documentation to enforce security deposit deductions — but they capture photos on their phone with no structure, leaving them unable to produce organized evidence in small claims disputes. This mobile-friendly web app guides the landlord through a room-by-room inspection checklist, captures photos attached to each line item, timestamps every entry, and generates a formatted PDF report with photos organized by room that can be sent to the tenant and filed for potential small claims court use. Monetization: $12-18/mo per landlord account. Distribution: BiggerPockets landlord community, r/landlord, Avail and TurboTenant user communities. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 4. Podcast Host Guest Research Brief Generator
Podcast hosts spend 2-3 hours per episode researching each guest — reading articles, scanning LinkedIn, finding prior podcast appearances, identifying quotable positions to probe. This tool accepts a guest name and LinkedIn URL, aggregates publicly available content (LinkedIn summary, published articles, prior podcast appearances via Listen Notes API, and public social bio), and generates a structured interview brief: guest background summary, 8-10 suggested questions tied to their recent work, notable positions to challenge or explore, and a pre-interview email template the host can send before recording. Monetization: $15-25/mo subscription or $12 per brief. Distribution: r/podcasting, podcast host Facebook groups, Podchaser and Listen Notes community pages, podcast production Discord servers. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Contractor State License Renewal Reminder Service
General contractors, electricians, plumbers, and HVAC technicians hold state contractor licenses with renewal deadlines, CE requirements, and insurance bond verification steps that vary by state and license class. Missing a renewal means operating without a license — a serious liability and potential criminal violation in most states. This service maintains a simple profile for each contractor (name, state, license number, expiration date, CE hours required if any), sends email reminders at 90, 60, and 30 days before expiration with the direct link to the state licensing board's renewal portal, and tracks confirmation once the renewal is submitted. Monetization: $6-10/mo per license tracked. Distribution: NARI member communities, contractor trade association newsletters, home builder association member directories, contractor licensing exam prep communities. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 6. Equipment Dealer Aged Inventory Markdown Planner
Small equipment dealers (skid steers, trailers, forklifts, landscaping equipment) carry used inventory that depreciates the longer it sits on the lot, but they have no systematic way to determine when and by how much to mark down aging units. This tool accepts inventory entries (unit ID, make/model, purchase date, acquisition cost, current list price) and outputs an aging dashboard: days on lot, implied daily holding cost, recommended markdown percentage based on configurable aging thresholds (30/60/90 days), and a formatted listing description ready to paste into Equipment Trader, Machinery Pete, or Facebook Marketplace. Monetization: $30-55/mo per dealer location. Distribution: AED (Associated Equipment Distributors) member communications, equipment dealer Facebook groups, Equipment Trader dealer community. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.
