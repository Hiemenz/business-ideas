# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-14 (Session 72)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- Simple, fast-to-build workflow trackers — no regulatory compliance angle.
- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.
- **No repeats.** This session's six operator types — real estate wholesalers, storage-unit auction buyers/resellers, longarm quilters, mobile IV hydration providers, independent heavy-equipment operators for hire, and independent car flippers/auction resellers — are distinct from every prior session: real estate wholesaling has never appeared (an entirely new operator type never touched by any prior session — a wholesaler's core problem is a per-deal pipeline tracking a property from lead through an under-contract stage with its own inspection-period and closing-date countdown, then through marketing to a segmented cash-buyer list, to an assigned or closed outcome, a contract-deadline-and-buyer-matching problem no landlord, property-manager, or real-estate-agent tracker in this repo holds); storage-unit auction buying/reselling has never appeared (distinct from the online reseller inventory tracker and the estate sale liquidator idea already in this repo, both of which track already-acquired inventory rather than the acquisition event itself — a storage-auction buyer's core problem instead is a per-lot cost/revenue reconciliation weighing a winning bid plus cleanout costs against resale proceeds spread across several different sales channels, plus a multi-facility auction-date calendar for often-short-notice auctions, an acquisition-cost-and-multi-channel-resale problem no other reseller tracker in this repo holds); longarm quilting has never appeared (an entirely new craft-service operator type never touched by any prior session — a longarm quilter's core problem is a per-quilt job-intake record capturing a customer's chosen pattern, batting type, and thread color at drop-off, a batting/backing-fabric inventory tracker, and a square-inch-based pricing calculator, since longarm work is priced by surface area rather than a flat job fee, a pattern-and-batting-choice-memory problem no other maker or textile tracker in this repo holds); mobile IV hydration therapy has never appeared (an entirely new mobile-service operator type never touched by any prior session — a mobile hydration provider's core problem is a per-client booking and intake record with a screening questionnaire completed before every visit, a per-visit vitals log, and a supply/inventory tracker by drip formula and expiration date, a mobile-visit-and-formula-inventory problem no other mobile health or wellness tracker in this repo holds); ### 1. Real Estate Wholesaler Deal Pipeline & Assignment Contract Tracker

Independent real estate wholesalers who put distressed or off-market properties under contract with a motivated seller and then assign that purchase contract to a cash-buyer investor for an assignment fee need to track a per-deal pipeline stage — lead, under contract, marketing to buyers, assigned, closed — with its own contract-deadline countdown, since every signed contract carries an inspection-period deadline, a closing date, and effectively a deadline to find an assignee before earnest money is genuinely at risk, and a wholesaler juggling 10-20 active contracts across different counties can't hold every deal's deadline in their head at once. The tool also needs a segmented cash-buyer list — organized by price range, target area, and property type — since matching a specific under-contract property to the right buyer fast is the entire business model, and a wholesaler managing a buyer list of 50-150 contacts by memory or a static spreadsheet misses obvious matches, plus a per-deal assignment-fee and profit tracker reconciling what was actually collected against what was projected at the time the contract was signed, because a wholesaler running several deals at once loses track of which assignment fees actually closed versus which are still pending. Missing a contract's closing deadline while still searching for a buyer is exactly the kind of mistake that risks a forfeited earnest-money deposit or a seller's lawsuit for specific performance, and it's the failure mode this tool is built to prevent.

The tool maintains a per-deal pipeline tracker with contract-deadline countdowns (inspection period, earnest-money-at-risk date, closing date), a segmented buyer's list with matching criteria, and a per-deal assignment-fee/profit tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/realestateinvesting and r/wholesaling threads, and local REIA (Real Estate Investors Association) chapter meetings where wholesalers already network for buyer-list contacts. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Storage Unit Auction Buyer & Reseller Lot Acquisition & Resale Tracker

Independent buyers who bid on abandoned storage units at auction (through platforms like StorageTreasures and Lockerfox or in-person facility auctions) need to track a per-lot cost-and-revenue reconciliation weighing the winning bid plus auction-house fees and cleanout/hauling costs against the actual resale proceeds collected across every sales channel an item eventually sells through — eBay, Facebook Marketplace, a local consignment shop, a flea-market booth — since a $400 winning bid can look like a bargain until cleanout costs and unsellable-junk disposal fees are counted, and a reseller running lots from 3-5 units a month has no reliable way to know which units were actually profitable without adding up every receipt by hand. The tool also needs a multi-facility auction-date calendar, since auctions across several storage-facility chains in a buyer's driving radius are often announced with only a few days' notice and missing a listing means missing the auction entirely, plus a per-item "still to sell" inventory tracker so nothing from a cleaned-out unit sits forgotten in a garage for months. A reseller juggling several active lots at different stages of listing and sale can't rely on memory for which items from which unit are still unsold versus already flipped.

The tool maintains a per-lot cost/revenue reconciliation across all resale channels, a multi-facility auction-date calendar, and a per-item "still to sell" inventory tracker with channel and listing status.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per account. Distribution: r/StorageWars and r/Flipping threads, and local storage-auction buyer Facebook groups where regulars already coordinate on upcoming auction dates. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Longarm Quilting Service Quilt Job Intake & Batting/Thread Tracker

Independent longarm quilters who finish other quilters' pieced tops as a paid service need to track a per-quilt job-intake record capturing the customer's quilt-top dimensions, chosen quilting pattern/design, batting type, and thread color agreed at drop-off, since a home-based longarm studio running 15-25 quilts through its queue at once can't rely on memory for which customer chose wool batting versus cotton, or which pattern was discussed weeks earlier at intake — and forgetting a customer's chosen details is exactly the kind of mistake that damages a repeat-customer relationship in a small, word-of-mouth-driven craft business. The tool also needs a batting and backing-fabric inventory tracker, since batting is purchased by the roll or yard and running out mid-job during a busy holiday season stalls the entire queue, plus a square-inch-based pricing calculator — the standard longarm pricing unit — layering in batting and thread add-ons, because underestimating a quilt's square footage by eyeballing it quietly erodes margin on every single job. A quilter running a steady stream of drop-offs and pickups across several weeks of queue depth needs the intake details, inventory, and pricing to stay linked to the specific job rather than scattered across a paper tag and a memory of the conversation at drop-off.

The tool maintains a per-quilt intake record (dimensions, pattern, batting/thread choice, drop-off/pickup dates), a batting/backing-fabric inventory tracker, and a square-inch-based pricing calculator.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per account. Distribution: r/quilting threads, APQS and Handi Quilter longarm-owner Facebook groups, and local quilt guild meetings. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Mobile IV Hydration Therapy Provider Client Booking, Intake & Supply Inventory Tracker

Independent mobile IV hydration providers who travel to a client's home, hotel, or event to administer vitamin and hydration IV drips need to track a per-client booking and intake record — including a signed consent form and a basic health screening questionnaire covering allergies and known contraindications — completed before every single visit, since a provider running 8-15 mobile visits a week across a metro area can't rely on memory for which client's intake paperwork is already on file versus still outstanding, and showing up to a booking without a completed intake creates an awkward last-minute delay on-site. The tool also needs a per-visit vitals log recording blood pressure and pulse before and after the infusion, and a medical-supply and IV-bag/additive inventory tracker spanning several drip formulas (a Myers' cocktail blend, NAD+, a vitamin-C blend) tracked by lot number and expiration date, since a provider juggling multiple formulas across a busy booking week needs to know which lot is nearing its expiration before it gets loaded into the mobile kit for the next run. A provider managing a full week of scattered addresses and appointment windows needs booking, intake status, and supply freshness to stay linked rather than tracked across a paper intake form and a mental count of what's left in the cooler.

The tool maintains a per-client booking/intake tracker with consent-and-screening status, a per-visit vitals log, and a supply/inventory tracker by drip formula, lot number, and expiration date.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: mobile IV/med-spa operator Facebook and Instagram communities, the American IV Association member directory, and local mobile-wellness meetup groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Heavy Equipment Operator for Hire Job Dispatch & Machine Hours/Maintenance Tracker

Independent operators who own a mini-excavator, skid steer, or bobcat and hire out for site work — grading, demolition, land clearing — on a per-job or hourly basis need a job-dispatch calendar recording each client's site address, job scope, and quoted hours or day rate, since a solo operator juggling 5-10 active site bookings across different clients can accidentally double-book their one or two machines without a central calendar to check against. The tool also needs a per-machine hours-based maintenance tracker logging engine hours against the manufacturer's service interval — oil changes, hydraulic filter swaps, greasing schedule — because running a machine past its service interval on an active job site is the fastest way to have an expensive breakdown mid-job with no backup machine to swap in, and a solo operator can't rely on memory for exactly how many hours have accumulated since the last service. A per-job invoice generator rounds out the tool, computing billed hours against the quoted rate plus any travel or mobilization fee, since an operator running jobs back-to-back across a full week can't rely on memory for which client still owes an invoice versus which one already paid.

The tool maintains a job-dispatch calendar per machine, an hours-based maintenance-interval tracker per machine, and a per-job invoice generator.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/Excavation and r/heavyequipment threads, and local equipment-operator Facebook and Marketplace groups where solo operators already advertise for-hire availability. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Independent Car Flipper & Auction Reseller VIN Acquisition Cost & Resale Profit Tracker

Independent car flippers who buy vehicles at dealer/public auto auctions (Copart, IAAI, Manheim) or from private sellers and resell them for a margin, operating below most states' occasional-sale licensing threshold rather than as a licensed dealership, need a per-VIN acquisition-cost ledger tracking the purchase price, auction fees, transport, title/registration cost, and repair or reconditioning spend against the eventual resale price, since a flipper running 4-8 vehicles at once across different stages of reconditioning can't tell which vehicles are actually profitable without manually adding up every receipt. The tool also needs a reconditioning checklist and cost log per vehicle tracking parts and labor against a target sale price — an easy budget to blow through without a running total — and a title-and-paperwork status tracker confirming each vehicle's title has actually transferred and is ready to hand over at sale, since selling a car without a clean title in hand is the single most common way a flip deal falls apart at the last minute. A flipper juggling several vehicles at different reconditioning stages can't rely on memory for which VIN is still waiting on a salvage-title reissue versus which one is ready to list.

The tool maintains a per-VIN cost/profit ledger reconciling acquisition, repair, and resale, a reconditioning checklist/budget tracker per vehicle, and a title/paperwork status tracker per VIN.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/flipping threads, local Copart/IAAI buyer Facebook groups, and regional car-flipper meetup communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
