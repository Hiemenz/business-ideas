# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-16 (Round 197)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a costume maker, a piñata maker, a karaoke host, a flea-market reseller, a stationery designer and a cosplay prop builder.

---

### 1. Costume Maker Commission, Fitting & Deadline Tracker

A costume maker for theater, conventions and parties has to track measurements, fabric purchases, fittings and event deadlines. Convention dates and opening nights are fixed, and fabric costs vary.

The tool stores measurements per client, tracks fittings and deadlines, logs fabric cost and shows margin per commission.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per maker. Distribution: costuming communities, r/costuming and local theater networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Piñata Maker Order, Design & Pickup Tracker

A piñata maker has to track orders by design, size, fill and party date. Each piñata takes days to build and dry, and orders cluster around holidays and birthdays.

The tool schedules builds backward from party dates, tracks materials, records design approvals and sends pickup reminders.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per maker. Distribution: party-vendor Facebook groups, Etsy seller communities and local craft-fair boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Karaoke Host Venue, Song-Book & Booking Tracker

A karaoke host who runs weekly bar nights and private parties has to track venues, pay, equipment and crowd requests. Venues rebook the hosts who draw crowds, and private parties need deposits.

The tool tracks venues and pay, logs attendance, handles private-party deposits and flags the best nights.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per host. Distribution: karaoke-host communities, r/karaoke and local bar-owner groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Flea-Market Reseller Haul, Price & Profit Tracker

A reseller who buys at flea markets and estate sales to resell has to track each haul's cost, item condition, listing price and sale. Without records, it is unclear which finds and which markets pay.

The tool logs hauls with photos and cost, tracks listing and sale price, shows profit by item and by market.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per reseller. Distribution: reseller communities, r/Flipping and local flea-market groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Stationery Designer Collection, Print-Run & Wholesale Tracker

A designer who sells cards and planners has to track collections, print runs, stock, wholesale orders and seasonal launches. Holiday card lines must be printed months ahead, and wholesale buyers order in lots.

The tool tracks collections and print runs, shows stock by item, manages wholesale orders and counts down to seasonal deadlines.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per designer. Distribution: stationery-designer communities, r/Etsy and trade-show groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Cosplay Prop Builder Commission, Material & Convention Tracker

A prop builder who makes foam armor and weapons for cosplayers has to track commissions, material use, build stages and convention deadlines. Customers need pieces before a specific convention, and materials are costly.

The tool tracks builds with stages and deadlines, logs material cost, manages deposits and flags convention crunch weeks.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per builder. Distribution: cosplay communities, r/cosplay and convention-vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
