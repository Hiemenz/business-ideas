# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-30 (Round 152)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo's prior 151 rounds, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Independent Computer & Mobile Device Repair Technician Client Device, Parts & Warranty Tracker

A solo computer and phone repair technician running a shop or a mobile/house-call repair business has to track each device's intake specs (make, model, serial/IMEI, reported issue) and diagnostic notes against the exact replacement part ordered — a specific screen assembly, battery, or logic-board component tied to that device's exact model revision — since ordering the wrong part revision for a customer's device is a wasted part and a delayed repair in a business where same-day turnaround is the main competitive edge over a manufacturer's mail-in service. A technician juggling a repair queue at different stages (diagnosed, parts ordered, in repair, ready for pickup) also needs a simple warranty record per repair, since a repaired device that fails again within the technician's own 90-day warranty window needs to be instantly recognizable as a warranty redo rather than a new billable job, and a technician wants a fast way to see which customers have paid versus which invoices are outstanding on a completed pickup.

The tool maintains a per-device intake record (make/model/serial or IMEI, reported issue, diagnostic notes) tied to a customer profile; a parts-ordering log matching the exact part and revision needed to each device; a repair-queue stage tracker (diagnosed, parts ordered, in repair, ready for pickup) with a warranty-window flag on repeat repairs; and a per-customer invoice and payment-status tracker. Monetization: $15-25/mo per technician. Distribution: independent-repair-shop Facebook groups and the r/talesfromtechsupport and device-repair communities where solo technicians already trade parts-sourcing tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Freelance Book & Manuscript Editor Client Manuscript, Style-Sheet & Revision-Pass Tracker

An independent developmental editor, copyeditor, or proofreader working with self-published authors and small presses on several manuscripts at once has to track each project's exact scope (a full developmental edit versus a line edit versus a proofread pass) and the author's specific style-sheet decisions — chosen spelling variants, a fictional world's invented terms and their correct capitalization, a character's name spelled one specific way throughout — since an editor juggling six manuscripts at different stages can't rely on memory for which of last month's authors prefers serial commas or which project's style sheet says a certain word is always lowercase. An editor also has to track each manuscript's revision-pass history — which round of edits addressed which chapters — and a per-project deadline and invoice schedule, since editing is typically billed per-project or per-word with a deposit and final-payment split, and a missed self-imposed deadline on one of several concurrent projects is the kind of slip that damages an editor's reputation in a referral-driven business.

The tool maintains a per-manuscript project record (edit type, word count, deadline) with an attached style sheet carrying forward corrections and terminology decisions across every revision pass; a revision-pass history log tracking which chapters or sections were addressed in each round; and a per-project deposit/final-invoice tracker. Monetization: $15-25/mo per editor. Distribution: the Editorial Freelancers Association (EFA) member directory and r/editors and self-publishing-author Facebook groups where editors already find client referrals. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent Martial Arts Instructor Multi-Location Student Rank, Attendance & Belt-Testing Tracker

An independent martial arts instructor teaching classes across several rented studio spaces, community centers, or a single small dojo has to track each student's current belt rank, the specific curriculum requirements (forms, techniques, sparring hours) they still need before their next rank test, and attendance toward whatever minimum-class-count a rank promotion requires, since an instructor running classes for dozens of students across multiple age groups and skill levels can't rely on memory for exactly which student is eligible for their next belt test and which one is still missing a required sparring session. An instructor also has to track family/student billing on a monthly tuition or class-pack basis, since a martial arts school's revenue depends on catching a lapsed autopay or an expired class pack before a student quietly stops showing up, and needs a belt-testing event scheduler that rolls up which students across the whole program are actually eligible before the next testing date is set.

The tool maintains a per-student rank and curriculum-progress record (forms/techniques completed, sparring hours logged) tied to attendance history; a belt-test eligibility roll-up flagging which students meet the requirements before the next testing date; and a per-family tuition and class-pack billing tracker with lapsed-payment alerts. Monetization: $19-29/mo per instructor. Distribution: martial arts instructor forums on Bullshido and r/martialarts, and regional martial arts association (state Taekwondo, Karate, or Jiu-Jitsu association) member newsletters. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Independent Vacation Rental Co-Host & Turnover Manager Multi-Property Booking, Cleaning & Restock Tracker

An independent co-host managing short-term rental bookings, guest communication, and turnover logistics for a portfolio of 8-20 host properties across multiple listing platforms has to track each property's exact turnover checklist — linen count, specific restock items, a particular property's quirky check-in instructions — and coordinate a cleaning crew's schedule against a tight same-day checkout-to-checkin window, since a missed restock item or a cleaner arriving late between a Saturday-morning checkout and a same-day check-in is the kind of failure that shows up in a guest's public review and directly threatens the property's ranking on the platform. A co-host juggling bookings synced across Airbnb, Vrbo, and direct-booking channels also needs a consolidated calendar to avoid a double-booking across platforms, and a per-property owner-payout and co-host-fee reconciliation tracker, since a co-host typically earns a percentage of booking revenue and owners expect a clear monthly accounting of what came in and what was paid out.

The tool maintains a per-property turnover checklist (linens, restock items, check-in quirks) tied to a cleaning-crew scheduling calendar synced against a multi-platform booking calendar; a same-day turnover alert flagging tight checkout-to-checkin windows; and a per-property owner-payout and co-host-commission reconciliation tracker. Monetization: $25-40/mo per co-host. Distribution: the r/AirBnBHosts and r/vacationrentals co-host and property-manager communities, and the Vacation Rental Management Association's (VRMA) independent-operator member directory. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Independent CSA/Small Produce Farm Operator Member-Share, Harvest & Distribution Tracker

An independent farmer running a Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) subscription program alongside farmers-market and wholesale sales has to track each subscribing member's share size and any customization — a half-share, an add-on egg or flower subscription, a dietary substitution swapped out at pickup — against a weekly harvest plan built around what's actually ready in the field that week, since a farmer packing dozens of member boxes by hand off a printed spreadsheet has no reliable way to confirm every box actually matches what that specific member signed up and paid for. A farmer running multiple pickup locations and a farmers-market booth at the same time also needs to plan harvest volume across all three channels (CSA boxes, market, wholesale) from one crop-planning view, since over-committing a crop to CSA boxes and then running short at the market booth is a lost sales opportunity that's hard to see coming without a unified plan, and needs a simple way to track which members have paid for the season versus which are on an installment plan.

The tool maintains a per-member share-size and customization profile tied to a weekly harvest-to-box packing list; a cross-channel harvest-allocation planner spanning CSA boxes, farmers-market, and wholesale commitments from one crop plan; a multi-pickup-location routing schedule; and a per-member season-payment and installment-plan tracker. Monetization: $19-35/mo per farm. Distribution: the LocalHarvest CSA farm directory and state sustainable-agriculture association (e.g., a state NOFA chapter) member newsletters. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks, timed ahead of spring CSA sign-up season.

### 6. Independent Pilates Instructor Multi-Studio Client Equipment-Setting & Progress Tracker

An independent Pilates instructor teaching reformer and mat classes across several studios' rented time slots, or running a small home studio, has to track each client's specific equipment settings — reformer spring tension, box height, strap length for their body type — and session-progress notes (an injury modification, a strength milestone) tied to a rotating class or private-session schedule, since an instructor seeing 20-40 clients across different studio locations can't rely on memory for which client needs a modified exercise for a shoulder injury or which client's reformer springs need adjusting before they even get on the machine. An instructor also has to track each client's session-package balance across possibly different pricing at different studio locations, since a client running low on a 10-session package needs a renewal conversation before their sessions quietly run out, and needs a simple way to see which clients have paid versus which invoices from private in-home sessions are still outstanding.

The tool maintains a per-client equipment-setting and progress-note record carried across sessions and studio locations; a session-package balance tracker with renewal-due alerts, priced per studio if rates vary; and a per-client invoice and payment-status tracker for private and in-home sessions. Monetization: $15-25/mo per instructor. Distribution: the Pilates Method Alliance (PMA) member directory and independent-instructor Facebook and Instagram professional groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
