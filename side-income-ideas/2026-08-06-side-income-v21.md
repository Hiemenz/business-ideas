# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 21)

### 1. Pet Sitter & Dog Walker Visit Report Card Generator
Pet service providers — dog walkers, in-home pet sitters, and doggy daycare operators — send end-of-visit text updates but no structured documentation, which leaves pet owners anxious and makes the service feel generic. This tool lets the sitter fill out a quick 10-field form per visit (pet name, duration, activities, meals given, bathroom breaks, mood/energy, any notes) and generates a branded, visually formatted "Pup Report Card" PDF that gets emailed or texted to the owner automatically. Clients love the transparency; providers look more professional. Monetization: $10-16/mo per sitter account, unlimited report cards. Distribution: Rover and Wag sitter communities, pet business owner Facebook groups, r/petsitting. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Construction Bid Scope Gap Highlighter
General contractors and subcontractors reviewing project specification documents (CSI-format specs, often 200-400 pages) before submitting a bid routinely miss scope items buried in Division 01 general requirements or cross-referenced between specification sections — gaps that become costly change orders post-award. This tool accepts a pasted or uploaded specification excerpt, identifies potentially ambiguous or cross-referenced scope items using pattern matching, and generates a list of clarifying questions the contractor should send to the architect or owner before bid day. Prevents underbidding from missed scope without requiring the contractor to hire a spec consultant. Monetization: $20-35/mo or $15/project. Distribution: r/construction, general contractor Facebook groups, AGC (Associated General Contractors) member communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Nonprofit Grant Progress Report Narrative Generator
Nonprofits that receive foundation or government grants must submit narrative progress reports — typically 3-10 pages — describing activities completed, outcomes achieved, and funds spent against the grant budget. Grant managers dread writing these reports and frequently submit them late or incompletely because the writing feels daunting. This tool accepts structured inputs (grant goals, activities completed this period, participants served, outcomes measured, challenges encountered, and budget spend-to-date) and generates a draft progress report narrative organized to match the funder's typical reporting structure, ready for the program director to review and personalize. Monetization: $12-20/mo subscription or $20/report. Distribution: nonprofit program manager communities, GrantStation and Instrumentl user communities, r/nonprofit. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Real Estate Agent Open House Lead Follow-Up Email Sequence Generator
Real estate agents collect sign-in sheets at open houses but rarely have a structured follow-up plan beyond one phone call. This tool accepts inputs about the open house (address, listing price, key features, neighborhood, days on market) and the lead's sign-in information (name, email, buyer vs. curious neighbor, timeline notes), then generates a personalized 5-email follow-up sequence: a same-day thank-you with the property disclosure link, a 3-day check-in with comparable listings, a 1-week market update, a 2-week softer touch, and a 30-day re-engagement. Agents get structured nurture without having to write from scratch. Monetization: $15-25/mo per agent. Distribution: r/realestate, real estate agent Facebook groups, KW and RE/MAX agent communities, ActiveRain. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Bar & Brewery Daily Waste & Comp Tracker
Bars, taprooms, and small breweries lose significant gross margin to untracked pour waste (dropped pints, overpours, tapped-out kegs), comped drinks that aren't logged against marketing budgets, and spillage during keg changes. Without tracking, operators have no data to identify waste patterns by shift, server, or product. This tool provides a simple end-of-shift entry form (product wasted, quantity, reason, server, comp reason if applicable) and generates a weekly loss summary: total waste cost by product, cost by shift and server, comp spend vs. authorized budget, and a rolling 30-day waste trend. Monetization: $15-25/mo per location. Distribution: r/bartenders, craft brewery owner Facebook groups, taproom manager communities, Brewers Association member forums. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Residential Solar Output vs. Utility Net Metering Reconciliation Tool
Homeowners with rooftop solar panels receive monthly utility statements with net metering credits that don't clearly show whether their system is performing as expected. This tool accepts the homeowner's inverter production data (manual entry or CSV export from Enphase/SolarEdge apps), their monthly utility bill net metering credit, and the utility's buyback rate, then calculates: expected monthly production based on system size and local irradiance, actual vs. expected production variance, whether the net metering credit received matches the production exported, and a 12-month ROI tracking chart showing payback progress against the original system cost. Monetization: $6-12/mo or one-time $29 purchase. Distribution: r/solar, Enphase and SolarEdge owner communities, solar homeowner Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
