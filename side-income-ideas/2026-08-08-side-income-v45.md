# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-08 (Round 45)

### 1. Mobile Marine & Boat Mechanic Client Vessel & Engine Service History Tracker
Independent marine mechanics who travel to marinas, storage yards, and private docks to service outboard, inboard, and stern-drive engines need each boat's exact powertrain specs on hand before arriving — engine make/model/hours, drive type, impeller and water pump service intervals, winterization/de-winterization status, and what was done at the last haul-out — because a service call without the right impeller kit or oil filter can mean a wasted trip to a boat that may be an hour from the shop by truck and trailer. This is currently tracked, if at all, in a phone notes app or a paper folder that doesn't hold up dockside.

This tool maintains a per-vessel profile (owner, hull ID/HIN, engine make/model/serial, drive type, and current engine hours), a service visit log (date, service performed, parts used, and hour-meter reading at time of service), a wear-interval and seasonal-task tracker (impeller and water pump due dates calculated from engine hours or time, winterization/de-winterization status by season), and a route-ordered appointment calendar grouping the day's marina and yard stops by dock or storage-lot cluster.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per mechanic account. Distribution: American Boat & Yacht Council (ABYC) certified technician directory, marina bulletin boards and dockmaster referral networks, r/boatbuilding and r/MechanicAdvice marine-focused threads. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Mobile Spray Tan Technician Client Formula & Appointment Tracker
Mobile spray tan technicians who travel to clients' homes for weddings, events, and recurring maintenance sessions need to remember each client's exact solution formula (DHA percentage, base color — violet, bronze, or dark — and any custom blend), skin sensitivity notes, and how the last tan actually developed, because using the wrong solution strength on a client with sensitive skin or forgetting a client always needs an extra-dark formula for photos wastes the appointment and risks a bad result the client will post about. Most technicians currently track this in a phone notes app or rely on memory across a growing client list.

This tool maintains a per-client tan profile (skin tone, solution brand and DHA strength, base color preference, known sensitivities, and development time notes), a session log (date, solution used, event the tan was for, and result notes from client feedback), an event-based appointment calendar with automatic reminders for wedding parties needing coordinated multi-person bookings, and a rebooking prompt based on each client's typical fade timeline.

Monetization: $10-18/mo per technician account. Distribution: mobile spray tan Facebook groups, r/spraytanning, Airbrush Tanning Professionals networks, and Norvell/Sjolie/VersaSpa certified-technician communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent Wellness & Holistic Nutrition Coach Client Intake & Session Progress Tracker
Independent wellness coaches and holistic nutrition consultants (non-clinical, not billing insurance) working with 15-40 ongoing clients need a running record of each client's intake history — health goals, dietary restrictions, current protocol or supplement plan — and session-by-session progress notes, because a coach juggling weekly or biweekly check-ins across dozens of clients loses track of what was recommended last session and what the client actually followed through on. This currently lives in scattered Google Docs or a generic notes app with no structure connecting intake to ongoing sessions.

This tool maintains a client intake profile (goals, dietary restrictions, current protocol/supplement plan, and relevant health history the client has disclosed), a session log (date, topics discussed, adherence to prior recommendations, and new action items assigned), a protocol version history showing how a client's plan has evolved over time, and a session calendar with pre-session prep pulling up the client's last three sessions' notes automatically.

Monetization: $15-22/mo per coach account. Distribution: the National Association of Nutrition Professionals (NANP) member directory, Institute for Integrative Nutrition (IIN) alumni network, and holistic health coach Facebook and Circle communities. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Wedding & Event DJ Gig Contract, Equipment & Timeline Tracker
Independent wedding and event DJs booking 40-80 gigs a year juggle a signed contract and deposit schedule per client, a detailed event timeline (ceremony start, first dance, cake cutting, last call) built from a client planning questionnaire, a must-play/do-not-play song list, and which equipment package (speakers, lighting, wireless mics) is reserved for which date — all of which currently lives across email threads, a shared Google Doc template, and a paper gig calendar that makes it easy to double-book equipment or lose track of an unsigned contract until the week of the event.

This tool maintains a gig record per event (client, venue, date, contract and deposit status, and equipment package reserved), a timeline builder generated from the client's planning questionnaire responses with editable event-of-day cues, a music preference list (must-plays, do-not-plays, special dance dedications) referenced during the event, and an equipment availability calendar preventing the same gear from being double-booked across two same-day events.

Monetization: $18-28/mo per DJ account. Distribution: Mobile Beat/DJ industry Facebook groups, the American Disc Jockey Association (ADJA) member directory, r/DJs wedding-focused threads, and WeddingWire/The Knot vendor community forums. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 5. Junk Removal & Hauling Operator Job Quote & Load Disposal Tracker
Solo junk removal and hauling operators quote jobs by estimated truck-load volume — a garage cleanout, a single furniture item, a full estate cleanout — each priced differently depending on load size and disposal fees for specific item types (mattresses, appliances, electronics, construction debris often carry separate dump or recycling fees). Most operators quote from memory or a rough mental average, leading to underbid jobs once actual dump fees are tallied, and have no systematic record of which items went to the landfill, a donation center, or a recycling facility — useful both for cost tracking and for customers who want proof an item was donated rather than dumped.

This tool maintains a client job record (address, estimated load size in truck-fraction or cubic yards, item categories, and quoted price against the operator's own cost-per-load history), a load disposition log per job (landfill, recycling facility, or donation center, with disposal/tipping fee recorded), a before/after photo log per job for dispute-proofing and marketing use, and a route-ordered same-day job calendar for multi-stop days.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per operator account. Distribution: r/JunkRemoval and Junk Removal Business Owners Facebook groups, the Junk Removal Authority (JRA) community, and local trade Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Independent Mobile & Booth-Rent Nail Technician Client Service History Tracker
Independent nail technicians — whether renting a booth at a salon or traveling to clients for mobile services — need to remember each client's exact service history: gel vs. acrylic vs. dip powder preference, nail shape, custom color mixes or nail art requested last time, and any product sensitivities, because starting a fill or full set without knowing what the client had last time means re-asking questions every single appointment and risking a product reaction the tech should have flagged. Most technicians currently rely on memory or a phone photo roll with no structure.

This tool maintains a per-client service profile (preferred service type, nail shape, standing color or design preferences, and any product sensitivities or allergies noted), a visit log (date, service performed, product/brand used, and a photo of the finished result for reference), a rebooking reminder based on each client's typical fill interval, and a simple day-of appointment calendar with each client's profile one tap away before they sit down.

Monetization: $12-20/mo per technician account. Distribution: Nailpro and NAILS Magazine professional communities, r/Nailtechs, and local booth-rent nail tech Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
