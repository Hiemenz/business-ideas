# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-11 (Round 174)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a thrift-arbitrage seller, a custom invitation designer, a walking-tour guide, a solo lawn-route operator, a senior tech helper and a pop-up screen printer.

---

### 1. Thrift-Arbitrage Seller Sourcing-Trip, Item & Profit Tracker

A person who buys from thrift stores and estate sales to resell online has to track each sourcing trip's spend, items bought, listing status and sale. Without it, they cannot see which stores or days produce profit, or how long items sit unsold. Time spent sourcing and listing is the hidden cost.

The tool records trips with spend and hours, tracks each item from bought to sold with cost and sale price and shows profit per store and per hour.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per seller. Distribution: reseller Facebook groups, r/Flipping and eBay and Poshmark seller communities. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Custom Invitation Designer Order, Proof & Print-Deadline Tracker

A freelance designer who creates wedding, birthday and event invitations has to track each order's details (names, date, wording), proof rounds, print quantity and the event date that sets the deadline. With a busy season, proofs wait for client approval and a typo on the printed set is expensive.

The tool tracks orders with proof rounds and client approval status, a countdown to the print deadline and a final-details checklist before print.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per designer. Distribution: Etsy invitation-seller groups, r/weddingplanning vendor circles and graphic-design freelancer communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Walking-Tour Guide Booking, Group & Tip Tracker

An independent guide who leads paid walking tours has to track bookings by tour, group size, meeting point and weather cancellations. Tour revenue often combines ticket price and tips, and booking platform fees complicate income. Knowing which tours and time slots fill best decides the schedule.

The tool tracks bookings by tour date and slot, shows fill rate per tour, records ticket revenue, platform fees and tips and flags weather-risk days with a rebooking message.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per guide. Distribution: tour-guide Facebook groups, r/travel guide threads and local tourism networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Solo Lawn-Route Operator Property, Schedule & Payment Tracker

A solo lawn-care operator with 30-60 weekly properties has to track each property's schedule, gate codes, special instructions, price and payment status. Rain delays shift the week, and a missed property is a lost customer. Customers who pay late need chasing, and new-lead quotes need tracking.

The tool shows a weekly route by property with rain-delay rescheduling, per-property notes, payment status with overdue reminders and a quote list for new leads.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per operator. Distribution: lawn-care Facebook groups, r/lawncare business threads and local landscaper networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Senior Tech Helper Client, Device & Visit Tracker

A person who helps older adults with phones, computers and smart-TV setup has to track each client's devices, passwords handled with the client's own notes, previous issues and visits. A client calls back about the same issue, and the helper needs to see what was done last time. Many clients prefer prepaid visit packages.

The tool stores per-client device lists and visit notes, a history of issues solved, visit-package balances and a reminder for a check-in visit.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per helper. Distribution: senior-services Facebook groups, local library and community-center networks and r/techsupport freelancer threads. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Pop-Up Screen Printer Event, Design & Garment-Count Tracker

A person who sets up a live screen-printing booth at events and markets has to track each event's designs, garment sizes and counts, ink and blanks stock and sales. Running out of a popular size mid-event is lost revenue, and leftover blanks are wasted money. Margin per event is hard to see without counting.

The tool tracks inventory by garment size and color, an event packing list from past sales, sales per design at each event and profit per event.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per printer. Distribution: screen-printing Facebook groups, r/screenprinting and maker-market organizer networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
