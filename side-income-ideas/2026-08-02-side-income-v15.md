# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-02 (Round 15)

### 1. Hydroponics & Indoor Grow Nutrient Dosing Log
Hydroponic and indoor plant growers manage multiple reservoirs and grow systems, each requiring precise nutrient solution EC, pH, and temperature tracking alongside each dosing event (which nutrients, how much, what brand/lot) to diagnose crop problems and repeat successful runs. This app provides a per-system parameter log with drift alerts when readings fall outside target ranges, a nutrient dosing journal with batch lot recording, and a grow-cycle history chart showing how parameters correlated with final yield and plant health. Monetization: $7-14/mo or $39/yr. Distribution: r/hydro, r/microgrowery, indoor growing Facebook groups, hydroponic supply store communities. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 2. Home Inspector Report Builder & Photo Annotator
Certified home inspectors write 30-50 page reports for every inspection, spending 1-2 hours in specialized report software that is either expensive ($400-600/yr) or clunky — slowing down how many inspections they can schedule per week. This lightweight report builder provides a library of pre-written deficiency descriptions organized by home system (roof, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, structure), one-tap insertion into the report, in-app photo annotation with arrow and callout overlays, and branded PDF export. Monetization: $25-45/mo per inspector. Distribution: ASHI (American Society of Home Inspectors) and InterNACHI community forums, home inspector Facebook groups, inspector licensing course communities. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 3. CACFP Meal Tracking App for Family Childcare Homes
Family childcare home providers participating in the USDA Child and Adult Care Food Program must record every meal served to every child each day, documenting that food components meet program meal pattern requirements — all to submit a monthly claim to their sponsoring agency. This app lets providers quickly log attendance and meals served in a few taps per sitting, checks that each meal meets CACFP component requirements, flags errors before claim submission, and generates the monthly claim report formatted for their sponsoring organization. Monetization: $10-20/mo per provider. Distribution: CACFP sponsoring agency onboarding materials, family childcare home association newsletters, child care provider Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 4. Laser Cutter & CNC Shop Job Queue and Material Stock Tracker
Small laser cutting and CNC routing shops that produce custom orders (acrylic signs, wooden parts, engraved products) manage a job queue of customer files with varying material specs and machine assignments, alongside a material inventory of sheet stock that depletes as jobs are cut and must be reordered before running out mid-job. This app tracks each job through its queue stages (received → material confirmed → queued → in progress → QC → shipped/picked up), manages material inventory by type, thickness, and sheet count, and flags jobs where the required material is below threshold. Monetization: $35-60/mo per shop. Distribution: r/lasercutting, LaserGods Facebook group, Glowforge owner community forums, CNC fabrication Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 5. Data Analyst Portfolio Project Prompt Generator
Aspiring data analysts building job-hunt portfolios spend more time wondering what to build than actually building it — and the projects they choose are often the same five overused datasets that hiring managers have seen hundreds of times. This tool generates tailored project ideas from real-world datasets matched to the user's target industry (healthcare, finance, retail, logistics, etc.), provides a structured analysis brief (business question, suggested approach, key metrics to surface), and outputs a portfolio write-up template that frames the completed project for a resume or GitHub README. Monetization: $12-20/mo subscription or $39 one-time project pack purchase. Distribution: r/dataanalysis, r/learnpython, data science Discord servers, analyst job-prep YouTube creator affiliate partnerships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Custom Knife Maker Commission Queue & Steel Inventory App
Custom bladesmith shops taking commission orders — hunting knives, kitchen knives, fixed blades — manage a backlog of customers with specific steel alloy requests, handle material specs, and engraving details, while also tracking their raw material inventory (bar stock by alloy, dimensions, and quantity remaining) to know what they can commit to before ordering. This app manages the commission queue from deposit to delivery, stores per-order specs and customer communication notes, tracks steel and handle material inventory with reorder alerts, and estimates queue completion dates based on the smith's typical build time per knife type. Monetization: $20-35/mo per maker. Distribution: r/bladesmith, American Bladesmith Society member community, bladesmithing YouTube creator audiences, custom knife Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
