# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 33)

### 1. Backyard Chicken Keeper Flock Health Log & Egg Production Tracker
Backyard chicken keepers with 4-40 hens track egg production, health observations, and feed consumption in their heads or on a whiteboard — making it impossible to notice that production dropped when feed brand changed, or that one hen hasn't laid in 10 days. This tool maintains a flock roster (each bird by name or band number, breed, hatch date, and current production status — laying/molting/broody), a daily egg count log (total eggs and optionally per-bird counts from rollaway nest boxes), a health observation log (any bird exhibiting symptoms — lethargy, odd droppings, respiratory signs — with date and action taken), and a feed and medication log (feed type, consumption rate, and any supplements or treatments administered). A 30-day egg production chart shows weekly production trends — the keeper can see the production dip that coincided with the new feed or the molt that started in September. Monthly summary: total eggs, feed cost per dozen, and any birds flagged with health observations. Monetization: $5-8/mo per keeper or $30/year flat. Distribution: BackYardChickens.com community, r/BackYardChickens, Chicken Chick Facebook communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Portrait & Newborn Photographer Client Gallery Delivery & Print Order Tracker
Portrait and newborn photographers who shoot 3-8 sessions per week deliver online galleries (via Pic-Time, Shootproof, Pixieset) and fulfill print and product orders — but track gallery expiration dates, client download deadlines, pending print orders, and outstanding payments in a fragmented mix of their gallery platform, a spreadsheet, and email threads. When a gallery expires before a client orders and there's no record that the client was reminded, the photographer loses a print sale and gets a complaint. This tool maintains a per-session record (client name, session date, gallery delivery date, gallery expiration date, print credit included, and outstanding print order status), sends a reminder when a gallery is 7 days from expiration, tracks print orders from order placement to lab submission to delivery confirmation, and logs payments received vs. outstanding on add-on orders. A monthly revenue dashboard shows: sessions shot, galleries delivered, print order revenue, and average revenue per session. Monetization: $15-22/mo per photographer account. Distribution: Shootproof and Pic-Time user communities, r/photography, The Portrait System community, newborn photography Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Online Reseller Inventory Cost Basis & Net Profit Tracker
Resellers who flip items on eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, or Facebook Marketplace often don't know if they're making money after platform fees, shipping costs, and their time — because they track purchases in a notes app and sales in the platform dashboard, and never reconcile the two. This tool maintains a per-item inventory: item description, purchase price, sourcing location (thrift store, liquidation, retail arbitrage), listing platform, list price, sale price, platform selling fee (configurable percentage), shipping cost, and net profit. A dashboard shows: total items in inventory, total capital tied up, items sold this month, total revenue, total fees, total shipping, and net profit — the actual number after all costs. A sourcing location breakdown shows which channels (Goodwill vs. garage sales vs. liquidation pallets) generate the highest average profit per item. Optional: an unsold inventory aging report showing items that have been listed for 60+ days without selling. Monetization: $8-12/mo per reseller account or $50/year. Distribution: r/Flipping, r/Poshmark, Reselling Academy and Flipper University communities, Bolo Bros podcast listener groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Indoor Cycling Studio Bike Maintenance & Safety Inspection Log
Indoor cycling studios (Peloton-style facilities, spin studios) with 20-50 stationary bikes need to track bike maintenance — cleated pedal replacement, handlebar adjustment bolt tightening, flywheel resistance calibration, seat post inspection — on a regular schedule, but most do it reactively (when a rider complains). A bike that hasn't been inspected in 90 days is a slip-and-fall or injury liability risk. This tool maintains a per-bike record (bike number or ID, installation date, last inspection date, and any open maintenance items), a maintenance log (inspection date, technician name, items checked — pedals, handlebars, seat, flywheel, resistance mechanism — pass/fail, and any corrective action performed), and an inspection schedule calendar (configurable: weekly safety inspection for high-use bikes, monthly full maintenance inspection). A studio-facing dashboard shows every bike's last inspection date and flags any bike that is overdue by more than 7 days. When a bike is flagged out of service, the dashboard shows it clearly so instructors don't assign a member to a non-functioning bike. Monetization: $15-25/mo per studio account. Distribution: Indoor cycling studio owner Facebook groups, SPINNING Education (Mad Dogg Athletics) instructor community, boutique fitness studio association networks. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Community Theater Production Costume & Prop Inventory Tracker
Community theater companies and high school theater programs managing costumes and props across multiple productions have a recurring problem: where is the Victorian corset from the 1998 production? Was the sword lent to another theater group? What does the wardrobe room actually contain that could be reused for next season? This tool maintains a catalog of costume and prop inventory (item description, category, size or era, storage location in the wardrobe room, condition, and any production it was last used in), tracks items checked out to cast members or loaned to other theater organizations (with the borrower, checkout date, and expected return), and generates a pre-production inventory check for an upcoming show — the director specifies what items are needed, and the tool shows which are already in inventory vs. need to be acquired. A production history log shows which items were used in which production years. Bonus: a costume assignment tracker for the current production (actor name, character, each costume piece assigned). Monetization: $10-15/mo per theater organization account. Distribution: American Association of Community Theatre (AACT) member networks, high school theater director Facebook groups, r/TheaterMaking. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Short-Term Rental Host Supply & Consumable Restocking Tracker
Airbnb and VRBO hosts managing 1-5 properties track their supply restocking — toiletries, coffee pods, paper products, cleaning supplies — by walking through the property and mentally noting what's low, then ordering from memory. When a busy rental gets back-to-back bookings, supplies run out between guest stays because there was no systematic check. This tool lets the host define a standard supply list for each property (item, par level, reorder quantity, and vendor), log supply checks after each turnover (quantities on hand for each item), and automatically identify items below par level that need restocking before the next check-in. A weekly restock order summary combines all properties' restocking needs into a single shopping list by vendor. Optional: a cost tracker that logs what was spent on supplies per month per property so the host can monitor supply costs against the property's income. Monetization: $8-12/mo per host account (up to 5 properties). Distribution: Airbnb Host Community forums, BiggerPockets STR investor communities, r/airbnb host communities, Hospitable and Hostfully user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
