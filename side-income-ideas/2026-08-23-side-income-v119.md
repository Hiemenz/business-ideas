# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-23 (Round 119)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Surfboard Shaper & Custom Surfboard Maker Order Spec, Production-Stage & Blank Inventory Tracker

An independent surfboard shaper working out of a home or shared shaping bay takes on custom orders each needing its own precise spec — length, width, thickness, rocker curve, and fin-box placement — and has to move a blank through a specific sequence of production stages (shaped, glassed, cured, sanded, pinlined, finished), since handing a board off before its resin has fully cured is a board that yellows or delaminates weeks later and a customer who won't order a second one. A shaper running six to ten boards through the bay at different stages at once currently tracks specs and stage progress on a chalk-marked blank and a paper order pad, and a board glassed with the wrong fin-box placement because the spec sheet got smudged is a redo that eats the week's margin, while a foam blank or resin color that runs out mid-order with no reorder flagged is a board that can't move to its next stage on schedule.

The tool maintains a per-order board spec sheet (dimensions, rocker, fin setup, and glass weight/tint) tied to a production-stage tracker with a cure-time gate before sanding can begin; a blank, resin, and glass-cloth inventory tracker by size and type with reorder alerts; and a client order history for repeat surfers building out a quiver.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per shaper. Distribution: r/surfboardshaping and r/surfing shaper-referral threads, the Surfboard Builders Guild community, and local shaping-bay and surf-shop Facebook groups where independent shapers already trade production-stage and blank-inventory workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Milliner & Custom Hat Maker Client Measurement, Block-to-Trim & Derby-Season Rush Tracker

An independent milliner making custom hats for weddings, Kentucky Derby season, and theatrical costume work has to track each client's head measurement and a specific design spec — which block or form was used, the trim and feather sourcing, and the exact color match to an outfit or costume — while managing a blocking-and-steaming cure time before a hat can safely be trimmed, and a seasonal rush concentrated hard around spring derby and wedding season or a theater production's opening date. A milliner running a home studio currently tracks head measurements, design specs, and event deadlines on a paper client card file and a phone photo roll, and a hat blocked to the wrong head measurement is a redo that blows a derby-week deadline, while a specific trim or feather color that runs out mid-rush with no reorder flagged is an order that can't be finished in time.

The tool maintains a per-client head-measurement and design-spec profile (block/form, trim sourcing, and event date) with a blocking/steaming cure-time tracker gating when trim work can start; a seasonal-rush order queue sorted by event date with a derby/wedding-season priority flag; and a trim/feather/material inventory tracker with reorder alerts ahead of the spring rush.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per milliner. Distribution: r/millinery and r/hatmaking practitioner threads, the Milliners Guild community, and derby-season and bridal-market vendor Facebook groups where independent hat makers already trade blocking-schedule and rush-order tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Basket Weaver & Fiber Artist Commission Spec, Material-Soak & Wholesale Account Tracker

An independent basket weaver selling handwoven baskets at craft fairs and through wholesale gallery and gift-shop accounts has to track each piece's specific weave pattern and material — reed, willow, pine needle, or a client-commissioned custom fiber — and a material soak or prep time that has to run its full course before weaving can begin without the fiber cracking, while managing a wholesale account roster with standing-order quantities and restock timing distinct from a one-off craft-fair sale. A weaver running a home studio currently tracks material soak schedules, commission specs, and wholesale restock dates on a paper order pad and a memory of which gallery still owes a check, and a basket started before its reed finished soaking is a piece ruined mid-project, while a wholesale gallery's restock order missed because nobody flagged the due date is a consignment relationship strained right before the holiday craft-fair season.

The tool maintains a per-commission project spec (pattern, material, and client dimensions) tied to a material soak/prep-time tracker gating when weaving can start; a wholesale/gallery account roster with standing-order quantities and restock-due dates; and a raw-material (reed, willow, dye) inventory tracker with reorder alerts ahead of the fall craft-fair rush.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per weaver. Distribution: r/basketry and r/weaving practitioner threads, the National Basketry Organization (NBO) member directory, and regional fiber-arts guild Facebook groups where independent weavers already trade soak-schedule and wholesale-account workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Kayak, Canoe & Paddleboard Rental Operator Fleet Status & Return-Condition Tracker

An independent waterfront shop renting kayaks, canoes, and stand-up paddleboards by the hour or day has to track which numbered unit is out, to which renter, and when it's due back, since a shop running dozens of units on a busy summer weekend can't rely on memory to know which kayak is overdue versus which one is back on the rack and ready for the next customer, while every returned unit needs a quick condition check — hull cracks, a missing paddle, worn PFD straps — logged before it goes back out, since a damaged unit rented to the next customer is a liability incident waiting to happen. A shop running a fleet across a single dock or a few seasonal locations currently tracks unit status and condition on a paper sign-out sheet and a whiteboard, and a unit that never gets marked "returned" because the sign-out sheet got busy on a packed Saturday is a rental the shop can't offer the next customer in line even though it's sitting back on the rack, while a cracked hull nobody flagged from the last renter's damage report is a safety incident the next renter walks straight into.

The tool maintains a per-unit fleet status board (out, available, needs-repair) with renter name and due-back time; a per-return condition-check log (hull, paddle, PFD) flagging any unit that needs repair before its next rental; and a seasonal-demand booking calendar for reserving popular unit types ahead of a busy weekend.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per shop. Distribution: r/kayaking and r/paddleboarding rental-shop threads, the America Outdoors Association member network, and regional outfitter and marina Facebook groups where independent rental operators already trade fleet-tracking and turnaround workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Bookbinder & Book Restoration Specialist Intake Condition, Treatment-Plan & Materials-Match Tracker

An independent bookbinder repairing and restoring antique books, family bibles, and hand-bound journals for private collectors has to document each book's condition on intake — a broken spine, foxed pages, detached boards — with photos and notes before any treatment begins, since a before-treatment record is the reference point a client or a future appraiser will compare against, then track a specific multi-stage treatment plan (resewing, rebacking, leather or cloth matching, gilding) for a piece that may sit in the bindery for weeks. A binder running eight to twelve concurrent pieces at different treatment stages currently tracks intake condition, treatment plans, and material matches — leather grain, thread color, endpaper pattern — on a mix of a camera roll and a paper studio log, and a book returned to a client without a clear before/after condition record is a binder with no documentation if a client later disputes what was done, while a leather or thread color that isn't recorded precisely is a binder who can't match a future companion volume from the same collection years later.

The tool maintains a per-book intake condition report (photos, damage notes, and provenance) with a treatment-plan and materials-match log tracked through each bindery stage; a before/after photo comparison generated automatically for client handoff; and a client/collection history for collectors who bring in multiple volumes over time.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per bindery. Distribution: r/bookbinding and r/BookRestoration practitioner threads, the Guild of Book Workers member directory, and independent bindery Facebook groups where restorers already trade condition-reporting and materials-match workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Bonsai Nursery & Bonsai Tree Care Specialist Species Schedule & Client-Maintenance Tracker

An independent bonsai artist selling trees and offering client house-call maintenance or a "boarding" service for a client's tree while they travel has to track each tree's species-driven repotting interval — a deciduous bonsai might need repotting every two years, a conifer every three to five — a wiring and styling schedule where wire left on too long scars the bark, and a watering/fertilizing regimen that varies by species and season. An artist running a nursery of several dozen trees across multiple client-maintenance contracts currently tracks repotting due-dates, wire-removal deadlines, and each client tree's specific care history on a handwritten tag tied to the pot and a paper logbook, and a tree left wired too long because nobody flagged the removal date is permanent bark scarring that ruins months of styling work, while a client's boarded tree watered on the wrong schedule because its species-specific regimen wasn't on file is a tree that comes back stressed or dead.

The tool maintains a per-tree species profile (repotting interval, wiring/wire-removal schedule, and seasonal watering/fertilizing regimen) with an automatic due-date calculator; a client-maintenance and boarding tracker logging each visit's care against the tree's specific schedule; and a retail-inventory view distinguishing trees ready for sale from trees still in training.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per artist/nursery. Distribution: r/Bonsai and r/bonsai practitioner threads, the American Bonsai Society member directory, and regional bonsai club Facebook groups where growers already trade repotting-schedule and client-boarding workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
