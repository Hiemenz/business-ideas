# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-15 (Round 192)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a chocolatier, a kettle-corn vendor, a private yoga teacher, a mobile bartender, a fresh-pasta seller and a handmade-jewelry designer.

---

### 1. Chocolatier Batch, Mold & Holiday-Order Tracker

A small chocolatier has to track batches by flavor, mold and temper schedule, plus shelf life and holiday orders. Valentine's Day and the winter holidays bring large orders, and cocoa and packaging costs vary.

The tool schedules production backward from pickup dates, tracks batch cost per piece, logs stock by flavor and manages gift-box orders.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per chocolatier. Distribution: chocolate-maker communities, r/chocolate and craft-market groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to the holidays.

### 2. Kettle-Corn Vendor Event, Batch & Profit Tracker

A kettle-corn vendor at fairs and festivals has to track event fees, batches made, supplies and sales. Some events pay off and others do not, and supply runs mid-event cost sales.

The tool logs fees and sales per event, plans supply loads and batches, shows profit per event and flags events worth repeating.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per vendor. Distribution: food-vendor Facebook groups, r/foodtrucks and fair-vendor boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Private Yoga Teacher Client, Package & Class Tracker

A private yoga teacher with one-on-one clients and small groups has to track client goals, lesson packages, attendance and notes. Package renewals and cancelled sessions are easy to miss.

The tool tracks clients and sessions, counts package balance, sends renewal reminders and stores class sequences.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per teacher. Distribution: yoga-teacher communities, r/yoga and local studio networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Bartender Event, Menu & Stock Tracker

A mobile bartender for private events has to track bookings, guest counts, cocktail menus, stock and staff. Stock needs differ per event, and per-drink cost determines pricing.

The tool estimates stock by guest count and menu, tracks per-drink cost, manages deposits and sends a pre-event checklist.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per bartender. Distribution: event-staffing groups, r/bartenders and wedding-vendor networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Fresh-Pasta Seller Batch, Order & Delivery Tracker

A home-based pasta maker selling at markets and by subscription has to track batches, flours and fillings, orders and delivery days. Fresh pasta has a short shelf life, so production must match orders.

The tool schedules batches from pre-orders, tracks cost per pound, manages subscriptions and plans delivery routes.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per maker. Distribution: home-chef communities, r/Cooking and farmers-market vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Handmade-Jewelry Designer Piece, Material & Sales Tracker

A jewelry maker has to track pieces, material cost (metals, stones), time per piece, prices and sales by channel. Material prices move, so margins shrink without anyone noticing, and custom pieces need approvals.

The tool tracks pieces and materials, computes cost and margin, manages custom orders and shows best sellers by channel.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per designer. Distribution: jewelry-maker communities, r/jewelrymaking and Etsy seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
