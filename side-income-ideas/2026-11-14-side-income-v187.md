# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-14 (Round 187)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a custom-vinyl crafter, a bonsai grower, a longarm quilter, a cheesemaker, a pickleball coach and a resin artist.

---

### 1. Custom Vinyl Crafter Order, Design & Material Tracker

A person who sells custom vinyl decals, tumblers and shirts has to track orders, approved designs, material stock and shipping dates. Custom text changes late, and running out of a vinyl color stalls orders. Craft-fair seasons bring rushes.

The tool tracks orders with design proofs, material stock by color, cost per item and promised ship dates.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per crafter. Distribution: Cricut and craft-seller Facebook groups, r/cricut and Etsy seller forums. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Bonsai Grower Tree, Training & Sales Tracker

A bonsai grower has trees that take years of pruning, wiring and repotting, each on its own schedule. Forgetting a repotting window can set a tree back a year, and a sale needs the tree's history.

The tool tracks each tree's age, styling work, next task by season, photo timeline and sale price.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per grower. Distribution: bonsai clubs, r/bonsai and nursery-seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Longarm Quilter Quilt, Batting & Turnaround Tracker

A longarm quilter finishes quilts for customers and must track each quilt's size, pattern, thread, batting and drop-off date. Price depends on size and pattern density, and a backlog builds before holidays.

The tool quotes by size and density, tracks queue and thread used, and sends ready-for-pickup messages.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per quilter. Distribution: quilting guilds, r/quilting and longarm Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Artisan Cheesemaker Batch, Aging & Sales Tracker

A small cheesemaker has to track batches by milk source, make date, aging room position and ready date. Wheels age for weeks or months, and sales to markets and shops depend on what is ready.

The tool tracks batches and aging, logs yield and cost per wheel, and shows what will be ready in the next 30 days.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per maker. Distribution: farmstead-cheese groups, r/cheesemaking and market-vendor boards. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Pickleball Coach Student, Lesson & Package Tracker

A pickleball coach with private clients and clinics has to track student levels, lesson packages, court bookings and drill notes. Clinics need sign-up lists, and package renewals are easy to miss.

The tool tracks students and levels, counts package lessons, manages clinic rosters and sends renewal reminders.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per coach. Distribution: pickleball Facebook groups, r/Pickleball and local club boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Resin Artist Commission, Cure & Shipping Tracker

A resin artist who makes tables, coasters and jewelry has to track pours, cure times, material cost and commission deadlines. A pour cannot be rushed, and a ruined pour wastes expensive material.

The tool tracks pours with cure countdowns, material cost per piece, commissions with deadlines and shipping.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per artist. Distribution: resin-art communities, r/resinart and Etsy seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
