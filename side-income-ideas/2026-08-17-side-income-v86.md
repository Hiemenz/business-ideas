### 1. Independent Wig Maker & Hair Extension Specialist Client Measurement, Unit-Inventory & Maintenance-Cycle Tracker

Independent wig makers and hair extension specialists who build custom and semi-custom units for medical, theatrical, and everyday clients need to track a per-client head-measurement and unit specification tracker capturing cap construction, density, color level, and length, since a specialist running 10-20 concurrent orders can't rely on memory to know which client's unit was specified with a lace-front cap versus a full-cap construction, and a unit built to the wrong specification is an expensive, mostly unfixable mistake on custom-ordered hair. The tool also needs a hair-unit inventory log tracked by texture, color, and origin, since human-hair wefts and bundles vary batch to batch the way stone or glass stock does, and a specialist matching a repeat client's existing unit or building a multi-piece order needs hair pulled from a compatible batch or the color and texture will visibly mismatch. It also needs an install-and-maintenance appointment cycle tracker, because a unit needs periodic re-tightening, root touch-up coloring, or re-ventilating on its own recurring schedule that varies by how often the client wears and washes the piece, and a specialist running a full client roster can't rely on memory to know which unit is coming due for maintenance this month. Most independent wig makers currently manage this across a paper order form and a bin of loose hair bundles, with no structured link between a specific client's original specification and the batch of hair actually used to build it.

The tool maintains a per-client head-measurement and unit specification tracker, a hair-unit inventory log organized by texture/color/origin batch, and an install-and-maintenance appointment cycle tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per specialist account. Distribution: r/wigs and r/HairExtensions threads, the International Alliance of Hair Restoration Specialists member directory, and medical/alopecia-focused wig-maker Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Per-Diem Event Medical & EMT Standby Coverage Provider Multi-Promoter Booking & Kit-Currency Tracker

Independent EMTs and paramedics who work per-diem standby medical coverage at concerts, sporting events, and film sets need to track a multi-promoter, multi-venue booking calendar, since a standby medic picking up shifts from several different event producers, sports leagues, and production companies in a busy season can't rely on memory to know which promoter booked them for Saturday's festival versus which production still needs Sunday confirmed, and a double-booked event date burns a relationship with whichever promoter loses the coverage. The tool also needs a certification and equipment-kit currency tracker, since every booking requires a current CPR/ACLS or PALS card and a fully stocked jump bag, and a medic who shows up to a film set or event with an expired certification card or a bag missing a key supply is turned away on the spot, losing the day's pay. It also needs a per-event billing tracker distinct from any clinical-practice invoice, because standby coverage typically bills a flat event-day or hourly rate rather than a per-patient charge, and a medic working a dozen events a month loses real income to a booking they forget to invoice. Most independent standby medics currently manage this across a group text with promoters and a mental tally of what's stocked in the bag, with no structured link between a specific event's booking and the medic's own certification and kit readiness.

The tool maintains a multi-promoter/venue booking calendar, a certification and equipment-kit currency tracker, and a per-event billing tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per provider account. Distribution: r/ems and r/NewToEMS threads, the National Association of Emergency Medical Technicians (NAEMT) member directory, and per-diem event-medic Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Freelance Landscape Designer Site-Plan, Plant-Palette & Installer-Handoff Tracker

Independent landscape designers who create planting plans and hardscape layouts for residential and small commercial clients — without doing the installation themselves — need to track a per-project site-plan and design-revision tracker, since a designer running 6-10 concurrent projects can't rely on memory to know which client's plan is still on revision two versus which one was finally approved, and handing an installer an outdated plan version is a costly on-site mistake once plants and materials are already ordered. The tool also needs a plant-palette and material-specification list per project, because a planting plan has to name exact species, sizes, and quantities along with hardscape material specifications that must stay consistent from the approved drawing through whichever installation contractor the client ultimately hires, and a designer who can't quickly pull up a project's finalized palette risks a substitution slipping through that changes the design's intended look. It also needs an install-contractor handoff and site-visit tracker, since a designer's reputation depends on the finished installation actually matching the approved plan, and a designer managing several projects moving into installation at once needs a clear record of which site visits are scheduled to confirm the installer's work against the design intent. Most independent landscape designers currently manage this across a folder of PDF plan revisions and a mental sense of which project is installation-ready, with no structured link between a specific project's approved palette and the installer who's now building from it.

The tool maintains a per-project site-plan/design-revision tracker, a plant-palette and material-specification list, and an install-contractor handoff/site-visit tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per designer account. Distribution: r/landscaping and r/gardendesign threads, the Association of Professional Landscape Designers (APLD) member directory, and regional landscape-design guild meetups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Custom Cabinetmaker & Millwork Shop Cut-List, Finish-Schedule & Install Punch-List Tracker

Independent cabinetmakers and millwork shop owners who build kitchens, built-ins, and custom casework need to track a per-job cut-list and material-yield tracker, since a shop running several kitchens and built-ins at once can't rely on memory to know whether a specific job's actual sheet-good and hardwood usage is tracking against the quoted material estimate, and a pattern of yield running over the quoted material quietly erodes margin across a busy season before anyone notices. The tool also needs a finish-schedule tracker keyed to each job's stain, paint, or clear-coat system and its cure time, because a shop running multiple jobs through the finish room at once needs a clear view of which pieces are safe to handle or deliver today versus which are still curing, and moving a piece before it's cured risks a finish defect that becomes a costly redo. It also needs an install punch-list tracker for the on-site fitting and adjustment phase, since cabinetry and built-ins require a final on-site install with its own list of adjustments — scribe fitting, hardware alignment, filler pieces — that a standalone furniture commission never requires, and a shop juggling several installs in a week needs a clear record of which punch-list items are still open on which job. Most independent cabinetmakers currently manage this across a paper shop-drawing set and a mental tally of what's left to install, with no structured link between a specific job's cut-list and its actual material yield.

The tool maintains a per-job cut-list/material-yield tracker, a finish-schedule tracker keyed to cure times, and an install punch-list tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per shop account. Distribution: r/cabinetry and r/woodworking threads, the Cabinet Makers Association (CMA) member directory, and regional custom-cabinetry shop Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Eyelash Extension & Lash Lift Technician Lash-Map, Fill-Cycle & Adhesive-Batch Tracker

Independent eyelash extension and lash lift technicians who apply and maintain lash sets for a repeat client base need to track a per-client lash-map and patch-test record capturing curl, length, and diameter specification per eye along with any documented adhesive-sensitivity patch-test result, since a technician running 15-25 regular clients can't rely on memory to know which client is mapped for a natural, staggered set versus a dramatic volume look, and applying the wrong specification — or skipping a patch test for a client with a known sensitivity — is both a redo and a safety issue around the eyes. The tool also needs a fill-cycle scheduler tracking each client's 2-3 week refill window, because lash extensions shed naturally with the client's own lash-growth cycle and need maintenance on a tighter, more precise cadence than a general skincare regimen, and a technician running a full book loses real revenue when a client's fill window is missed and they book with someone else instead. It also needs an adhesive-batch and supply-inventory tracker, since lash adhesive has a short shelf life once opened and its bonding performance degrades well before the printed expiration date, and a technician applying a set with degraded adhesive risks a poor retention outcome that becomes a client complaint. Most independent lash technicians currently manage this across a paper client card and a mental sense of who's due for a fill, with no structured link between a specific client's lash-map and the adhesive batch actually used on their last visit.

The tool maintains a per-client lash-map/patch-test record, a fill-cycle scheduler tied to each client's refill window, and an adhesive-batch/supply-inventory tracker.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per technician account. Distribution: r/lasheseyelashextensions and r/Esthetics threads, the National Association of Lash Artists (NALA) member directory, and local lash-studio and booth-renter Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Freelance Architectural Drafter & CAD Production Specialist Multi-Firm Queue & Revision-Set Tracker

Independent architectural drafters who produce CAD and BIM production drawings as overflow capacity for several architecture and design-build firms need to track a multi-firm project queue, since a drafter taking on work from 5-8 different firms at once can't rely on memory to know which firm's floor-plan set is due back tomorrow versus which one just landed and hasn't been started, and a missed deliverable deadline on a permit-driven project schedule is a client relationship that rarely survives a second miss. The tool also needs a revision-set version tracker, because architectural plan sets iterate through multiple rounds as they move through a firm's internal review and then municipal permit review, and a drafter working from an outdated revision without realizing a firm issued a new set is a rework-generating mistake that erodes trust with the hiring firm. It also needs a per-deliverable, per-firm invoice tracker, since drafters typically bill per sheet, per revision round, or per hour depending on the firm and project, and a drafter juggling deliverables across several firms in a month loses real income to a completed sheet set they forget was never actually invoiced. Most independent drafters currently manage this across a shared file-transfer folder and a personal task list, with no structured link between a specific firm's current revision number and the deliverable the drafter is actively working from.

The tool maintains a multi-firm project queue, a revision-set version tracker, and a per-deliverable/per-firm invoice tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per drafter account. Distribution: r/architecture and r/AutoCAD threads, the American Design Drafting Association (ADDA) member directory, and freelance-CAD-production Facebook and LinkedIn groups where overflow drafting work is routinely subcontracted out by small firms. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
