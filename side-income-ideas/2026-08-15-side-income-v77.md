# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-15 (Session 77)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.

### 1. Independent Sports Official (Umpire/Referee) Assignment & Game-Fee Tracker

Independent umpires and referees who work games assigned through multiple assignors across different leagues and sports need to track a per-game assignment log capturing which assignor and league sent the game, the venue and report time, the fee owed, and mileage, since an official working 4-8 games a week across youth, high school, and adult-league assignments can't rely on memory for which of several assignor platforms (ArbiterSports, RefQuest, or a league's own spreadsheet) actually confirmed a specific game, and showing up to a game that was reassigned to another official — or missing one that wasn't — damages the official's standing with that assignor immediately. The tool also needs a no-show/cancellation history log protecting the official's own record when a game is weather-cancelled or a league cancels late, since assignors track no-show patterns closely when deciding who gets next season's better assignments, and an official without their own documented record of which cancellations were league-initiated has no way to dispute a mistaken mark against them, plus a multi-league dues and rules-clinic renewal tracker, because different leagues and state associations each require their own annual rules-clinic attendance or dues payment before an official is eligible to be assigned games that season. Most independent officials currently manage this across whatever assignor app each league uses plus a personal notebook, with no single structured view of every fee owed across every league.

The tool maintains a per-game assignment log with fee and mileage tracking, a no-show/cancellation history protecting the official's assignor standing, and a multi-league dues/rules-clinic renewal tracker.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per official account. Distribution: r/Officiating and r/Umpires threads, the National Association of Sports Officials (NASO) member directory, and state high school athletic association officials' registries. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Independent Property Preservation & REO Trashout Contractor Work Order & Photo Documentation Tracker

Independent contractors hired by national vendor-management companies to secure, winterize, and maintain foreclosed properties on behalf of mortgage servicers need to track a per-work-order, time-stamped before/after photo set matching the specific investor guideline attached to that property — Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, and HUD each require different photo angles and counts — since a contractor running 15-30 concurrent properties can't rely on memory for which investor's photo checklist applies to which address, and submitting a work order with the wrong investor's photo set is exactly what triggers a payment chargeback. The tool also needs a per-property recurring-maintenance-cycle tracker for lawn cuts, debris removal, and winterization deadlines that repeat on a set interval through the season, since a missed recurring cut on a single property can trigger a penalty from the vendor-management company, and a contractor managing a growing portfolio loses track of which properties are due this week without a structured schedule, plus a multi-vendor-management-company invoice reconciliation tracker, because a contractor working for several national vendor-management companies (such as Safeguard Properties, Cyprexx, or MCS) simultaneously deals with different pay schedules and different photo-based chargeback rules for each one. Most independent property preservation contractors currently manage this across a phone camera roll and a spreadsheet with no structured link between a specific work order's photos and the investor guideline it was submitted against.

The tool maintains a per-work-order photo-documentation tracker matched to investor guidelines, a per-property recurring-maintenance-cycle schedule, and a multi-vendor-management-company invoice reconciliation log.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per contractor account. Distribution: property preservation contractor Facebook and Discord groups, the National Association of Mortgage Field Services (NAMFS) member directory, and REO/field-services trade forums. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Mobile Heavy-Duty & Diesel Truck Mechanic Fleet Client & Repair-Ticket Tracker

Independent diesel mechanics who service commercial trucking fleets on-site — at a yard, a truck stop, or roadside — need to track a per-fleet-client repair-ticket and parts log spanning multiple trucks per account, since a mechanic servicing 6-10 fleet accounts can't rely on memory for which specific truck in a client's fleet had which repair last month when the same fault code shows up again, and re-diagnosing a problem the mechanic already fixed once wastes billable time and looks unprofessional to the fleet dispatcher. The tool also needs a road-call/breakdown response log distinguishing scheduled shop-style work from emergency roadside repairs, since roadside calls typically carry a different rate and urgency than scheduled maintenance, and a mechanic without a structured log of which jobs were emergency calls can't accurately invoice the premium rate those calls justify, plus a per-truck service-history record a fleet's dispatcher can reference when planning which unit is due for downtime next, because a fleet client relying on one independent mechanic across a dozen trucks needs to know a specific unit's repair history to plan routes around it. Most independent diesel mechanics currently manage this across a paper work-order pad and memory, with no structured per-truck history a fleet client can be handed on request.

The tool maintains a per-fleet-client, per-truck repair-ticket and parts tracker, a road-call/breakdown response log, and a per-truck service-history export for fleet dispatchers.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per mechanic account. Distribution: r/Diesel and r/Truckers threads, the American Trucking Associations' Technology & Maintenance Council (TMC) network, and TruckersReport forum classifieds. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Medical Scribe Multi-Clinic Shift & Billing Tracker

Independent medical scribes who contract directly with multiple small clinics and urgent cares — rather than working through a scribe staffing company — need to track a multi-clinic shift-scheduling calendar preventing double-booking across accounts, since a scribe covering 3-5 clinics with different open-hours and staffing needs can't rely on memory alone to avoid accepting two overlapping shifts, and a double-booked shift means one clinic is left without scribe coverage with no notice. The tool also needs a per-clinic EHR-credential and training-completion tracker, since each clinic runs its own electronic health record system (Epic, Athenahealth, a smaller urgent-care-specific EHR) requiring its own login access and initial training before a scribe can work a shift there, and a scribe whose access was revoked after a staff change or system update discovers it only when arriving for a shift, plus a per-clinic hours and invoice log, because pay rates and invoicing cadence differ by clinic and shift type (weekday vs. weekend, physician vs. NP supervision), and reconciling actual hours worked against what each clinic pays out requires a clinic-by-clinic breakdown. Most independent scribes currently manage this across a personal calendar app and a mental tally of hours, with no structured link between a specific shift and the clinic-specific rate it should invoice against.

The tool maintains a multi-clinic shift-scheduling calendar, a per-clinic EHR-credential and training tracker, and a per-clinic hours/invoice log.

Monetization: $9-19/mo per scribe account. Distribution: r/scribes threads, the American College of Medical Scribe Specialists (ACMSS) community, and independent scribe Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Independent Land Surveyor Field Crew Chief Multi-Firm Job & Deliverable Tracker

Independent survey crew chiefs who contract their own field crew and equipment to multiple surveying and engineering firms on a per-job basis need to track a per-job field log capturing the site, the equipment checked out for that job, and any weather-delay days, since a crew chief running concurrent jobs for 3-6 different firms can't rely on memory for which firm's job is behind schedule due to a rain delay versus which one is simply waiting on the office to process the data, and an unexplained delay reported late damages the crew chief's standing with a contracting firm relying on that data for its own client deadline. The tool also needs a raw-field-data-to-CAD-deliverable handoff tracker per contracting firm, since each firm has its own format and delivery-method preference for receiving field data, and a crew chief unable to confirm whether a specific job's data was actually received and accepted by the firm risks a dispute over whether the deliverable was ever properly handed off, plus a multi-firm day-rate invoice tracker, because a crew chief working under different day-rate or per-job agreements with several firms simultaneously needs a clear per-firm accounting to invoice accurately. Most independent survey crew chiefs currently manage this across a field notebook and a folder of raw data files, with no structured link between a specific job's field log and the contracting firm's confirmed receipt of the deliverable.

The tool maintains a per-job field log with equipment and weather-delay tracking, a per-firm data-handoff and deliverable-acceptance tracker, and a multi-firm day-rate invoice log.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per crew-chief account. Distribution: r/Surveying threads, the National Society of Professional Surveyors (NSPS) member network, and state land surveying society job boards. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Independent Skip Tracer & Asset Locator Case Assignment & Fee Tracker

Independent skip tracers who work cases for repossession companies, process servers, collection agencies, and bail bond agencies need to track a per-case assignment log recording which of several client agencies sent the case, that agency's specific fee schedule (a flat per-case fee versus a contingency percentage on a located asset), and the case's target subject details, since a skip tracer working cases for 5-10 different agencies at once can't rely on memory for which agency's rate applies to which case, and misapplying one agency's fee structure to another agency's case is an invoicing error that erodes trust fast. The tool also needs a lead-verification log distinguishing a confirmed address or employer hit from an unverified one, since a skip tracer's reputation depends on not handing an agency a lead that turns out to be stale or wrong, and a structured record of which leads were actually verified before being reported protects the tracer when a client questions a bad lead later, plus a multi-client invoice tracker reconciling different pay structures and payment timing across agencies, because contingency-fee agencies typically pay only once an asset is recovered while flat-fee agencies pay on report delivery, and tracking which invoices are still pending payment against a recovery event requires a structured per-agency view. Most independent skip tracers currently manage this across a personal case notebook and memory of which agency pays what, with no structured link between a specific case's verified leads and the fee actually owed.

The tool maintains a per-case assignment log with client-specific fee schedules, a lead-verification log distinguishing confirmed from unverified hits, and a multi-client invoice tracker reconciling flat-fee and contingency payment timing.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per skip-tracer account. Distribution: r/BountyHunter threads, PInow.com's skip-tracer and investigator directory, and repossession/bail-industry trade forums. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
