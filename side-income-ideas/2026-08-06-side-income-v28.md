# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 28)

### 1. Music Lesson Studio Tuition Billing & Payment Tracking
Independent music lesson studios — piano, guitar, violin, voice — billing monthly tuition to 20-80 students have a recurring admin headache: tracking which families have paid, which are on autopay, which owe for make-up lessons or recital fees, and which students are on holds reducing their monthly charge. Most manage this in QuickBooks, Square, or a binder. This tool maintains a student roster with monthly tuition rate, payment method, any recurring discounts, and current balance; generates monthly invoices per student; records payments with date and method; and produces a monthly collections summary the studio owner can review in 5 minutes — who paid, who still owes, and the month's total revenue vs. expected. Optional: a payment reminder email draft for overdue accounts. Monetization: $18-28/mo per studio account. Distribution: Music Teachers National Association (MTNA) member communities, piano teacher Facebook groups, Jackrabbit Music and Studio Helper user forums. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Wedding Photographer Shot List & Day-of Timeline Generator
Wedding photographers spend 60-120 minutes per client building a custom shot list (family formals with all the combination groupings, bridal party portraits, detail shots) and a day-of timeline (ceremony start, cocktail hour, portrait blocks, reception events) — work that follows a consistent formula but requires gathering inputs from each couple and their venue. This tool accepts structured inputs (wedding party size and member list, family combination list from the couple, venue ceremony/reception times, portrait location, and golden hour time) and generates: a sequenced family formal shot list organized by family grouping for efficient execution, a bridal party portrait sequence, a day-of timeline with recommended time blocks per section and built-in buffer, and a vendor contact sheet combining all the inputs. Output is a shareable PDF the photographer can send to the couple and officiant for review. Monetization: $12-20/mo per photographer (unlimited weddings). Distribution: r/WeddingPhotography, Sprout Studio community, professional wedding photographer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Youth Swim Team Heat Sheet & Relay Lineup Builder
Swim team coaches preparing for dual meets and invitationals need to build heat sheets (assigning each swimmer to their event heats based on seed times, with faster swimmers in later heats) and relay lineups (selecting the 4-swimmer relay team for each relay event to optimize total relay time). This is currently done manually or in expensive meet management software like Meet Manager. This tool accepts a roster with each swimmer's best times by event, the meet's event list, and any swimmer conflicts (unavailable for certain events), and generates: a heat sheet with heat assignments in conformance with seeding rules, relay lineups optimized by total time for each relay event, and an athlete schedule showing each swimmer which events they're in and in what order. Coaches get a complete meet preparation package in minutes instead of hours. Monetization: $10-20/mo per team or $25-40/meet for coaches without a monthly subscription. Distribution: USA Swimming club director communities, SwimCloud user communities, swim team parent volunteer coordinator groups, r/Swimming. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Massage Therapist Client Intake Form & SOAP Note Generator
Licensed massage therapists in private practice or working independently need to maintain client intake forms (health history, contraindications, areas of concern) and SOAP notes for each session (Subjective — client-reported complaints; Objective — therapist observations and techniques used; Assessment — outcomes; Plan — next session recommendations). This is required for insurance billing, liability protection, and continuity of care. Most mobile therapists use paper forms or generic apps not designed for massage therapy. This tool maintains a client health profile with intake history, and after each session the therapist fills a quick structured form (areas worked, techniques used, client-reported outcomes, and next session plan) that generates a formatted SOAP note as a PDF stored in the client's record. Optional: the intake form generates a pre-session summary the therapist reviews before each client arrives. Monetization: $12-20/mo per therapist account (unlimited clients). Distribution: AMTA (American Massage Therapy Association) member communities, r/MassageTherapists, massage therapy school alumni networks, MindBody massage therapist communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Landlord Rent Ledger & Late Fee Calculator
Small landlords with 2-20 rental units tracking rent payments in a spreadsheet lose track of partial payments, late fee accumulation, and which tenant's balance is growing over multiple months of partial non-payment — especially when tenants pay different amounts at different times. This tool maintains a per-unit rent ledger: expected monthly rent, payment entries with date and amount, running balance, and automatic late fee calculation when a payment is recorded after the lease's due date (configurable: flat late fee or per-day fee per the lease terms). A monthly landlord summary shows: every unit's current balance, total expected rent, total collected, outstanding balance, and any units with growing delinquency. Optional: a tenant-facing balance statement PDF for sending to tenants who dispute their balance. Monetization: $10-18/mo per landlord account (up to 25 units). Distribution: r/Landlord, BiggerPockets small landlord communities, RentRedi and TenantCloud user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. Independent Bookstore New Arrival Shelf Talker Generator
Independent bookstores that merchandise with staff recommendation cards — shelf talkers that tell customers "why we love this book" in 40-60 words — produce them inconsistently: some books get beautiful cards, most get none because writing them is time-consuming. This tool lets a bookseller enter the book title, author, a few notes about why they recommend it, and who it's perfect for (beach readers, fans of X, people who liked Y), and generates 2-3 shelf talker draft options in different tones (enthusiastic, literary, accessible) that the bookseller picks from, edits lightly, and prints. Optionally generates a social media caption version of the same recommendation. Monetization: $12-18/mo per store account. Distribution: American Booksellers Association (ABA) IndieCommerce community, independent bookstore owner Facebook groups, r/bookstore, Edelweiss catalog user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
