## Who this is for

### 1. Mobile Car Detailing Technician Service-Tier History & Product-Inventory Tracker

Independent mobile car detailing technicians who wash, polish, paint-correct, and apply ceramic coatings at customers' homes and workplaces need to track a per-vehicle detail-package history recording which service tier was performed (basic wash, paint correction, multi-year ceramic coating) and the coating's expected reapplication or maintenance-wash timeline, since a technician juggling 15-25 recurring clients alongside one-off bookings can't rely on memory to know which customer's ceramic coating is approaching its recommended maintenance-wash interval versus which one just had a full correction done last month. The tool also needs a product and supply inventory tracked by product line and bottle/gallon count, since a technician stocking specific coating chemistries, polishing compounds, and microfiber stock needs to know what's on hand before confirming a same-week paint-correction appointment that could use a full bottle of a specific coating product. It also needs a mobile route board spanning driveway and workplace-lot appointments, since a technician running multiple jobs a day needs an efficient route rather than crisscrossing a service area between bookings. Most independent mobile detailers currently manage this across a paper job ticket and a rough memory of which client got which coating product, with no structured link between a specific vehicle's coating history and its actual maintenance-due date.

The tool maintains a per-vehicle detail-package history (service tier, product used, coating cure/reapplication timeline) tied to a client address, a product/supply inventory tracked by line and quantity, and a mobile route board for the day's scheduled jobs.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/AutoDetailing and r/CarDetailing, the International Detailing Association (IDA) member directory, and local car-club and dealership referral relationships (dealerships routinely refer buyers wanting a post-purchase ceramic coating). Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Independent Seamstress & Tailor Fitting-Round & Client-Measurement Tracker

Independent seamstresses and tailors handling garment alterations, custom suit and dress fittings, and hemming work for individual clients need to track a per-garment fitting-round status — measure, pin, first fitting, final alterations, ready for pickup — since a wedding dress or a custom suit typically requires two to three in-person fitting appointments spaced weeks apart, and a tailor juggling 10-15 concurrent garments can't rely on memory to know which dress is still waiting on a second fitting versus which suit is ready for pickup. The tool also needs a per-client body-measurement profile, since a repeat client returning for a new alteration or a seasonal wardrobe refresh expects the tailor to already have their measurements on file rather than re-measuring from scratch on every visit. It also needs a due-date tracker tied to each garment's event date (wedding, formal event, work deadline), since a garment finished after its event date is worthless to the client regardless of how well it fits. Most independent tailors currently manage this across a paper tag pinned to each garment and an index card of client measurements, with no structured link between a specific garment's fitting-round status and the event date it's racing against.

The tool maintains a per-garment fitting-round tracker (measure, pin, first fitting, alterations, ready) with an event-date deadline, a per-client body-measurement profile for repeat alterations, and a pickup/delivery status log.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/sewing and r/tailoring, the Association of Sewing and Design Professionals (ASDP) member directory, and bridal-shop and formalwear-retailer referral relationships (shops routinely refer clients needing alteration work not done in-house). Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Freelance Wedding & Event Photographer Deliverable Pipeline & Usage-Rights Tracker

Independent wedding and event photographers shooting for individual clients and small event-planning accounts need to track a per-shoot deliverable pipeline — shot list confirmed, images culled, editing pass, gallery delivered — since a wedding shoot generates thousands of raw images that move through a distinct multi-stage post-production process, and a photographer juggling 4-8 concurrent weddings across a busy season can't rely on memory to know which couple's gallery is still in the editing queue versus which one has already been delivered. The tool also needs a per-client licensing and usage-rights tracker, since couples, corporate clients, and event planners negotiate different terms — personal print use versus a venue or vendor's right to use images in their own marketing — and a photographer who loses track of what rights were actually granted on a specific shoot risks a dispute when a vendor later reposts an image without permission. It also needs a shoot-day timeline and vendor-coordination log capturing ceremony start time, key vendor contacts, and must-have shot requests, since a missed key moment on wedding day can't be reshot. Most independent wedding photographers currently manage this across a folder of exported galleries and a mental note of which client granted which usage rights, with no structured link between a specific shoot's deliverable status and the licensing terms attached to it.

The tool maintains a per-shoot deliverable-pipeline tracker (shot list, culling, editing, gallery delivered), a per-client licensing/usage-rights log, and a shoot-day timeline with vendor-contact and must-have-shot notes.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/WeddingPhotography and r/photography, the Professional Photographers of America (PPA) member directory, and wedding-planner and venue-coordinator referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Professional Organizer Project-Stage & Product-Inventory Tracker

Independent professional organizers helping clients declutter and reorganize homes, offices, and garages need to track a per-client project-stage tracker — initial assessment, sort/purge, categorize, container/label, styled — since a single closet, kitchen, or home-office project typically spans several multi-hour sessions over a few weeks, and an organizer juggling 5-8 concurrent client projects can't rely on memory to know which client's pantry is still mid-purge versus which is ready for final container styling. The tool also needs a product and bin inventory tracked by size, category, and supplier, since organizers frequently purchase and install specific storage bins, labels, and shelving as part of a project and need to know what's on hand or on order before a scheduled install session. It also needs a before/after photo log per space, since organizers rely on documented transformations both to show a client the project's progress and to build a portfolio for marketing future work. Most independent professional organizers currently manage this across a phone full of undated photos and a paper project checklist, with no structured link between a specific client's project stage and the products actually ordered for it.

The tool maintains a per-client project-stage tracker (assessment, sort/purge, categorize, container/label, styled), a product/bin inventory by size/category/supplier, and a before/after photo log per space.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/organization and the National Association of Productivity and Organizing Professionals (NAPO) member directory, plus local interior-designer and real-estate-agent referral relationships (agents routinely refer sellers needing pre-listing decluttering). Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Pressure Washing Technician Surface-History & Seasonal-Rush Route Tracker

Independent mobile pressure washing technicians cleaning driveways, siding, decks, and commercial storefronts need to track a per-property service-history record noting surface type, chemical treatment applied, and before/after photos, since a technician servicing 100-200 residential and commercial accounts across a service area can't rely on memory to know which driveway just had a full soft-wash treatment versus which one is overdue for its annual algae/mildew cleaning. The tool also needs a chemical and equipment inventory sized to the week's scheduled jobs, since specific surfaces (composite decking, painted siding, natural stone) require specific chemical dilutions and a technician arriving without the right treatment wastes a trip. It also needs a seasonal-rush route board, since pressure washing demand spikes hard in spring and early summer as algae and mildew buildup becomes visible after winter, and a technician without a structured route plan ends up backtracking across the same neighborhoods during the year's busiest weeks. Most independent pressure washing technicians currently manage this across a paper job list and a rough memory of which property needs which chemical treatment, with no structured link between a specific property's surface history and its actual next-service-due date.

The tool maintains a per-property service-history record (surface type, chemical treatment, before/after photos) tied to a client address, a chemical/equipment inventory sized to scheduled jobs, and a seasonal-rush route board prioritizing spring/early-summer bookings by neighborhood.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/PowerWashing and local exterior-cleaning Facebook groups, the Power Washers of North America (PWNA) member directory, and property-management-company referral relationships for recurring commercial storefront accounts. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Independent Custom Picture Framer Proof-Approval & Moulding-Inventory Tracker

Independent custom picture framers producing framed artwork, shadow boxes, and gallery-wall commissions for individual clients need to track a per-order design-proof approval cycle, since almost every custom order — a mat-color and frame-profile selection, a glazing-type choice for a valuable print — requires the customer to sign off on a proof before any moulding is cut, and a framer who cuts before final approval risks wasting an expensive moulding length on a design the customer wanted changed. The tool also needs a frame-moulding and mat-board inventory tracked by profile, finish, and length or sheet size, since a shop stocking dozens of moulding profiles and mat colors needs to know what's on hand before quoting a job that specifies an exact match to a client's existing framed piece. It also needs a production queue spanning quick same-week shadow-box or print-framing orders alongside larger multi-piece gallery-wall commissions that take weeks, since a framer juggling both order types needs to see which quick jobs can be worked in between longer commissions rather than letting small orders stall behind a big one. Most independent custom framers currently manage this across a paper order form and a rough visual check of the moulding rack, with no structured link between a specific order's proof-approval status and whether it's actually cleared to cut.

The tool maintains a per-order proof-approval tracker gating cutting until the customer signs off, a frame-moulding/mat-board inventory by profile, finish, and length/sheet size, and a production queue spanning quick orders and longer gallery-wall commissions.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/framing and r/ArtistLounge, the Professional Picture Framers Association (PPFA) member directory, and local art-gallery and interior-designer referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
