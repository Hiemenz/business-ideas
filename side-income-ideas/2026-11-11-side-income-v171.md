# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-11 (Round 171)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a wedding day-of coordinator, a photo booth operator, a balloon and event decor artist, a freelance translator, a house sitter and a kids' party entertainer.

---

### 1. Wedding Day-Of Coordinator Timeline, Vendor Contact & Final-Payment Tracker

A day-of coordinator who steps in for the last 4-6 weeks has to take over the couple's vendor list, timeline and final payments. With 15-25 weddings a year, each with a dozen vendors, they need every vendor's arrival time, contact number and final-balance status in one place. A missed vendor call on the day is the failure the couple remembers.

The tool stores per-wedding vendor lists with arrival times and contacts, builds a minute-by-minute timeline and tracks which vendors are paid and confirmed 7 days out.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per coordinator. Distribution: wedding-professional Facebook groups, r/weddingplanning vendor threads and local planner networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Photo Booth Rental Operator Booking, Equipment & Print-Supply Tracker

A photo booth operator with 2-4 booths has to track bookings, which booth and props go to which event, print paper and ink stock, and setup/teardown times. Double-booking a booth or running out of print paper at an event is a refund. Deposits and balances from many events need tracking.

The tool tracks event bookings with booth assignment and travel time, supply stock with a low-stock alert, and a deposit and balance tracker.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per operator. Distribution: photo-booth operator groups, r/weddingplanning vendor circles and event-rental Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Balloon & Event Decor Artist Order, Materials & Install-Schedule Tracker

A balloon and event decor artist builds arches, garlands and backdrops, and needs to track each order's design, materials (balloon colors, frames, flowers), install time and pickup/teardown. With several jobs in a weekend, materials are miscounted and an install runs late. Quotes depend on materials cost and labor hours that are often guessed.

The tool tracks orders with design photos, a materials list generated from the design, an install and pickup schedule and per-job cost versus price.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per artist. Distribution: balloon-artist communities, r/eventplanning and decor-vendor Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Freelance Translator Project, Word-Count & Deadline Tracker

A freelance translator working with agencies and direct clients has to track each project's word count, rate per word, deadline and invoice status. Several jobs with different agencies and varying rate cards make it easy to lose track of what is owed. Repeat terminology and client glossaries also need to be kept.

The tool logs projects with word count, rate and due date, calculates invoice totals, tracks payment status per agency and keeps a glossary per client.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per translator. Distribution: translator communities (ProZ, r/TranslationStudies) and language-service Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. House Sitter Booking, Home-Instructions & Check-In Tracker

A professional house and pet sitter has to track each home's dates, instructions (alarm code process, pet feeding, plant watering), key handoff and owner updates. With back-to-back bookings, it is easy to confuse one home's instructions with another's, and owners expect daily photo updates.

The tool stores per-home instruction sheets, tracks booking dates and key custody, logs daily check-ins with photos and handles deposits and final payments.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per sitter. Distribution: house-sitting Facebook groups, r/housesitting and local pet-sitter networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Kids' Party Entertainer Booking, Character & Package Tracker

A kids' party entertainer (magician, character performer, face painter) has to track bookings, the child's name and age, the requested character or theme, travel distance and package add-ons. Parents often change the character a week out, and an entertainer with 10+ parties on a weekend cannot mix up costumes and props. Deposits and rebooking prompts for next year's birthday are forgotten.

The tool tracks bookings with child details and theme, a costume-and-props checklist per party, travel time between parties and a next-year birthday rebooking reminder.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per entertainer. Distribution: party-entertainer Facebook groups, r/entertainment performer threads and local parent groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
