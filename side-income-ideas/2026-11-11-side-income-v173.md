# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-11 (Round 173)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a KDP low-content publisher, an in-home dog boarder, a Shopify freelancer, a mobile bike mechanic, a sticker and stationery maker and a freelance transcriptionist.

---

### 1. KDP Low-Content Book Publisher Title, Keyword & Royalty Tracker

A publisher of planners, journals and puzzle books on Amazon KDP has to track dozens of titles: niche, keywords, launch date, price, sales and royalties. Without records, the publisher cannot see which niches earn and which should stop getting new titles. Cover and interior revisions are also easy to lose track of.

The tool tracks titles with niche, keywords, price and monthly royalties, shows earnings by niche and flags titles with no sales after 90 days.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per publisher. Distribution: KDP communities, r/KDP and self-publishing Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. In-Home Dog Boarder Booking, Dog Profile & Daily-Update Tracker

A person who boards dogs in their home has to track each dog's stay dates, feeding schedule, medications, behavior notes and owner contact. With 2-4 dogs at once and back-to-back bookings, they need to know who arrives and leaves when and who needs medication. Owners expect daily photo updates.

The tool stores dog profiles with feeding and medication notes, a calendar of stays with capacity warnings, daily update logs with photos and deposit and balance tracking.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per boarder. Distribution: pet-sitter Facebook groups, r/dogs pet-business threads and local dog communities. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Shopify Freelancer Client Task, Access & Retainer-Hours Tracker

A freelancer who customizes Shopify themes and apps for small stores has to track tasks per client, store access details, hours used against monthly retainers and pending client approvals. With 5-10 clients, requests arrive over email and chat and get lost. Clients dispute hours, so a log of tasks and time matters.

The tool tracks tasks with status and hours per client, stores access notes, compares retainer hours used versus purchased and generates a monthly hours summary.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per freelancer. Distribution: Shopify expert communities, r/shopify and freelancer Discords. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Bike Mechanic Job, Parts & Customer Follow-Up Tracker

A mobile bike mechanic who services bikes at customers' homes and offices has to track each job's repair list, parts ordered and customer's bike details. With parts arriving later, the mechanic needs to know who is waiting for which part and to book the second visit. Follow-up reminders (a tune-up after 6 months) bring repeat business.

The tool logs jobs by customer and bike, tracks parts-waiting status, schedules return visits and sends a 6-month tune-up reminder.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per mechanic. Distribution: cycling and bike-mechanic communities, r/bikemechanics and local cycling clubs. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Sticker & Stationery Maker Inventory, Order & Print-Batch Tracker

A small maker who sells stickers, notebooks and prints through Etsy and markets has to track inventory per design, print batches, materials and orders. Running out of a best-seller before a market is a lost sale, and ordering a print batch without knowing sales speed is guesswork. Margin per design is unclear without material costs.

The tool tracks stock by design with a reorder alert based on sales speed, records print batches with cost and shows margin per design and per channel.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per maker. Distribution: Etsy seller forums, r/etsy and craft-fair maker groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Freelance Transcriptionist Audio-Job, Turnaround & Pay Tracker

A freelance transcriptionist working for agencies and direct clients has to track each file's audio length, rate per audio minute, deadline and payment status. Several clients with different rates and turnaround tiers make it hard to prioritize. Without tracking audio minutes per week, the transcriptionist cannot see their effective hourly rate.

The tool logs files with audio minutes, rate and due date, orders the queue by deadline, tracks payment per client and calculates effective hourly pay.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per transcriptionist. Distribution: transcription communities, r/transcription and freelance-work Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
