# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-12 (Round 176)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: an audiobook narrator, a retro console refurbisher, a private dance instructor, a game coach, a backyard beekeeper selling honey and a youth sports official.

---

### 1. Audiobook Narrator Title, Recording & Royalty-Share Tracker

An audiobook narrator takes projects from authors and publishers, paid per finished hour or on a royalty share. They have to track each title's chapters recorded, edits pending, finished hours delivered and payment. A narrator with 4-8 titles in production can lose track of which chapters are recorded and which are waiting for corrections. Royalty-share income arrives over months from different platforms.

The tool tracks titles by chapter status, logs finished hours against rate, records royalty payments by platform and shows income per title.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per narrator. Distribution: ACX and narrator communities, r/audiobooks narrator threads and voice-acting Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Retro Console Refurbisher Unit, Parts & Resale Tracker

A person who buys broken retro game consoles, repairs them and resells them has to track each unit: purchase price, fault, parts used, time spent and sale price. Without it, they cannot see which consoles earn a margin after parts and labor. Several units in progress at once need stage tracking.

The tool tracks each unit from bought to listed to sold, records parts and hours and shows margin and margin per hour by console type.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per refurbisher. Distribution: retro-gaming communities, r/consolerepair and eBay seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Private Dance Instructor Student, Lesson & Package Tracker

A private dance instructor (ballroom, wedding first dance, kids' classes) has to track each student's level, choreography progress, lesson packages and upcoming events. A wedding first-dance client has a fixed date and a set of lessons to fit before it. Students buy packages and the instructor must know the balance.

The tool tracks students with choreography notes, package balances with renewal prompts and a countdown to each client's event date.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per instructor. Distribution: dance-instructor Facebook groups, r/ballroom and wedding-vendor networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Video Game Coach Client Session, Goal & Replay-Review Tracker

A paid coach for competitive games (Valorant, League, chess) has to track each client's goals, rank progress, replays reviewed and homework. With 10-20 clients, the coach needs to recall each player's weaknesses and what was assigned last time. Clients judge value by rank gains, so progress needs a record.

The tool stores per-client goals and weaknesses, session notes and replay-review links, assigned homework and rank history charts.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per coach. Distribution: game-coaching Discords, r/ValorantCoaching and esports communities. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Backyard Beekeeper Hive, Harvest & Honey-Sales Tracker

A beekeeper who sells honey at markets and online has to track each hive's health inspections, harvest dates, yields and honey jar inventory. With 10-30 hives, it is hard to tell which hives produce and which need attention. Jar sales across markets and online orders need a stock count.

The tool logs hive inspections with notes, records harvest yields per hive, tracks jar inventory and sales by channel and shows yield per hive.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per beekeeper. Distribution: beekeeping clubs, r/Beekeeping and farmers-market vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Youth Sports Official Game Schedule, Mileage & Payment Tracker

A freelance referee or umpire working youth and rec-league games has to track assigned games, fees, mileage and payment status from several leagues. With 10-25 games a month, double-booking and missed payments are common, and leagues pay on different schedules. Mileage and cancellations affect actual earnings.

The tool tracks games with league, fee and travel distance, flags schedule conflicts, shows unpaid games by league and totals earnings and mileage.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per official. Distribution: referee and umpire Facebook groups, r/Referees and local league networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
