# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-16 (Round 195)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a babysitter, a shellfish seller, a nanny-share organizer, a scrapbook and memory-book maker, a photo restorer and a college move-out hauler.

---

### 1. Babysitter Family, Schedule & Rate Tracker

An independent babysitter with several regular families has to track schedules, rates, kids' notes, allergies and payment. Last-minute requests overlap, and holiday dates book early.

The tool tracks families and notes, shows a schedule with conflicts, logs hours and payments and sends a holiday-availability request.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per sitter. Distribution: local parent groups, Facebook neighborhood groups and r/Parenting. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Shellfish Seller Harvest, Order & Pickup Tracker

A small oyster or clam grower has to track harvest dates, counts per order, tide-based schedules and customer pickups. Pre-orders must match what can be harvested, and cold storage time is short.

The tool tracks harvests and orders, schedules pickups by tide and storage, and shows revenue per harvest.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per grower. Distribution: shellfish-grower groups, r/aquaculture and local market vendor boards. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Nanny-Share Organizer Family, Schedule & Cost-Split Tracker

A person who organizes a nanny share between families has to track schedules, cost splits, caregiver pay and sick-day rules. Money questions cause friction, so clear records matter.

The tool tracks families and days, splits cost by hours, logs payments and records sick-day swaps.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per organizer. Distribution: parent communities, local mom groups and r/NewParents. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Memory-Book Maker Project, Photo & Proof Tracker

A person who makes scrapbooks and memory books for families has to track projects, photos received, page layouts, proof approvals and delivery dates. Gift deadlines (holidays, anniversaries) are firm, and families send photos late.

The tool tracks projects with photo counts, manages proofs and approvals, counts down to the gift date and tracks deposits.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per maker. Distribution: scrapbooking communities, r/scrapbooking and local craft-fair groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to the holidays.

### 5. Photo Restorer Job, Scan & Delivery Tracker

A person who restores and digitizes old photos has to track each family's batch, scan quality, edits done and delivery. Customers hand over irreplaceable originals, so a clear record of what was received matters.

The tool logs intake with counts and photos, tracks edits per image, sends proofs and records return of originals.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per restorer. Distribution: photo-restoration communities, r/estoration and genealogy groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. College Move-Out Hauler Booking, Load & Storage Tracker

A student or local hauler who helps with college move-outs has to track bookings, dorm or apartment locations, load size, summer storage and pickup dates. Everything happens in the same two weeks in May.

The tool tracks bookings by time slot, estimates load size, manages storage and schedules the fall delivery.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per hauler. Distribution: campus Facebook groups, local student boards and r/college. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to May.
