# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 24)

### 1. Horse Boarding Stable Monthly Billing & Care Log Generator
Equine boarding stable owners charge monthly board plus a variable list of add-on services that differ horse by horse each month: farrier visits, deworming, vaccinations, extra hay bales, blanketing fees, arena rental, training sessions, and medication administration. Tracking these add-ons per horse and generating an accurate monthly invoice is done in notebook ledgers or spreadsheets that get difficult to reconcile when a horse's care log changes mid-month. This tool maintains a horse roster with each horse's monthly board rate, a daily care log entry per horse (services rendered, product used, quantity), and automatically generates an itemized monthly invoice for each owner at month-end: base board plus each line-item add-on with date, service description, and rate. Owners receive a PDF invoice via email; the barn owner has a per-horse care history for any disputes. Monetization: $20-35/mo per stable account (unlimited horses). Distribution: equine stable owner Facebook groups, horse boarding facility associations, r/Equestrian, barn management software communities (Equo, Equine Genie). Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Notary Mobile Appointment Scheduler & Notarial Journal
Mobile notaries — primarily serving real estate loan closings (RON and in-person signings) and general notarizations — juggle appointment requests from multiple signing services simultaneously, calculate drive time between appointments to avoid double-booking, log mileage for tax deductions, and in most states must maintain a notarial journal recording every act performed (date, type of notarization, signer name, ID type and number, fee charged). This tool provides a calendar interface for scheduling signing appointments with location, estimated drive time from the previous appointment, and signing service contact info; a mileage log that auto-calculates reimbursable miles between appointments using the day's appointment sequence; and a notarial journal that captures the required fields for each act performed (date, document type, signer information, ID type) and exports a compliant journal PDF. Monetization: $12-20/mo per notary. Distribution: Notary Rotary and 123notary signing agent communities, r/notary, NNA (National Notary Association) member communities, signing service recruiter boards. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Vacation Rental Guest Welcome Book & House Rules PDF Generator
Short-term rental hosts on Airbnb and VRBO are expected to provide guests with a digital or printed welcome guide covering: check-in/check-out procedures, Wi-Fi password, appliance instructions (smart TV, dishwasher, washer/dryer, thermostat), house rules (no smoking, quiet hours, pet policy, trash day), local restaurant and activity recommendations, emergency contacts, and parking instructions. Most hosts cobble this together in a Google Doc that is hard to format attractively, hard to update, and rarely includes all the information guests need — resulting in repeat questions and poor reviews for "unclear directions." This tool accepts the host's property details through a structured form and generates a branded, professionally formatted welcome book PDF with sections organized per the Airbnb/VRBO expectation: welcome message, property overview, check-in steps, appliance guides, house rules, and local recommendations. The PDF is shareable as a link before arrival and printable for the physical property. Monetization: $10-18/mo per host account (unlimited properties and updates). Distribution: r/airbnb, Airbnb host Facebook groups, STR investor communities, Hostfully and Touchstay user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Personal Trainer Client Progress Photo Comparison Organizer
Personal trainers who track client body composition progress through periodic progress photos face an organizational problem: photos from 8-12 clients taken at monthly intervals accumulate across text messages, email, and camera roll folders with no systematic before/after comparison tool. Manually creating a before/after side-by-side for each client — to show at check-in sessions or share with the client — takes 15-30 minutes of Lightroom or Canva work per client. This tool lets the trainer maintain a client profile with dated photo uploads, automatically generates a side-by-side comparison between any two dates (configurable by the trainer — first session vs. current, 30-day comparison, 90-day comparison), adds a branded overlay with the client's name and date range, and exports a shareable image or PDF for the check-in session. Trainers save setup time; clients get a cleaner, more professional progress presentation. Monetization: $15-25/mo per trainer account (unlimited clients). Distribution: r/personaltraining, NASM and ACE certified trainer communities, fitness business coaching Facebook groups, Trainerize and TrueCoach user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Children's Consignment Sale Pricing & Tag Generator
Twice-yearly children's consignment sales — community events run by PTAs, churches, and independent consignment organizers where parents sell outgrown children's clothing, gear, and toys — attract 200-600 seller participants who each tag 30-150 items with a seller number, item description, size, and price. The tagging process is entirely manual: sellers write tags by hand or use a generic word processor template, producing inconsistent tag formats and tags that fall off or become unreadable. Consignment sale management platforms like MyConsignment require sellers to learn a new system for a twice-annual event. This tool accepts the seller's item list (spreadsheet import or quick-entry form: description, size, price, condition), applies the sale's required tag format (which the sale organizer configures once), and generates a print-ready PDF of all the seller's tags with barcodes or QR codes for scan-based checkout — formatted to print on standard 3"x5" cardstock or Avery label sheets. Monetization: $3-5/seller per sale event (paid by the seller) or $50-150/event license for sale organizers to offer to all their sellers. Distribution: consignment sale organizer Facebook groups, JBF (Just Between Friends) franchise community, MOPS and PTA parent community networks. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. Small Distillery/Brewery Taproom Event RSVP & Capacity Manager
Craft brewery and distillery taprooms regularly host events — tap takeovers, whiskey tastings, live music nights, trivia nights, pairing dinners — that require managing RSVPs, collecting ticket payments, and tracking capacity limits that vary by event type (a seated pairing dinner has a hard 30-person cap; a general admission tap takeover has a 120-person fire-code limit). Eventbrite is overkill for a $10 trivia night and takes a service fee; Facebook Events has no capacity enforcement. This tool provides a lightweight event setup page (event name, date, ticket types with individual limits, total capacity cap, payment optional), a shareable RSVP link for customers, a real-time capacity dashboard for the taproom manager showing tickets sold vs. remaining capacity by ticket type, a guest check-in list for the event door, and a post-event revenue summary. The operator keeps 100% of revenue; no platform fee. Monetization: $15-25/mo per taproom account (unlimited events). Distribution: Brewers Association member forums, craft distillery association communities, taproom manager Facebook groups, r/TheBrewery. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
