# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-31 (Round 157)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow trackers for six operator types not yet covered in this repo: an independent freight dispatcher, a freelance Amazon PPC/ads manager, an independent catastrophe/field insurance adjuster, an independent contract mortgage loan processor, a seasonal Christmas tree lot retailer, and an independent last-mile delivery route contractor.

---

### 1. Independent Freight Dispatcher Load-Board, Rate & Multi-Driver Settlement Tracker

An independent freight dispatcher — hired by several owner-operator truckers to find loads, negotiate rates, and handle paperwork without ever touching a truck themselves — has to track each driver client's current location, available hours-of-service window, and preferred lane before searching load boards, since booking a load that puts a driver's remaining drive-time or a required reset in jeopardy is a mistake that costs the driver money and the dispatcher the client relationship. A dispatcher juggling 5-15 driver clients at once also has to track every booked load's negotiated rate-per-mile, broker contact, and pickup/delivery window across drivers who are each somewhere different on the map, since quoting a rate to one driver and forgetting to invoice the broker for a different driver's load is exactly the kind of mix-up that erodes a client's trust in the dispatcher's own bookkeeping. Because dispatchers are typically paid a flat percentage of each load's rate, a dispatcher also needs a clean per-driver settlement statement showing every load booked that pay period and the dispatcher's cut, since a driver who can't easily verify the math is a driver who starts shopping for a new dispatcher.

The tool maintains a per-driver profile (current location, hours-of-service window, and lane preferences) feeding into a load-booking log recording rate, broker, and pickup/delivery windows across all active drivers; a per-driver settlement statement calculating the dispatcher's percentage against that period's booked loads; and a broker contact and rate-history log so a dispatcher can quickly reference what a specific broker paid for a similar lane last time.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per dispatcher. Distribution: r/Truckers and r/FreightBrokers dispatcher-referral threads, and independent-dispatcher Facebook groups where dispatchers already trade load-board tactics and driver-onboarding tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Freelance Amazon PPC/Ads Manager Campaign, ACOS & Client-Budget Tracker

An independent Amazon PPC and advertising manager who runs sponsored-product and sponsored-brand campaigns for third-party sellers has to track each client account's campaign structure, daily ad spend against a client-approved monthly budget, and the ACOS (advertising cost of sale) and TACOS (total advertising cost of sale) trend per product, since a manager running 8-15 client accounts can't rely on memory for which client's campaign just crossed its budget cap versus which one has room to scale spend on a strong-performing keyword. A manager also has to log every bid adjustment, negative-keyword addition, and campaign-structure change made per client, since a client asking "why did my ad spend jump last week" needs a specific, dated answer rather than the manager reconstructing the change from Amazon's own reporting after the fact, and needs a simple way to flag which accounts are approaching a renewal or contract-review date so a strong ACOS trend can be turned into the retention conversation before a client considers switching to someone else.

The tool maintains a per-client account profile (monthly ad-spend budget, target ACOS/TACOS, and current campaign structure) with a daily spend-versus-budget tracker and an alert before a client's budget cap is reached; a change log recording every bid adjustment, negative-keyword addition, and structural change per client with a date and rationale; and a per-client contract-renewal and performance-summary tracker.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per manager. Distribution: r/AmazonSeller and r/PPC freelancer threads, and Amazon-seller-agency Facebook and Discord communities where independent PPC managers already source client referrals and trade optimization tactics. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent Catastrophe/Field Insurance Adjuster Deployment, Claim-File & Expense Tracker

An independent catastrophe (CAT) adjuster who deploys on short notice to hurricane, hail, or wildfire-affected regions on behalf of independent adjusting firms has to track which firm currently has them under a deployment contract, the assigned claim-file load for that catastrophe event, and each individual claim's inspection deadline and current file status, since an adjuster juggling 20-40 assigned files during a single deployment can't rely on memory for which homeowner still needs a callback versus which file is ready to submit to the carrier. An adjuster also has to log daily mileage, lodging, and per diem expenses against the deploying firm's reimbursement policy, since CAT adjusters travel constantly between deployments and a missed expense-submission window on a multi-week catastrophe deployment is real money left on the table, and needs a deployment-history record showing which firms they've worked for, at what daily or per-claim rate, and how quickly that firm actually paid out, since an adjuster choosing between two competing deployment offers needs their own payment-reliability history to decide.

The tool maintains a per-deployment claim-file tracker (assigned files, inspection deadlines, and current status per claim) with an at-a-glance overdue-inspection flag; a daily mileage, lodging, and per-diem expense log matched against the deploying firm's reimbursement policy; and a per-firm deployment-history record tracking rate, claim volume, and actual payment turnaround.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per adjuster. Distribution: the National Association of Independent Insurance Adjusters (NAIIA) member directory and r/InsuranceAdjusters and CAT-adjuster Facebook groups where independent adjusters already trade deployment offers and firm-reliability tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Independent Contract Mortgage Loan Processor Multi-Broker File & Condition-Deadline Tracker

An independent mortgage loan processor who works per-file for several small mortgage brokerages — assembling and verifying a borrower's documentation, ordering title and appraisal, and clearing underwriting conditions, distinct from the loan officer who originates the loan — has to track each active file's current stage (application, processing, underwriting, conditional approval, clear-to-close) and every outstanding underwriting condition's specific deadline, since a processor juggling 15-25 concurrent files across multiple brokerage clients can't rely on memory for which file's appraisal just came back versus which one is three days from missing its rate-lock expiration. A processor also has to track which brokerage each file belongs to and that brokerage's specific documentation-submission format and turnaround expectations, since brokers vary in how they want conditions cleared and submitted, and a processor who submits one broker's preferred format to a different broker's underwriter creates exactly the kind of rework that costs a broker relationship. Because processors are typically paid a flat per-file fee on closing, a processor also needs a simple per-brokerage invoice tracker showing which closed files have been paid versus which are still outstanding.

The tool maintains a per-file status tracker (stage, outstanding conditions, and rate-lock expiration) with an overdue-condition alert; a per-brokerage client profile recording documentation-format preferences and submission expectations; and a per-file, per-brokerage invoice tracker showing paid versus outstanding fees after closing.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per processor. Distribution: the National Association of Mortgage Processors (NAMP) member directory and r/MortgageProcessing and contract-processor Facebook groups where independent processors already source brokerage-client referrals. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Seasonal Christmas Tree Lot Retailer Perishable Inventory & Daily-Markdown Tracker

An independent seasonal Christmas tree lot operator who buys wholesale trees in bulk from a grower or wholesaler in November and resells them retail over a compressed four-to-six-week window has to track exactly how many trees of each species and height class remain in inventory against the daily sales pace, since a tree lot's entire inventory is a perishable asset with a hard end-of-season deadline — every unsold tree on December 26th is a total loss — and an operator running a lot with several hundred trees can't rely on a walk-the-lot headcount every evening to know whether they're on pace to sell through or sitting on excess inventory that needs a price cut. An operator also has to track daily cash reconciliation against a mix of cash, card, and occasional wholesale-to-landscaper bulk sales, since a seasonal lot often runs with rotating part-time staff across shifts and a till that doesn't reconcile at closing is money the operator has no way to trace back to a specific shift. As the season progresses toward Christmas, an operator needs a clear view of which price tier each remaining tree should move to, since a lot still pricing trees at opening-week rates a week before Christmas is a lot that ends the season with a lot full of unsold inventory instead of the markdown revenue it should have captured.

The tool maintains a per-species, per-height-class inventory count with a daily sell-through-pace calculation flagging trees at risk of going unsold by season's end; a daily cash/card/bulk-sale reconciliation log per shift; and a season-countdown markdown schedule recommending price-tier changes as Christmas approaches.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per lot (billed for the active season). Distribution: the National Christmas Tree Association's retailer resources and seasonal-tree-lot-operator Facebook groups where independent lot operators already trade wholesale-sourcing and pricing tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks (timed to the November lot-opening rush).

### 6. Independent Last-Mile Delivery Route Contractor Stop-Count, Package-Volume & Vehicle-Maintenance Tracker

An independent last-mile delivery route contractor — a driver under a fixed daily contract with a delivery service partner (Amazon DSP-style) or an independent service provider (FedEx ISP-style), distinct from a long-haul owner-operator trucker — has to track each day's assigned stop count and package volume against their contracted route capacity, since a route that consistently runs over the contracted stop count without documentation is a conversation the contractor needs hard numbers for when negotiating their next rate or route assignment. A contractor running their own vehicle also has to track maintenance intervals and mileage against the delivery service's own vehicle-condition standards, since a vehicle that fails a scheduled inspection or breaks down mid-route costs a full day's contracted pay and can jeopardize the contractor's standing with the service partner, and needs a simple log of daily on-time performance and any delivery exceptions (a blocked driveway, a refused package, a missed time window), since a contractor's route-renewal eligibility often depends on a documented on-time-delivery track record they otherwise have no way to produce beyond the delivery service's own app data.

The tool maintains a per-day stop-count and package-volume log compared against the contracted route capacity; a vehicle-maintenance and mileage tracker with alerts ahead of the delivery service's scheduled inspection intervals; and a daily on-time-performance and delivery-exception log a contractor can reference when negotiating route assignments or contesting a performance dispute.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per contractor. Distribution: r/AmazonDSPDrivers and r/FedEx contractor threads, and independent-route-contractor Facebook groups where last-mile drivers already trade route-negotiation and vehicle-maintenance tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
