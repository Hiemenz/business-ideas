# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-11 (Round 172)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a furniture flipper, a podcast show-notes writer, a phone screen repair tech, a pet photographer, a closet organizer and a kids' game-server builder.

---

### 1. Furniture Flipper Sourcing, Restoration & Resale-Margin Tracker

A person who buys used furniture, restores it and resells it has to track each piece: purchase price and source, restoration materials, hours spent, listing platform and final sale price. Without records, the flipper does not know which categories (mid-century dressers, solid-wood tables) earn the best margin per hour. Several pieces in progress at once make it easy to lose track of which stage each one is in.

The tool tracks each piece through sourced, in-restoration, listed and sold, records materials and hours, and shows margin and margin per hour by category.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per flipper. Distribution: furniture-flipping Facebook groups, r/furnitureflipping and reseller communities. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Podcast Show-Notes Writer Episode, Deliverable & Client Tracker

A freelancer who writes show notes, timestamps and social snippets for podcasts has to track each client's episode release schedule, the transcript received and the deliverables due. With 6-12 shows, a late transcript delays everything, and the writer needs a record of each show's style guide. Per-episode pricing needs counting to invoice correctly.

The tool tracks episodes per show with transcript-received and due dates, a style sheet per show and a monthly episode count for invoicing.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per writer. Distribution: podcast-production communities, r/podcasting and freelance writer groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Phone Screen Repair Tech Job, Parts & Warranty-Call Tracker

A mobile phone repair tech working from a home bench or at pickups has to track jobs, parts ordered (screens, batteries), parts cost, price charged and the follow-up if a repair has a problem. With 10-20 jobs a week, they can lose track of which customer is waiting on a part. A short in-house guarantee on each repair means they also need the repair date on record.

The tool tracks jobs by customer and device with parts status, logs cost and margin per repair, stores the guarantee end date per job and reminds the tech to follow up with waiting customers.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per tech. Distribution: phone-repair Facebook groups, r/mobilerepair and local repair-shop networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Pet Photographer Session, Shot-List & Print-Order Tracker

A pet photographer has to track each session's pet details (name, temperament, special treats), shot list, gallery delivery date and print or album orders. With seasonal rushes (holiday cards), they need to know which galleries are delivered and which clients have not ordered prints. Print orders are the biggest upsell and are often forgotten.

The tool tracks sessions with pet notes and shot list, a gallery delivery countdown and a print-order follow-up sequence with reminders.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per photographer. Distribution: pet-photographer Facebook groups, r/petphotography and local pet-business networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Closet Organizer Client Project, Product & Install Tracker

A professional closet and home organizer has to track each client's space measurements, product list (bins, shelving), purchase and install dates and before/after photos. With several projects at once, products for the wrong client arrive, and measurements are lost. Before-and-after photos are the best marketing and are rarely organized.

The tool stores measurements per project, builds a product list with costs and client price, tracks order and install dates and keeps before/after photo sets.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per organizer. Distribution: professional-organizer groups, r/organization and local home-service networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Kids' Game-Server Builder Server, Player & Subscription Tracker

A teenager or young adult who runs a paid Minecraft or Roblox server or builds custom worlds for clients has to track servers, player subscriptions, hosting costs and client commissions. Players cancel or fall behind on payments, and hosting bills arrive monthly. Knowing profit per server decides which to keep running.

The tool tracks servers with hosting cost and subscribers, flags failed or late payments and shows monthly profit per server and per commission.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per builder. Distribution: Minecraft and Roblox developer Discords, r/admincraft and game-dev freelancer groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
