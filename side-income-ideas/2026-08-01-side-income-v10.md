# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-01 (Round 10)

### 1. Estate Sale Inventory & Preview Site Generator
Estate sale companies need to photograph and catalog hundreds of items on-site, generate a public preview listing before the sale date, and track sold items with payment totals during the event — currently done with spreadsheets and manual Facebook posts. A mobile-first app lets operators photograph each item, assign a category and price, and auto-publish a shareable preview page; during the sale, staff mark items sold and totals accumulate in real time. Monetization: $40-70/mo per estate sale company. Distribution: National Estate Sale Association member directory, active estate sale operator Facebook groups, and cold outreach to companies listed on EstateSales.net. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 2. Beekeeping Hive Inspection Log & Mite Trend Tracker
Beekeepers inspect each hive on a regular schedule and need to track structured health data over time — queen sightings, brood pattern quality, varroa mite wash counts, honey super weight estimates — to catch colony decline before it becomes a full loss. This mobile app provides structured inspection forms per hive, charts varroa mite counts and honey stores as trends across the season, and generates a printable apiary inspection record for state apiary inspector visits. Monetization: $9-18/mo or $49/yr for multi-hive tracking. Distribution: r/beekeeping (active, highly engaged), American Beekeeping Federation community newsletters, local beekeeping club listservs and Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 3. Genealogy Vital Record Request Tracker
Genealogy researchers submit dozens of vital record requests to county clerks, state archives, and national repositories — each with a different fee paid, submission method, typical processing time, and expected return date — and track these across a mix of email threads and sticky notes. An app stores each pending request with the record type, repository, fee amount, submission date, and expected return window, sends follow-up reminders when a request exceeds typical turnaround, and maintains a log of received records with file attachments. Monetization: $9-15/mo or $39/yr. Distribution: r/genealogy (very active), Ancestry.com and MyHeritage community forums, Family Tree Magazine subscriber communities, and genealogy society newsletters. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 4. Self-Storage Lien Law Auction Compliance Tracker
When a storage tenant defaults, facility operators must follow a strict state-specific lien law process — sending certified notices at legally defined intervals, advertising the auction in approved publications, and documenting every step to comply with state storage association standards and avoid liability exposure. This compliance tool walks operators through each required step for each defaulted unit, tracks certified mail send dates and confirmation receipts, auto-calculates the legally required waiting periods between steps, and generates the required auction advertisement text in the format each state mandates. Monetization: $20-35/mo per facility. Distribution: Self Storage Association member communications, state storage association newsletters, and storage operator Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 5. Farmers Market Vendor Booth Assignment & Fee Tracker
Farmers market managers juggle 20-60 vendor relationships per market day — collecting seasonal booth fees, assigning vendor placement by product category and booth size needs, managing waitlists, and sending weekly setup instructions to confirmed vendors — all currently done in email threads and spreadsheets. This tool maintains vendor profiles with product category, booth size, and electrical access requirements, provides a simple visual booth layout grid for weekly assignment, tracks fee payment status per vendor per season, and sends automated weekly confirmation emails with booth assignment and setup time. Monetization: $25-50/mo per market manager. Distribution: Farmers Market Coalition member network, USDA Local Food Directories community forums, and market manager Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 6. Podcast Episode Show Notes & SEO Description Generator
Podcasters spend 30-90 minutes per episode manually writing show notes, timestamps, SEO episode descriptions, and social media post copy — work that can be automated from a transcript or audio file upload. This tool accepts an episode transcript (or generates one via Whisper-based transcription), extracts key topics and timestamps, drafts structured show notes with guest bio, resources mentioned, and key takeaways, and generates a platform-optimized episode description for Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube simultaneously. Monetization: $19-39/mo subscription with episode credit tiers, or $2-4 per episode credits for low-volume podcasters. Distribution: r/podcasting, Podcast Movement community forums, Buzzsprout and Captivate host user communities, and podcaster Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
