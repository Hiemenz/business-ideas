# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-09 (Round 165)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a commission-only sales closer, an appointment setter, a short-form video editor, a ghostwriter, a podcast guest-booking agent and a cold-email deliverability freelancer.

---

### 1. Commission-Only Sales Closer Call, Commission & Pipeline Tracker

A freelance closer who takes sales calls for coaches and agencies, usually for 10-20% commission, has to track every call: show/no-show, outcome, objection, the offer's price, and when the client's payment actually clears. A closer working with several offers at once cannot remember which client owes a commission from a payment plan that lands in installments. Without a log, the closer also cannot tell their close rate per offer, which decides which clients to keep.

The tool logs calls and outcomes, computes close rate and cash collected per offer, and tracks commission owed versus received per installment, with an "unpaid 14+ days" flag.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per closer. Distribution: closer communities on Skool and Discord, and r/sales threads where closers swap offer-vetting tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Appointment Setter Dial, Booking & Show-Rate Tracker

A remote appointment setter who books calls for a client's sales team is paid per booked or per held appointment, so every booked call that fails to show is lost pay. They need a daily activity log (dials, conversations, bookings), show-rate per client and a reminder routine for booked calls. A setter handling two or three clients also needs to see whose list is quietly underperforming before the monthly invoice dispute.

The tool tracks daily activity against targets, booked-versus-held counts per client, and an invoice-ready monthly summary.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per setter. Distribution: Skool setter communities, r/remotework and Discord setter groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Short-Form Video Editor Client Retainer, Revision & Deliverable Tracker

An editor on monthly retainers (for example 12 shorts per month per creator) has to track deliverables due, revision rounds used, and raw footage received. Late footage from the creator is the usual reason deadlines slip, but without a record the editor takes the blame. Scope creep on revisions is also the most common reason an editor's effective hourly rate falls.

The tool logs per-client monthly deliverable counts, revision rounds with a cap warning, footage-received dates, and effective hourly rate per client.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per editor. Distribution: editor Discords, r/VideoEditing and creator-economy Twitter/X editor circles. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Ghostwriter Client Voice-Notes, Draft & Approval Tracker

A LinkedIn or newsletter ghostwriter writes in a client's voice and relies on a short call each month for raw material. They have to track voice notes, each client's tone rules ("never use emojis"), draft stages and which posts the client approved or changed. A ghostwriter with 6-10 clients cannot hold voice rules in memory and loses time re-learning them.

The tool keeps a per-client voice sheet, a content calendar with draft/approved/published status, and a record of client edits to learn from.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per ghostwriter. Distribution: LinkedIn ghostwriter communities, r/freelanceWriters and creator-economy newsletters. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 5. Podcast Guest-Booking Agent Pitch, Reply & Booking-Fee Tracker

An agent who pitches clients as guests on podcasts is typically paid a monthly retainer plus a bonus per booking. They need to track every pitch sent per client, replies, follow-up dates, recorded and air dates, and which shows have been pitched before to avoid double-pitching a host. A reply rate per client shows which positioning works.

The tool logs pitches by client and show, schedules follow-ups, tracks recorded/aired status, and reports reply and booking rates per client.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per agent. Distribution: podcast-booking communities, r/podcasting and PR/publicist Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 6. Cold-Email Deliverability Freelancer Domain Health & Client Report Tracker

A freelancer who sets up and maintains cold-email infrastructure for agencies manages many sending domains and mailboxes per client, each with its own warm-up age, daily send volume and bounce/spam rate. Burnt domains need replacing before a client's campaign dies, and a freelancer with 8-12 clients cannot watch every mailbox by hand.

The tool tracks domains and mailboxes per client with warm-up age and bounce/spam rates, flags unhealthy mailboxes, and produces a monthly client health report.

Monetization: $29-49/mo per freelancer. Distribution: cold-email Discords, r/coldemail and agency-owner Skool groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
