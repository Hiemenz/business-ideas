# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-14 (Round 189)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a shoe repairer, an alterations seamstress, a puppeteer, a cheer coach, an ice-cream cart operator and a holiday-light installer.

---

### 1. Shoe Repairer Ticket, Repair & Pickup Tracker

A cobbler takes in shoes, boots and bags, each needing a quote and a pickup date. Tickets pile up, customers forget pickups and unclaimed items clog shelves.

The tool issues ticket numbers with photos, tracks repair status, texts ready-for-pickup and reminds customers about unclaimed items.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per repairer. Distribution: cobbler and leatherwork communities, r/Leathercraft and local shop groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Alterations Seamstress Order, Fitting & Deadline Tracker

A seamstress who hems, tailors and alters clothing has to track each garment, the work agreed, fitting appointments and the due date. Wedding and prom seasons bring tight deadlines and many fittings.

The tool logs garments with photos, books fittings, tracks deadlines by event date and records price per job.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per seamstress. Distribution: sewing communities, r/sewing and local bridal-shop networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Puppeteer Show, Booking & Prop Tracker

A puppeteer who performs at schools, libraries and parties has to track bookings, show lengths, travel and prop or puppet condition. Different venues want different shows.

The tool tracks bookings and deposits, logs show type per venue, schedules prop repairs and sends a yearly rebooking message.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per performer. Distribution: children's-entertainer groups, r/puppetry and library networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Cheer Coach Team, Routine & Fundraiser Tracker

A private cheer or tumbling coach has to track athletes, class attendance, routine progress, competition dates and fundraiser sales. Parents ask about fees, and competition travel needs planning.

The tool tracks athletes and skills, monitors fees and attendance, manages competition schedules and tracks fundraiser totals.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per coach. Distribution: cheer-coach Facebook groups, r/cheerleading and local gym networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Ice-Cream Cart Route, Stock & Event Tracker

An operator with an ice-cream cart or truck has to track routes, event bookings, flavor stock and sales per location. A hot day at a park out-earns a cold one, and an event booking needs stock planned.

The tool logs sales by location and weather, plans stock per event and shows profit per stop.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per operator. Distribution: food-cart communities, r/foodtrucks and local event-organizer groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to summer.

### 6. Holiday-Light Installer Quote, Install & Takedown Tracker

A person who installs holiday lights has to track customers, measurements, quotes, install dates, takedown dates and stored lights. Every job needs a takedown visit, and the best slots book early.

The tool quotes by roofline length, schedules install and takedown pairs, tracks light inventory and sends a rebooking offer each September.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per installer. Distribution: lighting-installer groups, r/landscaping and local neighborhood groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks, timed to the season.
