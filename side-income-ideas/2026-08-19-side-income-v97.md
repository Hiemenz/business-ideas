### 1. Mobile RV Repair Technician Unit Service-History & Parts Tracker

Independent mobile RV repair technicians who travel to campgrounds, storage lots, and owners' driveways to fix appliances, plumbing, slide-outs, and 12V/shore-power electrical systems need to track a per-unit service-history record tied to a specific RV's make, model, and VIN, since a technician servicing 100+ units across a metro or regional service area can't rely on memory to know which motorhome just had its slide-out motor replaced versus which travel trailer is still waiting on a warranty part for its water heater. The tool also needs a parts-on-order tracker linking backordered RV-specific components (appliance control boards, slide-out motors, specific brand water pumps) to the exact job waiting on them, since RV parts often ship from a handful of specialty suppliers with unpredictable lead times and a customer left without an ETA update is a customer who calls a competitor next time. It also needs a seasonal-rush route board, since RV repair demand spikes hard in the weeks before spring and summer travel season when owners rush to get units road-ready, and a technician without a structured route plan ends up backtracking across the same county multiple times during the year's busiest stretch. Most independent RV technicians currently manage this across a paper work-order pad and a phone full of texted photos, with no structured link between a specific unit's repair history and the parts order still pending against it.

The tool maintains a per-unit service-history record (make/model/VIN, system serviced, parts used, warranty status) tied to owner contact info, a parts-on-order tracker linking backordered components to the specific job waiting on them, and a seasonal-rush route board prioritizing pre-travel-season bookings by location.

Monetization: $19-32/mo per account. Distribution: the RV Technician Association of America (RVTAA) member directory, r/RVLiving and r/gorving technician-referral threads, and campground and RV-park owner referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Monument & Headstone Engraver Inscription-Proof & Cemetery-Coordination Tracker

Independent monument and headstone engravers producing custom grave markers for grieving families need to track a per-order inscription-proof approval cycle, since every engraving — a name, dates, an epitaph, sometimes a custom design element — requires the family's explicit sign-off on a proof before the granite or marble is actually cut, and an engraver who cuts stone before final approval risks both a costly re-cut and re-opening a family's grief over an avoidable mistake. The tool also needs a granite/marble slab inventory tracked by color and size, since a shop stocking a range of stone colors and dimensions needs to know what's on hand before quoting a job that specifies an exact match to an existing family plot marker. It also needs a cemetery-placement coordination tracker recording each specific cemetery's foundation, setting, and permit requirements, since cemeteries vary widely in what they require before a monument can be installed (foundation specs, setting fees, advance notice windows) and an engraver managing placements across several cemeteries in a service area can't rely on memory to get each one right. Most independent monument engravers currently manage this across a paper proof folder and a phone call to each cemetery's office, with no structured link between a specific order's proof-approval status and whether it's actually cleared to cut.

The tool maintains a per-order proof-approval tracker gating stone-cutting until the family signs off, a granite/marble slab inventory by color and size, and a per-cemetery placement-requirements reference tied to each order's scheduled installation.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: the Monument Builders of North America (MBNA) member directory, regional monument dealer associations, and funeral home referral relationships (funeral directors routinely refer families to a trusted local monument company). Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Freelance Live-Event & Concert AV/Sound Technician Gig Kit & Venue-Profile Tracker

Independent AV and sound technicians who set up and run audio, lighting, and video for weddings, corporate events, and small concert bookings need to track a per-gig equipment-kit checklist confirming every mixer, speaker, cable snake, and wireless microphone component is packed before load-out and returned intact afterward, since a technician running 3-6 gigs a week across rented and owned gear can't rely on memory to know whether last weekend's XLR cables actually made it back into the kit. The tool also needs a venue-profile log capturing each recurring venue's specific power availability, rigging points, load-in access, and noise-ordinance cutoff time, since a technician working the same reception hall or club repeatedly needs those details available instantly rather than re-scouting the room on every booking's tight setup window. It also needs a gig calendar tracking load-in, soundcheck, show, and load-out timing across a season of overlapping bookings, since double-booking a Saturday or underestimating a load-in window is the fastest way to damage a freelance AV tech's reputation with event planners who book repeat business. Most independent AV technicians currently manage this across a gear-bag mental checklist and a phone calendar, with no structured link between a specific gig's equipment kit and whether everything actually came back from the last show.

The tool maintains a per-gig equipment-kit checklist (packed/returned status per item), a venue-profile log (power, rigging, load-in access, noise cutoff) tied to recurring venues, and a gig calendar spanning load-in through load-out across overlapping bookings.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/eventprofs and r/livesound threads, the Event Safety Alliance and regional AV-tech Facebook groups, and wedding/event planner referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Trailer Hitch & Towing Equipment Installer Vehicle-Fitment & Route Tracker

Independent mobile hitch installers who fit trailer hitches, wiring harnesses, and towing accessories at customers' homes and shops need to track a per-vehicle fitment record matching a specific make/model/year to the correct hitch receiver class and trailer-wiring connector type, since getting the class wrong (a Class II hitch quoted for a job that actually needs Class III towing capacity) or the wrong wiring harness connector is a comeback job that costs both parts and reputation. The tool also needs a parts inventory tracked by hitch class and wiring-harness type, since an installer stocking a range of receiver classes and connector styles needs to know what's on hand before confirming a same-day install appointment. It also needs a mobile route board spanning driveway installs and shop appointments, since a mobile installer juggling both needs to plan efficient daily routes rather than crisscrossing a service area between jobs. Most independent hitch installers currently manage this across a paper work order and a fitment guide printed from a manufacturer's website, with no structured link between a specific vehicle's confirmed fitment and the parts actually pulled for that day's route.

The tool maintains a per-vehicle fitment tracker (make/model/year, hitch class, wiring connector type) linked to job history, a parts inventory by hitch class and harness type, and a mobile route board spanning driveway and shop installs.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/AskMechanics and towing/trailer-owner Facebook groups, local RV and boat dealer referral relationships (dealers routinely refer buyers needing a hitch installed), and U-Haul and trailer-rental-adjacent installer networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Freelance Children's Book Illustrator Page-Spread & Licensing Tracker

Independent illustrators working on children's picture books for publishers and self-publishing authors need to track a per-book page-spread status tracker, since a 32-page picture book moves through dozens of individually sketched, revised, and finaled spreads rather than one single manuscript pass, and an illustrator juggling 2-4 concurrent book contracts can't rely on memory to know which spread in which book is still awaiting author feedback versus which is approved and ready for final color. The tool also needs a licensing and usage-rights tracker per project, since publishers and self-published authors negotiate very different terms — one-time print-use rights versus broader terms that include merchandising or foreign-edition use — and an illustrator who loses track of what rights were actually granted on a specific project risks a dispute months later when a book gets adapted or licensed further. It also needs a client/publisher rate and royalty tracker distinct from a flat freelance fee, since illustration contracts often mix an upfront advance against a per-copy-sold royalty, and an illustrator needs a clear record of what's been paid against what's still owed as sales reports come in. Most independent children's book illustrators currently manage this across a folder of tracked-revision PDFs and a mental note of which contract granted which rights, with no structured link between a specific book's spread-approval status and the licensing terms attached to it.

The tool maintains a per-book page-spread status tracker (sketch, revision round, final art, delivered), a per-project licensing/usage-rights log, and a client/publisher rate-and-royalty tracker distinguishing advances from ongoing royalty payments.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the Society of Children's Book Writers and Illustrators (SCBWI) member directory, r/childrensbooks and r/illustration threads, and self-publishing author Facebook groups where authors post looking for illustrators. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Independent Pond & Water Feature Maintenance Technician Fish-Stock & Seasonal-Task Tracker

Independent pond and water-garden maintenance technicians servicing residential koi ponds and ornamental water features need to track a per-pond profile recording fish stock (koi and goldfish count and species), pump/filter/UV-clarifier equipment specific to water-garden systems, and water-chemistry readings, since a technician servicing 40-80 ponds across a service area can't rely on memory to know which pond's UV clarifier bulb is due for replacement versus which pond just had a fish loss that needs following up on with the owner. The tool also needs a seasonal-task tracker for spring startup (draining winter debris, restarting pumps and biological filtration), summer algae-control dosing, and fall shutdown (installing de-icers, reducing feeding, netting for leaf debris) — a task cadence with no equivalent in a chlorinated swimming-pool service cycle and one a technician juggling both pond and pool accounts can't manage on the same mental checklist. It also needs a route view grouping ponds by neighborhood for efficient seasonal service pushes, since spring startup and fall shutdown both generate a compressed multi-week rush across the entire client base. Most independent pond technicians currently manage this across a paper service log and a rough memory of which pond is due for its seasonal transition, with no structured link between a specific pond's fish stock and its current equipment and water-chemistry status.

The tool maintains a per-pond profile (fish stock, pump/filter/UV equipment, water-chemistry readings) tied to a client address, a seasonal-task tracker (spring startup, algae-control dosing, fall shutdown/de-icer installation) with due-date alerts, and a route view for efficient seasonal-rush scheduling.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the International Society of Backyard Aquatic Professionals (BAP) and regional water-garden/koi society Facebook groups, r/ponds, and local nursery and water-garden retailer referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
