# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-02 (Round 14)

### 1. Volunteer Fire Department Call Log & LOSAP Tracker
Volunteer fire departments must track each member's call attendance and training hours to qualify them for LOSAP (Length of Service Award Program) benefits — currently done in spreadsheets that lose data when the chief changes and generate errors at quarterly reporting time. This app logs each incident call with attending members, tracks training course completions and certification expiration dates per member, and auto-generates the quarterly LOSAP eligibility report for submission to the program administrator. Monetization: $30-55/mo per department. Distribution: National Volunteer Fire Council (NVFC) member communications, state volunteer firefighter association newsletters, fire department administrator Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 2. Podcast Guest Booking & Pre-Interview Logistics Manager
Interview-format podcast hosts juggle a guest pipeline — tracking prospects identified, outreach sent, confirmed recording slots, pre-interview questionnaire responses, and post-episode promotion agreements (guest shares, quote cards, newsletter mentions) — across a mix of email threads and calendar notes with nothing tied together. This app manages the full guest lifecycle from first outreach through post-episode follow-up: a guest CRM with stage tracking, automated questionnaire delivery on booking confirmation, recording link and prep notes delivery, and a post-episode promotion checklist. Monetization: $20-35/mo per show. Distribution: r/podcasting, Podcast Movement community, Riverside.fm and Squadcast user communities. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 3. 4-H & FFA Youth Livestock Project Record Book App
4-H and FFA members who show livestock (cattle, pigs, sheep, goats, poultry) are required to maintain detailed project record books covering their animal's daily feed costs, weight gain, veterinary expenses, and show results — currently kept in paper workbooks that are tedious to maintain and easy to lose before county fair judging. This mobile app structures the required record entries (daily feed log, health treatments, weight measurements, show entries and placings), auto-calculates feed conversion ratios and cost of gain, and exports a formatted record book PDF in the format required by most 4-H and FFA programs. Monetization: $5-10/mo or $24/yr per member. Distribution: county 4-H extension office newsletters, FFA chapter advisors, livestock show parent Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 4. Auction House Consignment & Post-Sale Settlement Manager
Small regional auction houses (antiques, farm equipment, estate auctions) receive consignments from multiple sellers, each with their own commission rate, lot reserves, and settlement terms — and must reconcile which items sold, at what price, to which bidder, and generate a per-consignor settlement check after the auction concludes. This app manages consignment intake (consignor info, item list, commission rate, reserve prices), tracks bidder registration, records sale prices per lot, and auto-calculates each consignor's settlement statement after deducting the house commission and any applicable fees. Monetization: $40-70/mo per auction house. Distribution: National Auctioneers Association member directory, auction industry Facebook groups, state auctioneers association newsletters. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 5. Wedding Photographer Album Sales & Gallery Proofing Pipeline
Wedding photographers who deliver online galleries rarely have a structured post-delivery follow-up system: they don't know which clients have viewed their gallery, which are warm for album upsells, and which have stalled in album design review — leaving thousands in album revenue on the table. This app integrates with gallery delivery platforms (Pixieset, ShootProof, Pic-Time) to track client gallery opens and favorites activity, triggers automated album sales sequences based on gallery engagement, manages the album design approval workflow (draft submitted → client feedback → revision → approval → order placed), and sends anniversary reminders timed to generate repeat portrait bookings. Monetization: $22-38/mo per photographer. Distribution: wedding photographer Facebook groups, Honeybook and Studio Ninja user communities, Photography Business School audiences. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Youth Sports League Referee Assignment & Pay Tracker
Recreational youth sports leagues need to assign referees to each game on the schedule, confirm referee availability, track which games each ref worked, and cut payment at the end of the season — currently managed by an assignor texting a dozen refs and keeping a spreadsheet that never quite reconciles when payment time comes. This app lets the assignor post game slots, referees claim available slots or accept assignments, and automatically tallies each referee's worked games and pay owed by the end of the season, generating a payment summary the treasurer uses to cut checks or Venmo. Monetization: $25-45/mo per league assignor or league administrator. Distribution: USA Soccer, USAV, and USA Basketball referee assignor communities, youth recreational league administrator Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
