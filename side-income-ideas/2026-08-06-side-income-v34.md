# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 34)

### 1. Freelance Grant Writer Client Application Deadline & Funder Relationship Tracker
Freelance grant writers managing 5-15 active clients simultaneously track grant deadlines, funder relationships, and application submission status across email threads, a shared Google calendar, and their own mental model — making it easy to miss a deadline when two clients have applications due the same week, or to forget that Funder X declined Client Y last year with feedback to reapply in a different program area. This tool maintains a per-client grant application pipeline: funder name, grant program, deadline, application status (research/drafting/submitted/pending/awarded/declined), requested amount, awarded amount, and any funder feedback notes. A master deadline calendar shows every application due date across all clients, color-coded by status — the grant writer's weekly prioritization view. A funder relationship database logs each funder's program interests, past award history with each client, and any submission restrictions (only 1 application per organization per cycle, referral required, etc.). Monthly summary: applications submitted, total requested, total awarded, and win rate by funder type. Monetization: $15-25/mo per grant writer account. Distribution: Grant Professionals Association (GPA) member communities, r/nonprofit grant writer communities, GrantStation and Instrumentl user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Mobile Car Detailing Service Client Vehicle Profile & Service History Tracker
Mobile auto detailers who service client vehicles at home or office maintain client records in a notes app or paper index cards — making it impossible to remember that the 2019 F-150 in bay 3 has swirl marks that the client complained about last time, or that the black Tesla needs hand-washing only because the owner is paranoid about automatic washes. This tool maintains a per-client vehicle profile (make, model, year, color, paint type, any known paint defects or owner preferences, and special product restrictions), a service history log (date, service package performed, products used, technician notes, and any issues identified during the service), and a before/after photo log linked to each service record. Before each appointment, the detailer pulls up the client's profile to review their preferences and last service notes. A rebooking prompt appears after each completed service with the suggested next service interval and package. Client-facing: an optional post-service summary email generated from the session notes. Monetization: $12-18/mo per detailer account (unlimited clients). Distribution: Mobile Detailing Business Facebook groups, r/AutoDetailing, IDA (International Detailing Association) member communities, DetailKing and Autogeek forum communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Author Manuscript Submission Tracker (Literary Agents & Publishers)
Fiction and non-fiction authors who are querying literary agents or submitting directly to publishers manage a complex submission process: each agent or publisher has different submission requirements (some want the first 5 pages, some want 50, some want a synopsis, some don't), different response timeframes (some respond in 2 weeks, some in 6 months), and a one-at-a-time exclusivity requirement for many submissions. Authors currently track this in a spreadsheet, missing the "it's been 3 months with no response, should I nudge?" signals. This tool maintains a submission log (agent/editor name, agency, submission date, format submitted — query only/partial/full manuscript, any specific notes from the submission guidelines, expected response timeframe, and current status — waiting/received partial request/received full request/rejected/offered representation). A pending-response aging view shows every submission ranked by how long it's been waiting, highlighting any that have exceeded the agency's stated response window. An exclusivity tracker flags if a full manuscript is out on exclusive submission so the author doesn't accidentally send it elsewhere. Statistics: total queries sent, response rate, full request rate, and offer rate. Monetization: $8-12/mo per author or $40/year. Distribution: r/writing, r/pubtips, QueryTracker.net community, AbsoluteWrite forum communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Music Venue Artist Booking Contract & Settlement Tracker
Independent music venue managers and booking agents who book 10-40 shows per month manage artist contracts, rider requirements, and post-show financial settlements in email threads and a spreadsheet — making it easy to miss a deposit due date, forget to communicate a rider requirement to the stage manager, or miscalculate a settlement when a show's actual ticket sales differ from the guarantee. This tool maintains a per-show record: artist name, show date, venue, deal structure (flat guarantee vs. percentage of door vs. vs. hybrid), deal memo executed, deposit amount and payment status, technical rider requirements (stage plot, backline requested, sound system needs), hospitality rider requirements, and settlement calculation. After the show, the settlement is logged: actual ticket sales, door revenue, any bar guarantee, and the resulting calculation of what the artist is owed under the deal. A deposit payment calendar shows every upcoming deposit due date across booked shows. A rider preparation checklist generates a to-do list for each show's advancing process (what needs to be confirmed with the artist's tour manager 2 weeks out). Monetization: $20-35/mo per venue or booking agent account. Distribution: Independent Venue Week community, National Independent Venue Association (NIVA) member networks, r/WeAreTheMusicMakers, concert promoter Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Church or Organization Summer Camp/VBS Enrollment & Volunteer Assignment Tracker
Churches, camps, and community organizations running week-long Vacation Bible School programs, summer day camps, or youth programs with 50-400 participants coordinate registration, age-group assignments, volunteer assignments, and special needs documentation across a paper registration form, a spreadsheet, and a coordinator's email inbox. When a parent calls to ask which group their child is in, or when a child with a severe allergy needs to be flagged for the snack station, the answer requires digging. This tool maintains a participant roster (child name, age, grade, group assignment, parent/guardian contact, emergency contact, any medical or dietary flags, and registration payment status), a volunteer roster (volunteer name, role, assigned group, background check status, and any certifications like first aid), and a daily group summary showing each group's participants and assigned volunteers. Allergy and medical flags appear prominently on the group summary so no staff member can miss them. A registration completion tracker shows which families have submitted all required forms vs. which are still missing a health form or signed waiver. Check-in and check-out logs for each day. Monetization: $15-30/event or $8/mo for year-round programs. Distribution: Lifeway and Group Publishing church ministry administrator communities, r/churchIT, Children's Ministry Magazine community networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Amateur Sports League Team Registration & Season Schedule Manager
Recreational and amateur sports league organizers running adult softball, kickball, volleyball, or cornhole leagues with 8-30 teams manage team registration (team captain contact, roster size, fee payment status), game scheduling, field assignments, and standings in a spreadsheet that requires manual updating after every game. This tool maintains a team roster (team name, captain name and contact, fee payment status, player count, and any division they're in), a game schedule (each game's date, time, field, and the two teams playing), and a standings tracker (updated after each game is logged with the winning score). Captains get a link to their team's upcoming schedule. The league organizer's view shows upcoming games by date across all fields, plus a quick standings table per division. At season end, the tool generates a playoff bracket from standings. Optional: a game day check-in feature that marks teams as present/forfeit when games begin. Monetization: $10-20/season per league or $5/mo year-round for multi-sport leagues. Distribution: Playfly Sports, LeagueApps, and ZogSports competitor community forums, r/sportsrec, adult recreational league administrator Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
