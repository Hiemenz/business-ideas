# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-09 (Session 46)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

---

### 1. Independent Esthetician & Skincare Specialist Client Regimen & Treatment History Tracker
Solo estheticians running facials, chemical peels, microdermabrasion, and skincare consultations out of a rented suite or home studio need to remember each client's exact treatment history — skin type, known sensitivities and product reactions, which peel strength or facial protocol was used last visit, and how the client's skin responded — because starting a new treatment without that history risks a reaction the esthetician should have flagged, and re-asking the same intake questions every visit reads as unprofessional to repeat clients. Most solo estheticians currently track this on paper intake cards or scattered notes-app entries that don't travel between the treatment room and the front desk.

The tool maintains a per-client skincare profile (skin type, known sensitivities/allergies, current home-care regimen, and product reactions on file), a treatment visit log (date, treatment performed, products/strength used, and skin response notes with before/after photos), and a rebooking reminder calculated from each client's typical treatment interval so peels and maintenance facials get scheduled before results fade.

Monetization: $15-22/mo per account. Distribution: the Associated Skin Care Professionals (ASCP) member community and local esthetician Facebook groups, plus r/estheticians. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Mobile Welding & Metal Fabrication Contractor Job Quote & Material Tracker
Independent welders and metal fabricators bidding one-off jobs — a custom gate, a trailer repair, a structural repair on a piece of farm equipment — need to track material costs (steel stock by size and grade, consumables like rod and gas) against each quote, and a multi-stage job timeline that can run from initial measurement through fabrication to on-site installation. Most solo welders currently quote from memory or a rough per-job estimate, leading to underbid jobs once actual material costs and shop time are tallied, and job-to-job material tracking happens on paper scraps that get lost in the shop.

The tool maintains a job record per client (scope description, measurements/sketch photos, material list with cost, and quoted price against the welder's own cost-per-job history), a job-stage tracker (quoted, materials ordered, in fabrication, on-site install, complete), and a material inventory log tracking remnant stock on hand so leftover steel from one job can be matched against a future quote instead of over-ordering.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/Welding and r/metalworking communities, the American Welding Society (AWS) local section networks, and local mobile welder/fabricator Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Residential & Local Moving Company Crew Job & Inventory Tracker
Small local moving companies (2-6 crews, no long-haul interstate operations) juggle a full day's job schedule — multiple households moving on the same morning, each with its own inventory of boxes and furniture, special-handling items (pianos, glass, antiques), and a crew assignment — while tracking which crew is where and whether a job is running long enough to affect the next booking's start time. Most small movers currently coordinate this by phone call and a paper day sheet, which breaks down on a busy Saturday when three jobs are running simultaneously and a customer calls asking when the crew will arrive.

The tool maintains a job record per move (customer, origin/destination addresses, inventory list with special-handling flags, and crew assigned), a day-of dispatch board showing each crew's job sequence and real-time status (loading, in transit, unloading, complete), and a claims/damage log per job with photo documentation so disputed items are backed by a timestamped record rather than a he-said-she-said conversation weeks later.

Monetization: $19-35/mo per account. Distribution: the American Moving & Storage Association (AMSA) state affiliate directories and r/MovingCompanies, plus local mover Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Caterer Event Order, Staffing & Prep Tracker
Solo and small-crew caterers booking weddings, corporate events, and private parties need to track a detailed per-event order (guest count, menu selections, dietary restriction flags, and equipment/rental needs like chafing dishes or linens) alongside a staffing plan (which servers and kitchen help are booked for which event) and a kitchen prep timeline working backward from the event start time. Most independent caterers currently manage this across a mix of email threads, a shared spreadsheet, and sticky notes on the kitchen wall, which breaks down during a busy wedding season weekend with three events booked back to back.

The tool maintains an event order record (client, date, guest count, menu with dietary-restriction flags, and equipment/rental checklist), a staffing assignment calendar mapping servers and kitchen staff to specific events, and a prep timeline generator that works backward from the event's serving time to lay out shopping, prep, and load-out deadlines so nothing gets missed on a multi-event weekend.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: the National Association for Catering and Events (NACE) member directory, r/CateringBusiness, and local catering Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Auto Window Tint & Car Audio/Electronics Installer Job & Warranty Tracker
Independent auto window tint installers and car audio/electronics installers who work out of a mobile van or small shop need to track each vehicle's exact job spec — tint shade/percentage by window, or the specific head unit, speakers, and wiring harness installed — along with a warranty period on the work (most tint and audio installs carry a multi-year workmanship warranty) so a customer's return visit with a bubbling tint strip or a rattling speaker can be instantly checked against what was actually installed and when. Most solo installers currently track this on paper invoices stuffed in a folder, making warranty claims a guessing game.

The tool maintains a per-vehicle job record (customer, vehicle make/model, tint shade per window or audio components installed with part numbers, install date, and price), a warranty tracker per job (warranty period, terms, and expiration) so a comeback visit can be checked against active coverage in seconds, and a parts/film inventory log with reorder alerts by roll or component so a popular tint shade or head unit model doesn't run out mid-week.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/CarAV and r/WindowTinting communities, the Window Film and Glass Coatings Association (WFGCA) member directory, and local mobile installer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Private Golf & Tennis Instructor Lesson Package & Student Progress Tracker
Independent golf pros and tennis instructors teaching private lessons at a club or public course/court need to track which students have purchased which lesson package (a 5-pack, 10-pack, or season-long arrangement), how many sessions remain on each package, and session-by-session progress notes (swing or stroke mechanics worked on, drills assigned, what to focus on next) so a student's next lesson picks up where the last one left off instead of starting from scratch. Most independent instructors currently track package balances on a notepad and progress notes from memory, which falls apart once a roster grows past a dozen regular students.

The tool maintains a student profile with lesson package type and remaining session balance (auto-decrementing as sessions are logged, with a low-balance alert prompting a package renewal conversation), a session log (date, skills/drills covered, and instructor notes for next time), and a lesson calendar showing the week's bookings with each student's remaining balance and last session's focus one tap away before they arrive.

Monetization: $15-22/mo per instructor account. Distribution: the Professional Golfers' Association (PGA) of America teaching professional network and the United States Professional Tennis Association (USPTA) member directory, plus local club pro-shop referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
