# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 22)

### 1. Airbnb/STR Cleaning Turnover Checklist Generator
Short-term rental hosts with one or more properties hand off cleaning to a rotating roster of cleaners — sometimes a professional service, sometimes a neighbor, sometimes a family member — who each interpret "clean the unit" differently, leading to missed tasks, inconsistent guest experience, and negative reviews. This tool accepts the host's property details (number of beds/baths, amenities, check-in time, checkout time, special host notes) and generates a branded, room-by-room cleaning and restocking checklist as a printable PDF and shareable link: bedroom strip and remake with linen-change confirmation, bathroom deep clean tasks, kitchen reset with consumables restock check, trash removal, welcome amenities placement, and a "before-you-leave" photo checklist so the cleaner documents the completed state. The host gets a consistent protocol every turn; the cleaner gets unambiguous task clarity. Monetization: $10-18/mo per host account (unlimited properties). Distribution: r/airbnb, Airbnb host Facebook groups, STR investor communities on BiggerPockets, Hospitable and Hostaway user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Job Application Tracking Kanban Dashboard
Active job seekers applying to 20-60+ roles at a time manage their pipeline in a spreadsheet, a notes app, or memory — missing follow-up windows, losing track of application status, and not knowing which sources (LinkedIn, Indeed, company career page, referral) are converting to interviews. This tool provides a lightweight kanban board: five columns (Applied, Phone Screen Scheduled, Interview Stage, Offer/Negotiation, Closed/Rejected) with a quick-add card for each application (company, role, source, date applied, recruiter contact, next step, notes). A weekly digest email summarizes the pipeline: applications by stage, follow-up reminders for items older than 7 days with no status update, and a source conversion rate showing which application channels are producing interviews vs. rejections. No AI needed — just clean workflow and reminder automation. Monetization: $7-12/mo subscription. Distribution: r/jobs, r/cscareerquestions, LinkedIn job seeker communities, coding bootcamp job-search support channels. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Small Business Social Media Content Calendar Generator
Small business owners who know they "should be posting on social media" consistently fail to do so because they have no content plan and face a blank-page problem every time they sit down to create content. This tool accepts business details (business type, target customer, 2-3 core differentiators, tone/voice preference, platforms — Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn) and a planning period (2 weeks or 1 month), then generates a day-by-day content calendar: post topic, post type (educational tip, behind-the-scenes, customer story, promotion, holiday content), a starter caption draft, and a suggested image concept or prompt. The user gets a complete roadmap they can execute themselves or hand to a VA — not a finished post, but a structured plan that removes the blank-page paralysis. Monetization: $12-22/mo or $15 per calendar generated. Distribution: r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, small business owner Facebook groups, SCORE mentor community alumni lists. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Remodeling Contractor Change Order Request Generator
Residential remodeling contractors frequently perform additional work that wasn't in the original scope — a customer asks for an upgraded fixture, demo reveals a rotted subfloor, or an unknown wall condition adds hours — but don't document it formally. Verbal agreements and casual texts lead to non-payment disputes when the customer contests the charge at invoice time. This tool accepts the original scope item, the change description, the reason for the change (customer request, concealed condition, plan error), labor hours, material costs, and markup percentage, then generates a professional change order document: a numbered CO with the change description, a detailed cost breakdown, original contract price, revised contract price, a signature line for customer approval, and a note on schedule impact. The contractor sends it immediately while still on-site; the customer signs digitally. Monetization: $15-25/mo unlimited change orders, or $5/CO for occasional users. Distribution: r/HomeImprovement (contractor side), contractor Facebook groups, BuildZoom and Houzz Pro communities, QuickBooks Online contractor community. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Commercial Photography Usage License Tracker
Freelance commercial photographers license their images for specific uses — a product photo licensed for 6 months of online advertising, a portrait licensed for editorial use only, a food photo licensed to one restaurant location but not the chain — but rarely have a system to track which image is licensed to whom, for what use, through what date. License expiration goes unnoticed, clients use images beyond their licensed scope without consequence, and upsell opportunities (renewing or expanding a license) are missed. This tool maintains a license record per image or shoot: client name, licensed use category (editorial, advertising, social media, print run, geographic restriction), license start and end date, fee paid, and renewal status. A monthly digest flags licenses expiring in the next 60 days with a draft renewal outreach email, and a dashboard shows active licenses, expired licenses, and total licensing revenue by client and year. Monetization: $12-20/mo per photographer. Distribution: r/photography, American Photographic Artists communities, commercial photographer Facebook groups, Photo Mechanic and Capture One user communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Small Landlord HCV/Section 8 Payment Reconciliation Tool
Small landlords accepting Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) tenants receive split rent payments: the housing authority pays a portion directly to the landlord (the HAP — Housing Assistance Payment), and the tenant pays the remaining tenant portion. Landlords with 2-10 HCV tenants track this split payment structure manually, frequently failing to reconcile the HAP portion (which varies if the payment standard changes or the unit's rent is adjusted), missing HAP increases they're entitled to when the payment standard rises, and not noticing when a HAP payment is late or reduced without prior notice. This tool maintains one record per HCV unit: contract rent, payment standard, HAP amount, tenant portion, HAP payment date each month, tenant payment date, and variance flags. A monthly reconciliation report shows: total contract rent vs. total received (HAP + tenant), any HAP underpayment vs. the contracted HAP amount, and late payment flags. A utility allowance calculator (many PHAs reduce the tenant portion by a utility allowance) helps landlords confirm they're collecting the correct tenant portion. Monetization: $10-18/mo per landlord account (up to 20 units). Distribution: r/Landlord, BiggerPockets Section 8 discussion threads, small landlord Facebook groups, NAAHQ (National Apartment Association) small independent landlord communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
