# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-22 (Round 113)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. 3D Printing Service Bureau Print Queue, Filament Inventory & Order Tracker

An independent 3D printing service bureau running a farm of several to a dozen printers takes custom print orders from individual customers, Etsy shoppers, and small businesses needing prototypes or short-run parts, with each order specifying a particular material (PLA, PETG, resin, nylon), color, and print settings, and needing to be assigned to a specific printer with the right nozzle and material loaded, while a failed print partway through wastes hours of machine time and material on a job that now has to be re-queued and re-quoted. An operator running a farm of printers with a backlog of a dozen or more orders currently tracks which printer is running which job, how much of each filament color and material is left on the spool, and which orders are done versus still printing versus failed and need a reprint on a shared spreadsheet and a sticky note taped to each printer, and a customer order forgotten mid-queue because the printer it was on got repurposed for a rush job is a customer who doesn't reorder.

The tool maintains a print queue by printer showing each machine's current job, material loaded, and estimated completion time, a filament/resin inventory tracker by material and color with reorder alerts before a spool runs out mid-print, a per-order specification sheet (file, material, color, quantity, and any post-processing requested) tied to order status from queued through printed, post-processed, and shipped, and a failed-print log tracking which jobs needed a reprint and why, to catch a recurring printer or settings problem.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per bureau. Distribution: r/3Dprinting and r/functionalprint business threads, the Print Farm Owners Facebook group, and Etsy seller forums for the growing community of sellers running multi-printer farms for custom order fulfillment. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Deck & Outdoor Structure Builder Job Takeoff, Material Order & Crew Schedule Tracker

An independent deck builder running two to four crews through a spring-to-fall build season quotes each job by square footage and material tier (pressure-treated, composite, or hardwood decking, plus railing style and any pergola or stair add-ons), then has to convert that quote into an accurate lumber and hardware takeoff, order materials with enough lead time that a delayed composite-decking shipment doesn't leave a crew standing idle on-site, and sequence crews across several concurrent builds so nobody shows up to a site where the footings haven't cured yet. A builder running eight to fifteen active jobs at different stages across a build season currently tracks each job's material list, order status, and crew assignment on a paper takeoff sheet and a whiteboard in the shop, and a lumber order placed against the wrong takeoff, or a crew dispatched to a site before an inspection sign-off on the footings, is a full day of wasted labor and a client watching their project sit unfinished.

The tool maintains a per-job takeoff and material-order tracker (linear footage of decking and railing, joist and footing count, hardware list) checked against supplier order and delivery status, a build-stage tracker per job (footings, framing, decking, railing, final walkthrough) with inspection-hold flags, and a crew scheduling board sequencing which crew is on which job each day across the full active job list.

Monetization: $20-35/mo per builder. Distribution: r/decks and r/carpentry contractor threads, the North American Deck and Railing Association (NADRA) member directory, and regional deck-builder Facebook groups where independent builders already trade takeoff and scheduling workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Independent Virtual Assistant Agency Client Task, Retainer-Hours & Subcontractor Tracker

A virtual assistant agency owner running a small roster of contract VAs serving a dozen or more small-business clients has to keep each client's recurring task list and monthly retainer-hour allotment straight — one client wants weekly inbox triage and calendar management, another wants monthly bookkeeping data entry and social media scheduling — while assigning specific tasks to whichever subcontracted VA has capacity that week and tracking hours actually worked against each client's contracted hour bank so nobody blows past a retainer without a billed overage. An agency owner managing several VAs across a growing client list currently tracks client task lists, hours logged per client, and which VA is assigned to which account on a shared spreadsheet that falls out of sync the moment a VA logs hours in their own separate time tracker, and a client whose retainer hours quietly ran over without anyone flagging it before month-end billing is a client disputing an invoice, while a recurring task that slipped through the cracks because the assigned VA was out sick is a client wondering why their newsletter didn't go out.

The tool maintains a per-client recurring-task checklist with assigned VA and due cadence (weekly, biweekly, monthly), a retainer-hours tracker per client showing hours used against the contracted monthly bank with an overage alert before the client is billed, a subcontractor-VA roster showing each VA's current client assignments and weekly capacity, and a task-handoff log so a covering VA can see exactly where a client's recurring work stands.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per agency account. Distribution: r/VirtualAssistant and the Virtual Assistant Networking Association Facebook group, plus the Association of Virtual Assistants and independent-VA agency owner communities on Facebook, where agency owners already trade retainer-tracking and subcontractor-scheduling workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Oil Change & Fluid Service Route Operator Vehicle History & Membership Tracker

A mobile oil change operator drives a service van to customers' homes and workplace parking lots, running six to ten appointments a day across a metro service area, and needs to know each returning customer's vehicle make, model, and oil type (conventional, synthetic blend, full synthetic) along with when they're next due, while also tracking a growing base of subscription customers who've prepaid for three or four scheduled oil changes a year. An operator running a full daily route plus a subscription base currently tracks appointment locations, each vehicle's oil-type and filter-part-number history, and which subscription customers are coming due for their next visit on a booking app's generic calendar and a mental note of who drives what, and a subscription customer who falls through the cracks because nobody flagged their due date is a canceled membership, while a technician who shows up without the right filter part number for a specific vehicle is a wasted trip back to the shop.

The tool maintains a route/appointment calendar with address and service-window per stop, a per-vehicle service history (oil type, filter part number, mileage at last service, and computed next-due date or mileage), a subscription/membership tracker flagging which prepaid customers are due for their next scheduled visit, and a supply inventory tracker for oil and filter stock carried on the van with reorder alerts before a route day runs short.

Monetization: $20-30/mo per operator. Distribution: r/MobileMechanics and r/AutomotiveBusiness threads, the National Institute for Automotive Service Excellence (ASE) community boards, and regional mobile oil-change franchise-independent operator Facebook groups where operators already trade route and membership-retention workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Windshield & Auto Glass Repair Technician Dispatch, Glass Inventory & Insurance Billing Tracker

An independent mobile windshield repair and replacement technician responds to calls at customers' homes and workplaces, and for each job needs to know the exact windshield or window part number for that specific vehicle's year, make, model, and trim (a windshield with built-in rain sensors or a heads-up-display strip is a different part than a base-model version of the same car), while a large share of jobs bill directly to the customer's auto insurance carrier rather than the customer, each with its own claim number and approved-amount paperwork to track before payment comes through. A technician running eight to twelve jobs a week across a service area currently tracks dispatch locations, which glass part was ordered for which vehicle, and insurance claim numbers and payment status on a paper work-order pad and a stack of insurance approval printouts, and a technician who shows up with the wrong windshield part number for a car with an ADAS camera bracket is a wasted trip and a customer stuck without a usable vehicle, while an insurance claim that never gets followed up on is revenue sitting uncollected for weeks.

The tool maintains a dispatch/appointment log with vehicle year/make/model/trim and required glass part number, a glass inventory tracker flagging low stock on commonly ordered windshield part numbers, an insurance claim tracker per job (carrier, claim number, approved amount, and payment-received status), and a same-day ADAS recalibration flag for vehicles whose windshield replacement requires a camera recalibration before the job is considered complete.

Monetization: $20-30/mo per technician. Distribution: r/AutoGlass and the Auto Glass Safety Council (AGSC) member directory, plus regional mobile auto-glass technician Facebook groups where independent technicians already trade parts-sourcing and insurance-billing workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Fence Installation Contractor Job Takeoff, Material Order & Crew Schedule Tracker

An independent fence installation contractor quotes jobs by linear footage and fence type (wood privacy, vinyl, chain-link, or ornamental aluminum), then has to convert each quote into a post-spacing and material takeoff, order the specific post, panel, and hardware quantities from a supplier with enough lead time to avoid a crew standing around on install day, and often needs a property line survey confirmation or an HOA approval on file before a crew can legally start digging post holes. A contractor running several crews through a full booking season currently tracks each job's takeoff, material order status, and property-line/HOA-approval documentation on a paper quote sheet and a filing folder, and a crew that shows up to install a fence before a call-before-you-dig utility locate has cleared, or before an HOA approval is confirmed on file, is a job that has to stop mid-install and a client questioning why the project is delayed.

The tool maintains a per-job takeoff and material-order tracker (linear footage, post spacing and count, panel/hardware quantities) checked against supplier order and delivery status, a pre-install documentation checklist (utility locate confirmation, property-line survey, HOA or permit approval where required) with a hard flag before a job is marked ready to schedule, and a crew scheduling board sequencing which crew is on which job across the full active job list.

Monetization: $20-30/mo per contractor. Distribution: r/Fencing and r/Landscaping contractor threads, the American Fence Association (AFA) member directory, and regional fence-contractor Facebook groups where independent contractors already trade takeoff and pre-install documentation workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
