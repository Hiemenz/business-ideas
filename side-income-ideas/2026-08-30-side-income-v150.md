# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-30 (Round 150)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo's prior 149 rounds, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Freelance CAD Drafter & Overflow Drafting Service Job, Revision & Client-Spec Tracker

An independent CAD drafter who takes overflow drafting work from several small architecture, engineering, and general-contracting firms at once has to track each job's exact drawing standard — layer naming conventions, title-block format, and dimension style — since every client firm has its own CAD standard, and a drawing delivered in the wrong layer structure gets bounced straight back for rework that eats into a fixed-bid job's margin. A drafter juggling several concurrent jobs also needs a clear revision-round history per drawing set, since a client emailing "please fix the door swing on sheet A3, third time" needs the drafter to know exactly which prior round already addressed which markup, and a drafter relying on a folder of file versions named "final_v3_REAL" has no reliable way to prove what changed between rounds when a client disputes a billed revision.

The tool maintains a per-client CAD-standard profile (layer conventions, title-block template, file-naming convention) that carries over automatically to every new job for that client; a per-drawing-set revision-round tracker logging each markup and its resolution; and a per-job time and deliverable log supporting both hourly and fixed-bid billing. Monetization: $19-29/mo per drafter. Distribution: r/AutoCAD and r/civilengineering freelance-drafting threads, and the American Design Drafting Association (ADDA) member directory. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Independent Equine Massage & Bodywork Therapist Multi-Barn Client & Treatment-History Tracker

An independent equine massage and bodywork therapist traveling to multiple barns to treat performance and pleasure horses has to track each horse's treatment history — problem areas identified, technique used, and the horse's response — since a therapist returning to the same horse weeks or months later without that history either re-assesses from scratch or misses a recurring pattern (a persistently tight hindquarter, a resistance to a specific stretch) that would otherwise point toward a deeper issue worth flagging to the owner or the horse's veterinarian. A therapist running a full day of barn visits across a spread-out rural or suburban service area also needs to sequence appointments by drive time and track each barn's specific access details (gate codes, which stall or cross-tie area to use), since a mobile practice's entire day is built around minimizing drive time between widely spaced barns.

The tool maintains a per-horse treatment-history record (problem areas, technique, response, and any veterinary referral note) tied to an owner and barn profile; a route-sequenced appointment calendar by barn location and drive time; and a per-client invoice and package-credit tracker for owners on a recurring monthly treatment plan. Monetization: $15-25/mo per therapist. Distribution: the Equine Sports Massage Association (ESMA) and International Association of Animal Massage & Bodywork (IAAMB) practitioner directories, and regional horse-boarding-stable Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent Custom & Repair Jeweler Bench Job, Metal/Stone Inventory & Appraisal Tracker

An independent bench jeweler running a mix of custom design commissions and repair work — resizing, prong retipping, stone replacement, chain soldering — has to track each job's exact specifications (metal type and karat, stone cut and carat weight, customer-supplied materials versus shop stock) and its current bench stage (quoted, metal ordered, in production, quality check, ready for pickup), since a custom engagement-ring commission and a same-week repair queue running through one bench at once means a job with the wrong stone substituted, or a customer's own heirloom stone mixed up with another job's inventory, is the kind of mistake that ends a client relationship instantly. A jeweler also has to track loose-stone and precious-metal inventory by type, weight, and cost basis, since scrap gold and stone stock tied up in unfinished jobs is real working capital a bench jeweler needs visibility into, and needs a simple appraisal/valuation document generator for insurance-replacement purposes on higher-value custom pieces, since a customer's insurer routinely asks for one before covering a new piece.

The tool maintains a per-job specification and bench-stage tracker distinguishing customer-supplied materials from shop stock; a metal and loose-stone inventory log by type, weight, and cost basis; and a simple appraisal-document generator for insurance-replacement valuation on completed custom pieces. Monetization: $19-35/mo per jeweler. Distribution: the Jewelers of America member directory and independent-bench-jeweler forums on Ganoksin and r/jewelrymaking. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Boat & Yacht Broker Listing, Sea-Trial & Documentation Tracker

An independent boat and yacht broker representing several sellers at once has to track each listing's full specification sheet (make, model, hull number, engine hours, equipment list) alongside a sea-trial and survey scheduling calendar coordinated across the buyer, the seller, and a marine surveyor, since a broker juggling a handful of active listings can't rely on memory for which listing still needs updated photos, which buyer's financing contingency is about to expire, or which survey needs rescheduling around a marina's haul-out slot availability. A broker closing a sale also has to track the documentation chain to a clean title or USCG documentation transfer — bill of sale, lien release, state registration or Coast Guard abstract of title — since a closing that stalls on a missing lien release is lost commission sitting in escrow with no clear way to see what's actually still outstanding.

The tool maintains a per-listing specification sheet and photo/document library; a sea-trial and survey scheduling calendar coordinating buyer, seller, and surveyor availability; and a per-sale closing-documentation checklist tracking bill of sale, lien release, and title/documentation transfer status through to a completed closing. Monetization: $29-49/mo per broker. Distribution: the Yacht Brokers Association of America (YBAA) member directory and regional boat-broker Facebook and marina-office referral networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Ski & Snowboard Tuning Technician Seasonal Route & Equipment-Service Tracker

An independent mobile ski and snowboard tuning technician running a van-based route of drop-off and pickup stops through a resort town's condos and rental shops during ski season has to track each customer's equipment specs (ski/board length and binding model, boot sole length for binding DIN-setting calculations) and service history — edge sharpen, base grind, wax type applied — since a technician returning gear with the wrong DIN setting or the wrong wax for that week's snow temperature is both a safety issue and a lost repeat customer in a business that depends entirely on word-of-mouth during a compressed winter season. A technician also has to sequence a full day's drop-off and pickup route across a spread-out mountain town while tracking a tuning-supplies inventory (wax by temperature range, edge-sharpening stones, base-repair P-tex), since running short on a specific wax mid-week during a cold snap means turning away same-day requests at the exact moment demand peaks.

The tool maintains a per-customer equipment-spec and service-history record (binding DIN calculation inputs, edge/wax service log); a route-sequenced daily pickup/drop-off schedule by neighborhood; and a tuning-supplies inventory tracker with a reorder alert keyed to the week's forecast snow temperature. Monetization: $15-25/mo per technician, seasonal (billed October-April). Distribution: the Professional Ski Instructors of America/Ski Techs forums, r/skiing and r/snowboarding gear-tech threads, and local ski-shop and resort-condo-management referral partnerships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks (start of ski season).

### 6. Independent Custom Leatherworker & Leather Goods Maker Order, Hide-Inventory & Pattern Tracker

An independent leatherworker making custom bags, belts, holsters, and other leather goods to order has to track each commission's exact specification — hide type and thickness, hardware selection, stitching pattern, and any personalization (monogramming, tooling design) — against a customer's original request, since a maker running several concurrent commissions can't rely on memory to know which order gets brass versus nickel hardware or which pattern variation a repeat customer prefers from their last order. A leatherworker also has to track hide inventory by type, thickness, and remaining usable area, since a full hide is an expensive, irregularly shaped piece of raw material and a maker who doesn't track how much usable leather remains on a partially cut hide risks either wasting material on an oversized cut or discovering mid-project that a hide can't actually yield the piece it was set aside for.

The tool maintains a per-order specification and personalization record tied to a customer profile and pattern library; a hide and hardware inventory tracker by type, thickness, and remaining usable area; and a production-stage tracker (cut, stitch, finish, ready for pickup/shipping) across concurrent commissions. Monetization: $15-25/mo per maker. Distribution: the Leathercrafters & Saddlers Journal community, r/leathercraft, and independent-maker Etsy-seller Facebook groups focused on leather goods. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
