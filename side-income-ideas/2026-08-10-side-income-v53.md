# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-10 (Session 53)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- Simple, fast-to-build workflow trackers — no regulatory compliance angle.
- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.
- **No repeats.** This session's six operator types — independent doulas, mobile bartenders/event beverage services, independent farriers, freelance voiceover artists, home/cottage food bakers, and residential house cleaning operators — are distinct from every prior session: doula work has never appeared as its own operator type (distinct from the licensed midwife idea in an earlier main-income compliance session, which tracks a state-licensed clinician's regulatory credentials, transfer agreements, and statutory birth-record filing deadlines; a doula provides non-medical labor/postpartum support with no clinical license at all, and needs an entirely different tracking problem — due-date/birth-plan preferences and a postpartum visit schedule, with no clinical credential or vital-records filing component); mobile bartending and event beverage service has never appeared (distinct from every event-adjacent idea already in this repo — the wedding planner idea tracks vendor payment schedules for a planner coordinating other vendors, and the party rental/bounce house idea tracks a physical inflatable/furniture fleet; a mobile bartender instead tracks per-event staffing assignments and a liquor/mixer/garnish inventory consumed and replenished event to event, a completely different inventory type and booking structure than either prior idea); farriery has never appeared (distinct from the small-herd livestock rancher idea in an earlier session, which tracks cattle/goat/sheep breeding pairs and medication withdrawal periods for a rancher's own herd — a farrier instead serves horses across dozens of client stables on a recurring 6-8 week trim/reshoe cycle with no breeding or herd-health record at all, a fundamentally different animal, service frequency, and client relationship); freelance voiceover work has never appeared (distinct from the freelance copywriter/content writer idea in an earlier session, which tracks written-word deliverables and revision rounds — a voiceover artist instead tracks audio-file deliverables, script versions, and per-job usage-rights/licensing terms, an entirely different production medium and rights-tracking problem no text-based freelance idea touches); home/cottage food baking has never appeared (distinct from the CSA farmer idea in an earlier session, which tracks a subscription produce-share harvest and member roster — a cottage baker instead tracks custom per-order commissions with allergen/ingredient specifics and delivery/pickup scheduling, a completely different product and order structure); and residential house cleaning has never appeared (distinct from the mobile pet waste removal idea in an earlier session, which tracks a recurring unattended-yard route with no client interaction — a house cleaner instead tracks room-by-room client preferences, supply/access notes, and a recurring in-home service schedule with direct client communication, a different service model and client relationship entirely).

### 1. Independent Doula Client Due-Date, Birth Plan & Postpartum Visit Tracker
Independent birth and postpartum doulas supporting multiple expecting clients at once need to track each client's due date, birth plan preferences (pain management approach, support-person roles, hospital/birth-center or home-birth setting), and on-call coverage window as the due date approaches, because a doula juggling 3-6 active clients across overlapping due-date windows needs to know at a glance who's entering their on-call period and what that specific client's birth-plan preferences are the moment labor starts — reconstructing preferences from memory during a 2am labor call is exactly the failure mode clients are paying a doula to prevent. Postpartum doulas separately need to track a recurring in-home visit schedule per client (newborn care support, feeding support, recovery check-ins) across the weeks following birth. Most independent doulas currently manage this across a personal notes app and a paper intake form that doesn't travel to the birth setting.

The tool maintains a per-client profile (due date, birth plan preferences, support team contacts, and on-call window start date), a birth-support log (labor start notification, support provided, and birth outcome notes) building a record per client, and a postpartum visit scheduler tracking recurring in-home visits against each client's postpartum care plan.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per doula account. Distribution: DONA International's certified doula directory, r/doula and r/birthworkers threads, and independent doula collective Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Mobile Bartender & Event Beverage Service Booking, Staffing & Inventory Tracker
Independent mobile bartenders and small event beverage service operators booking weddings, corporate events, and private parties need to track each event's staffing assignment (which bartenders are working which event), the specific beverage package or menu contracted, and liquor/mixer/garnish inventory consumed per event against what needs restocking before the next booking, because double-booking a bartender across two Saturday events is a direct no-show risk, and running out of a key spirit or mixer mid-event with no backup on the truck is the kind of failure that kills repeat bookings and referrals. Most independent operators currently track this across a shared group chat for staffing and a mental inventory count that doesn't survive a busy wedding season.

The tool maintains an event booking record (date, venue, guest count, beverage package/menu, and assigned staff), a per-event inventory checklist (spirits, mixers, garnishes, and bar equipment needed) checked against the truck's current stock, and a post-event inventory reconciliation log flagging what was consumed so restocking happens before the next booking rather than the morning of.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per operator account. Distribution: r/bartenders and r/eventplanning threads, the United States Bartenders' Guild (USBG) local chapter directory, and local mobile bar service Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent Farrier Client Stable & Shoe/Trim Cycle Tracker
Independent farriers serving horses across dozens of client stables need to track each horse's shoeing/trimming cycle (typically due every 6-8 weeks), shoe type and size used, and any hoof issue noted at the last visit, because a farrier running a route of 100+ horses across multiple stables can't hold every horse's due date and prior shoe spec in memory, and showing up to reshoe a horse without remembering last visit's note about a developing abscess or a lameness issue is both a service gap and a liability concern. Most independent farriers currently track this on a paper route book that gets left in the truck and doesn't cross-reference a horse's due date against the actual route being run that week.

The tool maintains a per-horse profile (owner, stable location, shoe type/size, and hoof condition notes), a service history log (visit date, work performed, and any issue flagged) building a running record per horse, and a due-date route planner showing which horses are coming due within the farrier's next stable visits so a route can be planned efficiently rather than reactively.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per farrier account. Distribution: the American Farrier's Association (AFA) member directory, r/farriers and r/horses threads, and regional horseshoeing association Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Freelance Voiceover Artist Job, Script Version & Usage Rights Tracker
Independent voiceover artists recording commercial, e-learning, audiobook, and IVR/phone-system jobs for multiple clients need to track each job's script version (client revisions often arrive after an initial recording), delivered audio file status, and the specific usage-rights or licensing terms agreed per job (broadcast vs. web-only, exclusivity period, geographic restriction, buyout vs. time-limited license), because delivering the wrong script version wastes a costly studio session, and losing track of which client licensed a recording for web-only versus full broadcast use creates a real contract-dispute risk if the client's usage later exceeds what was actually paid for. Most independent voiceover artists currently track this across a folder of audio files and email threads with no structured link between a specific recording and the usage terms attached to it.

The tool maintains a per-job record (client, script version received, recording date, and quoted rate), a delivery log tracking which audio files were sent and when, and a usage-rights ledger per job (license type, exclusivity period, geographic/media restrictions, and expiration date where applicable) so an artist can instantly confirm what a client is actually licensed to do with a given recording.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per artist account. Distribution: r/VoiceActing and r/voiceover threads, the World-Voices Organization (WoVO) member directory, and independent voice talent Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Home & Cottage Food Baker Custom Order, Allergen & Delivery Tracker
Independent home bakers and cottage food producers taking custom cake, cupcake, and dessert orders need to track each order's exact specifications (flavor, filling, design details, size/serving count), client-disclosed allergies or dietary restrictions, and the delivery or pickup date and time, because a custom cake order has zero room for error on a wedding or birthday deadline, and a missed allergen note (nut-free, gluten-free, dairy-free) is a genuine health risk to a client, not just a customer-service miss. Most independent home bakers currently track this across a paper order form per cake and a separate calendar app that doesn't link the order's allergen notes to the actual baking-day checklist.

The tool maintains a per-order record (client, design/flavor specifications, serving count, and price), an allergen/dietary-restriction flag attached directly to the order so it's visible on the baking-day checklist rather than buried in a separate note, and a delivery/pickup scheduler showing which orders are due on which date so a baking queue can be planned around overlapping deadlines.

Monetization: $12-20/mo per baker account. Distribution: r/cottagefood and r/baking threads, the Cottage Food Community Facebook groups organized by state, and local home baker collective directories. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Residential House Cleaning & Maid Service Operator Client Preference & Route Tracker
Independent house cleaners and small residential cleaning operators running recurring weekly, biweekly, or monthly appointments across dozens of homes need to track each client's specific preferences (which rooms to prioritize, products to avoid due to allergies or pet sensitivity, and access instructions like a garage code or key location), because a cleaner running 8-10 homes a day can't remember which client wants the guest room skipped or which client's cat needs the basement door kept shut, and getting a client's preference wrong repeatedly is exactly what turns a recurring client into a canceled one. Most independent cleaners currently manage this across a mix of memory and a paper note per house that doesn't travel between team members if someone calls in sick.

The tool maintains a per-client property profile (service frequency, room-by-room preferences, product restrictions, and access notes), a route schedule organizing the day's appointments in order with drive-time between stops, and a per-visit completion log (date, tasks completed, and any issue flagged like a broken item or a supply that's run out) so any team member covering a route has the same client context the regular cleaner would.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per operator account. Distribution: r/CleaningTips and r/smallbusiness threads, the Association of Residential Cleaning Services International (ARCSI) member directory, and local house cleaning Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
