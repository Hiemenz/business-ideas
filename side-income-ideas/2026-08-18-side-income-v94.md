# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-18 (Session 94)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow trackers. Six new self-employed operator types not previously covered in this repo.

---

### 1. Independent Owner-Operator Truck Driver Load, Fuel & Factoring Settlement Tracker

Owner-operators running under their own or a leased motor carrier authority juggle load-by-load economics that a generic spreadsheet doesn't capture well: rate confirmation vs. actual paid amount, deadhead miles to pickup vs. loaded miles, fuel cost per load against a moving average of diesel prices, and — for drivers who factor their invoices — the gap between the invoice amount submitted to the factoring company and the discounted amount actually deposited. Without a structured per-load ledger, an owner-operator can't easily answer "was that lane actually profitable after fuel and factoring fees," which matters enormously when choosing which broker loads to accept next.

The tool maintains a per-load ledger (broker, rate confirmation number, origin/destination, loaded and deadhead miles, fuel cost, factoring fee if applicable, net settlement amount) alongside a rolling per-mile profitability view by lane and by broker, plus a quarterly IFTA-adjacent mileage-by-state summary pulled from the same load records.

Monetization: $19-35/mo per account depending on load volume tier. Distribution: OOIDA (Owner-Operator Independent Drivers Association) member forums, r/Truckers and TheTruckersReport.com owner-operator subforums, and Facebook groups for leased-on owner-operators at specific carriers. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Fractional CFO / Outsourced Controller Client Engagement & Monthly Close Calendar Tracker

A fractional CFO or outsourced controller juggling 6-15 small-business clients simultaneously needs to track a very different rhythm per client: which clients are on a monthly close cadence versus quarterly board-reporting cadence, which deliverables are due when (management reports, cash flow forecasts, board decks, budget-vs-actual variance analysis), and which client conversations from the last call still need a follow-up item closed out before the next one. Doing this across a personal calendar and a shared drive means deliverables slip quietly — a board deck delivered two days late looks unprofessional in a way that erodes the trust a fractional CFO's entire value proposition depends on.

The tool maintains a per-client engagement profile (scope, cadence, hourly/retainer terms), a rolling close-calendar across all clients showing which deliverable is due for which client this week, and an action-item tracker tied to each client call so nothing raised in a meeting gets forgotten by the next one.

Monetization: $25-45/mo per account. Distribution: the Fractional CFO/Controller LinkedIn community, the Institute of Management Accountants (IMA) local chapter networks, and the r/fractionalCFO and r/accounting subreddits. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 3. Independent Real Estate Fee Appraiser AMC Assignment Order & Turnaround-Time Tracker

Independent real estate appraisers who take assignments from multiple appraisal management companies (AMCs) rather than working for one shop juggle wildly different turnaround-time requirements, fee schedules, and revision-request processes across each AMC relationship. An appraiser working with 6-10 AMCs at once needs to know, at a glance, which order is closest to its promised turnaround deadline, which AMC pays fastest and which chronically pays late, and which orders have an open revision request sitting unanswered — since a blown turnaround deadline is the single fastest way an AMC drops an appraiser from its panel.

The tool maintains a per-order tracker (AMC, property address, order date, promised turnaround date, fee, payment status) with a turnaround-deadline countdown sorted across all active AMC relationships, plus a revision-request log and a per-AMC scorecard showing average pay speed and order volume over time.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: the Appraisal Institute local chapter mixers, the AppraisersForum.com community, and state real estate appraiser board CE-course networking. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Reiki Practitioner / Energy Healer Client Session Package & Intake Tracker

An independent Reiki practitioner or energy healer running a solo wellness practice needs to track something a generic scheduling app doesn't handle well: client intake notes on presenting concerns and intentions for each session, which sessions belong to a prepaid multi-session package versus a drop-in, and how a client's stated goals or physical sensations have evolved session over session — details clients expect their practitioner to remember without being re-asked at every visit, and that build the sense of continuity that turns a one-time visitor into a repeat client.

The tool maintains a client intake and session-note log (presenting concern, chakra/area of focus, sensations reported, practitioner observations), a prepaid package credit tracker (sessions remaining, expiration date), and a simple booking calendar tied to package usage.

Monetization: $12-22/mo per account. Distribution: the International Association of Reiki Professionals (IARP) practitioner directory community, local wellness-center practitioner co-op groups, and Reiki-practitioner Facebook and Instagram communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Independent/Mobile Boat Broker Listing, Showing & Deal-Pipeline Tracker

A solo yacht or boat broker representing a rotating inventory of 10-30 listed vessels across multiple marinas needs to track each listing's status (price, days on market, price reductions, survey/sea-trial history) alongside a deal pipeline that moves from inquiry to showing to offer to sea trial/survey to closing — each stage with its own follow-up timing, since a buyer who goes quiet after a sea trial often just needs one well-timed check-in to close. Brokers currently manage this across a CRM built for real estate (a poor fit for vessel-specific fields like hull number, engine hours, and survey contingency deadlines) or plain notes.

The tool maintains a listing inventory (vessel details, marina/slip location, list price and history, survey status), a deal pipeline by stage with automated follow-up reminders, and a co-broke commission-split tracker for deals involving another brokerage's buyer's agent.

Monetization: $29-49/mo per account. Distribution: the Yacht Brokers Association of America (YBAA) member network, regional marine trade associations, and boat-broker groups on Facebook tied to major boat-show circuits. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 6. Independent Mobile Agricultural Equipment (Tractor/Combine) Repair Technician Service Call & Seasonal-Rush Route Tracker

A mobile technician who repairs tractors, combines, and other farm equipment on-site at customers' farms faces an extreme seasonal load pattern — planting and harvest windows generate a surge of urgent, weather-sensitive service calls where a broken-down combine mid-harvest is an emergency, while the off-season is comparatively quiet. Without a structured system, the technician juggles which farm is next in the route, which repair is waiting on a backordered part, and which equipment has a service history worth remembering (recurring hydraulic leak on a specific baler, a combine that's needed the same sensor replaced twice) — details that matter enormously to a farmer deciding whether to call this technician again during the next crunch.

The tool maintains a per-customer equipment profile with service and repair history by machine, a route/dispatch board prioritized by urgency during planting and harvest rushes, and a parts-on-order tracker linking backordered parts to the specific job waiting on them.

Monetization: $19-32/mo per account. Distribution: local farmer/rancher Facebook equipment groups, FFA alumni and county extension-office bulletin boards, and farm-equipment-dealer parts-counter referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.
