# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-13 (Round 180)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a freelance subtitler, a book interior formatter, a piano tuner, an alterations seamstress, a miniature painter and a mobile tire changer.

---

### 1. Freelance Subtitler Project, Language & Deadline Tracker

A freelance subtitler and captioner working for creators and agencies has to track each video's length, language pair, rate per minute, deadline and delivery format. With several clients in several languages, overlapping deadlines and format requests (SRT, VTT, burned-in) are easy to mix up. Per-minute pay requires accurate duration records for invoices.

The tool logs projects with duration, language pair and rate, tracks the deadline queue and format checklist and totals invoices per client.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per subtitler. Distribution: subtitling and translation communities, r/translator and creator freelance groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Book Interior Formatter Manuscript, Trim-Size & Revision Tracker

A freelancer who formats book interiors for self-published authors has to track each manuscript's trim size, fonts, front and back matter, proof rounds and print-ready file versions. Authors send late changes after approval, and the formatter needs to know which version went to the printer. Per-project pricing depends on page count and complexity.

The tool tracks projects with specs and version history, counts revision rounds against the quote, stores print-ready file links and tracks deposit and balance payments.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per formatter. Distribution: self-publishing communities, r/selfpublish and author-services Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Piano Tuner Client, Instrument & Return-Visit Tracker

A piano tuner has to track each client's instrument (make, age, condition), the last tuning date and the recommended return interval. With 150-300 clients, reminders that a piano is due are the main source of repeat business. Notes on repairs needed (a sticky key, a loose pin) support follow-up work.

The tool keeps an instrument record per client, schedules return-visit reminders by interval, logs repair notes and shows a route-friendly list of clients by area who are due.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per tuner. Distribution: piano-technician communities, r/piano and local music-teacher networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Alterations Seamstress Order, Fitting & Pickup Tracker

A seamstress who does clothing alterations has to track each order's garment, work required, fittings scheduled, price and pickup date. Wedding and prom seasons bring a rush of fittings with hard event dates. Customers forget pickups, and garments pile up unclaimed.

The tool tracks orders with garment tags and work list, schedules fittings, shows a countdown to each event date and sends pickup reminders with unclaimed-garment follow-up.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per seamstress. Distribution: sewing and alterations Facebook groups, r/sewing and local bridal-shop networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Miniature Painter Commission, Model-Count & Deposit Tracker

A painter who takes commissions for tabletop miniatures and army painting has to track each customer's model count, paint standard (tabletop, display), deposit, progress and shipping. With hundreds of models in the queue, status questions are constant, and a quote depends on the number of models and level of detail.

The tool tracks commissions with model counts and paint standard, shows progress per batch with a customer-facing link, records deposits and balances and estimates the queue's completion date.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per painter. Distribution: miniature-painting communities, r/minipainting and Warhammer commission Discords. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Mobile Tire Changer Job, Inventory & Route Tracker

A mobile tire technician who swaps and repairs tires at customers' homes has to track each job's vehicle and tire size, the tires ordered or in stock, route stops and payment. With several jobs a day, getting to a customer without the right tire size is a wasted trip. Wholesale orders must match the day's bookings.

The tool tracks jobs with tire sizes and stock status, builds a day route with a parts-needed list for tomorrow and records payments and margins.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per technician. Distribution: mobile-mechanic Facebook groups, r/MechanicAdvice business threads and tire-industry groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
