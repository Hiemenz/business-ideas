# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-26 (Round 132)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

### 1. Cattery & Cat Boarding Facility Operator Per-Cat Care-Plan, Vaccination & Occupancy Tracker

An independent cattery operator boarding cats in individual condos or suites has to track each guest's specific dietary needs, medication schedule, and litter preferences, since a boarding cat put on the wrong food or missing a scheduled medication dose is a health incident the owner finds out about at pickup, not something the operator can quietly fix after the fact. An operator also has to verify every incoming cat's current vaccination record — rabies and FVRCP at minimum — before check-in, since a single unvaccinated or contagious cat mixed into a facility with shared ventilation can trigger an outbreak that shuts down boarding for every other guest in the building. A cattery running a full house of suites across a busy holiday boarding season currently tracks care instructions and vaccination status on an index card taped to each suite door and a paper check-in log at the front desk, and a missed medication dose because the card fell off the door, or a boarding reservation accepted without checking the vaccination record first, is a sick cat and a liability the operator can't undo once the guest is already in the building.

The tool maintains a per-cat care-plan record tracking diet, medication schedule, and litter/handling preferences that surfaces automatically at check-in; a vaccination-currency checker flagging any incoming reservation whose rabies or FVRCP record is expired or missing before boarding is confirmed; and a suite-occupancy calendar showing which units are booked, checking out, and available across the facility's full boarding season. Monetization: $19-29/mo per cattery. Distribution: r/CatBoarding-adjacent pet-care Facebook groups, the Cat Fanciers' Association (CFA) boarding-facility network, and the Pet Care Services Association's cattery-member directory where independent operators already trade care-plan and vaccination-tracking tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Mobile Windshield Repair & ADAS Recalibration Technician Job-Queue, Glass-Spec & Insurance-Claim Tracker

An independent mobile windshield repair and replacement technician has to match every job to the exact glass specification a vehicle calls for — a windshield with an embedded rain sensor or heads-up-display layer isn't interchangeable with a generic pane — since ordering the wrong part means a truck rolled to a customer's driveway with glass that doesn't fit, wasting the appointment and the day's route slot. A technician working on newer vehicles also has to track which jobs require post-installation ADAS (advanced driver-assistance system) camera recalibration before the car can be safely returned to the customer, since a windshield replaced without the required forward-camera recalibration leaves lane-keep and automatic-braking systems reading a slightly misaligned world, a liability the technician doesn't want tied back to their invoice if anything goes wrong afterward. Because most jobs bill through an insurance claim rather than the customer's own wallet, a technician also has to track each job's claim number, approved glass-and-labor rate, and payment status separately from the day's route, since a job completed without the claim properly logged is a payment that gets lost in an insurer's system for weeks. A technician running a full week of mobile appointments across a scattered service area currently tracks glass specs, recalibration requirements, and claim numbers on a paper work order and a phone notes app, and a truck that shows up with the wrong glass, or a completed job with no claim number ever logged, is a wasted appointment or unpaid work in a business that runs on same-day throughput. The tool maintains a per-job glass-specification lookup matching vehicle year/make/model to the exact part needed before the truck rolls; an ADAS recalibration flag identifying which jobs require post-installation camera calibration with a completion checkbox before the vehicle is released; and an insurance-claim tracker logging claim number, approved rate, and payment status per job through to reconciliation. Monetization: $19-29/mo per technician. Distribution: r/AutoGlass and mobile-glass-technician Facebook groups, the Auto Glass Safety Council (AGSC) member network, and regional auto-glass supplier trade shows where independent mobile techs already trade parts-lookup and claim-tracking tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Piano Moving Company Crew-Scheduling, Access-Assessment & Damage-Waiver Tracker

An independent piano moving company has to assess every job's specific access challenges — a tight stairwell, a narrow doorway, a piano that has to go up and over a balcony railing — before crew and equipment show up, since a move quoted as a standard two-person job that turns out to need a full crew with a piano board and extra rigging is a stalled truck blocking a residential street while the operator scrambles to call in reinforcements. Every piano also has its own weight and dimension profile — an upright, a baby grand, a full concert grand — that determines which dolly, ramp, and crew size the job actually needs, and a crew dispatched with the wrong equipment for a grand piano's disassembled-leg reassembly is a job that runs hours over its quoted window. A mover also has to track each customer's signed damage waiver and each piano's pre-move condition photos, since a scratch discovered after delivery with no pre-move documentation on file is a dispute the company can't resolve in its own favor. A company running several crews across a full week of scheduled moves currently tracks access notes, equipment needs, and waiver/photo documentation on a paper job ticket and a crew lead's phone camera roll with no link back to a specific move, and a crew that shows up without the right equipment because the access notes never made it off the intake call, or a damage claim with no pre-move photos to point to, is a blown schedule or a dispute the company eats the cost of. The tool maintains a per-job intake record capturing piano type, weight/dimension profile, and access challenges (stairs, doorway width, elevator availability) that determines required crew size and equipment; a crew-scheduling board sequencing the week's moves against equipment and crew availability; and a pre-move condition-photo and signed-waiver tracker tied to each specific piano and customer. Monetization: $19-29/mo per company. Distribution: r/moving and piano-mover Facebook groups, the Piano Technicians Guild's mover-referral network, and regional moving-and-storage association meetups where independent piano movers already trade access-assessment and crew-scheduling tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Heavy-Duty & Diesel Truck Roadside Repair Technician Call-Queue, Parts-Inventory & Fleet-Account Tracker

An independent mobile diesel mechanic responding to breakdown calls for semi-trucks, box trucks, and heavy equipment has to triage each incoming call by urgency and location before deciding which job to take next, since a driver stranded on a highway shoulder with a disabled trailer is a different priority than a fleet yard's routine service call, and dispatching to the wrong call first can leave a driver stuck for hours in an unsafe roadside spot. A technician running a service truck also has to track parts and consumables inventory by exact part number — a specific fuel filter, a particular air-brake valve — since arriving on a roadside call only to discover the truck doesn't stock the one part needed for that specific engine model turns a 45-minute repair into a return trip and an angry dispatcher. A mechanic servicing standing fleet accounts alongside one-off roadside calls also has to track each fleet's specific truck models and service history, since a repeat customer's fleet manager expects a technician who already knows which of their trucks has a recurring issue rather than starting from zero on every call. A technician running a full week of calls across scattered highway and yard locations currently tracks call priority, truck-stock inventory, and fleet-account history on a dispatch radio log and a paper parts list taped inside the service truck, and a call taken out of priority order that leaves a driver stranded longer than necessary, or a roadside job that stalls because the truck's stock didn't have the one part needed, is a bad review or a fleet account that calls a competitor next time. The tool maintains a call-queue tracker prioritizing incoming breakdown calls by urgency and location; a service-truck parts inventory log by part number with a restock alert before a route runs the truck's critical stock too low; and a per-fleet-account service history tracker noting truck models and recurring issues for standing commercial accounts. Monetization: $19-29/mo per technician. Distribution: r/Diesel and mobile-diesel-mechanic Facebook groups, the American Trucking Associations' owner-operator and fleet-maintenance network, and truck-stop bulletin-board and CB-radio-community referral networks where independent roadside techs already trade call-priority and parts-stocking tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Mobile DJ & Wedding/Event Entertainment Operator Booking, Equipment-Fleet & Music-Library Tracker

An independent mobile DJ running weddings, corporate events, and private parties has to lock in each event's specific timeline — first dance, toast cues, cake cutting, last song — well before the date, since a DJ improvising a wedding's run-of-show because the couple's timeline sheet never made it out of an email thread is a reception that runs off the rails in front of every guest. A DJ also has to track equipment fleet status across a busy weekend of back-to-back bookings — speakers, mixers, lighting rigs, wireless mics — since gear that's still packed in yesterday's venue van when today's setup starts is a scramble that delays a client's own event start time. Every event also comes with its own must-play and do-not-play list and licensing considerations for the specific venue, and a DJ who shows up without a client's do-not-play list top of mind risks killing the mood at the exact moment that matters most. A DJ running a full wedding-season calendar of Friday-through-Sunday bookings currently tracks event timelines, equipment checklists, and music preferences on a mix of a paper contract folder and memory, and a missed toast cue because the timeline sheet got left in the car, or equipment left behind because nobody checked the gear list before loadout, is a couple's once-in-a-lifetime event that didn't go the way they were promised. The tool maintains a per-event timeline and cue-sheet tracker capturing must-play/do-not-play lists and key moment cues; an equipment-fleet checklist confirming every speaker, mixer, and lighting piece is packed and returned across a weekend's back-to-back bookings; and a booking calendar tracking deposit status, contract signature, and final-payment due dates per event. Monetization: $19-29/mo per DJ. Distribution: r/DJs and mobile-DJ Facebook groups, The Bash and GigSalad vendor community boards where mobile DJs already list their services, and the American Disc Jockey Association's member network where independent DJs already trade timeline-management and gear-checklist tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Gutter Installation & Cleaning Contractor Job-Measurement, Route-Scheduling & Seasonal-Contract Tracker

An independent gutter installer and cleaning contractor has to measure each job's exact linear footage, downspout count, and gutter-guard requirements before ordering material, since a seamless-gutter job quoted off a rough estimate that comes up short on material mid-install is a crew standing around waiting on a supply-house run in the middle of a job. A contractor running standing seasonal cleaning contracts alongside one-off installation jobs also has to track which properties are due for their fall and spring cleaning visits, since a homeowner on an annual cleaning contract whose gutters overflow because the scheduled visit got missed is a customer who cancels the contract and tells the neighborhood why. A contractor running a full crew across a scattered residential route currently tracks job measurements and seasonal cleaning due-dates on a paper estimate pad and a wall calendar, and a job under-measured because the estimate sheet got a number transposed, or a seasonal cleaning contract that lapsed because nobody was tracking which properties were coming due, is a wasted supply run or a lost recurring account in a business that depends on both accurate one-time quotes and reliable seasonal repeat visits. The tool maintains a per-job measurement and material-requirement calculator computing linear footage, downspouts, and gutter-guard needs before ordering; a seasonal-cleaning contract tracker flagging every property due for its next scheduled visit by season; and a route-scheduling board sequencing the week's installation and cleaning jobs by service area. Monetization: $19-29/mo per contractor. Distribution: r/Roofing-adjacent exterior-contractor Facebook groups, the National Gutter Cleaning Association's member network, and local home-services trade shows and Nextdoor contractor communities where independent gutter contractors already trade measurement and seasonal-route tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
