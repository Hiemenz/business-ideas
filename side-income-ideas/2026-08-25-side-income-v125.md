# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-25 (Round 125)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Custom Bicycle Frame Builder Build-Queue, Tube-Miter & Customer-Fit Tracker

An independent frame builder hand-brazing or TIG-welding custom steel and titanium bicycle frames to a rider's exact geometry has to track each build's specific tube set, mitered angles, and fit measurements against a customer's stated riding style, since a frame built to the wrong seat-tube angle or top-tube length is a mistake discovered only after the customer rides it, and by then the tubing has already been cut and joined. A builder running several frames through different build stages at once — tube mitering, jig alignment, brazing or welding, and post-braze alignment check — currently tracks each customer's fit numbers and build-stage progress on a paper build sheet and a tube-stock shelf sorted by diameter and butting profile, and a frame brazed before its miter angles were actually double-checked against the fit sheet is a scrapped tube set and a blown delivery promise, while a custom paint or decal order placed without confirming frame completion date is a finished frame sitting bare for weeks waiting on a painter's queue slot.

The tool maintains a per-build fit-and-geometry record (tube set, miter angles, and the customer's original fit measurements) tied to a build-stage tracker sequencing mitering, jig setup, joining, and alignment check across multiple concurrent frames; a tube and lug inventory view flagging low stock of specific diameters and butting profiles before a build is scheduled against them; and a post-braze/weld alignment and paint-handoff checklist confirming a frame is actually true before it ships to the painter or customer.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per builder. Distribution: r/framebuilding and the Framebuilders Collective forum, the United Bicycle Institute (UBI) frame-building alumni network, and the North American Handmade Bicycle Show (NAHBS) exhibitor community where independent builders already trade build-queue and tube-sourcing workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Vintage & Classic Car Restoration Shop Multi-Year Project & NOS-Parts Sourcing Tracker

An independent restoration shop rebuilding vintage and classic cars to concours or driver-quality standard has to track each project's specific year/make/model correctness requirements, sourced-parts provenance (NOS, reproduction, or donor-car salvage), and a build timeline that can stretch across multiple years, since a single incorrect reproduction part installed on a numbers-matching restoration can cost the car its judging eligibility at a concours event the owner has been planning toward for years. A shop running several long-term restoration projects through different stages at once — teardown and documentation, media blasting and bodywork, mechanical rebuild, and final assembly — currently tracks each project's parts-sourcing status and stage progress on a paper project binder and a parts-shelf sorted by car, and a project that sits mid-teardown for months with no tracked parts list is an owner repeatedly calling for a status update the shop can't confidently give, while a hard-to-find NOS part ordered without checking whether a better-condition unit already turned up at a swap meet is money spent twice on the same component.

The tool maintains a per-project build-stage tracker (teardown/documentation, bodywork, mechanical rebuild, final assembly) with target-completion milestones; a parts-sourcing log distinguishing NOS, reproduction, and donor-salvage components with provenance notes for numbers-matching authenticity; and an owner-facing project-status view showing exactly which stage a car is in and what parts are still outstanding.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per shop. Distribution: r/AutoRestoration and classic-car-forum restoration threads, the Antique Automobile Club of America (AACA) member and judged-meet network, and regional car-club and swap-meet Facebook groups where independent restoration shops already trade parts-sourcing and project-timeline tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Mobile Chainsaw & Outdoor Power Equipment Blade-Sharpening Route Service Tracker

An independent mobile sharpening technician servicing chainsaw chains, mower blades, and hedge-trimmer blades for tree services, landscaping crews, and arborists has to track each commercial account's specific equipment inventory — chain pitch and gauge, blade length, and how many spares that crew wants sharpened and ready on a rotating basis — since a landscaping crew that runs out of sharp blades mid-route on a Monday is a crew burning fuel and labor hours cutting with dull equipment, and a tree service that gets the wrong chain pitch back after a drop-off is equipment that doesn't fit the saw at all. A technician running a weekly or biweekly multi-stop route across several commercial accounts currently tracks each account's equipment specs and pickup/drop-off cadence on a paper route sheet and a bin of loose blades, and a hedge-trimmer blade sharpened to the wrong bevel angle because nobody recorded the crew's preference is a redo that costs a return trip, while a chainsaw chain returned to the wrong crew because the tags got mixed up mid-route is two accounts calling to complain in the same afternoon.

The tool maintains a per-account equipment inventory (chain pitch/gauge, blade specs, and preferred bevel angle) tied to a rotating drop-off/pickup schedule; a route board sequencing weekly or biweekly stops with per-stop item counts; and a sharpening-turnaround log flagging any account whose spares are running low before the next scheduled route day.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per technician. Distribution: r/Arborists and r/landscaping trade threads, the Tree Care Industry Association (TCIA) member network, and regional landscaping-contractor and lawn-care Facebook groups where independent sharpening route operators already trade account-scheduling and blade-inventory tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Indoor Climbing & Bouldering Gym Route-Setting Schedule & Hold Inventory Tracker

An independent operator running a small indoor climbing or bouldering gym has to track each wall section's current route-reset date, the specific hold sets used on each problem, and a route-setting crew's rotation schedule, since climbing holds wear smooth and routes go stale, and a gym that doesn't reset sections on a predictable cadence loses the repeat members who come specifically for fresh problems to try. A gym running several walls and a rotating cast of part-time route setters currently tracks each section's last-reset date and hold usage on a whiteboard behind the desk and a setter's personal notes, and a wall left unreset past its usual cycle because nobody flagged the date is member complaints piling up on the gym's social media, while a hold set pulled for one section without checking whether it's already committed to another section's build is a route setter showing up to a work session short on the exact holds the plan called for.

The tool maintains a per-wall-section reset-date tracker with a target-cadence calculator flagging any section overdue for a reset; a hold-set inventory view showing which holds are currently mounted on which section versus available for the next build; and a route-setting crew schedule coordinating setter availability against planned reset sessions.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per gym. Distribution: r/climbing and r/bouldering gym-operator threads, the Climbing Wall Association (CWA) member directory and its annual Climbing Wall Industry Summit, and regional gym-owner Facebook groups where independent operators already trade route-setting-schedule and hold-inventory tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Disc Golf Course Designer & Pro Shop Operator Layout-Commission & Disc Inventory Tracker

An independent disc golf course designer who also runs a small pro shop has to track each course-design commission's hole-by-hole layout spec, basket and tee-pad placement, and land-survey constraints against a client municipality or private-course owner's site plan, since a basket placed without accounting for an easement or a mandatory tree-buffer setback is a redesign the client demands after the course is already built. A designer running several course-design commissions at different stages — site walk, layout draft, client revision rounds, and final stake-out — alongside a pro-shop disc inventory sorted by mold, plastic type, and stability rating currently tracks commission status and stock on a paper site-plan folder and a shop shelf, and a layout revision requested by a client but never logged is a stake-out crew installing the wrong hole placement on install day, while a popular disc mold that sells out during league night without anyone noticing is a shop missing sales on its busiest night of the week.

The tool maintains a per-commission layout tracker (hole-by-hole spec, revision-round history, and stake-out completion status) tied to client sign-off; a pro-shop disc inventory view by mold, plastic, and stability rating flagging low stock before a league-night rush; and a client-facing project-status view showing exactly which design stage a course commission is in.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per designer/shop. Distribution: r/discgolf practitioner and course-design threads, the Professional Disc Golf Association (PDGA) course-designer directory, and regional disc golf club and league Facebook groups where independent designers and shop operators already trade layout-commission and inventory tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Kiteboarding & Kitesurfing Instructor Student Certification & Wind-Window Scheduling Tracker

An independent kiteboarding instructor teaching students toward an International Kiteboarding Organization (IKO) certification level has to track each student's specific skill progression — body-dragging and kite-control fundamentals before board-start attempts, board-start competency before independent riding — alongside real-time wind-speed and direction conditions at the teaching beach, since a lesson attempted outside a safe wind window is a real safety risk with a powered kite, and a student's certification level signed off without the logged water-time to back it up is a credential that doesn't hold up if the student takes a lesson with a different school later. An instructor running a rotating roster of students at different certification stages across a single beach season currently tracks per-student water-time and skill sign-offs on a paper logbook and checks wind conditions on a separate weather app with no link back to which lessons are actually safe to run that day, and a lesson scheduled on a day the wind never settles into the safe teaching range is a wasted beach trip for both instructor and student, while a certification level signed off without enough logged independent-riding time is a rating an instructor may not be able to defend if IKO ever asks for the record.

The tool maintains a per-student skill-progression and water-time log counting logged sessions toward each IKO certification level; an instructor-facing wind-window scheduling board cross-referencing forecasted wind speed and direction against the teaching beach's safe range before a lesson is confirmed; and a certification sign-off record tied to the actual logged water-time supporting each level issued.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per instructor. Distribution: r/kiteboarding and r/Kitesurfing practitioner threads, the IKO-affiliated school and instructor directory, and regional kite-beach Facebook groups where independent instructors already trade wind-window and student-progression tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
