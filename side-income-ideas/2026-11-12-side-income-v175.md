# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-12 (Round 175)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a stand-up comedian, a commission illustrator, a paid tabletop-RPG game master, a mobile coffee cart operator, a live sound engineer and a genealogy researcher.

---

### 1. Stand-Up Comedian Gig, Booker & Pay Tracker

A working comedian has to track every gig: venue, booker, date, pay (flat fee, door split or free), travel cost and whether they were paid. With 8-20 shows a month across several bookers, it is hard to remember who owes money or which rooms pay well. Booker relationships also matter, since the next gig comes from them.

The tool logs gigs with pay and costs, tracks unpaid shows, keeps a booker contact list with last-booked date and shows income per venue and per hour spent.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per comedian. Distribution: comedy communities, r/standupcomedy and open-mic Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Commission Illustrator Queue, Deposit & Revision Tracker

An illustrator who takes paid commissions (character art, pet portraits, book covers) has to track the commission queue, the customer's brief, deposit paid, sketch approval and final delivery. With 10-20 open commissions, a customer asking for the status needs an answer, and unlimited revisions erode the hourly rate. A limited number of open slots is part of the offer.

The tool shows a commission queue with stage and slot count, tracks deposits and revision rounds against a cap and posts a status link for the customer.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per illustrator. Distribution: illustrator communities, r/commissions and artist Discords. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Paid Tabletop-RPG Game Master Session, Player & Campaign Tracker

A paid game master who runs D&D and other campaigns for groups has to track each campaign's players, sessions, story notes, payment per player and scheduling. With 3-6 groups, a missed detail breaks immersion, and a player who falls behind on payment needs a nudge. Scheduling across time zones is a constant task.

The tool stores campaign notes per group, a session calendar with player availability, per-player payment status and a recap generator for the next session.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per game master. Distribution: game-master communities, r/DnD and paid-GM Discords. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Mobile Coffee Cart Event, Inventory & Sales Tracker

A mobile coffee cart operator who works weddings, markets and offices has to track event bookings, milk and syrup stock, cups sold per event and spoilage. Over- or under-stocking an event is costly, and a minimum guarantee from the client changes pricing. Profit per event depends on travel and labor.

The tool tracks bookings with a stock list per event size, records sales and waste per event and shows profit per event and per hour.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per operator. Distribution: coffee-cart and mobile-barista Facebook groups, r/barista and event-vendor networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Live Sound Engineer Gig, Gear & Invoice Tracker

A freelance live sound engineer who mixes bands and events has to track gigs, the gear packed for each venue, load-in times and invoices. Forgetting a cable at the venue costs the gig. Several venues each with specific needs make a packing list valuable, and late-paying venues need chasing.

The tool tracks gigs with load-in time and venue notes, generates a gear checklist per venue and tracks invoices with overdue reminders.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per engineer. Distribution: live-sound communities, r/livesound and local music-scene networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Genealogy Researcher Client Tree, Source & Report Tracker

A freelance genealogist researching family trees for clients has to track each client's research questions, sources searched (records, archives), findings, dead ends and report drafts. With several clients, repeating a search already done wastes hours, and clients ask for progress updates. Hours billed need support.

The tool logs research questions per client, records sources searched with results, tracks hours and drafts a progress summary.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per researcher. Distribution: genealogy communities, r/Genealogy and professional-genealogist Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
