# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-15 (Round 194)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a font designer, a photo-preset seller, a print-on-demand designer, a Twitch streamer, a sheet-music arranger and a UGC video creator.

---

### 1. Font Designer Typeface, License Sale & Request Tracker

An independent type designer has to track typefaces in progress, glyph sets, sales by marketplace and custom-work requests. Customers ask for extra glyphs and languages, and sales are spread across several platforms.

The tool tracks typefaces by stage, logs sales by platform, records custom requests with quotes and shows revenue per family.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per designer. Distribution: type-design communities, r/typography and font-marketplace seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Photo-Preset Seller Pack, Sales & Update Tracker

A photographer who sells editing presets has to track packs, versions, sales by channel, affiliate partners and support questions. Each camera and software update means a pack update.

The tool tracks packs and versions, shows sales by channel, logs support questions and flags packs needing updates.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per seller. Distribution: photographer communities, r/photography and creator Discords. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Print-on-Demand Designer Design, Listing & Sales Tracker

A designer who sells shirts, mugs and posters through print-on-demand services has to track designs, listings across shops, sales per design and trends. A few designs bring most of the sales, but nobody sees which.

The tool tracks designs and where they are listed, shows sales and profit per design and flags winners to extend into new products.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per designer. Distribution: print-on-demand communities, r/printondemand and Etsy seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Twitch Streamer Schedule, Sponsor & Revenue Tracker

A part-time streamer has to track stream schedules, viewer counts, sponsor deals, subscriptions and donations. Sponsors want deliverables on time, and revenue comes from several sources.

The tool logs streams and viewers, tracks sponsor deliverables with deadlines and shows monthly revenue by source.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per streamer. Distribution: streamer communities, r/Twitch and creator Discords. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Sheet-Music Arranger Commission, Revision & Sales Tracker

An arranger who writes custom arrangements for choirs, bands and soloists has to track commissions, instrumentation, revisions, deadlines and catalog sales. Performances create hard deadlines.

The tool tracks commissions with deadlines, counts revision rounds, stores instrumentation notes and shows catalog sales.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per arranger. Distribution: music-arranger communities, r/musictheory and school-band director groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. UGC Video Creator Brand Deal, Deliverable & Payment Tracker

A creator who makes short videos for brands has to track deals, scripts, deliverables, revision rounds, usage terms and payments. Late payments are common, and each brand has different asks.

The tool tracks deals and deliverables by deadline, counts revisions, logs payment due dates and shows income by brand.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per creator. Distribution: UGC-creator communities, r/UGCcreators and creator Discords. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
