# Side Income Ideas - 2026-09-02 (Round 164)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow trackers for six operator types not yet covered in this repo: an independent divorce coach, a kids' coding & robotics instructor, a passport & visa expediting service, an independent leisure/group travel agent, an independent funeral & life-celebration celebrant, and an independent relocation concierge specialist.

---

### 1. Independent Divorce Coach Client Case-Stage, Document Checklist & Session Tracker

A divorce coach who supports clients emotionally and logistically through separation and divorce — distinct from the attorney handling the legal filing — has to track exactly where each client stands in a process that can run anywhere from a few months to over a year, since a coach running 15-25 active clients across different stages (pre-filing decision, active negotiation, mediation, post-decree adjustment) can't rely on memory for which client just finished a hard mediation session versus which one is still deciding whether to file at all, and opening a session with the wrong emotional context is the kind of mistake that damages the trust the entire relationship depends on. A coach also has to track a client-specific document and task checklist — financial disclosures gathered, co-parenting plan drafted, attorney meetings scheduled — since a client juggling this alongside a full-time job and kids needs the coach to be the one keeping the checklist straight, not another source of stress. Because coaching is typically sold as a multi-session package or a monthly retainer rather than a single appointment, the coach also needs a simple way to track each client's remaining sessions and flag a renewal conversation before the package quietly runs out mid-process.

The tool maintains a per-client case-stage tracker (pre-filing, negotiation, mediation, finalized, post-decree) with private session notes; a document-and-task checklist per client tied to that stage; and a session-package balance tracker flagging renewal-due clients before their block runs out.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per coach. Distribution: the International Association of Divorce Coaches member directory, r/Divorce coach-recommendation threads, and independent-divorce-coach Facebook and LinkedIn groups where coaches already trade case-stage and client-retention tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Kids' Coding & Robotics Instructor Student Project, Curriculum & Class-Roster Tracker

An independent instructor teaching kids' coding, Scratch/Python, and robotics (LEGO Mindstorms, VEX) classes through after-school programs, summer camps, and private lessons has to track each student's current project and curriculum milestone, since an instructor running 20-40 students across staggered skill levels and age groups can't rely on memory for which student just finished their first working robot arm versus which one is still stuck debugging a loop, and restarting a student's session by re-explaining a concept they already mastered is a lesson that feels wasted to a parent paying by the hour. An instructor running multiple concurrent class sections (a Tuesday after-school group, a Saturday robotics team, a private one-on-one) also has to track which students are enrolled in which section and what curriculum unit that specific section is on, since a sub-in instructor or the operator themselves needs to know at a glance what today's group covered last time without digging through a notebook. Because instructors are commonly paid per class block (an 8-week session, a summer camp week) rather than per single lesson, they also need a simple way to track enrollment and remaining sessions per student to flag a re-enrollment conversation before a session block ends.

The tool maintains a per-student project and curriculum-milestone tracker with session notes; a class-roster and section-schedule board showing which students are enrolled in which section and that section's current curriculum unit; and a session-block balance tracker flagging re-enrollment-due students before their block runs out.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per instructor, scaling to $49/mo for a small multi-instructor coding school. Distribution: the Code.org educator community, r/coding_bootcamp and STEM-educator Facebook groups, and local VEX Robotics and FIRST Robotics coach networks where independent instructors already trade curriculum-pacing tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent Passport & Visa Expediting Service Application Status, Document & Rush-Deadline Tracker

An independent passport and visa expediting agent who helps clients navigate rushed applications ahead of imminent travel has to track exactly which documents each client has submitted against the specific application type's requirements, since a passport renewal, a first-time passport, and a foreign-country visa application each require a different document set (proof of citizenship, photo specs, consular fees, appointment confirmations), and an expediter running a dozen concurrent rush cases can't rely on memory for which client's birth certificate is still missing versus which one is fully packaged and ready to submit. An expediter also has to track each case's hard travel deadline and the specific processing tier purchased (standard, expedited, life-or-death emergency), since a client's flight date is the entire reason they're paying a premium fee, and a case that slips past its processing window because the expediter lost track of the deadline is a client who now can't travel at all. Because expediting work involves physically walking documents to a regional passport agency or courier-shipping them to a consulate, the expediter also needs a simple way to track where each client's original documents physically are — in the office safe, at the agency counter, in transit back to the client — since a lost passport or birth certificate mid-process is a liability nightmare.

The tool maintains a per-client application tracker (application type, document checklist, and consular/agency appointment details) with a submission-completeness flag; a hard-deadline countdown per case tied to the client's travel date and processing tier purchased; and a document custody log showing exactly where each client's original paperwork physically is at every stage.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per expediter. Distribution: the National Association of Registered Agents and the American Society of Travel Advisors (ASTA) expediting-service referral network, r/travel rush-passport threads, and independent-expediter Facebook groups where operators already trade regional-agency appointment-availability and deadline-tracking tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Leisure & Group Travel Agent Client Itinerary, Booking & Commission Tracker

An independent leisure travel agent working through a host agency to book honeymoons, family vacations, and group trips has to track each client's multi-vendor itinerary — flights, hotels, excursions, and travel insurance booked across several different suppliers — since a single trip can involve five or six separate confirmation numbers and an agent managing dozens of active bookings across a busy season can't rely on memory for which supplier still owes a final payment versus which leg is fully paid and confirmed. An agent also has to track each booking's commission status, since travel agent commissions are paid by the supplier on its own schedule (often 30-90 days after the client's trip completes) rather than at time of booking, and an agent with no structured way to reconcile expected commission against what's actually been received has no way to catch a supplier that simply never pays out. Because group trips (destination weddings, multi-family reunions) involve coordinating a dozen or more individual travelers against one shared itinerary and a rolling deposit deadline, the agent also needs a simple way to track which travelers in a group have paid their deposit and final balance before the group's payment deadline with the supplier arrives.

The tool maintains a per-client itinerary tracker spanning multiple suppliers and confirmation numbers with payment-status flags; a commission tracker reconciling expected commission per booking against what's actually been received, by supplier and expected payout date; and a group-trip roster tracking each traveler's deposit and final-payment status against the group's supplier deadline.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per agent, scaling to $69/mo for a small multi-agent host-agency team. Distribution: the American Society of Travel Advisors (ASTA) member forums, host-agency (Nexion, Avoya) independent-agent Facebook groups, and r/TravelAgent threads where agents already trade commission-reconciliation and group-booking-deadline tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Funeral & Life-Celebration Celebrant Ceremony Script, Family-Interview & Booking Tracker

An independent funeral and life-celebration celebrant who writes and officiates personalized memorial services — distinct from a funeral home's own staff chaplain and from a wedding officiant — has to track detailed family-interview notes for each ceremony, since crafting a genuinely personal eulogy and service structure depends on specific stories, family dynamics, and the deceased's own wishes gathered in a single emotionally difficult conversation with a grieving family, and a celebrant working several services a week can't rely on memory to keep one family's details from bleeding into another's script by mistake. A celebrant also has to track each ceremony's specific logistics — venue, requested readings or music, family members speaking, and any cultural or religious elements to incorporate — against a service date that is almost always set on short notice, since a celebrant booked with only a few days' turnaround has no room for a scheduling mixup with a funeral home's own service calendar. Because celebrants are typically paid a flat fee per ceremony and often work with several funeral homes as a referral source, the celebrant also needs a simple way to track which funeral home referred which booking and whether that ceremony's invoice has been paid.

The tool maintains a per-family interview and story-notes record feeding directly into a ceremony script draft; a per-ceremony logistics checklist (venue, readings, speakers, cultural elements) tied to the confirmed service date; and a referral-source and invoice-status tracker by funeral home.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per celebrant. Distribution: the In-Sight Institute and Celebrant Foundation & Institute graduate directories, the InSided Life-Cycle Celebrant network, and independent-celebrant Facebook groups where celebrants already trade family-interview and funeral-home-referral relationship tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Independent Relocation Concierge Specialist Client Move Timeline, Vendor & Destination-Orientation Tracker

An independent relocation concierge who coordinates a corporate transferee's or a self-funded family's cross-country move has to track a tight, multi-vendor timeline — moving company pickup and delivery windows, temporary housing dates, utility transfer and cancellation deadlines at the old address, and new-utility setup at the destination — since a single relocation can involve six or more vendors on overlapping schedules and a concierge managing a dozen active relocations at once can't rely on memory for whose moving truck is scheduled this week versus whose temporary housing lease is about to expire before their new home closes. A concierge also has to track each client's destination-area orientation needs — school district research, neighborhood tours, DMV and voter-registration appointments — since a family relocating for a new job with kids starting school in two weeks needs these scheduled well ahead of the move date, not scrambled together after the truck arrives. Because relocation work is commonly billed either as a flat package fee from a corporate HR client or as an hourly concierge rate for a self-funded family, the concierge also needs a simple way to track billable hours or package-milestone completion per client to invoice accurately without reconstructing the timeline from memory at month's end.

The tool maintains a per-client move timeline spanning every vendor's pickup, delivery, and utility-transfer deadline with an overdue flag; a destination-orientation checklist (schools, neighborhood tours, DMV/registration appointments) tied to the client's move-in date; and a billing tracker reconciling billable hours or package milestones per client against invoices sent.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per concierge, scaling to $69/mo for a small multi-concierge relocation firm. Distribution: the Worldwide Employee Relocation Council (ERC) member directory, corporate HR relocation-benefits vendor networks, and independent-relocation-specialist Facebook and LinkedIn groups where concierges already trade vendor-coordination and destination-orientation tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
