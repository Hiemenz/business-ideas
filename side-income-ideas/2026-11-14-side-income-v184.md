# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-14 (Round 184)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a vending-machine operator, a meal-prep cook, a pet-portrait artist, a drone photographer, a hair braider and a plant-nursery reseller.

---

### 1. Vending-Machine Operator Route, Restock & Margin Tracker

An operator with 5-30 machines has to track each machine's location, stock levels, items sold, spoilage and commission to the host. Visiting a machine that does not need restocking wastes a trip, and slow items tie up cash.

The tool logs restocks and sales per machine, flags machines due for a visit, shows margin per item and builds a shopping list for the route.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per operator. Distribution: vending-operator Facebook groups, r/vending and local business-owner boards. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Meal-Prep Cook Weekly Order, Batch & Delivery Tracker

A person who cooks weekly meals for neighbors has to track each client's plan, dietary preferences, portion counts, grocery needs and delivery slot. Wrong portions or a forgotten dislike lose clients, and ingredient costs drift.

The tool collects weekly orders, totals the grocery list and batch quantities, schedules delivery and shows margin per plan.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per cook. Distribution: meal-prep Facebook groups, r/MealPrepSunday and local neighborhood boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Pet-Portrait Artist Commission, Reference & Shipping Tracker

An artist who paints or draws pet portraits has to track each commission's pet photos, size, style, deposit, progress updates and shipping. Holiday gifting creates a rush with hard dates, and clients need preview approvals.

The tool tracks commissions with references and approvals, counts down to the holiday cutoff, records deposits and sends progress and tracking updates.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per artist. Distribution: art-commission communities, r/commissions and Etsy seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to the holidays.

### 4. Drone Photographer Shoot, Flight & Delivery Tracker

A drone photographer for real estate, roofs and events has to track each job's location, flight window, battery plan, files delivered and invoice. Weather reschedules are common, and clients want a delivery date.

The tool tracks jobs with weather-hold rescheduling, a shot checklist per job type, delivery links and payment status.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per pilot. Distribution: drone-pilot communities, r/drones and real-estate photographer groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Hair Braider Appointment, Style & Deposit Tracker

A braider whose sessions run 3-8 hours has to track each client's chosen style, hair length, hair-supply notes, deposit and appointment length. A no-show wastes a full day, and quotes depend on style and length.

The tool books by style-based duration, collects deposits, stores each client's style history and sends reminders with prep instructions.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per braider. Distribution: braider communities, r/BlackHair and local salon-suite groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Plant-Nursery Reseller Stock, Sale & Care-Note Tracker

A person who buys wholesale plants and resells at markets or online has to track purchase lots, losses, price per plant and sales channel. Plants die, and slow varieties waste cash. Buyers appreciate care notes with each sale.

The tool tracks lots and survival rate, shows margin per variety, flags aging stock for a discount and sends care notes to buyers.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per reseller. Distribution: plant-seller communities, r/houseplants market threads and Facebook plant groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
