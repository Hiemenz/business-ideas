# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-20 (Session 101)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- Simple, fast-to-build workflow trackers — no regulatory compliance angle.
- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.

### 1. Independent Farrier Shoeing-Interval & Hoof-Condition Tracker

Independent farriers trimming and shoeing horses across a roster of boarding stables, private barns, and equestrian facilities need to track a per-horse shoeing-interval record with an auto-calculated next-due date, since most working horses need attention every 6-8 weeks and a farrier servicing 80-150 horses across a scattered client base can't rely on memory to know which barn's horses are due this week versus which were just trimmed a month ago. The tool also needs a per-hoof condition log noting gait issues, corrective shoeing needs, and any lameness flag that a veterinarian should be looped in on, since a farrier who doesn't track a specific horse's recurring hoof problem across visits ends up re-diagnosing the same issue from scratch every time. It also needs a barn/stable route board grouping horses by physical location rather than a home address, since a farrier's client roster is scattered across boarding facilities where several unrelated horse owners' animals live at the same stable, and an efficient route depends on grouping by barn rather than by owner. Most independent farriers currently manage this across a paper index card per horse and a rough memory of shoeing intervals, with no structured link between a specific horse's hoof-condition history and its actual next-due date.

The tool maintains a per-horse shoeing-interval tracker with an auto-calculated next-due date, a per-hoof condition/lameness log across visits, and a barn-based route board grouping horses by stable location.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the American Farriers Association (AFA) member directory, r/farriers, and boarding-stable and equine-vet referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Mobile Dog Groomer Pet-Profile & Route Tracker

Independent mobile dog groomers running a van-based grooming service to residential clients need to track a per-pet grooming profile recording breed, coat type, temperament notes, and last-groomed date, since a groomer running a recurring route of 60-120 dogs can't rely on memory to know which double-coated breed is due for its 6-week deshedding cut versus which short-haired dog only needs an occasional bath and nail trim. The tool also needs a route board grouping recurring residential stops by neighborhood and service-day cadence, since a mobile groomer working a dispersed client base needs an efficient driving loop rather than crisscrossing the same area across separate visits. It also needs a vaccination-reminder field per pet, since most mobile groomers require a current rabies certificate on file and a groomer arriving to find an expired vaccination record has to turn away a scheduled job. Most independent mobile groomers currently manage this across a paper appointment book and a rough memory of which dog's coat needs which cut, with no structured link between a specific pet's grooming history and the technician's actual route for the day.

The tool maintains a per-pet grooming profile (breed, coat type, temperament, last-groomed date), a route board grouping recurring stops by neighborhood and cadence, and a vaccination-record reminder field per pet.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/doggrooming and local mobile-groomer Facebook groups, the National Dog Groomers Association of America (NDGAA) member directory, and veterinary-clinic referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Freelance Bridal & Special-Effects Makeup Artist Trial & Kit Tracker

Independent bridal and special-effects makeup artists booking trial sessions ahead of weddings, photoshoots, and film/theater work need to track a per-client trial record — consultation notes, trial-application look, and the confirmed event-day booking — since a bridal client typically pays for a trial weeks before the wedding and expects the artist to replicate that exact look on the day, and an artist juggling 5-10 concurrent bookings across a busy wedding season can't rely on memory to know which client's trial notes call for a matte base versus which requested a dewy finish. The tool also needs a kit and product inventory tracked by shade, brand, and expiration date, since makeup products degrade and a booked event can't be serviced with an expired foundation or mascara, and an artist needs to know what's running low before confirming a new booking. It also needs an event-day timeline noting call time, travel buffer, and touch-up kit contents, since a late arrival or a missing touch-up item on wedding day can't be redone. Most independent makeup artists currently manage this across a phone full of trial photos and a paper client list, with no structured link between a specific client's trial notes and the exact products used to achieve that look.

The tool maintains a per-client trial tracker (consultation, trial look notes, event-day booking), a kit/product inventory by shade, brand, and expiration date, and an event-day timeline with call time and touch-up kit checklist.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/MUA and r/weddingplanning vendor threads, the National Cosmetology Association (NCA) member network, and bridal-boutique and wedding-planner referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Junk Removal & Hauling Operator Job & Disposal-Route Tracker

Independent junk removal and hauling operators clearing out homes, garages, and offices need to track a per-job estimate-and-inventory record — item list, truck-load volume estimate, quoted price, and before/after photos — since most jobs are quoted on-site the same day and an operator needs a documented record if a customer later disputes what was actually hauled or the price charged. The tool also needs a route/load board sequencing same-day pickups by truck capacity and drive time, since an operator running 4-8 jobs a day needs to plan which loads can be combined into one truck run versus which require a separate trip. It also needs a disposal-site tracker noting which load went to the landfill, a recycling center, or a donation drop-off, since a single truckload is routinely split across multiple disposal destinations and an operator needs a record of where specific items ended up, both for the customer's own accounting and for the operator's own disposal-cost tracking. Most independent junk haulers currently manage this across a paper estimate slip and a rough memory of which truckload went where, with no structured link between a specific job's inventory and its actual disposal destination.

The tool maintains a per-job estimate/inventory tracker (item list, volume estimate, price, before/after photos), a route/load board sequencing same-day pickups, and a disposal-site tracker per load.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/JunkRemoval and local Facebook Marketplace hauler groups, local waste-hauler and property-management-company referral relationships, plus HomeAdvisor/Angi contractor forums where independent haulers already coordinate job leads. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Welding & Fabrication Technician Job & Consumable-Inventory Tracker

Independent mobile welders performing on-site repair and fabrication work — gate and fence repair, trailer welding, agricultural equipment fixes — need to track a per-job record (site visit, material needed, work performed, follow-up needed), since jobs range from a 30-minute trailer-hitch weld to a multi-day custom gate fabrication, and a welder juggling both same-day service calls and longer fabrication projects can't rely on memory to know which job is fully complete versus which is waiting on a follow-up visit to finish a weld that needs to cool and be inspected. The tool also needs a welding-rod, wire, and sheet-metal-stock inventory sized to the week's scheduled jobs, since a mobile welder arriving without the right rod type or gauge of stock wastes a trip back to the shop mid-route. It also needs a route board for on-site service calls, since a welder running multiple job sites in a day needs an efficient route rather than backtracking across a service area. Most independent mobile welders currently manage this across a paper work order and a rough memory of what's stocked in the truck, with no structured link between a specific job's material needs and the technician's actual current inventory.

The tool maintains a per-job tracker (site visit, materials needed, work performed, follow-up), a consumable/stock inventory sized to scheduled jobs, and a route board for on-site service calls.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/Welding and r/farming equipment-repair threads, the American Welding Society (AWS) member directory, and agricultural-equipment dealer referral relationships for on-farm repair calls. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Independent Estate Sale Organizer & Liquidator Inventory & Commission Tracker

Independent estate sale organizers and liquidators running multi-day sales for a deceased or downsizing client's family need to track a per-sale item-and-pricing inventory — category, appraised or listed price, and sold status — since a single estate sale can list hundreds of individually priced items across furniture, collectibles, and household goods over a 2-3 day event, and an organizer running 2-4 concurrent sales can't rely on memory to know which items sold on day one versus what's still on the floor for markdown pricing on day two. The tool also needs a client contract and commission-split tracker, since liquidators typically work on a percentage-of-proceeds basis and need a clear, exportable accounting back to the estate's executor or family showing gross sales, the organizer's commission, and net proceeds. It also needs a before/after staging photo log per space, since organizers rely on documented before/after transformations both for the sale listing itself and for building a portfolio to win future estate referrals. Most independent estate liquidators currently manage this across a paper price-tag system and a spreadsheet reconciled by hand after the sale closes, with no structured link between a specific item's sold status and the commission accounting owed to the client — a fundamentally different problem from a professional organizer's decluttering project, since the estate liquidator is selling the client's belongings to a paying public rather than helping the client keep and reorganize their own.

The tool maintains a per-sale item-and-pricing inventory with sold-status tracking, a client contract/commission-split accounting log, and a before/after staging photo log per space.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/EstateSale and the American Society of Estate Liquidators (ASEL) member directory, plus probate-attorney and senior-move-manager referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
