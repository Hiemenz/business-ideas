# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-31 (Round 155)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow trackers for six operator types not yet covered in this repo: a custom sneaker artist, a public speaking and presentation skills coach, an independent small-batch spice blend maker, an independent patient advocate, an independent running and triathlon coach, and an independent special education (IEP) advocate.

---

### 1. Custom Sneaker Artist & Restoration Studio Job, Materials & Turnaround Tracker

An independent custom sneaker artist who hand-paints, dyes, and restores sneakers for individual collectors and resellers has to track each job's exact spec — the client's reference design, the specific paint or dye system and color-matching formula used on a given shoe's material (leather, suede, mesh each take a different prep and product), and whether the job is a full custom paint job versus a restoration bringing a yellowed sole or scuffed upper back to original condition — since an artist juggling a dozen concurrent pairs can't rely on memory for which pair is still curing versus ready to ship, and shipping a client the wrong color-matched pair or a job that skipped a prep step is a ruined pair of often-expensive sneakers and a public callout on social media where this business gets most of its referrals. An artist also has to track a paint, dye, and prep-material inventory by color and brand, since running out of a specific base coat mid-job on a rush custom order means missing the client's promised turnaround date, and needs a per-order deposit-and-balance tracker since custom work is typically billed with an upfront deposit and a final payment on completion or before shipping.

The tool maintains a per-job intake record (client reference images, shoe material and size, color-match formula, and job type — custom paint vs. restoration) tied to a build-stage tracker (prepped, base coat, detail work, sealed/cured, shipped); a paint/dye/prep-material inventory by color and brand with a low-stock alert; and a per-order deposit-and-final-balance tracker.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per artist. Distribution: r/sneakercustomization and r/CustomSneakers threads, and custom-sneaker-artist Instagram and Facebook communities where independent artists already source client referrals and trade technique and product-sourcing tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Public Speaking & Presentation Skills Coach Client Goal, Session-Feedback & Speech-Draft Tracker

An independent public speaking and presentation skills coach — working with executives prepping for a keynote, professionals building general confidence, or clients prepping for a specific high-stakes talk — has to track each client's specific goals and recurring trouble spots (a client who rushes through nervous energy versus one who needs help structuring a talk's narrative arc), a session-by-session feedback log building on what was worked on last time, and, for clients prepping a specific speech or presentation, the current draft version and delivery-date countdown, since a coach running a full roster of recurring clients can't rely on memory for which client already worked through their opening hook versus which one is still stuck on it, and a coach walking into a session without a clear recap of the last one comes across as generic rather than personally invested in a business built entirely on that perception. A coach also needs a simple way to log video-recorded practice-run notes against an earlier recording, since a client paying for ongoing coaching wants to see concrete, comparable progress rather than take the coach's word for it.

The tool maintains a per-client goal and recurring-trouble-spot profile with session-by-session feedback notes; a per-speech draft-version and delivery-date tracker for clients prepping a specific talk; and a practice-recording log comparing an early run against a recent one so a coach can show measurable improvement in a session.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per coach. Distribution: the National Speakers Association (NSA) member directory and LinkedIn communities for corporate communication and executive-presence coaches, where independent coaches already build client rosters through professional referrals. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent Small-Batch Spice Blend & Seasoning Maker Recipe, Batch & Wholesale-Account Tracker

An independent small-batch spice blend and seasoning maker selling custom rub and blend recipes at farmers markets, through a cottage-food storefront, and to a small roster of restaurant and specialty-shop wholesale accounts has to track each recipe's exact formula (ingredient ratios, sourcing notes for a specific chili variety or salt type, and any allergen flags like mustard or celery seed) and the batch log for every production run — lot number, batch size, and the specific ingredient lots that went into it — since a maker scaling a recipe from a home-kitchen test batch to a 20-pound production run needs the ratios to hold exactly, and a customer allergic reaction traced back to an unlabeled ingredient substitution with no batch-to-ingredient-lot record is both a health scare and a liability the maker has no documentation to defend against. A maker running recurring wholesale accounts alongside market and online direct sales also needs a per-account reorder tracker, since a restaurant account running low on a specific blend needs a heads-up before they run out mid-service, and a simple way to see which wholesale accounts are current on payment versus which invoices are outstanding.

The tool maintains a per-recipe formula and allergen-flag record; a per-batch production log linking lot number, batch size, and ingredient-lot sourcing back to the recipe; and a per-wholesale-account reorder and invoice-payment tracker.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per maker. Distribution: the American Spice Trade Association's small-producer resources and r/spicetrade and cottage-food-maker Facebook groups, and local farmers market vendor networks where independent spice makers already source wholesale referrals. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Independent Patient Advocate & Healthcare Navigator Client Case, Appointment & Insurance-Appeal Tracker

An independent patient advocate — hired directly by patients or their families to help navigate a complex diagnosis, coordinate care across multiple specialists, and fight insurance denials — has to track each client's full care picture: the specialists involved and their most recent notes or recommendations, an upcoming-appointment calendar the advocate often attends alongside the client, and a running case-history timeline, since an advocate juggling a dozen active clients with different diagnoses can't rely on memory for which client's oncologist just changed a treatment plan versus which one is waiting on a second opinion, and walking into an appointment without the latest case context undermines the exact trust-based relationship this business depends on. An advocate fighting insurance denials and coverage disputes also needs a per-claim appeal tracker — the denial reason, the appeal deadline (often a strict 30- or 60-day window), and the current appeal stage (first-level internal appeal, external review) — since missing an appeal deadline while juggling several clients' overlapping cases can permanently close off a client's only path to getting a treatment covered.

The tool maintains a per-client case file (diagnosis, specialists involved, and a running case-history timeline) linked to an appointment calendar with pre-visit prep notes; and a per-claim insurance-appeal tracker logging denial reason, appeal deadline, and current appeal stage with an overdue-deadline alert.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per advocate. Distribution: the Patient Advocate Certification Board (PACB) directory and the Alliance of Professional Health Advocates member community, where independent advocates already source client referrals and trade case-management workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Running & Triathlon Coach Athlete Training-Plan, Race-Calendar & Workout-Log Tracker

An independent running and triathlon coach building periodized training plans for recreational and competitive age-group athletes has to track each athlete's specific training block (base building, threshold work, taper) mapped against their target race date, since a coach running 15-30 athletes across staggered race calendars can't rely on memory for which athlete is three weeks from a marathon versus which one just started a base-building block after an injury layoff. A coach also has to log each athlete's actual completed workouts against the planned ones — pace, heart rate zone, and how the athlete reported feeling — since adjusting next week's plan without knowing whether last week's key workout actually got done, or got done at a much harder effort than prescribed, is exactly the kind of blind coaching decision that leads to overtraining or a missed fitness gain, and a coach juggling multiple athletes' training logs across a spreadsheet and a fitness-app export has no single view flagging who's behind on their plan before race day arrives.

The tool maintains a per-athlete periodized training-plan and race-calendar tracker; a workout-log comparing planned versus completed sessions (pace, heart-rate zone, perceived effort) with an at-a-glance behind-on-plan flag; and a per-athlete package or monthly-coaching-fee billing tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per coach. Distribution: the USA Triathlon (USAT) certified-coach directory and RRCA (Road Runners Club of America) certified-coach network, where independent coaches already source athlete referrals through certification-body listings. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Independent Special Education (IEP) Advocate Client Case, Meeting & Document-Deadline Tracker

An independent special education advocate — hired by parents to help navigate the IEP (Individualized Education Program) process, attend school meetings, and push back on a district's proposed services — has to track each family's case file: the child's current IEP goals and services, the evaluation and re-evaluation timelines school districts are legally required to meet, and a running history of every meeting and what was agreed to, since an advocate juggling a dozen active families across different districts and school years can't rely on memory for which child's triennial re-evaluation is coming due versus which family is mid-dispute over a proposed service reduction. An advocate also needs a document-deadline tracker for the specific procedural timelines that structure this work — a district's response window after a written request for an IEP meeting or an independent educational evaluation, and a state's due-process filing deadline if a dispute escalates — since missing one of these procedural windows can weaken or forfeit a family's position entirely, and an advocate relying on a notebook per family has no systematic way to see every family's next deadline across an entire active caseload at a glance.

The tool maintains a per-family case file (child's current IEP goals/services and evaluation timeline) with a meeting-history log recording what was discussed and agreed to at each meeting; a procedural-deadline tracker for district response windows and due-process filing deadlines with an overdue alert; and a per-family engagement and invoice tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per advocate. Distribution: the Council of Parent Attorneys and Advocates (COPAA) member directory and state special-education-advocate networks and Facebook groups, where independent advocates already source client referrals through parent-support communities. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
