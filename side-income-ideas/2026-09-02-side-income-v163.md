# Side Income Ideas - 2026-09-02 (Round 163)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow trackers for six operator types not yet covered in this repo: an independent mobile phone & computer repair technician, a TV mounting & smart home installer, an e-bike & electric scooter repair technician, an independent marine mechanic, a roll-off dumpster rental route operator, and an independent drone repair technician.

---

### 1. Independent Mobile Phone & Computer Repair Technician Device Intake, Parts & Warranty Tracker

An independent technician repairing cracked screens, batteries, and water damage on phones, tablets, and laptops out of a small shop or a mobile van has to track each device's specific intake condition and diagnosis, since a shop juggling a dozen open repairs at once can't rely on memory for which cracked-screen iPhone also needs a battery replacement versus which one is a straightforward glass-only job, and handing a customer back the wrong device or missing a second issue found mid-repair is a callback that costs the shop both the redo labor and the customer's trust. The technician also has to track parts inventory against open jobs, since a repair quoted for same-day turnaround that stalls because the specific screen or battery model wasn't actually in stock is a broken promise that drives a customer to a competitor, and a technician stocking dozens of screen and battery SKUs across multiple phone and laptop models needs to know at a glance which parts are running low before the next repair gets quoted. Because most repairs carry a 30-90 day warranty against the same part failing again, the technician also needs a simple way to track which completed repairs are still inside their warranty window, since a customer returning with the same issue eight weeks later needs an immediate answer about whether the fix is covered, not a technician digging through paper tickets to find the original job.

The tool maintains a per-device intake and repair-ticket log (model, diagnosis, parts used, and status) with a photo of the device's condition at intake; a parts inventory tracker flagging low-stock SKUs against currently open and commonly repaired models; and a per-repair warranty tracker showing which completed jobs are still inside their coverage window.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per technician, scaling to $49/mo for a small multi-technician shop. Distribution: r/framework and r/talesfromtechsupport independent-repair-shop threads, the Cellular Telecommunications Industry Association (CTIA) repair-network forums, and independent phone-repair Facebook and Discord groups where technicians already trade parts-sourcing and warranty-tracking tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. TV Mounting, Home Theater & Smart Home Installer Job-Spec, Equipment & Callback Tracker

An independent installer who mounts TVs and sets up home theater and smart home systems (whole-home audio, smart locks, video doorbells, networked cameras) has to track each job's exact wall-construction type and mounting hardware, since a TV mounted into drywall without confirming stud locations or using the right anchor for a plaster or brick wall is the kind of mistake that shows up as a customer's TV pulling loose weeks later, and an installer running several jobs a day across different home types can't rely on memory for which house needed a specialty anchor kit versus which one had studs in easy reach. The installer also has to track each smart-home device's network configuration and the specific app or hub it's paired to, since a customer calling three weeks later because their video doorbell stopped connecting needs the installer to know exactly what was configured — router model, Wi-Fi band, hub pairing — without a return site visit just to figure out what was installed. Because installers commonly bundle a labor warranty covering mounting hardware and wiring for a set period after the job, the installer also needs a simple way to track which completed jobs are still inside that warranty window and which have an outstanding callback request, since a customer complaint that sits unanswered for a week is a referral that doesn't happen.

The tool maintains a per-job spec sheet (wall type, mounting hardware, and cable-run notes) with a device-configuration log per smart-home component installed; a warranty-window tracker per completed job; and a callback/service-request queue flagging any customer issue awaiting a return visit.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per installer. Distribution: r/homeautomation and r/hometheater installer-services threads, TaskRabbit and Thumbtack pro forums where independent installers already coordinate job logistics, and local smart-home installer Facebook groups where operators trade wall-anchor and device-pairing tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. E-Bike & Electric Scooter Repair Technician Battery, Diagnostic & Parts-Sourcing Tracker

An independent technician repairing e-bikes and electric scooters has to track each unit's specific battery health and charge-cycle history, since a battery pack that's degraded past a safe capacity threshold is both a performance complaint and a fire-safety risk the technician is responsible for catching before handing the bike back, and a shop servicing dozens of units across a dozen different manufacturers can't rely on memory for which customer's battery was already flagged as marginal at the last visit. The technician also has to track each repair's diagnostic findings against the specific make and model's known failure patterns — a controller fault on one popular brand behaves differently from a motor-hall-sensor fault on another — since misdiagnosing the issue on a first visit means a second trip and a customer who now doubts the shop's competence. Because e-bike and scooter parts are frequently sourced from the original manufacturer, a third-party importer, or a salvaged donor unit with weeks-long shipping delays depending on the brand, the technician also needs a simple parts-backorder tracker showing exactly what's on order and its expected arrival, so a customer asking "when will my bike be ready" gets a specific answer instead of a guess.

The tool maintains a per-unit battery health and charge-cycle log with a safety-flag threshold; a per-repair diagnostic tracker referencing known failure patterns by make and model; and a parts-backorder tracker per job showing source, order date, and expected arrival.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per technician. Distribution: r/ebikes and r/ElectricScooters repair-shop threads, the Light Electric Vehicle Association (LEVA) technician network, and independent e-bike-shop Facebook groups where technicians already trade battery-diagnostics and parts-sourcing tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Marine Mechanic & Outboard Motor Repair Technician Job, Parts & Seasonal Haul-In Tracker

An independent marine mechanic servicing outboard motors, stern drives, and small watercraft engines for boat owners and marinas has to track each engine's service history and manufacturer-specific maintenance intervals, since a two-stroke outboard's carburetor-and-fuel-system needs look nothing like a four-stroke's valve-adjustment schedule, and a mechanic running a route across dozens of client boats can't rely on memory for which engine is due for a water-pump impeller replacement versus which one just had its lower unit serviced. The mechanic also has to manage a sharply seasonal intake surge — boat owners bringing engines in for spring commissioning after winter storage, then again for fall winterization before haul-out — where a shop relying on handwritten drop-off tags loses track of which engine came in first during the two- to three-week rush windows that bookend boating season in most climates. Because outboard and stern-drive parts for older or less common engine models can take weeks to arrive from a manufacturer or specialty marine-parts distributor, the mechanic also needs a per-job parts-backorder tracker, since a boat owner eager to get back on the water before a holiday weekend needs a specific, honest answer about when their engine will actually be ready.

The tool maintains a per-engine service-history and maintenance-interval log by make and model; a seasonal intake queue for spring commissioning and fall winterization keeping first-in-first-out order visible during rush periods; and a parts-backorder tracker per job showing source and expected arrival.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per mechanic, scaling to $49/mo for a small marina-affiliated shop. Distribution: r/boating and r/MarineMechanic repair-shop threads, the American Boat & Yacht Council (ABYC) technician network, and independent marine-mechanic Facebook groups where technicians already trade seasonal-surge and parts-sourcing tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks (timed ahead of spring commissioning season).

### 5. Independent Roll-Off Dumpster Rental Route Operator Container, Drop-Off & Tipping-Fee Tracker

An independent operator running a small fleet of roll-off dumpsters for residential cleanouts, construction sites, and small renovation jobs has to track exactly which container is sitting at which customer address and when it's due for pickup, since a route operator running 15-40 containers scattered across job sites can't rely on memory for which bin has been sitting full for two weeks past its rental period versus which one was just dropped yesterday, and a container left too long at a site is a customer complaint about blocked driveway access as much as it is lost rental revenue on a bin that isn't cycling back into the fleet. The operator also has to track each container's destination-landfill or transfer-station tipping fee and weight ticket, since fees vary by facility and material type (construction debris, yard waste, mixed junk) and a customer billed the wrong overage rate because the weight ticket wasn't matched to the right drop-off is a dispute that erodes margin on every load. Because most jobs are quoted as a flat rental rate with an included weight allowance and a per-ton overage charge above that, the operator also needs a simple way to reconcile each completed job's actual dump-ticket weight against the quoted allowance before generating a final invoice, since guessing at overage charges from memory either under-bills a heavy load or overcharges a customer who then disputes the bill.

The tool maintains a per-container location and rental-period tracker flagging any bin overdue for pickup; a per-job tipping-fee and weight-ticket log matched to the destination facility and material type; and an invoice reconciliation tracker comparing actual dump-ticket weight against the quoted allowance before billing.

Monetization: $19-35/mo per operator, scaling with fleet size. Distribution: r/smallbusiness and r/Construction dumpster-rental-startup threads, the National Waste & Recycling Association (NWRA) independent-hauler network, and roll-off-operator Facebook groups where operators already trade container-utilization and tipping-fee negotiation tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 6. Independent Drone Repair & Refurbishment Technician Intake, Parts & Calibration Tracker

An independent technician repairing crashed and malfunctioning consumer and commercial drones has to track each unit's specific damage assessment and repair plan, since a shop handling repairs across dozens of drone models — from consumer photography drones to commercial mapping and inspection units — can't rely on memory for which unit needs a gimbal replacement versus which one needs a full frame rebuild and a firmware recalibration, and a drone handed back without a proper post-repair calibration flight-test is a customer's next crash waiting to happen, one the shop will be blamed for regardless of the original cause. The technician also has to track parts sourcing across original-manufacturer parts, third-party compatible components, and salvaged donor units, since some older or commercial-grade drone models have parts with multi-week lead times, and a customer who was quoted a one-week turnaround deserves an honest update the moment a backordered part pushes that date out. Because commercial-drone customers (surveyors, real estate photographers, agricultural operators) often need a loaner unit to keep working while their drone is in the shop, the technician also needs a simple way to track which loaner units are currently checked out and to which customer, so the shop doesn't lose track of its own loaner fleet during a busy repair season.

The tool maintains a per-unit intake and damage-assessment log with a repair-plan checklist ending in a required post-repair calibration test; a parts-sourcing tracker across manufacturer, third-party, and salvage sources with backorder status; and a loaner-drone checkout tracker showing which units are out and to which customer.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per technician, scaling to $49/mo for a shop also servicing commercial fleets. Distribution: r/drones repair-services threads, the Commercial Drone Alliance member network, and independent-drone-repair Facebook and Discord groups where technicians already trade parts-sourcing and calibration tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
