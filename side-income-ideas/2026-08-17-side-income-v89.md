## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

### 1. Independent Bike Fitting Specialist Client Body-Measurement & Fit-Spec Tracker

Independent bike fitters who run dedicated fit studios or mobile fit services need to track a per-client body-measurement and fit-spec record capturing saddle height, fore-aft position, reach, stack, and cleat alignment across whatever bikes a client owns, since a fitter running 8-12 sessions a week across road, gravel, and triathlon disciplines can't rely on memory to know which client's aggressive tri-fit numbers differ from the upright commuter-bike fit done for the same person six months earlier, and re-measuring from scratch every visit wastes paid session time the client is billed for by the hour. The tool also needs a fit-history timeline per client per bike, since return clients often bring in a new frame or a different bike for the same body and expect the fitter to translate known numbers rather than start over, and a fitter who can't quickly pull up "what worked last time" loses the efficiency that justifies a premium hourly rate. It also needs a simple session-notes and follow-up-adjustment log, because minor post-fit tweaks (a saddle swap, a stem-length change after a week of riding) need to stay attached to the original fit record rather than living in a separate text thread. Most independent fitters currently manage this across a paper fit-bike worksheet and a folder of photos, with no structured link between a specific client's measurements and the bike they were taken on.

The tool maintains a per-client/per-bike body-measurement and fit-spec record, a fit-history timeline across visits, and a session-notes/follow-up-adjustment log.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per fitter account. Distribution: r/bikefit and r/cycling threads, the Retül and BikeFit.com certified-fitter directories, and regional bike-shop fit-studio networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Freelance Drone Light Show Operator Fleet-Battery, Choreography-File & Flight-Window Tracker

Independent operators who fly synchronized drone fleets for weddings, festivals, and municipal fireworks-alternative shows need to track a per-show fleet-battery-cycle tracker confirming every drone in that night's fleet — commonly 50-200 units — is charged, firmware-matched, and assigned a slot before launch, since a single drone launched on a stale firmware version or a battery below the safe threshold can drop out of formation mid-show in front of a paying client's live audience, a failure that's immediately visible and impossible to hide. The tool also needs a choreography-file version tracker, because show files are custom-built per event and revised up until showtime, and an operator running several bookings a month can't rely on memory to know which fleet folder has the client-approved final cut loaded versus an earlier draft. It also needs a per-venue flight-window and airspace-clearance tracker, since each show requires a confirmed launch/recovery field, a weather-hold contingency window, and — depending on airspace class — an FAA authorization on file before the fleet can fly, and an operator juggling multiple weekend bookings needs a clear view of which venue's clearance is confirmed versus still pending. Most independent light show operators currently manage this across a spreadsheet of drone serial numbers and a folder of show files, with no structured link between a specific fleet's battery/firmware readiness and the choreography file it's about to fly.

The tool maintains a per-show fleet-battery/firmware-readiness tracker, a choreography-file version tracker, and a per-venue flight-window/airspace-clearance tracker.

Monetization: $29-49/mo per operator account. Distribution: r/drones and drone-light-show operator Facebook and Discord communities, the DJI Enterprise reseller/show-operator network, and Drone Racing League-adjacent event-production forums. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Independent Childbirth Educator Class-Series Enrollment & Curriculum-Progress Tracker

Independent childbirth educators teaching multi-week Lamaze, Bradley Method, or HypnoBirthing series need to track a class-series enrollment tracker showing which expecting couples are enrolled in which cohort and which session topics — breathing techniques, pain-coping positions, partner-support role, postpartum basics — have already been covered, since an educator running two or three concurrent series at different weeks of the curriculum can't rely on memory to know which cohort is due for the labor-stages session versus which one just finished it, and repeating or skipping a topic undermines the structured progression the method is built around. The tool also needs a per-family due-date and makeup-session tracker, because pregnant students occasionally miss a session and need to be slotted into a different cohort's corresponding week to stay on track before their due date arrives, and an educator without a clear view of makeup availability loses a family who can't complete the series in time. It also needs a materials and handout tracker per curriculum week, since each method's session plan comes with its own worksheets and take-home practice guides that need to go out consistently across every cohort. Most independent childbirth educators currently manage this across a paper class roster and a binder of curriculum handouts, with no structured link between a specific family's due date and how many sessions remain before it arrives.

The tool maintains a class-series enrollment and curriculum-progress tracker, a per-family due-date/makeup-session tracker, and a materials/handout checklist per curriculum week.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per educator account. Distribution: r/BabyBumps and r/pregnant childbirth-class threads, Lamaze International and Bradley Method (AAHCC) certified-educator directories, and local birth-network Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Craft Hop Farmer & Grower Harvest Yield, Alpha-Acid Testing & Brewery Contract Tracker

Independent hop growers supplying craft breweries need to track a per-yard harvest-yield log recording pounds picked by variety and trellis row, since a small farm running several acres of mixed varieties (Cascade, Centennial, Citra) can't rely on memory to know which yard's yield is tracking against last season's numbers, and a grower who can't produce accurate per-variety yield history loses negotiating leverage when a brewery is deciding how many pounds to contract for next season. The tool also needs an alpha-acid lab-test result tracker per harvest lot, because brewers buy hops against a specific alpha-acid percentage range for their recipe, and a lot whose test result isn't matched correctly to the harvest date and variety risks a grower selling hops that don't meet the brewer's stated spec — a mismatch that damages a relationship a small farm depends on for repeat contracts. It also needs a brewery-contract tracker recording which brewery contracted which variety, how many pounds, at what price, and by what delivery date, since a grower supplying 8-15 small and mid-size breweries a season needs a clear view of which contracts are fulfilled versus still owed as harvest wraps up. Most independent hop growers currently manage this across a paper harvest log and a folder of lab-test PDFs from the testing co-op, with no structured link between a specific lot's alpha-acid result and the brewery contract it's earmarked for.

The tool maintains a per-yard harvest-yield log, an alpha-acid lab-test tracker per lot, and a brewery-contract fulfillment tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per farm account. Distribution: r/Hops and r/homebrewing grower threads, the Hop Growers of America member directory, and regional craft-brewery guild grower-relations networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Freelance Model Multi-Client Booking, Comp-Card & Usage-Rights Tracker

Independent freelance models working directly with photographers, agencies, and brands without a full-service talent agency managing their calendar need to track a multi-client booking calendar showing which shoot, casting call, or fitting is confirmed for which date, since a working model juggling print, e-commerce, and event bookings across several photographers and brands in a busy month can't rely on memory to know which Tuesday is already held for a callback versus open for a paid job, and a double-booked date is a burned relationship with whichever client loses the booking. The tool also needs a comp-card and portfolio-version tracker, because a model's comp card needs periodic updates as new tear sheets and measurements change, and a model submitting for a casting with an outdated comp card loses a booking to someone whose materials are current. It also needs a per-job usage-rights tracker, since every shoot's model release typically grants the client a specific, time-limited usage scope — a regional print ad licensed for one year reads very differently from a buyout for unlimited digital use — and a model who loses track of a usage window has no way to know when a brand's continued use of her image has run past what was actually paid for. Most independent freelance models currently manage this across a personal calendar app and a folder of signed releases, with no structured link between a specific job's usage terms and when that license actually expires.

The tool maintains a multi-client booking calendar, a comp-card/portfolio-version tracker, and a per-job usage-rights expiration tracker.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per model account. Distribution: r/modeling and Model Mayhem community forums, and independent-model Facebook and Instagram creative-community groups where freelancers already trade booking and rate-card advice. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Theatrical Scenic Artist & Set Painter Multi-Production Job Queue & Materials Tracker

Independent scenic artists who paint and finish sets for community theaters, regional playhouses, and school productions need to track a multi-production job queue showing which show's set is in the design-approval, paint, or load-in-ready stage, since a scenic artist working two or three productions at once across different theaters can't rely on memory to know which set's faux-finish sample is still awaiting director approval versus which one is already scheduled for load-in next week, and missing a load-in date on a hard opening-night deadline is a relationship-ending mistake with a theater that books scenic work every season. The tool also needs a paint and materials tracker per production, because color-matching a set's finish across multiple flats and platforms requires keeping an exact mixed-paint formula on file, and a scenic artist who can't reproduce last week's exact custom mix for a touch-up before opening night risks a visible color mismatch under stage lighting. It also needs a strike and materials-recovery tracker, since reusable set pieces, paint stock, and rented tools need to come back after a production closes before the next show's budget can count on them, and a scenic artist running several productions in overlapping seasons loses real money to materials that quietly don't make it back into inventory after strike. Most independent scenic artists currently manage this across a paper production calendar and a shelf of labeled paint cans, with no structured link between a specific show's approved color palette and the mixed-paint formula that actually produced it.

The tool maintains a multi-production job queue, a paint/materials formula tracker per production, and a strike/materials-recovery tracker.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per artist account. Distribution: r/theatre and r/TechTheatre threads, the United States Institute for Theatre Technology (USITT) member directory, and regional community-theater technical-director networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
