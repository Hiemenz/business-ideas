# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-14 (Round 188)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a woodturner, a henna artist, a hot-sauce maker, a kombucha brewer, a jerky maker and a candle maker.

---

### 1. Woodturner Commission, Blank & Finish Tracker

A woodturner who sells bowls, pens and custom pieces has to track wood blanks by species and drying date, commissions, finish cure times and sales. Wet wood needs months to dry, and a customer wants a specific species.

The tool tracks blanks and drying, commissions with deadlines, finish stages and sales per piece.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per turner. Distribution: woodturning clubs, r/woodworking and craft-fair groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Henna Artist Booking, Event & Design Tracker

A henna artist takes bookings for weddings, parties and festivals, and has to track each event's guest count, time per person, travel and deposit. Wedding season brings multi-day bookings, and paste needs preparing ahead.

The tool tracks events with deposits, estimates time by guest count, logs travel and sends a day-before reminder.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per artist. Distribution: henna artist communities, wedding-vendor groups and Instagram. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Hot-Sauce Maker Batch, Ingredient & Market Tracker

A hot-sauce maker has to track batches, pepper sourcing, bottle counts and market sales. Pepper harvests are seasonal, so a bulk buy has to be planned, and flavors sell differently at each market.

The tool tracks batches and yield, ingredient cost per bottle, stock by flavor and sales by market.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per maker. Distribution: hot-sauce communities, r/hotsauce and farmers-market vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Kombucha Brewer Batch, Fermentation & Keg Tracker

A small kombucha brewer has to track brew batches, fermentation days, flavoring and keg or bottle counts. Timing decides taste, and kegs placed at cafes must be collected and refilled.

The tool tracks batches with days fermented, flavor runs, keg locations and returns, and cost per gallon.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per brewer. Distribution: fermentation communities, r/Kombucha and local cafe networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Jerky Maker Batch, Dehydrator & Order Tracker

A jerky maker has to track batches by meat and marinade, dehydrator loads and drying times, packaging and orders. Yield varies by cut, so cost per bag needs tracking, and wholesale orders have deadlines.

The tool tracks batches and yield, cost per bag, stock by flavor and customer orders.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per maker. Distribution: jerky-maker Facebook groups, r/jerky and local market groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Candle Maker Pour, Cure & Wholesale Tracker

A candle maker has to track pours by scent, wax and fragrance load, cure time and stock. Candles cure for days before they burn well, and wholesale orders for holidays need planning.

The tool tracks pours and cure countdowns, cost per candle, stock by scent and wholesale orders.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per maker. Distribution: candle-making communities, r/candlemaking and Etsy seller groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to the holidays.
