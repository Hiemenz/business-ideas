# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-13 (Session 70)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- Simple, fast-to-build workflow trackers — no regulatory compliance angle.
- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.
- **No repeats.** This session's six operator types — farriers (horseshoeing technicians), mobile knife & blade sharpening operators, independent upholsterers, freelance court reporters/stenographers, professional organizers & closet designers, and mobile phone/device screen repair technicians — are distinct from every prior session: farriery has never appeared (distinct from the mobile pet groomer and dog walker/pet sitter ideas referenced elsewhere in this repo, neither of which involves large-animal hoof care at all — a farrier's core problem instead is a per-horse hoof profile and shoeing history (hoof measurements, shoe type, and any corrective or therapeutic notes) tied to a recurring 6-8 week reshoeing cycle per horse rather than a one-time grooming visit, and a multi-stop route across boarding stables and private barns scheduled around that recurring cycle, a hoof-care-and-recurring-cycle problem no small-animal grooming or walking tracker holds); mobile knife and blade sharpening has never appeared (distinct from the mobile pressure washing route idea and the window & gutter cleaning route idea referenced elsewhere in this repo, both of which clean exterior surfaces on a route — a sharpening operator's core problem instead is a per-account blade inventory (a restaurant kitchen's or salon's specific knife or shear count and steel type) serviced on a recurring route stop, and a drop-off/pickup ticket system for individual customers who hand off a knife set between visits, a blade-inventory-and-drop-off problem no surface-cleaning route tracker holds); independent upholstery has never appeared (distinct from the custom woodworking shop idea, the handmade jewelry maker idea, and the leatherworker idea already in this repo, all of which track a commission-and-material-inventory problem for a finished handmade piece — an upholsterer's core problem instead is a per-job fabric-yardage calculation matched against a client's chosen fabric bolt and pattern-repeat, and a piece-in-progress queue tracking a single item's frame, padding, and reupholstery stages that can span weeks, a fabric-yardage-and-reupholstery-stage problem no other maker's commission tracker holds); ### 1. Farrier (Horseshoeing Technician) Hoof Profile & Reshoeing Cycle Tracker

Independent farriers who trim and shoe horses at boarding stables and private barns need to track each horse's hoof profile — hoof measurements, shoe type and size, and any corrective or therapeutic shoeing notes for a lame or high-performance horse — since a horse's shoeing plan must stay consistent visit to visit or a mismatched shoe can affect the horse's gait or reinjure a healing hoof, a recurring 6-8 week reshoeing-cycle schedule per horse since hooves grow on a biological clock independent of any owner's calendar reminder, and a multi-stop route across boarding stables and private barns sequenced by which horses are actually due, because a farrier servicing 60-120 horses across a rural route can't rely on memory for which horse is three weeks into its cycle versus overdue by two, and showing up at a barn without the specific shoe size or corrective notes for a particular horse means a wasted trip or a shoeing job that doesn't match the horse's actual needs. Most independent farriers currently manage this across a paper index card per horse and a mental sense of "who's due soon" with no structured link between a horse's shoeing history and its next due date.

The tool maintains a per-horse hoof profile (measurements, shoe type, and corrective/therapeutic notes), a recurring reshoeing-cycle dashboard surfacing every horse approaching its 6-8 week due window, and a route board sequencing stable and barn stops by which horses are actually due that week.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per farrier account. Distribution: r/farrier and r/Horses threads, the American Farrier's Association (AFA) member directory, and local boarding-stable and equine-vet referral networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Mobile Knife & Blade Sharpening Operator Account Inventory & Route Tracker

Independent mobile knife and blade sharpening operators who service restaurant kitchens, salons, and individual customers need to track a per-account blade inventory — a restaurant's specific knife count and steel type, or a salon's shear inventory — serviced on a recurring route stop, since arriving at a kitchen without knowing how many blades are due for sharpening that visit means either an incomplete service or wasted time recounting on-site, a drop-off/pickup ticket system for individual walk-up or farmers-market customers who hand off a knife or shear set between visits, because a customer's blade left at a market booth needs a reliable claim ticket rather than a verbal promise, and a route schedule sequencing recurring commercial accounts by their service interval alongside one-off individual jobs, because an operator running a weekly restaurant route of 15-25 accounts plus a rotating set of individual drop-offs can't rely on memory for which kitchen's paring knives were sharpened last visit versus which account is overdue. Most independent sharpening operators currently manage this across a paper route sheet and loose claim tickets with no structured link between an account's blade inventory and its actual service history.

The tool maintains a per-account blade/shear inventory record tied to a recurring service interval, a drop-off/pickup ticket log for individual customer jobs, and a route board sequencing commercial stops by due date alongside scheduled individual pickups.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per operator account. Distribution: r/sharpening and r/KitchenConfidential threads, local restaurant association and chef Facebook groups, and farmers market vendor coordinator outreach. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent Upholsterer Fabric Yardage & Reupholstery Queue Tracker

Independent upholsterers who reupholster furniture for individual clients and antique-dealer accounts need to track a per-job fabric-yardage calculation matched against the client's chosen fabric bolt and its pattern repeat, since ordering short on a specific fabric run mid-job means a delayed reorder that may not even match the original dye lot, a piece-in-progress queue tracking each item's stage — frame repair, padding/webbing, and final upholstery — since a single reupholstery job can span several weeks and a shop running 10-20 pieces at once can't rely on memory for which client's wingback chair is still waiting on frame glue-up versus ready for final fabric, and a client fabric-selection record (fabric house, pattern number, and yardage ordered) since a client requesting a matching ottoman months later needs the exact original fabric reordered, not a close guess. Most independent upholsterers currently manage this across a paper work tag stapled to each piece and a fabric-swatch folder with no structured link between a piece's current stage and the exact fabric order it's waiting on.

The tool maintains a per-job fabric-yardage calculator matched to pattern repeat, a piece-in-progress queue tracking each item's frame/padding/upholstery stage, and a client fabric-selection record for exact reorder matching.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per upholsterer account. Distribution: r/Upholstery and r/furniture threads, the Upholstery Education Alliance (UEA) member directory, and local antique-dealer and interior-designer referral networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Freelance Court Reporter & Stenographer Assignment & Transcript-Deadline Tracker

Independent court reporters and stenographers who cover depositions, hearings, and arbitrations for multiple law firms need to track a per-assignment scheduling board across firms that often book on short notice, since double-booking two depositions on the same afternoon is a scheduling failure a freelance reporter can't recover from once it happens, a transcript-delivery deadline tracker since a rush transcript commonly carries a contractually shorter turnaround (24-48 hours) than a standard order (7-10 business days), and a per-page invoicing record tied to each client firm's specific negotiated rate, because reporters juggling 8-15 assignments across different firms in a given month can't rely on memory for which firm's rate applies to which job or which transcript is due back tomorrow versus next week, and missing a rush-transcript deadline for a firm heading into trial is exactly the kind of reliability failure that ends a referral relationship. Most independent court reporters currently manage this across a paper calendar and a separate invoicing spreadsheet with no structured link between an assignment's turnaround commitment and the firm's actual rate agreement.

The tool maintains a per-assignment scheduling board across client firms with conflict detection, a transcript-delivery deadline tracker distinguishing rush from standard turnaround, and a per-page invoicing log matched to each firm's negotiated rate.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per reporter account. Distribution: r/courtreporting threads, the National Court Reporters Association (NCRA) member directory, and local court reporting agency subcontractor networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Professional Organizer & Closet Designer Multi-Room Project & Vendor Tracker

Independent professional organizers and closet designers who take on multi-room decluttering and closet-redesign projects for individual clients need to track a per-client project tracker spanning several rooms or a full-home engagement, since a project that touches a kitchen, a home office, and a master closet across multiple visits needs a single view of what's been completed versus what's still pending, a before/after photo log documenting each space, since showing a client (and future prospects) a documented transformation is central to both the service and the organizer's own marketing, and a product and vendor recommendation list (storage bins, closet-system components, labeling supplies) tied to each project, because an organizer running 4-8 active client projects at once can't rely on memory for which client's pantry still needs a specific bin size ordered versus which closet system installation is scheduled for next week. Most independent organizers currently manage this across a shared Pinterest board and a paper project checklist with no structured link between a project's room-by-room status and the specific products recommended for it.

The tool maintains a per-client multi-room project tracker with room-by-room completion status, a before/after photo log per space, and a product/vendor recommendation list tied to each project and room.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per organizer account. Distribution: r/organizing and r/declutter threads, the National Association of Productivity and Organizing Professionals (NAPO) member directory, and local interior-designer and real-estate-stager referral networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Mobile Phone & Device Screen Repair Technician Parts & Same-Visit Ticket Tracker

Independent mobile phone and tablet repair technicians who drive to customers for on-site screen, battery, and charging-port repairs need to track a parts inventory organized by device model and generation, since a specific phone model's screen assembly or battery is rarely interchangeable with even the prior year's model from the same manufacturer, a repair ticket system built around a same-visit turnaround rather than a multi-day repair timeline, since most mobile screen-repair customers expect the technician to complete the job on-site in under an hour, and a daily route loaded with the specific parts the day's scheduled repairs actually require, because a technician running 5-10 repair appointments a day across different device models can't rely on memory for which van stock covers today's scheduled iPhone and Samsung repairs versus which specific model he's short a part for, and arriving at a customer's home or office without the right screen assembly for their exact device model turns a booked appointment into a rescheduled one. Most independent mobile repair technicians currently manage this across a phone-based parts spreadsheet and a booking app's calendar with no structured link between the day's scheduled device models and the parts actually loaded in the van.

The tool maintains a parts inventory organized by device model/generation with low-stock alerts, a same-visit repair ticket log tracking each job's device, issue, and completion status, and a daily route board matched against the specific parts each scheduled repair requires.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per technician account. Distribution: r/framerepair and r/electronics threads, local Facebook Marketplace and Nextdoor mobile-repair community groups, and Angi/Thumbtack pro community outreach. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
