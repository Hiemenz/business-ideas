# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 23)

### 1. Dance Studio Recital Costume Tracker
Dance studio owners coordinating annual recitals manage costume orders across 50-300 students in multiple classes and routines, each with its own costume style, colorway, and sizing run. Order tracking happens in email threads and spreadsheets that become out of sync the moment a size exchange is requested or a late enrollment needs a costume added. This tool maintains a roster per studio: student name, classes enrolled, costume assigned per class, size ordered, size confirmed (after try-on), any alteration notes, order status (pending / ordered / received / distributed), and a payment flag if costume fees are collected separately from tuition. A dashboard view shows the studio at a glance: costumes still pending measurement, costumes received but not yet distributed, and a per-routine headcount confirming that every student on the routine roster has a costume assigned. Monetization: $20-35/mo per studio account during recital season (Oct-May), $10/mo off-season. Distribution: dance studio owner Facebook groups, Dance Studio Life community, r/dance, DanceStudio Pro and Jackrabbit user communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Veterinary Practice Appointment No-Show Pattern Tracker
Veterinary clinics absorb significant lost revenue from no-show and last-minute cancellation appointments — an empty exam room for a 30-minute slot in a fully-booked practice costs $75-120 in opportunity cost. Most practice management software (EZYVET, AVImark, Cornerstone) records appointment history but provides no built-in report identifying which clients no-show repeatedly. This tool imports appointment history exports from the clinic's practice management system and generates: a per-client no-show rate over the trailing 12 months, a list of clients who have no-showed or cancelled within 24 hours more than twice, a recommended action for each (prepayment required, double-booking their next slot, confirmation call at 48 hours), and a monthly no-show cost summary (revenue at risk by appointment type). No AI needed — just pattern detection from existing data. Monetization: $20-40/mo per clinic. Distribution: Veterinary Business Management Association (VBMA) communities, r/vets, practice manager Facebook groups, EZYVET and AVImark user communities. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 3. Residential Cleaning Company Property Profile Manager
Residential cleaning companies send different cleaners to the same homes — a regular cleaner is sick, a new hire is being trained on the route, a backup team covers a holiday rush. Each new cleaner to a home needs to know: the access method (hidden key, lockbox code, garage code), alarm code if applicable, pets in the home and how to handle them, which areas are off-limits, the client's preferred cleaning products (allergies, unscented only, no bleach), and any special recurring notes ("always fold the toilet paper, the homeowner really cares about it"). This gets communicated via texts that disappear, or not communicated at all, resulting in unhappy clients and callbacks. This tool maintains a profile card per client property, shareable as a read-only link or PDF with the assigned cleaner before each visit. The company controls what's visible; cleaners access only their assigned property profiles. Monetization: $15-30/mo per cleaning company account (up to 50 properties). Distribution: cleaning company owner Facebook groups, r/housekeeping, Jobber and ZenMaid user communities, The Cleaning Business Academy community. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Podcast Native Ad Read Compliance Tracker for Advertisers
Brands paying for podcast host-read advertisements — a category where the host reads a personalized ad in their own voice during the episode — need to verify that their ad reads have aired, contain the required FTC endorsement disclosure ("paid partnership" or "ad" disclosure), include the correct tracking URL or promo code, and air in the correct ad position (pre-roll, mid-roll, or post-roll) specified in the insertion order. Manually checking dozens of podcast episodes per campaign across multiple shows is time-consuming and frequently skipped, leaving brands paying for ad reads they can't confirm aired or that dropped required disclosures. This tool accepts the campaign insertion order (show name, episode number or date range, required promo code, disclosure language required, ad position), lets the brand's team log each confirmed ad read with a timestamp from the episode, flags any episode where the promo code or disclosure language was absent or incorrect, and generates a campaign delivery report showing fulfilled vs. unfulfilled insertions for invoice reconciliation. Monetization: $25-50/mo or $200-400 per campaign tracked. Distribution: podcast advertising agency communities, r/podcasts creator side, brand marketing coordinator communities, Spotify for Podcasters advertiser resources. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Short-Term Disability Claim Income Documentation Organizer for Self-Employed
Self-employed individuals — freelancers, consultants, sole proprietors, independent contractors — who purchase individual disability insurance policies must document their pre-disability income when filing a disability claim. The insurer requires proof of income for the 12-24 months prior to the disability onset, typically including: Schedule C or business tax returns, 1099-NEC forms from clients, invoices and payment records, and bank statements showing business income deposits. Gathering this documentation while already dealing with a health crisis is difficult, and disorganized documentation delays claim approval or results in lower-than-entitled benefit calculations. This tool lets self-employed individuals maintain a running income documentation file during healthy periods: uploading and tagging annual tax returns, 1099s, and quarterly revenue summaries by year, and generating a pre-formatted "disability claim income package" PDF on demand — organized in the format disability insurers request for claim review. Monetization: $8-15/mo subscription (the value is maintaining the file continuously, not just at claim time). Distribution: r/freelance, r/selfemployed, disability insurance broker communities, AICPA self-employed tax practitioner communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Youth Sports Tournament Bracket & Game Schedule Manager
Youth sports league directors and tournament organizers managing weekend baseball, softball, basketball, or soccer tournaments with 12-32 teams build brackets and game schedules manually in Excel or Google Sheets, then field a dozen calls when a rainout forces a schedule revision or a field becomes unavailable. This tool accepts the tournament format (single elimination, double elimination, pool-play plus bracket, round-robin), the number of teams, the available fields/courts and their time windows, and the team roster list, and generates: a full bracket seeded by submitted standings or randomized draw, a game schedule assigned to fields and time slots, a rainout reschedule tool (when a field or time slot becomes unavailable, auto-generates an updated schedule accommodating the constraint), and shareable team-specific views (each team's coach gets a link showing only their team's game schedule, field assignments, and any schedule updates). Monetization: $25-50 per tournament or $30-60/mo for league directors running multiple events. Distribution: AYSO, Little League, and USA Softball volunteer director communities, youth sports parent and coach Facebook groups, SportsEngine and TeamSnap community forums. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
