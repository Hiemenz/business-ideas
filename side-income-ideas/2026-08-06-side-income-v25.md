# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 25)

### 1. Music Teacher Studio Recital Program Booklet Generator
Independent music teachers hosting spring and fall recitals — typically 20-60 students performing piano, violin, voice, or guitar — need a printed or digital program booklet listing each performer's name, piece title, composer, and performance order. Currently assembled by hand in Word or Canva, requiring 2-4 hours of layout work per recital. This tool accepts the teacher's student roster and a simple per-student form (student name, piece title, composer, grade/level optional) and generates a ready-to-print program PDF with consistent formatting: a cover page with studio name and recital date, a performance order list, and a back page for acknowledgments or sponsor notes. Optional: a parent-facing digital program link with a QR code. Monetization: $8-15/recital or $25-35/year for unlimited recitals. Distribution: Music Teachers National Association (MTNA) member communities, piano teacher Facebook groups, r/piano teacher communities, Jackrabbit Music and Studio Helper user forums. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Freelance Photographer Client Gallery Delivery Tracker
Wedding and portrait photographers who deliver client galleries via Pixieset, ShootProof, or CloudSpot often have no visibility into whether the client has viewed or downloaded their gallery — which matters for follow-up (a client who hasn't viewed their gallery 2 weeks post-delivery needs a reminder; a client who viewed but hasn't downloaded may need download instructions). This tool connects to a photographer's gallery delivery platform (or accepts manual gallery-sent events) and tracks, per client: gallery delivery date, first-view date, download date, number of files downloaded, and a follow-up status (no action needed / reminder scheduled / archive notice sent). A weekly digest surfaces "galleries not viewed in 14+ days" and "galleries viewed but not downloaded in 21+ days" so the photographer can send targeted follow-ups without manually checking each gallery platform. Monetization: $12-20/mo per photographer. Distribution: r/WeddingPhotography, Pixieset user communities, ShootProof community forums, photography business Facebook groups, Sprout Studio user communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Rental Property Annual Performance Summary Generator
Small real estate investors with 2-15 rental properties track rent collected, vacancy, repairs, and mortgage payments in spreadsheets that rarely produce a clean annual summary — the data to understand actual cash-on-cash return, net operating income, and year-over-year trend sits in the spreadsheet but requires manual calculation to surface. This tool accepts annual inputs per property (gross rent potential, actual rent collected, vacancy days, mortgage P&I, property tax, insurance, maintenance and repair total, management fees, and any capital expenditures) and generates a single-page annual performance summary per property: gross rent, vacancy loss, effective gross income, total operating expenses, NOI, debt service, cash flow, cash-on-cash return, and year-over-year comparison if prior year data is entered. Portfolio summary view aggregates all properties. Monetization: $10-18/mo or $49/year flat. Distribution: BiggerPockets community, r/realestateinvesting, small landlord Facebook groups, RentRedi and Avail user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Gym Membership Freeze & Pause Request Tracker
Independent gyms and fitness studios that allow membership holds and pauses — for medical recovery, extended travel, seasonal closures — track these manually in a binder or spreadsheet, frequently losing track of pause expiration dates and failing to reactivate billing on time. This tool maintains a hold/pause record per member: member name, membership type, hold start date, hold end date, hold reason, billing impact (billing paused or reduced during hold), and reactivation status. A daily digest surfaces all holds expiring within the next 7 days so the front desk team can prepare the reactivation. Automated email reminders to members approaching hold expiration reduce the reactivation no-show rate. Monetization: $15-25/mo per gym location. Distribution: r/gym, gym owner Facebook groups, MindBody and Pike13 user communities, independent gym owner mastermind communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Home Inspector Pre-Report Executive Summary Generator
Home inspectors write 40-120 page inspection reports packed with every minor maintenance observation alongside genuine defects — clients and real estate agents are overwhelmed and often miss the critical findings buried in the detail. This tool takes the inspector's summary notes (a quick structured input: 5-10 fields per major finding — location, system, defect type, severity, recommended action) and generates a clean 1-page executive summary PDF tiered by severity: Safety Concerns (address immediately), Major Defects (repair before close or negotiate credit), and Maintenance Items (monitor). The inspector attaches this summary to the front of the full report, providing immediate clarity for clients without replacing the full document. Monetization: $10-18/mo per inspector account (unlimited reports). Distribution: InterNACHI and ASHI (home inspector association) member communities, r/HomeInspections, home inspector Facebook groups, HomeGauge and Spectora software user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. Food Truck Weekly Menu & Daily Special Image Generator
Food truck owners post a weekly menu and daily special announcement to Instagram and Facebook to drive traffic — but creating an on-brand, visually consistent image from scratch each week in Canva takes 30-60 minutes that busy owner-operators don't have. This tool lets the owner enter their menu items and specials for the week (name, description, price, dietary tags — vegan, GF, spicy), select from branded template layouts matched to their truck's colors and fonts, and generate a print-ready social media image set: a weekly menu image, a daily special announcement image sized for Instagram Story and Feed, and a simple text-only version for SMS blasts. The branding is configured once; updates take 5 minutes. Monetization: $12-20/mo per food truck account. Distribution: r/foodtrucks, food truck owner Facebook groups, Roaming Hunger vendor community, Square for Restaurants merchant communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
