# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-01 (Round 11)

### 1. Third-Party API Health Monitor for Indie Developers
Indie developers who build products on top of third-party APIs (Stripe, Twilio, OpenAI, Plaid, etc.) have no lightweight way to monitor whether those specific endpoints are returning expected response schemas — not just "is the site up" but "did this field disappear from the response, did the error rate spike, did the latency double." This tool lets developers configure specific API endpoints with expected response shapes and alert thresholds, monitors them on a schedule, and sends Slack/email/webhook alerts when something drifts. Monetization: $9-19/mo per developer, tiered by number of monitored endpoints. Distribution: IndieHackers community, r/SideProject, Hacker News Show HN posts, developer-focused newsletter sponsorships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Youth Travel Sports Team Treasurer App
Volunteer treasurers for travel sports teams (baseball, volleyball, soccer, hockey) collect and track hundreds of dollars per family across tournament entry fees, hotel blocks, uniform orders, and coach travel reimbursements — all done in spreadsheets and group Venmo threads. This app tracks each family's running balance, records team-level expenses with receipt uploads, sends configurable payment-due reminders, and generates a seasonal financial summary PDF the board can review. Monetization: $12-20/mo per team treasurer or $49/season. Distribution: travel baseball and travel volleyball parent Facebook groups, youth sports league administrator communities, TeamSnap and SportsEngine user forums. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Small Inland Marina Slip & Dry Storage Management App
Small lake marinas that rent seasonal boat slips, dry stack spaces, and winter storage don't need the enterprise dock management platforms built for large coastal marinas — they need a simple tool to track which slips are occupied by which boat (with owner contact and vessel info), collect seasonal fees, manage a waitlist for popular slip sizes, and schedule haul-out and launch dates in spring and fall. This app handles that core workflow with a visual slip map, automated fee invoicing, and a waitlist queue. Monetization: $40-70/mo per marina. Distribution: marina operator Facebook groups, inland lake property owner associations, state marina association newsletters. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 4. Knitting & Crochet Project Tracker with Yarn Stash Manager
Fiber artists manage multiple simultaneous projects — each with pattern-specific row and stitch counts, notes per session, and yarn sourced from specific dye lots that must stay consistent within a project — while also maintaining a stash of purchased yarn they need to inventory before buying more. This mobile-first app combines a row counter and project journal with a yarn stash inventory (color, weight, yardage, dye lot) and a needs-list that cross-references current projects against what's already in the stash. Monetization: $5-10/mo or $34/yr. Distribution: r/knitting, r/crochet, Ravelry community threads, crafting YouTube creator affiliate partnerships. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Screen Printing Shop Blank Inventory Tracker
Small screen printing and heat-transfer vinyl shops order blank apparel from multiple distributors (SanMar, S&S Activewear, Alpha Broder) and struggle to track inventory of each blank style, color, and size across their shop — leading to over-ordering, mis-picking customer orders, and scrambling when a popular blank goes out of stock mid-job. This tool manages the blank inventory across distributors, lets staff receive and decrement stock per order, flags styles that fall below a reorder threshold, and generates a weekly reorder list pre-populated with distributor SKUs. Monetization: $35-60/mo per shop. Distribution: screen printing Facebook groups (large and active), r/screenprinting, Printavo and InkSoft user communities. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 6. Dog Boarding Kennel & Daycare Reservation + Vaccination Tracker
Small dog boarding kennels and doggy daycares require current vaccination records (rabies, Bordetella, DHPP) before accepting any dog, and they manage reservations manually via phone and paper calendars — with no system to flag when a regular client's vaccination records will expire before an upcoming stay. This app handles daily and overnight reservation management (runs/kennels by size, daycare groups), stores vaccination records per dog with expiration date alerts, and sends automated reminders to clients whose records will lapse within 30 days. Monetization: $30-55/mo per facility. Distribution: National Association of Pet Care Professionals membership communications, dog daycare owner Facebook groups, pet care industry forums. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.
