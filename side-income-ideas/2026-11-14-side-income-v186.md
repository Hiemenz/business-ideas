# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-14 (Round 186)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a taxidermist, a handmade soap maker, a mobile bike mechanic, a seedling seller, a fishing guide and a private swim instructor.

---

### 1. Taxidermist Job, Stage & Pickup Tracker

A taxidermist handles dozens of mounts at once during hunting season, each at a different stage (received, tanned, mounted, finishing, ready). Customers call asking for status, and a lost tag or missed deposit is costly. Turnaround runs months.

The tool tracks each job's species, stage, deposit, quote and promised date, sends status updates and ready-for-pickup texts.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per taxidermist. Distribution: taxidermy forums, hunting Facebook groups and r/hunting. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to the season.

### 2. Handmade Soap Maker Batch, Cure & Sales Tracker

A soap maker has to track batches by recipe, pour date and cure end date, plus stock per scent and sales by channel. Cold-process soap cures 4-6 weeks, so a missed pour means an empty table at the market.

The tool schedules batches backward from market dates, tracks cure countdowns, logs cost per bar and shows margin by scent.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per maker. Distribution: soap-making communities, r/soapmaking and craft-market groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Mobile Bike Mechanic Job, Parts & Route Tracker

A mechanic who repairs bikes at customers' homes and offices has to track jobs, parts used, travel routes and quotes. A day with six stops needs a sensible route and a parts list loaded in the van.

The tool plans routes, records job notes and parts, tracks quotes and sends a reminder for a yearly tune-up.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per mechanic. Distribution: cycling communities, r/bikemechanics and local bike clubs. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Seedling Seller Sow-Date, Stock & Pre-Order Tracker

A person who sells vegetable and flower seedlings in spring must sow dozens of varieties on a schedule so they are ready before planting season. Pre-orders from customers need to be matched to trays.

The tool backs sow dates from a sales date, tracks trays and germination rates, records pre-orders and shows sell-through per variety.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per grower. Distribution: gardening groups, r/gardening and farmers-market vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to spring.

### 5. Fishing Guide Trip, Deposit & Repeat-Client Tracker

A fishing guide has to track trip dates, deposits, client skill levels, gear provided and tides or seasons. Weather cancels trips and needs rebooking, and returning clients are the best source of income.

The tool tracks bookings with deposits, handles weather reschedules, logs client notes and sends a "same time next year" message.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per guide. Distribution: fishing-guide Facebook groups, r/fishing and local tackle-shop boards. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Private Swim Instructor Student, Skill & Package Tracker

A private swim instructor has to track each student's skill checklist, lesson package balance, pool booking and parent notes. Parents buy packages of 8 or 10 lessons and want to see progress.

The tool tracks skills per student, counts down package lessons, sends a renewal message at lesson 7 and shares progress notes with parents.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per instructor. Distribution: swim-coach communities, local parent groups and r/swimming. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
