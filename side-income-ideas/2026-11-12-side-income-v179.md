# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-12 (Round 179)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a Discord community moderator, a stock-photo contributor, a freelance QA tester, an app-store-listing optimizer, a UX research participant recruiter and a newsletter curator.

---

### 1. Discord Community Moderator Server, Shift & Incident Tracker

A paid Discord moderator working for several communities has to track each server's rules, shift hours, incidents handled and pay. With 3-6 servers, remembering which server has which rule is hard, and clients want proof of hours. Handoffs between moderators need an incident log.

The tool stores per-server rules, logs shifts and hours, records incidents with actions taken and generates a monthly hours-and-incidents report for each client.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per moderator. Distribution: community-management Discords, r/discordapp mod threads and creator-community groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Stock-Photo Contributor Upload, Keyword & Earnings Tracker

A contributor who sells photos on stock sites has to track each image: upload status per agency, keywords, rejections and earnings. With hundreds of images on several platforms, it is hard to see which subjects sell and which were rejected and why. Keywording takes time and consistent tags help sales.

The tool tracks images per agency with status and rejection reasons, a keyword library and earnings per image and per category.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per contributor. Distribution: stock-photography communities, r/photography contributor threads and microstock forums. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Freelance QA Tester Test-Cycle, Bug-Report & Payment Tracker

A freelance game or app tester has to track each test cycle: client, build, bugs found, reports accepted and pay (per bug or per hour). With several clients and platforms, a bug already reported for one build is easy to report twice, and payment per accepted bug needs a record.

The tool logs test cycles with builds and hours, tracks bug reports with accepted/rejected status, calculates pay owed and keeps a reusable bug-template library.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per tester. Distribution: QA-freelancer communities, r/QualityAssurance and game-testing Discords. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. App-Store-Listing Optimizer Client Keyword, Test & Ranking Tracker

A freelancer who optimizes app-store listings (titles, keywords, screenshots) for small developers has to track each app's keywords, rankings, listing changes and conversion results. With 5-10 clients, it is hard to link a change to its effect after a few weeks. Clients want a monthly report of what changed and what moved.

The tool tracks apps with keyword rankings over time, logs each listing change with its date and shows before-and-after conversion and ranking.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per freelancer. Distribution: indie-developer communities, r/iOSProgramming and app-marketing Slack groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 5. UX Research Participant Recruiter Panel, Session & Incentive Tracker

A freelancer who recruits participants for user-research studies has to track a panel of people with their attributes, study invitations, no-shows and incentive payments. A panel of 300 people needs to be searched by criteria, and a person invited too often drops out. Clients pay per completed session.

The tool stores participants with attributes and past participation, tracks study invitations and show-ups and records incentive payments owed and paid.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per recruiter. Distribution: UX-research communities, r/UXResearch and research-ops Slack groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.

### 6. Niche Newsletter Curator Source, Issue & Sponsor Tracker

A curator who runs a niche link-roundup newsletter has to track sources, saved items for each issue, issue schedule, subscriber growth and sponsor slots. Saved links get lost across bookmarks and chats, and a missed issue costs readers. Sponsor deals need placement tracking.

The tool collects saved links into issue drafts, tracks the publishing schedule, logs subscriber counts and open rates per issue and tracks sponsor placements and payment.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per curator. Distribution: newsletter-creator communities, r/Newsletters and indie-publisher groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
