### 1. Independent RV/Camper Pre-Purchase Inspector Inspection Checklist & Report-Deadline Tracker

Independent RV and camper pre-purchase inspectors who are hired by buyers to evaluate a specific unit before closing need to track a per-inspection checklist tool covering chassis, roof seals, appliances, electrical, and plumbing systems tests tied to that specific unit and buyer, since an inspector running 3-5 inspections a week across different makes and models can't rely on memory to know which class-A motorhome's generator test still needs to be documented versus which travel trailer's roof-seal photos are already complete, and a missed system on a report a buyer is relying on to negotiate price is a credibility-ending mistake. The tool also needs a report-turnaround deadline tracker, since buyers hiring a pre-purchase inspector are almost always mid-negotiation with a seller on a tight closing timeline, and an inspector who delivers a report a day late can cost a buyer their negotiating window entirely. It also needs a multi-region travel and route scheduler, because RV inspectors often travel to wherever the unit is located — a dealer lot, a private seller's driveway, or a storage yard — and need a clear view of which inspections are bookable on a given multi-stop travel day. Most independent RV inspectors currently manage this across a paper checklist clipboard and a personal calendar, with no structured link between a specific unit's inspection findings and the buyer's own closing deadline.

The tool maintains a per-inspection checklist tracker tied to a specific unit and buyer, a report-turnaround deadline tracker, and a multi-region travel/route scheduler.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per inspector account. Distribution: the National RV Inspectors Association (NRVIA) member directory, r/rving and r/GoRVing threads, and RV buyer/seller Facebook groups where pre-purchase inspection referrals are common. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Freelance Muralist & Custom Sign Painter Project, Weather & Material-Cost Tracker

Independent muralists and custom sign painters who paint large-scale outdoor walls, storefronts, and murals need to track a per-project design-approval and progress-photo log, since a muralist working a multi-day outdoor job can't rely on memory to know exactly which design revision the client signed off on versus an earlier draft, and a mismatch between the approved design and the finished wall is a dispute that can't be quietly repainted away on a public-facing storefront. The tool also needs a paint and material cost log per project, since large-format outdoor work consumes exterior-grade paint, primer, and clear-coat in quantities that vary wildly by wall size and surface condition, and a muralist pricing jobs by the square foot needs to know whether a specific project's actual material cost is tracking against the quote before a string of underpriced jobs erodes the season's margins. It also needs a weather-dependent scheduling tool, because outdoor mural painting can't proceed in rain or extreme heat and a muralist juggling several multi-day projects needs a clear view of which weather windows are open for which job. Most independent muralists currently manage this across a phone's photo album and a paper sketch pad, with no structured link between a specific wall's approved design and the weather-driven schedule needed to finish it.

The tool maintains a per-project design-approval and progress-photo log, a paint/material cost log per project, and a weather-dependent scheduling tool for outdoor work.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per muralist account. Distribution: r/muralist and r/signpainting threads, the Letterheads sign-painting community, and local mural/public-art commission Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Mobile/Independent Boat Delivery Captain Job, Weather-Window & Client Tracker

Independent boat delivery captains who reposition sailboats, powerboats, and yachts between marinas for owners and brokers need to track a per-delivery job tracker capturing departure and arrival marinas, vessel type, and the weather-window plan for a one-way repositioning trip, since a captain running several deliveries a month across different owners and brokers can't rely on memory to know which delivery's weather window closes this weekend versus which one has flexibility into next week, and missing a weather window on an offshore passage isn't just an inconvenience — it can strand the captain and the vessel in dangerous conditions. The tool also needs a multi-client scheduling tool, since delivery captains typically work for several boat owners and yacht brokers who each book deliveries independently with no shared calendar, and a captain without a consolidated view risks double-booking two deliveries that both need the same departure window. It also needs a fuel and expense-reimbursement log per delivery, because most delivery contracts reimburse fuel, dockage, and provisioning costs separately from the delivery fee, and a captain running multiple deliveries a month loses real income to expenses they forget to submit for reimbursement. Most independent delivery captains currently manage this across a paper logbook and a mental sense of which owner's boat needs to move by when, with no structured link between a specific delivery's weather window and the expenses actually incurred getting it there.

The tool maintains a per-delivery job tracker with weather-window planning, a multi-client scheduling tool across boat owners and brokers, and a fuel/expense-reimbursement log per delivery.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per captain account. Distribution: r/sailing and r/boating threads, the Trawler Forum and Cruisers Forum delivery-captain classifieds, and Maritime Professional Training (MPT) alumni networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Travel Advisor Itinerary, Commission & Travel-Document Tracker

Independent travel advisors who book flights, lodging, and activities for clients through multiple suppliers need to track a per-client trip-itinerary tracker spanning every booked component across several suppliers, since an advisor managing 15-25 active client trips at different stages of planning can't rely on memory to know which client's honeymoon itinerary still needs excursions booked versus which one is fully confirmed and just needs final documents sent. The tool also needs a host-agency commission tracker reconciling what each supplier actually pays against what was quoted at booking time, since commission rates and payout timing vary by supplier and by host-agency agreement, and an advisor booking dozens of trips a year loses real income to commissions they forget were never actually reconciled against what was owed. It also needs a client travel-document and deadline tracker flagging passport and visa expirations against upcoming trip dates, because a client whose passport expires within six months of international travel can be denied boarding, and an advisor without a structured cross-check has no way to catch that gap before it becomes the client's problem at the airport. Most independent travel advisors currently manage this across a supplier-by-supplier confirmation email folder and a personal spreadsheet, with no structured link between a specific client's trip date and their travel-document validity.

The tool maintains a per-client multi-supplier itinerary tracker, a host-agency commission reconciliation tracker, and a client travel-document/deadline tracker.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per advisor account. Distribution: the American Society of Travel Advisors (ASTA) member directory, host-agency (Avoya Travel, Nexion) affiliated-advisor networks, and r/travelagents threads. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Blacksmith & Custom Metalwork Artisan Commission & Forge-Stock Tracker

Independent blacksmiths and custom metalwork artisans who forge one-of-a-kind gates, railings, tools, and decorative pieces need to track a per-commission order tracker spanning design approval, forging stages, and delivery, since a blacksmith running 4-8 concurrent custom commissions can't rely on memory to know which client's gate design is approved and ready for forging versus which one is still waiting on a revised sketch, and losing track of a commission's actual stage is an easy way to blow a client's installation deadline. The tool also needs a raw-material stock and cost log tracking steel stock, coal or propane fuel, and consumables like grinding discs, since a blacksmith pricing a commission by the piece needs to know whether a specific job's actual material and fuel cost is tracking against the quote before margins disappear into an underpriced custom piece. It also needs a studio/forge-time scheduling tool balancing custom commission work against craft-fair and gallery inventory production, because a blacksmith splitting time between paid commissions and speculative inventory pieces needs a clear view of which forge sessions are committed to a paying client versus open studio time. Most independent blacksmiths currently manage this across a sketchbook of client designs and a mental tally of steel stock on hand, with no structured link between a specific commission's approved design and the raw stock consumed forging it.

The tool maintains a per-commission order tracker from design approval through delivery, a raw-material stock and cost log, and a studio/forge-time scheduling tool.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per blacksmith account. Distribution: the Artist-Blacksmith's Association of North America (ABANA) member directory, r/Blacksmith and r/bladesmith threads, and regional blacksmith guild meetups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Garage Floor Coating & Epoxy Flooring Installer Cure-Time & Job-Cost Tracker

Independent garage floor coating and epoxy flooring installers need to track a per-job cure-time and re-coat-window scheduler, since each layer of an epoxy or polyaspartic coating system must fully cure before the next coat can be applied and the client's garage or space is unusable for the full multi-day process, and an installer running several jobs in different stages of cure across the week can't rely on memory to know which garage is ready for its second coat today versus which one still needs another 24 hours. The tool also needs a material and mix-ratio cost log per job, since epoxy systems require precise resin-to-hardener ratios and coverage rates that vary by floor condition and square footage, and an installer pricing jobs by the square foot needs to know whether a specific job's actual material cost is tracking against the quote before a pattern of over-pouring erodes margins across the season. It also needs a multi-day job-site scheduling tool coordinating around a client's own vehicle-access needs, because a homeowner without a garage for several days needs clear communication about exactly when each coat goes down and when the space is usable again. Most independent epoxy installers currently manage this across a paper job ticket and a mental countdown of cure times, with no structured link between a specific job's coat schedule and the client's own access needs.

The tool maintains a per-job cure-time and re-coat-window scheduler, a material/mix-ratio cost log per job, and a multi-day job-site scheduling tool.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per installer account. Distribution: r/flooring and r/HomeImprovement threads, the Concrete Coatings Alliance member network, and garage-floor-coating contractor Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
