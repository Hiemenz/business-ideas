# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-24 (Round 122)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Independent Golf Cart Dealer & Repair Technician Unit Inventory, Battery-Service & Custom-Build Tracker

An independent golf cart dealer who sells refurbished units and handles battery, electrical, and lift-kit repair for both residential and course-fleet customers has to track each cart's specific chassis, battery pack age, and controller type across a used-inventory lot where two carts that look identical on the surface can need entirely different replacement parts, since a lead-acid six-battery pack and a lithium retrofit require completely different charging hardware and a technician who grabs the wrong charger can cook a pack in an afternoon. A dealer running a rotating lot of a dozen or more used carts alongside a repair queue of customer-owned units currently tracks chassis specs, battery-pack install dates, and custom-build order status (lift kit, wheels, paint, seat upholstery) on a paper shop tag and a phone photo roll, and a battery pack sold without its install date logged is a warranty claim the dealer can't verify months later, while a custom-build order missing a tracked parts list is a cart that sits half-finished because nobody knows which part is still on backorder.

The tool maintains a per-unit inventory record (chassis, battery-pack type and install date, and controller/charger compatibility) tied to a repair-queue status tracker; a custom-build order sheet (requested upgrades, parts ordered vs. in-hand, and target completion date); and a customer-owned-unit service history log for repeat course-fleet and residential maintenance accounts.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per dealer. Distribution: r/GolfCarts and golf-cart-dealer trade forums, the National Golf Cart Dealers Association member network, and regional course-superintendent and HOA-community Facebook groups where independent dealers already trade fleet-service and custom-build workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Independent Dietitian & Nutritionist Private Practice Client Meal-Plan & Session Tracker

An independent registered dietitian or nutritionist running a private practice outside a hospital system has to track each client's evolving meal plan, lab-result history, and dietary-restriction profile across a caseload where a single missed allergy note or an outdated macro target carried over from three sessions ago is a plan that actively works against a client's actual goals. A practitioner running a full session calendar across in-person and telehealth clients currently tracks meal plans, session notes, and follow-up lab-recheck dates on a mix of a clinical-notes app built for medical charting rather than nutrition coaching and a personal spreadsheet of macro targets, and a client whose meal plan wasn't updated after a new lab result came back is advice that's actively stale, while a follow-up lab recheck nobody flagged as due is a gap in care that erodes the client's trust in the plan.

The tool maintains a per-client profile (dietary restrictions, current macro/calorie targets, and lab-result history) tied to a versioned meal-plan log showing exactly which plan was active on which date; a session-and-follow-up scheduler flagging any client due for a lab recheck or a plan reassessment; and a progress-tracking view (weight, lab trends, and client-reported adherence) for session-to-session continuity.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per practitioner. Distribution: r/dietitian and r/nutrition practitioner threads, the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics (AND) member network and its Dietitians in Private Practice specialty group, and independent-practice nutrition Facebook groups where practitioners already trade meal-plan-tracking and client-scheduling workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Standby & Portable Generator Sales, Install & Maintenance Technician Service-Contract & Fuel-Type Tracker

An independent generator dealer who sells, installs, and maintains standby and portable generators for residential and small-commercial customers has to track each installed unit's fuel type, transfer-switch configuration, and manufacturer-required maintenance interval — a natural-gas standby unit, a diesel unit, and a propane unit each carry different service intervals and different parts, and a maintenance visit scheduled with the wrong oil filter or spark-plug type on the truck is a wasted trip during storm season, exactly when customers are calling because their unit didn't start. A technician running dozens of standing maintenance contracts across a service territory currently tracks unit fuel type, warranty status, and maintenance due-dates on a paper service card and a shop parts shelf, and a unit whose annual maintenance lapsed because nobody flagged the due date is a generator that fails to start during the next outage — the one moment the customer actually needed it — while a warranty claim filed without the install date and service history on hand is a claim the dealer can't substantiate to the manufacturer.

The tool maintains a per-unit service record (fuel type, transfer-switch model, and manufacturer maintenance interval) with an automatic due-date calculator; a service-contract renewal tracker flagging any customer approaching contract expiration; and a warranty-claim documentation log tying install date and service history to each unit for manufacturer claim submission.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per dealer/technician. Distribution: r/generators and r/homeowners generator-service threads, the Electrical Generating Systems Association (EGSA) member network, and regional storm-season and power-outage-prone-area contractor Facebook groups where independent generator technicians already trade service-contract and fuel-type workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Small-Batch Artisan Cheesemaker Aging-Cave Batch & Wholesale Account Tracker

An independent artisan cheesemaker producing small-batch aged cheeses for farmers markets and wholesale cheese-shop accounts has to track each batch's specific milk source, culture and rennet used, and aging timeline — a washed-rind cheese and a hard aged cheese need entirely different cave humidity, turning frequency, and rind-washing schedules, and a batch pulled too early or left too long past its target age window is lost product that can't be sold at the price point it was aged for. A cheesemaker running dozens of wheels through the cave at different stages currently tracks batch recipes, turning/washing schedules, and target pull dates on a paper cave log and a wall calendar, and a wheel that missed its scheduled rind-washing because the log got busy during a big production week is a batch that develops unwanted mold, while a wholesale cheese-shop order that isn't reconciled against actual cave inventory is a promised delivery the maker can't fulfill because the cheese isn't actually ready yet.

The tool maintains a per-batch recipe and aging record (milk source, culture/rennet, and cave-turning/washing schedule) with a target-pull-date calculator; a cave-inventory view showing every wheel's current age and readiness status; and a wholesale/farmers-market account tracker matching committed orders against actual available inventory before a delivery is promised.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per cheesemaker. Distribution: r/cheesemaking and r/artisancheese practitioner threads, the American Cheese Society (ACS) member directory and annual conference, and regional farmstead-creamery and cheesemonger Facebook groups where independent makers already trade cave-scheduling and wholesale-account workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Independent Motorcycle Repair Shop VIN-Specific Parts & Multi-Bay Job Tracker

An independent motorcycle repair shop servicing a mix of cruisers, sport bikes, and vintage restorations has to match each repair job's specific make, model, and VIN against a parts catalog where the same-looking part number can differ by model year, and a mechanic who orders a part based on the bike's general model without checking the VIN-specific year break is a repair delayed a week waiting on the correct part to arrive. A shop running several bikes through the bay at once across routine service, warranty work, and vintage-restoration projects currently tracks job status, VIN-specific part orders, and each customer's service history on a paper repair ticket and a shop-parts shelf, and a warranty repair completed without the original purchase date and mileage logged is a claim the shop can't substantiate to the manufacturer, while a vintage-restoration project that sits mid-teardown for weeks with no tracked parts list is a bike a customer keeps calling about with no clear answer on status.

The tool maintains a per-job VIN-specific parts-order tracker matched to the bike's exact model year; a multi-bay job-status board sequencing routine service, warranty work, and long-term restoration projects separately; and a per-customer service history log with warranty-claim documentation (purchase date, mileage, and prior service records).

Monetization: $19-29/mo per shop. Distribution: r/motorcycles and r/Justrolledintotheshop repair-service threads, the Motorcycle Industry Council (MIC) independent-shop network, and regional motorcycle-club and vintage-restoration Facebook groups where independent shops already trade VIN-parts-sourcing and job-status workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Limousine & Party Bus Operator Vehicle Maintenance, Chauffeur Scheduling & Event-Booking Tracker

An independent limousine and party bus operator running a small fleet for weddings, proms, and corporate events has to track each vehicle's DOT-required maintenance interval and each chauffeur's shift assignment against a booking calendar where a single Saturday in prom or wedding season can have every vehicle double-booked if nobody catches the conflict early. An operator running several vehicles and a roster of part-time chauffeurs currently tracks vehicle maintenance due-dates, chauffeur availability, and event bookings on a paper dispatch board and a shared calendar app not built for fleet scheduling, and a vehicle dispatched with an overdue maintenance item is a breakdown risk on a night when a bride's entire wedding-day timeline depends on that limo showing up on time, while a chauffeur double-booked across two overlapping events because the schedule wasn't cross-checked is a client left waiting curbside with no ride.

The tool maintains a per-vehicle maintenance-due-date tracker (oil change, inspection, tire rotation) gating dispatch eligibility; a chauffeur scheduling board preventing double-booking across overlapping event windows; and an event-booking calendar tracking deposit status, pickup/drop-off details, and vehicle assignment per booking.

Monetization: $25-40/mo per operator. Distribution: r/Limos and limousine-operator trade forums, the National Limousine Association (NLA) member network and annual show, and prom-season and wedding-vendor Facebook groups where independent operators already trade fleet-maintenance and scheduling workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
