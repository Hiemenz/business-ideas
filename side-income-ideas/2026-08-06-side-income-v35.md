# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 35)

### 1. Microgreens & Urban Farm Producer Harvest Yield Log & Customer Order Tracker
Small-scale microgreens growers and urban farmers selling to restaurants, farmers markets, and direct consumers track harvest quantities, standing orders, and delivery commitments in a notebook or a text thread — making it easy to under-seed a tray and disappoint a restaurant account, or to lose track of which customer gets what variety at what price. This tool maintains a grow schedule (each tray's crop variety, seeding date, projected harvest date, and target yield in ounces), a harvest log (actual yield per tray vs. projected, with notes on quality or germination issues), and a customer order book (each customer's standing weekly order by variety and quantity, and one-time orders with delivery date). After each harvest, the tool shows how much of each variety is available vs. committed to orders — surfacing any shortfalls before delivery day. A weekly prep view tells the grower exactly how many trays of each variety to seed this week to fulfill next week's standing orders. Monetization: $8-12/mo per grower account. Distribution: r/microgreens, Profitable Plants and Microgreens Farmer community forums, urban farming Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Freelance Social Media Manager Client Content Calendar & Monthly Analytics Report
Social media managers handling 5-10 brand clients simultaneously juggle content calendars, caption drafting, post scheduling, and monthly reporting across clients — each on different platforms, different post frequencies, and different aesthetic guidelines. The monthly analytics report — pulling engagement numbers, follower growth, and reach from each platform and formatting them into a client-facing summary — takes 2-3 hours per client per month. This tool maintains a per-client content calendar (platform, post date, content type — static image/carousel/reel/story, caption draft, visual brief, and approval status — waiting for client approval, approved, scheduled, published), a monthly analytics data entry form (the manager inputs platform metrics from native analytics), and a formatted monthly report template that generates a client-facing PDF with key metrics, trends from prior month, and top performing posts. An approval workflow sends the client a link to review and approve scheduled content. Monetization: $18-30/mo per manager account (unlimited clients). Distribution: r/socialmediamarketing, Social Media Manager Facebook groups, Later and Hootsuite power user communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Self-Published Author Book Sales & Royalty Tracker Across Retail Platforms
Authors publishing through KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing), IngramSpark, Draft2Digital, Findaway Voices, and other retail platforms receive royalty reports from each platform at different times, in different formats, with different payment schedules — making it nearly impossible to know, at any given moment, how many copies have sold and what net royalty income has been earned this quarter. This tool lets the author import or manually enter sales data from each platform (units sold by format — ebook, paperback, hardcover, audiobook — by month and by title), and produces a consolidated dashboard: total units sold by title and format this month, YTD, and all-time; royalty income by platform; and a 12-month sales trend chart per title. A quarterly royalty estimate projects the expected payout from each platform based on current-month sales and the platform's typical payment lag. For authors with multiple titles or series, a per-series and per-title breakout shows which titles are performing and which are stagnating. Monetization: $8-12/mo per author account or $50/year. Distribution: r/selfpublish, 20BooksTo50K community, Alliance of Independent Authors (ALLi) member networks, KBoards. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Small Tattoo Studio Appointment Deposit Tracker & Aftercare Follow-Up Log
Independent tattoo artists and small studios (1-4 artists) managing appointment bookings collect deposits ($50-200 typically) to hold appointment slots, but tracking which clients have paid their deposit, which have rescheduled (and whether their deposit transferred or was forfeited), and which have upcoming appointments with outstanding balances is a constant administrative burden managed through Venmo notification history and a paper appointment book. This tool maintains a per-client appointment record (client name and contact, tattoo concept summary, appointment date, artist assigned, estimated session length, quoted price, deposit amount and payment method, and balance due at appointment), a deposit ledger showing all outstanding deposits and whether each is confirmed or provisional, and a rescheduling and cancellation log that tracks whether a forfeited deposit has been applied or refunded per the studio's policy. Post-appointment: a 1-week aftercare check-in reminder generates a message draft asking the client how healing is going — building loyalty and prompting clients to post healed photos and refer friends. Monetization: $10-15/mo per artist or studio account. Distribution: r/tattoo, Tattoodo and InkBetter artist community forums, tattoo artist Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Online Fitness Coach Client Weekly Check-In & Program Progression Tracker
Online fitness and nutrition coaches managing 15-40 coaching clients deliver weekly check-ins, program adjustments, and accountability — typically through a mix of Google Forms for check-ins, a spreadsheet for tracking progress, and a notes app for program adjustments. When a client emails "I'm not seeing progress," the coach has no systematic place to pull up the last 8 weeks of check-in data to identify the trend. This tool maintains a per-client profile (current program, training days per week, nutrition target, and goal), a weekly check-in log (weight, measurements if tracked, training completion rate, nutrition adherence rate, energy and recovery notes, and any client comments), and a program note log (when the coach adjusts a client's program — adds volume, changes intensity, modifies nutrition — the change is logged with the date and reason). A client-facing view shows their own 12-week progress chart. A coach-facing dashboard shows every client's check-in completion status for the current week and flags clients who haven't checked in in 10+ days. Monthly: a client progress summary the coach can send to reinforce what's working. Monetization: $15-25/mo per coach account (up to 40 clients). Distribution: r/OnlineFitness, Online Trainer Academy community, fitness coaching Facebook groups, Trainerize and TrueCoach alternative-seeker communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. Custom Embroidery & Apparel Shop Order Production Queue Tracker
Custom embroidery shops and screen printing businesses taking on 20-100 orders per week manage their production queue through a whiteboard or a shared spreadsheet — making it difficult to communicate realistic turnaround times to customers, prioritize rush orders, and ensure each order moves through digitizing, approval, production, and shipping without falling through the cracks. This tool maintains a per-order record (customer name, contact, garment type and quantity, logo or design file, thread color selections or ink colors, placement specifications, rush status, promised ship date, and current stage in the production workflow — order received/artwork approved/in production/quality check/shipped), a production calendar showing orders due each day of the week, and a backlog dashboard showing total garments in queue by stage. A customer notification trigger generates an email draft when the order moves to each new stage (e.g., "Your order is now in production — estimated ship date X"). Optional: a pricing calculator for quoted estimates based on garment quantity, stitch count, and number of colors. Monetization: $15-25/mo per shop account. Distribution: NNEP (National Network of Embroidery Professionals) member communities, r/embroidery, Embroidery Hub and Decorated Apparel forum communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
