# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-18 (Session 91)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- Simple, fast-to-build workflow trackers — no regulatory compliance angle.
- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.

### 1. Mobile Pet Groomer Route, Pet Profile & Grooming-History Tracker

Independent mobile pet groomers running a van route need to track a per-pet grooming profile — breed, coat type, preferred cut style, temperament and handling notes (a dog that needs a muzzle, a cat that only tolerates a certain groomer) — since a groomer servicing 60-120 repeat pets across a weekly or biweekly route can't rely on memory to know which client's poodle gets a specific trim length versus which one needs an extra-gentle approach around the ears, and showing up without that context on a repeat visit reads as careless to an owner who's paying a premium for the mobile convenience. The tool also needs a last-groomed-date and rebooking-interval tracker, since most pets rebook on a fixed 4-8 week cycle and a groomer without a clear view of who's coming due loses billable route density to gaps in the schedule. It also needs a simple route-sequencing view for the day's stops and a per-pet product-usage note (a pet with sensitive skin that needs a specific shampoo), since restocking the van correctly matters when there's no quick trip back to a shop between appointments.

The tool maintains a per-pet grooming profile with coat/temperament notes, a last-groomed/rebooking-interval tracker, and a day-view route sequencer.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/doggrooming and mobile-groomer Facebook groups, the National Dog Groomers Association of America (NDGAA) member directory, and local pet-service referral networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Independent Chimney Sweep Property Service-History & Seasonal-Rush Route Tracker

Independent chimney sweeps servicing homes on an annual or biennial inspection cycle need to track a per-property service-history record — flue condition, creosote-buildup findings, appliance type (wood-burning, gas insert, pellet stove), and any repair recommendations made at the last visit — since a sweep running 300-600 properties across a service area can't rely on memory to know which chimney had a moderate creosote finding last year that needs a closer look this visit versus which one was clean and only needs a routine sweep. The tool also needs a seasonal-rush scheduling view, since the bulk of a sweep's annual volume compresses into the fall pre-heating-season rush, and a sweep without a clear view of which returning clients haven't rebooked yet loses density during the only weeks of the year that matter most. It also needs a simple inspection-report generator per visit, since homeowners and, in some cases, insurance companies want documentation that the chimney was inspected and any recommended repairs noted. Most independent sweeps currently manage this across a paper service card per address and a fall scramble to remember who's overdue, with no structured link between a specific chimney's last finding and this year's inspection.

The tool maintains a per-property service-history record, a seasonal-rush rebooking tracker, and a per-visit inspection-report generator.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/Fireplaces and chimney-sweep trade Facebook groups, the Chimney Safety Institute of America (CSIA) certified-sweep directory, and local hearth-and-fireplace-shop referral networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Freelance Professional Organizer & Closet Designer Project Pipeline & Product-Order Tracker

Independent professional organizers taking on home and closet decluttering projects need to track a per-client project pipeline showing which room or closet is in the consultation, sorting, product-ordering, or install stage, since an organizer running 3-6 concurrent multi-session engagements can't rely on memory to know whose pantry is still awaiting a bin-size decision versus whose closet system just needs a final install visit. The tool also needs a product/bin-order tracker recording exact measurements and SKUs ordered per room, since a container or shelving system ordered to the wrong dimension means a wasted trip and a delayed project, and organizers frequently order from multiple suppliers for a single space. It also needs a before-and-after photo log tied to each project, since organizers use documented transformations both as the client's own reference and as portfolio material for future bookings. Most independent organizers currently manage this across a phone photo roll and a notes app with no structured link between a specific room's measurements and the product order it fed into.

The tool maintains a per-client project pipeline, a product/bin-order tracker with measurements, and a before-after photo log per room.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/OrganizationPorn and professional-organizer Facebook groups, the National Association of Productivity and Organizing Professionals (NAPO) member directory, and local interior-designer referral networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Wedding Officiant Ceremony Script & Rehearsal-Timing Tracker

Independent wedding officiants booking multiple ceremonies a weekend during peak season need to track a per-couple ceremony-planning record — script draft status, vow-customization notes, readings and ring-exchange sequencing, and any specific cultural or religious elements requested — since an officiant with 4-8 weddings booked across a summer Saturday stretch can't rely on memory to know whose script is still awaiting final approval versus whose ceremony is locked and ready. The tool also needs a rehearsal-and-ceremony-timing tracker, since a Saturday with back-to-back bookings requires precise arrival, rehearsal, and ceremony-start windows across different venues, and a scheduling conflict discovered the week of two weddings is a business-ending mistake for whichever couple loses their officiant. It also needs a document-filing tracker for the signed marriage license and any state-specific paperwork deadline, since a license not filed within the state's required window after the ceremony can invalidate the marriage on a technicality that lands squarely on the officiant. Most independent officiants currently manage this across a shared Google Doc per couple and a personal calendar with no structured link between a specific ceremony's script status and its rehearsal timing.

The tool maintains a per-couple ceremony-planning and script tracker, a rehearsal/ceremony-timing scheduler, and a marriage-license filing-deadline tracker.

Monetization: $12-20/mo per account. Distribution: r/weddingplanning officiant threads, the American Marriage Ministries and Universal Life Church officiant communities, and local wedding-vendor Facebook referral groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Mobile Auto Detailer Route, Vehicle Service-History & Product Tracker

Independent mobile auto detailers running a van or trailer route need to track a per-vehicle service-history record — last detail date, service tier (wash-and-wax versus full paint correction or ceramic coating), and any paint-condition notes — since a detailer servicing 50-100 residential repeat clients plus a handful of commercial fleet accounts can't rely on memory to know which client's ceramic coating is still under its warranty window versus which vehicle is due for a routine maintenance wash. The tool also needs a route-sequencing view for the day's appointments, since drive time between stops directly eats into billable hours, and a product/inventory tracker for the specific waxes, coatings, and interior products used per vehicle, since running short on a specialty product mid-route stalls the day. It also needs a simple fleet-account tracker for commercial clients with multiple vehicles on a rotating service schedule, since a fleet manager expects a consolidated view rather than tracking each vehicle as a separate unrelated client. Most independent detailers currently manage this across a paper appointment book and memory of which car got which treatment, with no structured link between a specific vehicle's service history and its next-due date.

The tool maintains a per-vehicle service-history record, a day-view route sequencer, and a product-inventory/fleet-account tracker.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/AutoDetailing and mobile-detailer Facebook groups, the International Detailing Association (IDA) member directory, and local car-dealership and fleet-manager referral networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Freelance Genealogist & Family History Researcher Project & Source-Citation Tracker

Independent genealogists taking on family-history research projects need to track a per-client research-project record — the research question, generations and surnames in scope, and current status across what can be a months-long engagement — since a genealogist running 5-10 concurrent client projects can't rely on memory to know whose immigration-record search is still pending an archive response versus whose project is ready for a final report. The tool also needs a source-citation tracker recording every archive, database, and record consulted per finding, since genealogical work lives or dies on documented, verifiable sourcing, and a researcher who can't reproduce exactly where a claimed ancestor connection came from loses credibility with a client who may need that citation for a lineage society application. It also needs an archive-request and correspondence tracker, since many searches depend on records requested from courthouses, church archives, or foreign civil registries that can take weeks or months to arrive, and a researcher juggling a dozen outstanding requests across different institutions needs a clear view of what's still pending. Most independent genealogists currently manage this across a personal research binder and a folder of citation notes with no structured link between a specific client's project and the sources that support each finding.

The tool maintains a per-client research-project tracker, a source-citation log per finding, and an archive-request/correspondence tracker.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: r/Genealogy and the Association of Professional Genealogists (APG) member directory, plus regional genealogical society referral networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
