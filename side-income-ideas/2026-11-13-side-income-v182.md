# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-13 (Round 182)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a microgreens grower, a mushroom grower, a firewood seller, a wedding calligrapher, a coding-interview coach and a spreadsheet consultant.

---

### 1. Microgreens Grower Tray, Harvest & Standing-Order Tracker

A grower who sells microgreens to restaurants and at markets has to track trays by variety, sow date, expected harvest date and standing weekly orders. Microgreens are perishable, so a missed sowing means a missed delivery next week. Yield per tray and cost per tray decide which varieties to grow.

The tool schedules sowing dates backward from each delivery, tracks tray stage and harvest yield, records standing orders per customer and shows margin per variety.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per grower. Distribution: urban-farming Facebook groups, r/microgreens and local chef networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Mushroom Grower Block, Fruiting & Sales Tracker

A small mushroom cultivator has to track substrate blocks, inoculation dates, fruiting cycles, harvest weights and contamination losses. A grower with 100+ blocks cannot remember which are ready, and contamination wipes out work. Sales to restaurants and markets are tied to harvest timing.

The tool tracks blocks by species and stage, records harvest weight per block, logs contamination losses and shows yield and margin per species.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per grower. Distribution: mushroom-growing communities, r/mycology cultivation threads and farmers-market vendor groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Firewood Seller Delivery, Cord & Seasoning Tracker

A firewood seller has to track wood stacks by species and seasoning date, customer orders, delivery routes and cord quantities. Selling wood that is not yet dry hurts reputation, and fall demand spikes. Repeat customers reorder each year, so a reminder helps.

The tool tracks stacks with seasoning dates, schedules deliveries by route, records orders and amounts and sends a yearly reorder reminder.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per seller. Distribution: firewood-seller Facebook groups, r/Homesteading and rural community boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to fall.

### 4. Wedding Calligrapher Order, Envelope & Proof Tracker

A calligrapher who addresses envelopes and makes place cards has to track each order's quantity, guest-list file, proof approval, ink and paper stock, and delivery date. Wedding timelines are strict, and a guest list that changes late means rework. Per-piece pricing depends on counts.

The tool tracks orders with piece counts and price, guest-list versions, proof approvals and a countdown to the shipping deadline.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per calligrapher. Distribution: calligraphy communities, r/Calligraphy and wedding-vendor networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Coding-Interview Coach Student, Mock-Interview & Offer Tracker

A coach who prepares people for software interviews has to track each student's target companies, topics covered, mock interviews done and weak areas. With 10-20 students, recalling which problems a student has already seen matters. Coaches also track outcomes (offers) for their reputation.

The tool stores per-student targets and weak topics, logs mock interviews with scores, tracks lesson packages and records offers received.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per coach. Distribution: tech-career communities, r/cscareerquestions and coach Discords. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Spreadsheet Consultant Client Model, Version & Handover Tracker

A freelancer who builds spreadsheets and dashboards for small businesses has to track each client's models, versions, formulas changed and handover notes. Clients return months later asking to change one number, and the consultant needs to know what the file does. Retainer clients need hours tracking.

The tool tracks client files with version notes and a change log, stores handover documentation and tracks hours against retainers.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per consultant. Distribution: spreadsheet and Excel communities, r/excel consultant threads and freelance groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
