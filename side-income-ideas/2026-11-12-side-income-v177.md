# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-12 (Round 177)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a sports-card flipper, a freelance proofreader, a wedding videographer, a plant propagation seller, a ceramics maker and a private yoga teacher.

---

### 1. Sports-Card Flipper Purchase, Grading & Sale Tracker

A person who buys sports and trading cards to grade and resell has to track each card: purchase price, grading cost, grade received, listing platform and sale price. Grading takes weeks, and a flipper with 50-200 cards in the pipeline loses track of which are at the grader, which are listed and which are sold. Without records, margin per card after fees is unknown.

The tool tracks cards through bought, sent to grader, graded, listed and sold, records all costs and fees and shows profit per card and per category.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per flipper. Distribution: card-collecting communities, r/sportscards and card-flipper Discords. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Freelance Proofreader Manuscript, Word-Count & Deadline Tracker

A freelance proofreader and copyeditor has to track each manuscript's word count, rate per word, deadline, stage (first pass, second pass, author queries) and payment. With 5-10 projects, deadlines overlap and authors send revisions late. Per-word pay and rush fees need correct invoices.

The tool tracks manuscripts with word counts, stage and due date, calculates invoice totals and rush fees and logs author queries per project.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per proofreader. Distribution: editor communities, r/editors and freelance-writer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Wedding Videographer Package, Footage & Delivery Tracker

A wedding videographer has to track each couple's package, the shoot date, raw footage backups, editing stage and delivery date. Several weddings in the editing queue at the same time make it easy to promise a date that cannot be met. Couples expect a highlight reel soon after the wedding and a full film months later.

The tool tracks weddings with package and shoot date, footage backup status, edit stage with delivery countdown and balance payments.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per videographer. Distribution: wedding-videographer Facebook groups, r/weddingvideography and vendor networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. Plant Propagation Seller Cutting, Stock & Order Tracker

A hobbyist who propagates houseplants and sells cuttings online has to track each plant's cutting date, rooting stage, stock available and incoming orders. Selling a cutting that has not rooted yet disappoints a customer, and demand for rare plants changes quickly. Shipping delays in cold weather add another constraint.

The tool tracks cuttings by stage with expected ready dates, current stock by plant, orders against stock and a weather-hold flag for shipping.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per seller. Distribution: plant-seller Facebook groups, r/houseplants and Etsy seller communities. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Ceramics Maker Kiln-Batch, Piece & Market-Sales Tracker

A potter has to track pieces through throwing, drying, bisque, glazing and glaze firing. Kiln batches take days, and a failed firing loses pieces. Selling at markets and online means knowing what is finished and what is in the kiln. Pricing depends on knowing the cost and time of each piece.

The tool tracks each piece through stages with kiln-batch assignment, logs losses per firing, tracks finished stock by channel and shows margin per piece type.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per potter. Distribution: pottery communities, r/Pottery and craft-market maker groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 6. Private Yoga Teacher Student, Session & Package Tracker

A private yoga teacher works one-on-one and with small groups, and has to track each student's goals, injuries and limitations, session notes and package balance. With 15-30 students, the teacher needs to recall what was practiced and what to avoid. Package renewals are lost when a student's last session passes unnoticed.

The tool keeps a per-student profile with limitations and session notes, tracks package balances with a renewal prompt and shows a weekly schedule.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per teacher. Distribution: yoga-teacher Facebook groups, r/yoga teacher threads and local wellness networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
