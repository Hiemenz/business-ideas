# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 32)

### 1. Aerial Fitness Studio Equipment Rigging Inspection Log & Safety Certification Tracker
Aerial yoga studios, circus arts schools, and aerial silks facilities that hang equipment from rigging points (silks, lyra/hoop, trapeze, rope) have a safety-critical inspection obligation that most manage informally: a note on a whiteboard or in a binder saying when each rig point was last inspected, with no systematic record of who inspected it, what was checked, or when the next inspection is due. This tool maintains a per-equipment log (equipment type — silk/lyra/hammock/rope, rig point location, installation date, hardware specifications, and any manufacturer or rigger load ratings), a rigging inspection record (inspection date, inspector name and certifications, checklist items evaluated — carabiner condition, swivel function, ceiling anchor integrity, load bearing hardware, fabric/textile condition — pass/fail, and any corrective actions), and an inspection due date calendar showing every piece of aerial equipment and when the next inspection is required (configurable: monthly for load-bearing hardware, every 6 months for structural anchor points). When a student or instructor is injured, the studio has documented proof of systematic safety inspection. Bonus: a fabric/textile retirement tracker (silks and aerial fabrics have a recommended retirement period based on hours of use). Monetization: $15-22/mo per studio. Distribution: Aerial Arts and Circus Arts faculty Facebook groups, r/Aerials, Cirque du Monde and Aerial Arts Academy alumni networks. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Hobby Beekeeper Colony Health Log & Treatment Record Tracker
Hobbyist and sideline beekeepers with 2-20 hives conduct seasonal inspections throughout the year and need to remember — months later — when they last saw eggs in hive #3, what mite load was recorded in the August oxalic acid treatment, and which colony overwintered successfully vs. died out. Paper hive journals get lost; apps like Hive Tracks exist but are overkill for 5 hives and require a learning curve. This tool maintains a per-hive record (hive ID, installation date, queen source and lineage, hive body configuration) and a per-inspection log (date, weather, brood pattern observation — eggs/young larvae/capped brood, queen seen or evidence of queen, honey stores estimate, temperament rating, and any treatments applied with product, dose, and application method). An annual summary for each hive shows the season's inspection timeline, mite treatment history, and winter preparation status. Reminders suggest when the next inspection or treatment is due based on the last recorded date. Monetization: $5-8/mo per beekeeper or $30/year flat. Distribution: American Beekeeping Federation regional association networks, r/Beekeeping, BackYardHive and BeeMaster community forums. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Independent Bookkeeper Client Engagement Tracker & Monthly Deliverable Checklist
Self-employed bookkeepers serving 8-20 small business clients per month face a recurring coordination problem: each client has a different close date, different deliverables (bank reconciliation, P&L, categorization review, sales tax prep, payroll summary), and different communication style. Keeping track of which client's books are reconciled for April, which still need the credit card statement uploaded, and which need a payroll tax deposit reminded this week is a mental load that grows linearly with client count. This tool maintains a per-client profile (business type, accounting software, close date, monthly deliverables checklist, and billing amount), and for each month generates a per-client deliverable checklist (each item with status: waiting on client, in progress, completed). A monthly dashboard shows all clients with a completion percentage — the bookkeeper's weekly prioritization view. When a deliverable is blocked waiting on a client document, one click generates a "can you send me..." email draft. Optionally tracks billable hours per client per month for hourly clients. Monetization: $15-25/mo per bookkeeper account. Distribution: Bookkeeping Business Academy communities, r/Bookkeeping, QuickBooks ProAdvisor and Xero partner community forums. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Custom Woodworking Shop Client Project Quote & Material Order Tracker
Custom furniture makers and woodworking shops taking on client commissions — dining tables, built-ins, bedroom sets — lose money on projects when material costs aren't tracked against the original quote, and lose client trust when promised delivery dates slip without advance communication. This tool maintains a per-project record: client name, project description, wood species and estimated board feet, hardware list, quoted price, target completion date, and deposit received. As the project progresses, actual material costs are logged (lumber purchase receipts, hardware, finish supplies) against the quoted estimate — flagging when material overruns are eating the margin. A project timeline tracker shows each phase (design approval, material sourcing, rough milling, joinery, assembly, finishing, delivery) with planned and actual dates. A client communication log records the last update sent and generates a progress update email draft when a milestone is complete. Monetization: $12-18/mo per shop account. Distribution: r/woodworking, FineWoodworking and Popular Woodworking community forums, Sawdust & Shavings podcast listener communities, woodworking guild social media groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Youth Soccer Club Player Registration & Equipment Size Tracker
Youth soccer club administrators and recreational league coordinators handling 100-500 player registrations per season collect jersey sizes, birth certificate verification status, and parent emergency contacts through a combination of paper forms and email, then struggle to pull the "who still needs to submit a birth certificate?" list or "what size jersey did the U10 team need?" summary when equipment ordering time comes. This tool maintains a player registry per season (player name, age group/team assignment, date of birth, birth certificate verification status, parent contacts, jersey size, shorts size, sock size), tracks registration completeness per player (which required items have been received vs. outstanding), and generates equipment order summaries by size across the age group or whole club. A parent communication module drafts reminder emails for players with incomplete registrations. Coaches get a team roster card with each player's contact info and any medical notes flagged by the parent. Monetization: $20-40/season or $8-15/mo during active registration. Distribution: US Youth Soccer state association administrator networks, r/bootroom (soccer equipment communities), AYSO (American Youth Soccer Organization) volunteer administrator Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Meal Prep Service Client Preference & Weekly Delivery Log
Independent meal prep services and personal cooks who prepare and deliver weekly meals for 10-30 clients maintain client dietary information and preferences across text messages and a mental map — making it easy to accidentally send a client with a nut allergy a dish that was prepared near nuts, or forget that a client switched to high-protein meals last month. This tool maintains a per-client profile (household size, dietary restrictions by severity — allergy vs. preference, cuisine preferences, foods to exclude, and container return tracking if the service uses returnable containers), a weekly delivery log where the cook records which meals went to each client and any substitutions made, and a client preference update log when a client adjusts their profile. A weekly order summary shows all clients, their current preferences, and any notes from last week's delivery — the cook's reference before starting meal prep. A container tracking module logs how many containers each client has outstanding and triggers a polite reminder when more than 8 are outstanding. Monetization: $12-18/mo per cook account. Distribution: r/MealPrep professional cook communities, USPCA (United States Personal Chef Association) member networks, meal prep service Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
