# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-15 (Round 190)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a guitar repairer, a supper-club host, a pop-up market vendor, a dance instructor, a wedding officiant and a hot-tub service technician.

---

### 1. Guitar Repairer Instrument, Repair & Pickup Tracker

A guitar tech who sets up, refrets and repairs instruments has to track each instrument's condition on arrival, work quoted, parts and pickup date. Photos at intake protect both sides, and customers call asking for updates.

The tool logs intake photos, tracks repair stage and parts, records quotes and sends ready-for-pickup texts.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per tech. Distribution: guitar-tech communities, r/guitarrepair and local music-shop networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 2. Supper-Club Host Event, Menu & Guest Tracker

A host who runs paid dinner events has to track menus, guest counts, dietary needs, ticket sales and ingredient cost. Seats sell in waves, and repeat guests are the best source of the next sold-out night.

The tool tracks events and seat sales, stores guest dietary notes, computes cost per plate and keeps a repeat-guest list.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per host. Distribution: supper-club and home-chef communities, r/Cooking and local food groups. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 3. Pop-Up Market Vendor Event, Stock & Sales Tracker

A vendor who sells at weekend markets and pop-ups has to track which events to attend, booth fees, stock brought and sales. Some markets are worth it and some lose money, but few vendors compare them.

The tool logs event costs and sales, shows profit per event, tracks stock per trip and flags events worth repeating.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per vendor. Distribution: market-vendor Facebook groups, r/smallbusiness and craft-fair boards. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 4. Dance Instructor Class, Student & Package Tracker

An independent dance instructor who teaches private lessons and small classes has to track students, class packages, attendance and recital or showcase dates. Package renewals and no-shows are easy to miss.

The tool tracks students and class counts, sends renewal reminders, handles waitlists and manages showcase rosters.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per instructor. Distribution: dance-teacher communities, local studio networks and r/Dance. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.

### 5. Wedding Officiant Ceremony, Script & Booking Tracker

A wedding officiant has to track couples, ceremony dates, script drafts, rehearsal times and fees. Couples ask for script changes late, and dates cluster on weekends.

The tool tracks bookings with deposits, stores script versions per couple, schedules rehearsals and sends a day-before checklist.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per officiant. Distribution: wedding-vendor groups, local planner networks and r/weddingplanning. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks, timed to wedding season.

### 6. Hot-Tub Service Technician Route, Water & Recurring-Visit Tracker

A technician who services hot tubs has to track each customer's tub, chemical use, filter dates and visit schedule. Recurring route customers pay monthly, and filters and covers are sales opportunities.

The tool tracks tubs and visit schedules, logs water readings, reminds filter swaps and plans routes.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per technician. Distribution: hot-tub-service groups, r/hottub and local pool-service networks. Time to first dollar: 2 weeks.
