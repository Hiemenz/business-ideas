# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-08 (Session 43)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

---

### 1. Independent Handyman Job Estimate & Material Cost Tracker
Solo handymen bounce between small jobs all week — a leaky faucet Monday, a deck repair Wednesday, a drywall patch Friday — and most track estimates, material receipts, and job profitability in a paper notebook or not at all. The result: jobs quoted too low once material costs are tallied, receipts lost before tax season, and no clear picture of which job types (plumbing vs. carpentry vs. drywall) are actually profitable per hour.

The tool maintains a job record per client with estimate line items, actual material costs (photo receipt capture with running total), labor hours logged, and a computed profit-per-job and profit-per-hour figure. A dashboard view ranks job types by average margin so the handyman can steer toward the work that actually pays.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: r/Handyman and local Nextdoor/Facebook "handyman services" groups, plus Angi/Thumbtack pro communities where solo operators already discuss pricing struggles. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Lactation Consultant (IBCLC) Client Visit & Insurance Superbill Generator
International Board Certified Lactation Consultants typically see clients for a handful of visits around birth — an initial consult, a weight-check follow-up, a return visit for a new issue — and most insurance plans reimburse these visits under the ACA's breastfeeding support mandate, but only if the IBCLC provides the family a properly formatted superbill (CPT codes, diagnosis codes, NPI number) for out-of-network reimbursement claims. Solo IBCLCs currently recreate this document by hand in Word for every visit, and many skip it entirely because it's tedious, leaving money on the table for clients who'd otherwise pay out of pocket.

The tool maintains a client roster with due dates/birth dates, a visit log (date, duration, feeding issue addressed, weight data), and a one-click superbill generator pre-filled with the correct CPT/ICD-10 codes for lactation consultation visits. A simple caseload calendar view shows upcoming and overdue follow-up visits.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the International Lactation Consultant Association (ILCA) member forums and USLCA (United States Lactation Consultant Association) private practice group. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Chimney Sweep & Fireplace Service Technician Route & Service History Tracker
Independent chimney sweeps run a seasonal, route-heavy business — dense fall/winter bookings across a service area, each home needing service history (creosote buildup severity, last cap replacement, flue liner condition) referenced year over year. Most sweeps currently track this in a mix of paper inspection carbon-copies and a plain calendar, making it hard to proactively remind repeat customers ("you're due for your annual sweep") or to route a day's jobs efficiently by neighborhood.

The tool maintains a property record per address with chimney type, last service date, findings/photos from each visit, and recommended next-service date, plus a day-of route view that groups scheduled jobs by geographic cluster. An annual-reminder queue auto-generates the "you're due" outreach list each fall.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per account. Distribution: the Chimney Safety Institute of America (CSIA) certified-sweep directory community and local trade Facebook groups for chimney/hearth professionals. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks (seasonal — best launched in late summer ahead of fall booking season).

### 4. Freelance Spoken-Language Interpreter Assignment & Mileage Tracker
Freelance interpreters (medical, legal, ASL, or spoken-language) work per-assignment gigs booked through multiple agencies simultaneously — a hospital appointment via one agency, a deposition via another, a school IEP meeting via a third — each with different pay rates, mileage reimbursement rules, and invoicing cadences. Tracking which agency booked which assignment, whether it was cancelled within the no-fee window, and what's owed across 3-5 different agencies each month is currently a spreadsheet or notebook exercise most interpreters do poorly, leading to underbilled hours and missed mileage reimbursement.

The tool maintains an assignment log (agency, date, location, duration, rate type, cancellation status) with automatic mileage calculation between assignments, and a per-agency invoice generator that compiles the billing period's assignments into the format each agency expects.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per account. Distribution: the Registry of Interpreters for the Deaf (RID) member community for ASL interpreters, and National Association of Judiciary Interpreters and Translators (NAJIT) forums for spoken-language freelancers. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Tailor & Alterations Shop Order Ticket Tracker
Solo tailors and alterations shops handle a constant stream of small jobs — hem this, take in that, replace a zipper — each with a pickup deadline, specific measurements, and sometimes a customer's own fabric or garment that must not get mixed up with others. Most shops still use paper claim tickets stapled to garments, which works until a rush week hits and tickets get lost, measurements get misremembered, or a pickup date gets missed.

The tool maintains an order ticket per garment with customer info, alteration details, measurements, promised pickup date, and payment status, plus a queue view sorted by due date so the busiest days are visible at a glance. A searchable customer history lets repeat clients' prior measurements be pulled up instantly.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per account. Distribution: the Association of Sewing and Design Professionals (ASDP) member community and local tailor/alterations Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 6. Independent Janitorial & Commercial Cleaning Contractor Contract & Crew Tracker
Solo owners of small commercial cleaning operations juggle multiple client contracts (different buildings, different cleaning frequencies, different scope-of-work checklists per contract) and a small crew of part-time cleaners assigned across those sites. Tracking which crew member is assigned where, whether a site's supply closet needs restocking, and whether each contract's specific scope (windows monthly, floors weekly, trash nightly) was actually completed currently lives in text messages and a paper schedule that doesn't scale past a handful of accounts.

The tool maintains a client contract record with scope-of-work checklist and service frequency, a crew assignment calendar mapping cleaners to sites and shifts, and a supply inventory per site with low-stock flags. A completed-task log per visit gives the owner proof of service for client billing disputes.

Monetization: $19-35/mo per account. Distribution: the Building Service Contractors Association International (BSCAI) member community and local commercial cleaning business Facebook/Reddit groups (r/CleaningBusinessOwners). Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
