# Side Income Ideas - 2026-07-31 (Round 8)

### 1. Independent Tattoo & Piercing Studio Appointment & Aftercare Tracker
A booking and client management app for solo tattoo artists and small piercing studios: appointment scheduling with design deposit collection, a healed-photo submission flow, and automated aftercare reminder sequences by day post-session. Monetization: $25-45/mo SaaS subscription per artist. Distribution: tattoo artist Instagram/TikTok communities and subreddits (r/tattoo, r/TattooArtists). Time to first dollar: 30-45 days.

### 2. Short-Term Rental Cleaner Dispatch & Quality-Check App
A mobile-first tool for independent cleaning teams servicing Airbnb/VRBO properties: job dispatch with checkout time pulled from the host's calendar, a room-by-room photo checklist submitted to the host on completion, and automated "ready for guests" notification. Monetization: $30-60/mo per cleaning business. Distribution: Airbnb host Facebook groups (large, active, constantly discussing cleaning logistics). Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 3. Food Truck Health Inspection & License Renewal Tracker
Tracks every license, permit, and health inspection certificate a food truck operator must maintain: county health permits, commissary agreements, fire extinguisher certifications, vehicle registration, and state food handler cards — with deadline alerts and a document vault for roadside inspections. Monetization: $15-30/mo subscription. Distribution: food truck owner associations, Facebook groups, and food truck park operators who manage multiple vendors. Time to first dollar: 4-6 weeks.

### 4. Personal Trainer Client Progress & Session Package Tracker
A lightweight CRM for independent personal trainers: tracks each client's remaining sessions in a purchased package, logs workout data and body measurements per session, sends automated package-expiry nudges, and generates a PDF progress report the trainer shares with the client monthly. Monetization: $20-40/mo per trainer. Distribution: personal trainer certification communities (NASM, ACE alumni groups, r/personaltraining). Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 5. Academic Tutor Scheduling, Payment & Progress-Report App
Manages the full tutor workflow: recurring session scheduling with parent/student reminders, per-session payment collection via Stripe, a running session log (topics covered, homework assigned), and a monthly progress summary email to parents. Monetization: $15-35/mo per tutor or tutoring company. Distribution: tutoring business Facebook groups, Wyzant tutor community, education entrepreneur newsletters. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 6. Specialty Vehicle Registration & Title Tracking App (RVs, Boats, Trailers)
RV owners, boat owners, and trailer operators juggle registrations across multiple states, storage facility permits, and annual inspection stickers with no single tool to track them. This app stores all registration documents, tracks expiration dates by vehicle and state, and sends renewal alerts timed to each state's early-renewal window. Monetization: $8-18/mo subscription or $29 one-time purchase. Distribution: RV owner forums (iRV2, RVillage), boating subreddits, and Facebook groups for full-time RVers. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.
