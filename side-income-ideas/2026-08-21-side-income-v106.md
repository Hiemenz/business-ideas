# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-21 (Round 106)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses. These are NOT compliance tools — they're practical day-to-day workflow trackers that replace the messy spreadsheet, whiteboard, or notebook a solo operator currently uses to run their book of business. Each idea targets a different type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo, names a real distribution channel, and gives realistic per-account pricing and a time to first dollar.

---

### 1. Small-Batch Artisan Cheesemaker Batch Aging & Wholesale Account Tracker

A small-batch artisan cheesemaker running a farmstead or micro-creamery operation makes cheese in discrete batches — each with its own milk-source date, culture and rennet lot, make date, and a specific aging target that can run anywhere from a few weeks for a fresh chèvre to 12-24 months for an aged cheddar or alpine-style wheel — and currently tracks which wheels in the aging cave are ready to sell, which need turning or brine-washing this week, and which batch a specific wholesale account's standing order should be pulled from using a mix of chalk marks on the rind, a paper cave log, and memory. A maker running even a modest handful of batches at different aging stages loses real revenue when a wheel gets forgotten past its peak window, or when a farmers-market or restaurant wholesale order goes out from the wrong batch and breaks flavor consistency a repeat buyer was counting on.

The tool maintains a per-batch record (make date, milk source, culture/rennet lot, target aging window, and current aging-room location) with a "ready to sell" and "needs attention this week" (turn, wash, flip) view sorted by days-until-peak across every batch in the cave, and a wholesale account tracker linking each standing restaurant or retail order to the specific batch it should be fulfilled from, with a running tally of pounds committed versus pounds available per batch.

Monetization: $19-35/mo per account. Distribution: the American Cheese Society (ACS) member directory and conference, and regional farmstead-creamery associations and Facebook groups where small producers already trade aging-room and wholesale-account workflow tips. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 2. Independent Fishmonger & Fresh Seafood Market Dock-to-Case Inventory & Cold-Chain Tracker

An independent fishmonger or small fresh-seafood market buys directly off multiple boats at the dock — different species, different daily catch volumes, different prices per pound depending on that morning's landings — and has to move nearly all of it before it ages out of peak freshness, typically within a few days per species. Tracking which lot came off which boat on which date, what it cost per pound, how much is left in the case, and which lots are approaching their sell-by window is currently done with a grease-pencil case tag and a notebook by the register, and a market buying from 3-6 boats a week loses margin two ways: overpaying because yesterday's cost per pound wasn't checked before today's buy, and writing off product that aged out because no one flagged it was approaching its window until the smell did.

The tool maintains a per-lot intake record (boat/source, species, date landed, weight, and cost per pound) tied to a running case-inventory count by species, with a freshness-window countdown flagging any lot approaching its sell-by target, and a simple daily-sales log reconciling pounds sold against pounds received per lot to surface shrinkage and margin per species.

Monetization: $19-35/mo per account. Distribution: regional seafood dealer associations and dock-side co-op bulletin boards, and independent fish market owner groups on Facebook organized by coastal region. Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks.

### 3. Freelance / Per-Diem Embalmer & Removal Technician Multi-Funeral-Home Call Tracker

A freelance or per-diem embalmer and removal technician contracts with several small independent funeral homes that don't have enough case volume to justify a full-time embalmer on staff, taking on-call rotations across multiple firms simultaneously — a body removal or embalming case can come in at any hour, each funeral home has its own preferred procedures, casket/cosmetic preferences, and family-viewing timeline, and a technician juggling on-call coverage for 4-8 different homes has no single place to see which firm is on-call this week, which case is due for viewing prep by when, and what that specific firm's standard practices are, currently tracked across a personal phone log and separate texts from each funeral director.

The tool maintains a per-funeral-home client profile (standard procedures, preferred products, typical turnaround expectation, and per-case rate) and a case queue tracking each case's intake date/time, required completion (viewing or service date), and status, with an on-call rotation calendar showing which firm(s) the technician is covering this week and a completed-case invoicing log totaling cases per firm per pay period.

Monetization: $15-29/mo per account. Distribution: the American Society of Embalmers and state funeral director association per-diem/contract-staff bulletin boards, where independent embalmers already coordinate multi-firm on-call coverage. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Independent Letterpress & Custom Stationery Printer Job Queue, Plate & Paper-Stock Tracker

An independent letterpress printer producing wedding invitations, custom stationery, and small-batch commercial print work runs each job through a distinct sequence — proof approval, custom photopolymer plate order, paper stock selection and inventory check, press setup and run, then trim and packaging — with a typical wedding-season job needing to hit a mailing deadline the client set months in advance, and a printer running 6-12 concurrent jobs across different clients, plate vendors, and paper stocks currently tracks proof-approval status, plate arrival dates, and remaining paper inventory by specific stock and color across a paper planner and a stack of swatch samples, risking a missed mailing deadline when a plate order was never confirmed or a paper stock ran out mid-run with no reorder lead time built in.

The tool maintains a per-job record (client, proof-approval status, plate vendor and expected arrival date, paper stock/color/weight required, and target mail-by deadline) with a countdown view sorted by nearest deadline across all active jobs, and a paper-stock inventory tracker flagging any stock running low against jobs currently queued to use it.

Monetization: $15-29/mo per account. Distribution: the Printing Industries of America regional chapters and the letterpress-specific Boxcar Press and Briar Press community forums, where independent letterpress shop owners already coordinate around plate-vendor lead times and paper-stock sourcing. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Independent Perfumer & Custom Fragrance Maker Batch Formulation & Wholesale Order Tracker

An independent perfumer hand-blending custom eau de parfum and fragrance oils works from a formula — a specific list of aroma-compound percentages by weight that must be measured precisely and consistently batch to batch, since even a small formulation drift changes a signature scent's character — and maintains dozens of active formulas across a personal collection and wholesale/custom-client commissions, each needing to age (macerate) for a period ranging from a few weeks to several months before it's ready to bottle and ship. A perfumer running several formulas through maceration simultaneously, alongside wholesale boutique accounts expecting recurring restock shipments, currently tracks formula recipes, batch dates, and maceration-ready dates across a paper lab notebook and loses track of which batch is actually ready to bottle versus still aging, or restocks a wholesale account from a batch that hasn't finished maturing.

The tool maintains a per-formula recipe card (aroma-compound list and precise percentages) linked to a per-batch log (batch date, formula version used, target maceration period, and ready-to-bottle date) with a "ready to bottle" view across all active batches, and a wholesale account tracker showing each boutique's standing order quantity and last-restock date against current bottled inventory per scent.

Monetization: $15-29/mo per account. Distribution: the American Society of Perfumers and independent/indie perfumer communities on Basenotes and Fragrantica forums, where small-batch perfumers already trade formulation and batch-tracking workflow questions. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.

### 6. Independent Float Tank & Cryotherapy Studio Session Booking & Tank/Chamber Maintenance Tracker

A small independent float tank (sensory deprivation) or whole-body cryotherapy studio runs back-to-back client sessions on a tight turnaround — a float tank needs its water filtered, its Epsom-salt specific gravity checked, and its interior wiped down between every single float, and a cryotherapy chamber needs its liquid-nitrogen or refrigerant level checked and logged before every session — and a studio running 2-4 tanks or chambers back-to-back on a busy Saturday can't afford front-desk staff manually cross-checking whether the equipment was actually serviced between the last client and the next booking. Missing a between-session maintenance step isn't just a quality issue — a float tank with drifting salt concentration or a cryo chamber with an unlogged nitrogen level is exactly the kind of gap that shows up as a client complaint or, worse, a safety incident.

The tool maintains a booking calendar by tank/chamber and time slot, a per-session equipment checklist (water filtration cycle and specific-gravity reading for float tanks; nitrogen/refrigerant level and chamber temperature for cryotherapy) that must be marked complete before the next booking on that unit can check in, and a maintenance history log per tank/chamber showing filtration and salt-top-off intervals or nitrogen-refill dates over time.

Monetization: $29-49/mo per studio. Distribution: the Float Tank Association (FTA) member directory and conference, and independent cryotherapy studio operator groups on Facebook organized by region. Time to first dollar: 3-5 weeks.
