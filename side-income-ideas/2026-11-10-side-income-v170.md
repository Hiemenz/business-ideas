# Side Income Ideas - 2026-11-10 (Round 170)

Simple operational tracker tools for self-employed individuals and micro-businesses, picked through an Alex Hormozi lens (skill-for-money, performance-based offers, tracking the numbers that drive income). These are practical workflow trackers, not compliance software: a stream-clip editor, a restaurant social media manager, an email copywriter, a 30-day challenge host, a TikTok Shop live seller and a conversion-rate audit consultant.

---

### 1. Stream-Clip Editor Streamer, Clip Batch & Payout Tracker

A freelance editor who cuts clips from streamers' VODs for TikTok and Shorts has to track each streamer's batch size, clips delivered, platform posted to and views per clip. Many editors are paid per clip or per thousand views, so views must be recorded to invoice correctly. Without a log, it is unclear which streamer's content performs and which deal is worth keeping.

The tool logs clip batches per streamer, records posted links and 7-day views, calculates pay per clip or per view tier and shows earnings per streamer.

Monetization: $9-15/mo per editor. Distribution: clip-editor Discords, r/NewTubers and streamer-service Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Restaurant Social Media Manager Content Calendar, Shoot & Results Tracker

A freelancer who runs social accounts for 4-8 local restaurants has to track each client's posting calendar, shoot days, menu changes and results (reach, saves, bookings). Restaurants change specials weekly, so content goes out of date, and owners ask whether the posts bring customers in.

The tool keeps a per-client content calendar with a menu-update note, schedules shoot days, logs weekly results and creates a short client report.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per manager. Distribution: restaurant-marketing Facebook groups, r/restaurateur and local business networking groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Email Copywriter Client Brief, Campaign & Revenue-Result Tracker

A freelance email copywriter paid a retainer plus revenue bonus has to track briefs, drafts sent, campaigns launched and revenue per campaign. With 5-10 clients on e-commerce stores, the bonus calculation depends on data the client must supply, and chasing it takes time. The copywriter also wants to know which subject lines and angles win across clients.

The tool logs briefs and deadlines, records each campaign's open rate and revenue, tracks bonuses owed and keeps a swipe file of winning angles.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per copywriter. Distribution: email-marketing communities, r/emailmarketing and copywriter Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 4. 30-Day Challenge Host Participant, Check-In & Upsell Tracker

A coach who runs paid 30-day challenges (fitness, writing, money) has to track participants, daily check-ins, who is falling behind and who is ready for the next offer. Running several challenges per year, the host wants completion rate and upsell conversion for each. Messaging dozens of people individually is exhausting without a list.

The tool lists participants with check-in streaks, flags those who missed 3 days with a nudge template, and records completion and upsell conversions per challenge.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per host. Distribution: coach communities, Skool groups and r/Entrepreneur. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. TikTok Shop Live Seller Inventory, Live-Session & Sales Tracker

A seller who runs TikTok Shop live streams has to track inventory per product, live-session schedules, sales per session and returns. Running 3-5 lives a week, it is hard to know which products sell live and when stock will run out. Margin per product after fees is also unclear.

The tool tracks inventory by product with low-stock alerts, logs sales and viewers per live session, shows margin after fees and ranks products by revenue per live hour.

Monetization: $12-19/mo per seller. Distribution: TikTok Shop seller groups, r/TikTokShop and ecommerce Discords. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Conversion-Rate Audit Consultant Client Audit, Test & Result Tracker

A freelancer who sells conversion audits and A/B-test fixes to online stores has to track each audit's findings, recommendations, tests started and results. Clients want to see which fix produced what lift, and the consultant needs a library of proven fixes to reuse. Follow-up after an audit is where retainers are won, and many consultants forget it.

The tool stores audit findings per client, tracks each test with start date and result, keeps a library of proven fixes and sets 30-day follow-up reminders.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per consultant. Distribution: CRO communities, r/ecommerce and Shopify expert groups. Time to first dollar: 3 weeks.
