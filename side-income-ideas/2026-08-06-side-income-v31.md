# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 31)

### 1. RV Park & Campground Site Assignment and Utility Billing Tracker
Small RV parks, campgrounds, and tiny home communities with 20-80 sites manage site assignments, seasonal leases, and utility billing in a combination of paper ledgers and spreadsheets — resulting in double-booked sites, missed utility meter readings, and month-end billing calculations done manually per site. This tool maintains a site map (site number, hookup type — full hookup/water-electric/dry camping, monthly rate for seasonal tenants vs. nightly/weekly rate for transient guests, current tenant or reservation, and any site-specific notes like slide-out clearance or leveling requirements), tracks utility meter readings for sites with metered electric or water hookups (recording meter start and end readings and calculating the billed amount at the configurable rate per kWh or gallon), and generates monthly billing statements for seasonal tenants combining their site fee plus utility usage. A 30-day occupancy calendar shows reservations for all sites. Owner-facing dashboard: sites currently occupied, expected check-outs this week, utilities needing meter reads this month. Monetization: $20-35/mo per park account. Distribution: National Association of RV Parks and Campgrounds (ARVC) member communities, RV Park Investor and Campground Owners Facebook groups, r/RVing campground management discussions. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. High School Robotics Team Parts Inventory & Build Season Log
FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) and FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) teams spend their 6-week build season ordering parts, tracking what's been received vs. backordered, and logging which subsystems have been assembled and tested — often on a whiteboard that gets erased. When a mentor or student returns after a few days away, there's no reliable record of what was built, what was tested, and what parts are still needed. This tool maintains a parts inventory (vendor, part number, description, quantity ordered, quantity received, cost, and which robot subsystem it goes into), a build log (a per-day log of what was built or modified, which students worked on it, and any notes from testing), and a robot subsystem status tracker (each subsystem — drivetrain, intake, arm, shooter, climber — with its current build status: design/fabrication/assembly/testing/complete). A team-facing dashboard shows outstanding parts orders and subsystem status. Bonus: a pre-competition checklist the drive team uses the morning of competition to verify the robot is match-ready. Monetization: $8-12/mo per team or free with a team sponsor contribution option. Distribution: FIRST Robotics Community forums, FRC team mentor Facebook groups, r/FRC. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Personal Stylist Client Wardrobe Catalog & Outfit Formula Tracker
Personal stylists and wardrobe consultants who do in-home closet edits and personal shopping for clients build up a deep knowledge of what's in each client's closet — and then record it nowhere. When a client asks "what goes with this blazer I just bought?" six months after a session, the stylist has no record of the rest of the wardrobe to reference. This tool lets the stylist build a per-client wardrobe catalog after a closet edit (item category, color, brand, occasion type — work/casual/evening, and a photo from the stylist's phone), then tag "outfit formulas" — specific combinations of items the stylist has curated (blazer + specific pants + shoe option A or B). When the client buys a new piece, the stylist logs it and can see which existing outfit formulas it might work with. The client gets a shareable digital lookbook of their curated outfit formulas. Optionally: a capsule wardrobe gap analysis showing which categories are under-represented. Monetization: $15-22/mo per stylist account (unlimited clients). Distribution: Personal Stylist Facebook groups, r/femalefashionadvice style consultant communities, Wardrobe Consultant and Image Consultant professional association networks (AICI — Association of Image Consultants International). Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Farmers Market Manager Vendor Application & Booth Assignment Tool
Farmers market managers running 30-150-vendor markets handle vendor applications, booth assignment and layout, weekly attendance, and fee collection in a process that typically involves paper applications, email threads, and a paper map with sticky notes. When a new vendor applies mid-season, or an existing vendor needs to move booths, updating the layout is a manual process prone to double-booking. This tool maintains a vendor roster (business name, vendor category — produce, baked goods, meat, craft, etc. — contact info, application status, seasonal fee paid or outstanding, and any booth preferences or restrictions), a booth assignment grid (the market layout with each booth numbered, the assigned vendor, and any special notes like corner premium or power access), and a weekly attendance record (for each market day, which vendors are confirmed in, which called out, and which booth a substitute vendor is placed in). A market-day setup sheet shows each booth's assigned vendor sorted by booth number — the manager's reference for directing vendors as they arrive. Monetization: $15-25/mo per market account. Distribution: Farmers Market Coalition manager community, state farmers market association administrator networks, r/farming and r/localfood community manager discussions. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 5. Aquarium Maintenance Service Client Tank Parameter Log & Livestock Tracker
Independent aquarium maintenance technicians who service client fish tanks (typically freshwater or reef aquariums at offices, restaurants, and upscale homes) keep client records in notebooks or no records at all — making it difficult to track parameter trends over multiple visits, remember what livestock was added and when, or diagnose why a fish died based on the last few weeks of parameters. This tool maintains a per-client tank profile (tank size, filtration system, livestock inventory by species and approximate count, last restock date and source), and after each service visit the technician logs water parameters (pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, salinity for reef tanks, temperature) plus what was done (water change volume, filter media replaced, livestock additions or removals, equipment notes). Parameter trending charts show each metric over the last 6 service visits — the technician can see whether nitrate is creeping up across visits before it becomes a crisis. Optional: a client-facing visit summary email generated from the visit log. Monetization: $12-18/mo per technician account (unlimited clients). Distribution: r/ReefTank and r/Aquariums technician communities, Marine Aquarium Societies of North America (MASNA) professional member networks, aquarium maintenance Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. Online Tutoring Session Scheduler & Student Progress Tracker
Independent online tutors working with 10-30 students per week through Zoom, Google Meet, or Wyzant manage a complex scheduling calendar plus ongoing notes on each student's academic progress — often in separate tools that don't talk to each other. When a parent asks "how is Emma doing in algebra?" the tutor scrambles to find their notes from the last 4 sessions across a Word doc, a Google Sheet, and an email thread. This tool maintains a per-student profile (subject, grade level, school, parent contact, weekly session schedule, hourly rate, and payment method), a session log where the tutor records what was covered, what the student struggled with, and what to focus on next session, and a student progress summary that organizes the last 6 sessions' notes into a parent-facing email draft the tutor can review and send. A weekly schedule view shows all sessions across all students. An invoicing module calculates monthly tuition per student based on session count. Monetization: $12-18/mo per tutor account. Distribution: r/tutors, Wyzant and Tutor.com tutor community forums, private tutoring Facebook groups, TutorBird and Acuity Scheduling alternative-seeker communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
