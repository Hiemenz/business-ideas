# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 36)

### 1. Commercial Drone Operator FAA Part 107 Flight Authorization & Flight Log Tracker
Commercial drone operators flying under FAA Part 107 must maintain a valid Remote Pilot Certificate, obtain Facility Map (LAANC) airspace authorizations or manual FAA waivers before flying in controlled airspace, and keep a detailed flight log to document operations — especially when a Part 107 waiver grants exception to a standard limitation (flying at night, beyond visual line of sight, etc.). Most small commercial operators (real estate photography, inspections, mapping) track authorizations manually in a notes app and sometimes skip the flight log entirely — creating an FAA compliance gap if they are ever audited. This tool maintains a certificate tracker (remote pilot certificate number and expiration date — Part 107 certification must be renewed every 24 months via recurrent knowledge test — with a 60-day renewal alert), a flight authorization log (for each flight in controlled airspace: the LAANC authorization reference number, the UASFM facility map grid reference, authorized altitude, authorization date and expiration, and the flight date it was used), a Part 107 waiver tracker (for operators with active waivers: waiver number, the specific limitation waived, any conditions the waiver imposes, and expiration date), and a flight log (per-flight record: date, location, GPS coordinates of operation area, altitude flown, UAS registration number, remote pilot name, weather conditions, and any anomalies observed). Monetization: $8-12/mo per operator account. Distribution: r/drones, Commercial UAV Forum, AUVSI (Association for Unmanned Vehicle Systems International) member communities, DJI Enterprise forum. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Private Therapy Practice Client Waitlist & New Patient Inquiry Tracker
Independent therapists and small group practices running a waitlist — common when a therapist is at capacity but accepting future openings — manage new patient inquiries through email and voicemail, with no systematic way to track where each inquiry is in the intake sequence. When a slot opens, they scroll back through emails trying to find who contacted them first, what insurance they have, and whether they still need services. This tool maintains a per-inquiry record (prospective client first name, contact method, date of first contact, insurance or self-pay status, presenting issue in general terms, preferred schedule — mornings/evenings/weekends, desired appointment frequency, and current waitlist position), a waitlist prioritization view (sorted by wait date, with flags for insurance type and schedule compatibility with the therapist's current open slots), and a status tracker for each inquiry (active waitlist/intake form sent/intake form received/consultation scheduled/converted to active client/removed from list with removal reason). When a slot opens, the therapist views the waitlist filtered by the open slot's schedule and insurance compatibility — surfaces the best match in 30 seconds. A 30-day check-in prompt reminds the therapist to touch base with waitlisted clients who may have found services elsewhere. Monetization: $10-15/mo per therapist. Distribution: r/therapists, SimplePractice and TherapyNotes community forums, NASW member communities, psychology private practice Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Veterinary Relief Veterinarian Schedule & Invoice Tracker
Relief veterinarians — DVMs who work temporary shifts at different practices rather than maintaining a permanent position — manage their schedule, hours worked, mileage, and invoicing across 5-20 different practice clients using a calendar app and a manual spreadsheet. Billing the right practice for the right number of hours, tracking mileage deductions for each travel leg, and ensuring every completed shift results in an invoice submitted within a practice's payment terms window is an administrative burden that falls entirely on the relief vet. This tool maintains a per-shift record (practice name and location, shift date, scheduled start and end time, actual hours worked if different, compensation type — hourly flat rate or day rate, rate amount, any additional compensation agreed to such as travel stipend or emergency call premium), a mileage log linked to each shift (departure address, practice address, round-trip mileage, and running annual mileage total for tax deduction purposes), an invoice generation system (for each practice: an invoice summary of unbilled completed shifts — shift dates, hours, rates, any travel stipend — total due, and invoice submission date), and a payment tracking ledger (for each submitted invoice: invoice date, expected payment date per the practice's terms, actual payment received date, and any balance outstanding). Monetization: $10-15/mo per DVM account. Distribution: r/Veterinary, VetRelief community forums, National Association of Relief Veterinarians (NARV) member network, veterinary relief Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Home Staging Business Prop Inventory & Deployment Tracker
Home stagers who furnish and decorate vacant properties for sale own or rent a significant inventory of furniture, décor, art, rugs, plants, and accessories — often worth $30,000-150,000 in replacement value. Tracking which pieces are at which property, when the staging contract ends (and pieces need to be retrieved), and which items are available for the next staging job is an operational burden managed through a whiteboard or a spreadsheet that is rarely fully current. When a stager takes on a new project, they don't know at a glance whether the sectional sofa is available or committed until April, or whether the guest bedroom set is staged in a house that hasn't sold yet. This tool maintains a per-item inventory (item name, category — furniture/art/décor/textile/plant/accessory, quantity, condition, estimated replacement value, and a photo), a per-property deployment log (each staging contract: property address, contract start date, planned end date, and the list of inventory items deployed to that property from the catalog), an availability view (for each item: current status — available/deployed/in-transit/in-repair, and if deployed, the property address and expected return date), and a staging contract end alert (7 and 2 days before each contract's end date, alerting the stager to schedule retrieval). At the start of a new staging job, the stager opens the availability view for the room they're staging and sees exactly which items are available. Monetization: $12-20/mo per stager account. Distribution: Real Estate Staging Association (RESA) member network, r/HomeStaging, Staging Diva community forums, home staging Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Freelance Copy Editor Client Manuscript Log & Style Sheet Tracker
Freelance copy editors working on books, reports, academic manuscripts, and long-form content build a per-client style sheet for each project — documenting the author's preferred spellings, hyphenation decisions, capitalization rules, and any terminology specific to the work — that allows them to apply consistent editorial decisions throughout the manuscript. These style sheets live in a Word document or a folder of text files with inconsistent naming conventions, making it hard to retrieve the style sheet for a returning client's second book or a multi-volume project. This tool maintains a per-client record (client name, contact, genre or content type, and a link to their current project), a per-project style sheet (a searchable key-value store of editorial decisions: preferred spelling of domain-specific terms, hyphenation rules, capitalization conventions, name spellings, number formatting rules, series comma preference, and any editor notes for the author), a manuscript stage tracker (manuscript received/editing in progress/queries sent to author/final pass complete/files delivered), and a deadline calendar showing all active projects and their delivery dates. When starting a new chapter, the editor searches the style sheet for any term she wants to confirm — finds the documented decision in seconds rather than scrolling through a 3-page Word document. A project archive allows retrieval of style sheets from completed work when the client returns for a new project. Monetization: $8-12/mo per editor account. Distribution: Editorial Freelancers Association (EFA) member network, r/editors, Copyediting-L community list, ACES (American Copy Editors Society) member communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 6. Dance Competition Studio Season Routine Tracker & Competition Registration Manager
Dance studio owners and competition coaches who enter students in regional and national dance competitions manage a complex logistics layer: each student may be competing in multiple routines (solo, duet, group numbers), each routine is registered in a specific age division and style category, each competition has entry deadlines and costume requirements, and music tracks must be submitted in advance at specific file format requirements. Managing 20-60 competing students across 8-15 competitions per season in a spreadsheet results in missed deadlines, incorrect division entries, and last-minute costume emergencies. This tool maintains a per-routine record (routine name, style — lyrical/jazz/hip-hop/tap/contemporary/ballet, performing student(s), age division, competition level, assigned music track, costume status — ordered/arrived/altered/ready, and entry status at each competition), a per-competition registration tracker (for each competition the studio enters: registration deadline, entry fee payment deadline, music submission deadline, required music file format, and each routine's entry status — not entered/entry submitted/entry confirmed/costume check complete), a student competition schedule (for each student: which routines they perform, at which competitions, on which days — their personal competition schedule for the season), and a deadline calendar showing every upcoming competition registration, music submission, and payment deadline across all active competitions. A costume status dashboard shows the full studio's costume situation across all routines and flags any routine with a competition in the next 30 days that doesn't have a confirmed ready costume. Monetization: $15-25/mo per studio account. Distribution: Dance Teacher Magazine community, Dance Studio Owner association groups, Starpower and Energy competitions community networks, dance studio owner Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
