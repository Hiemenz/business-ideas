# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-06 (Round 37)

### 1. Homebrew Beer & Cider Batch Recipe Log & Fermentation Tracker
Homebrewers who brew 10-30 batches per year manage their recipe development, brew day notes, fermentation logs, and tasting notes in a hodgepodge of apps — some use BeerSmith, some use a spreadsheet, some use a notes app — but rarely have a single place that connects the recipe to the brew day outcome to the fermentation readings to the final tasting evaluation in a way that makes it easy to reproduce a successful batch or diagnose why a batch went sideways. This tool maintains a per-batch record: recipe name and style, batch size, grain bill with weights, hop additions with timing and IBU contribution, yeast strain and pitch rate, target OG and FG, mash temperature and duration, and any water chemistry additions. On brew day: actual OG reading logged and compared to target. During fermentation: a gravity reading log (date, specific gravity, temperature) that auto-calculates current ABV estimate and tracks fermentation curve day-over-day. At packaging: final gravity, calculated ABV, carbonation method and volumes of CO2 target. At tasting: appearance, aroma, flavor, and overall score notes. A recipe library saves each batch's parameters so the brewer can pull up a prior recipe as the starting point for the next iteration, with a version history showing how the recipe evolved across attempts. Monetization: $6-10/mo per brewer account or $45/year. Distribution: r/homebrewing, HomeBrewTalk forums, American Homebrewers Association (AHA) community, Northern Brewer and More Beer customer communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 2. Private Music Teacher Student Lesson Log & Recital Preparation Tracker
Independent music teachers running a private studio of 15-40 students teach piano, guitar, violin, voice, and other instruments in weekly 30- or 60-minute lessons — maintaining a mental model of where each student is in their repertoire and method book, what was assigned last week, and what needs extra attention next week. Between lessons, this mental model fades, and the teacher often starts the lesson by asking "so what did you practice?" instead of opening the lesson with specific purpose. This tool maintains a per-student profile: student name, age, instrument, current method book and page, current repertoire pieces in progress (title, composer, assigned date, target performance date), and any technique goals or theory concepts being developed. After each lesson: a brief lesson note (what was reviewed, what went well, what needs work, new assignment for the week). Before each week's lessons: the teacher reviews each upcoming student's prior lesson note and current repertoire to open the lesson with intention. A recital preparation tracker shows each student's recital piece, practice status logged by the teacher, and the performance date. At year-end: a student progress summary the teacher can share with parents. Monetization: $10-15/mo per teacher account. Distribution: Music Teachers National Association (MTNA) member communities, r/piano teacher communities, private music teacher Facebook groups, TakeLessons and Lessonface teacher communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 3. Handmade Jewelry Maker Inventory, Material Cost & Etsy/Fair Sales Tracker
Handmade jewelry makers who sell at craft fairs, Etsy, and local boutiques create pieces using beads, wire, findings, gemstones, and precious metals — but rarely know their actual per-piece material cost because materials were bought at different times in different quantities and the cost tracking lives in a notes app or nowhere at all. Without accurate material cost tracking, it's impossible to know whether a $28 earring is generating a meaningful margin or being sold at a loss. This tool maintains a materials inventory (material name, unit type — per bead, per foot of wire, per gram — quantity on hand, purchase price per unit, and current cost per unit), a per-piece production cost calculator (for each jewelry design: the materials list with quantities used, auto-calculated material cost total, any paid labor time if applicable, and suggested retail price at target markup), a finished goods inventory (pieces made, pieces sold, pieces in consignment at which boutique), and a sales log (Etsy sale or craft fair sale date, piece sold, sale price, platform fee if any, and net revenue). A monthly dashboard shows total pieces sold, total revenue, total material cost of goods sold, gross margin, and materials inventory status (which materials are running low). Monetization: $8-12/mo per maker account or $50/year. Distribution: r/jewelrymaking, Etsy seller forums and communities, craft fair vendor Facebook groups, Halstead (jewelry supply) and Fire Mountain Gems customer communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 4. Freelance Translator Client Project Log & Terminology Glossary Manager
Freelance translators handling technical, legal, and medical documents build domain-specific terminology glossaries for each client — ensuring consistent translation of specialized terms across a multi-document project or an ongoing translation relationship. These glossaries often live in a spreadsheet or a Word document with no systematic way to search them during a translation session or to share them with a client for review. This tool maintains a per-client record (client name, contact, primary subject domain — legal/medical/technical/marketing, source and target language pair, rate per word or per hour, and payment terms), a per-project record (project name, document type, word count, delivery deadline, delivered or pending status), a client terminology glossary (searchable key-value pairs: source term → preferred translation, with any usage notes such as "use this term in legal contracts; use alternative term in informal correspondence"), and a project pipeline calendar showing all active projects, delivery deadlines, and payment collection status. During a translation session, the translator searches the client's terminology glossary to confirm the preferred rendering of any domain-specific term in seconds — no scrolling through a 5-page spreadsheet. At project delivery, the tool shows the translator's billable word count and generates an invoice summary. Monetization: $10-15/mo per translator account. Distribution: American Translators Association (ATA) member communities, r/translator, ProZ.com translator community forums, Translators Without Borders volunteer communities. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.

### 5. Youth Travel Baseball/Softball Team Tournament Tracker & Travel Coordinator
Travel baseball and softball team managers and coaches coordinate 20-40 families for 8-20 tournament weekends per season — managing tournament registrations, hotel room blocks (reserved at the host hotel by the tournament organizer, often on a first-come basis), player availability, carpool coordination, and lineup decisions that depend on knowing which players are confirmed attending each tournament. This currently happens across a group text thread and an email chain that half the parents don't read. This tool maintains a per-tournament record: tournament name, location, registration deadline, entry fee and payment status, host hotel room block link and cutoff date, field address, and bracket format. For each tournament: a player availability roster — each family marks their player available, unavailable, or uncertain, with an optional note ("arriving Saturday only" or "can't make Friday games"). The team manager sees a per-tournament roster of confirmed attendees at a glance. A hotel room block deadline alert fires 7 days and 2 days before the block expires. A season overview shows all registered tournaments, fees paid vs. outstanding, and player attendance rate across tournaments. Optional carpool board: players seeking rides and players with open seats matched for each tournament location. Monetization: $12-20/mo per team account (shared across the team's families). Distribution: Baseball-Reference community forums, Perfect Game and PG Crosschecker travel baseball community groups, USSSA Baseball parent and coach Facebook groups, travel softball parent communities. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Podcast Host Guest Outreach Pipeline & Episode Production Tracker
Podcast hosts who publish 2-4 episodes per month and book guests to interview manage a guest pipeline — researching potential guests, sending outreach emails, following up, scheduling recordings, coordinating tech setup, and tracking which episodes are in pre-production vs. recorded vs. edited vs. published — across email threads, a calendar, and mental notes. When the host has 3 recorded episodes awaiting editing, 2 scheduled recordings next week, and 5 outstanding outreach emails from 3 weeks ago, knowing the actual pipeline status requires searching through email. This tool maintains a guest outreach pipeline (per-contact: potential guest name, contact method, outreach sent date, follow-up date, response status — no response/interested/scheduled/declined — and any notes from the conversation), a recording scheduler (confirmed guest name, recording date, meeting link, pre-interview questionnaire sent/received, and episode topic), and an episode production status tracker (per episode: guest name, recording date, current stage — recorded/edit queue/editing/review/scheduled/published, target publish date, and any show notes or episode page status). A weekly pipeline view shows outstanding guest outreach by age (surface who needs a follow-up), upcoming recordings this week, and episodes due for publication this week. A monthly analytics entry lets the host track download numbers per episode for their own reference. Monetization: $10-15/mo per podcast host account. Distribution: r/podcasting, Podcast Movement community, Buzzsprout and Anchor user communities, Creator Economy and podcast host Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 1-2 weeks.
