# Side Income Ideas - 2026-08-12 (Session 62)

## Who this is for
Simple, fast-to-build operational tracker tools for self-employed operators and micro-businesses — not compliance software, just practical day-to-day workflow tools that replace spreadsheets and sticky notes. Each idea targets a distinct type of self-employed operator not already covered in this repo.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- Simple, fast-to-build workflow trackers — no regulatory compliance angle.
- Monetization stated per account with a named distribution channel.
- Time to first dollar under 5 weeks.
- **No repeats.** This session's six operator types — mobile tire & oil change technicians, custom sign & vehicle wrap installers, independent college admissions consultants, art & antique restoration conservators, holiday & Christmas light installers, and mobile lash & brow technicians — are distinct from every prior session: mobile tire and oil change service has never appeared (distinct from the mobile mechanic/small engine repair idea and the RV & boat winterization technician idea referenced in earlier sessions, neither of which centers on a technician running a daily multi-stop route stocking specific tire sizes and oil/filter combinations per vehicle — a route-and-inventory problem closer to the mobile bicycle repair technician idea in Session 61 but for an entirely different vehicle class, parts catalog, and appointment structure); custom sign making and vehicle wrap installation has never appeared (distinct from the screen printing/custom apparel and embroidery shop ideas referenced in earlier sessions, which produce garments rather than rigid signage or vehicle graphics, and from the car detailing idea referenced in an earlier session, which cleans a vehicle's existing surface rather than fabricating and installing a custom design onto it — a sign/wrap shop's core problem is tracking a design-proof approval cycle and per-vehicle panel measurements no apparel or detailing tracker addresses); college admissions consulting has never appeared (distinct from the career coach, resume writer, and academic tutor ideas referenced in earlier sessions, none of which track a multi-year application timeline spanning standardized testing dates, a school-by-school essay and supplemental-requirement checklist, and application-portal submission deadlines specific to college admissions); art and antique restoration/conservation has never appeared (distinct from the antique appraiser, estate sale liquidator, upholsterer, and leatherworker ideas referenced in earlier sessions — a conservator instead performs hands-on restoration work on a client-owned piece, tracking condition documentation before and after treatment, materials/technique used, and a piece's chain of custody while in the studio, a treatment-and-custody problem none of those valuation, liquidation, or new-build trackers addresses); holiday and Christmas light installation has never appeared (distinct from the landscaping and general handyman ideas referenced elsewhere in this repo — it is a hard-deadline, single-season-window business with its own per-property lighting-plan design, bulb/strand inventory, and a mandatory takedown-date scheduling problem no year-round outdoor-service tracker addresses); and mobile lash and brow technician services have never appeared (distinct from the mobile hairstylist and mobile spray tan technician ideas referenced in earlier sessions, both of which are separate personal-care services with different product/timing requirements — a lash technician's core problem is tracking each client's fill-cycle interval, the specific lash curl/length/adhesive combination used, and a patch-test/allergy note that a hairstyling or spray-tan tracker was never built to hold).

### 1. Mobile Tire & Oil Change Technician Route, Vehicle Spec & Parts Inventory Tracker

Independent mobile tire and oil change technicians who drive to a client's home or office to swap tires or perform an oil change on-site need to track each client's vehicle specs (make, model, tire size, oil type/filter part number, and torque spec for lug nuts), the day's route with appointment windows across scattered addresses, and which tires, oil, and filters are actually loaded on the van for that day's jobs, because a technician running 6-10 stops a day can't rely on memory for which client's SUV needs a specific tire size versus a sedan's smaller fitment, and showing up without the right oil filter or a tire in stock means a wasted trip and a client who books a shop with a bay next time instead. Most independent mobile technicians currently manage this across a paper route sheet and a guess at what's loaded in the van with no structured link between a scheduled job and the parts it actually requires.

The tool maintains a per-client vehicle profile (make, model, tire size, oil/filter spec, and torque requirements), a daily route board (stops, appointment windows, and requested service per stop), and a parts-and-inventory loadout checklist generated from the day's scheduled jobs so nothing gets left behind at the shop or storage unit.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per technician account. Distribution: r/MobileMechanics and r/AutoDetailing threads, local mobile tire and oil change Facebook groups, and Mobile Tire Dealers Association member outreach. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 2. Custom Sign & Vehicle Wrap Installer Design-Proof, Measurement & Install Tracker

Independent sign makers and vehicle wrap installers who fabricate and install custom signage, banners, and vehicle graphics need to track each project's design-proof approval cycle (client-submitted logo/artwork, proof versions sent, and sign-off status before production starts), precise per-substrate or per-vehicle-panel measurements since a wrap or sign cut to the wrong dimension is unusable material, and a production-to-install status board across several concurrent jobs, because an installer running multiple storefront and fleet-vehicle projects at once can't rely on memory for which client's third proof revision was actually approved versus still pending, and installing an unapproved or outdated design version is exactly the kind of costly reprint mistake that erodes a client's trust before the relationship even gets going. Most independent sign and wrap shops currently manage this across an email thread of proof PDFs and a paper measurement sheet with no structured link between an approved proof and the job that gets produced.

The tool maintains a per-project design-proof log (versions sent, client feedback, and final sign-off with timestamp), a per-substrate/vehicle measurement record (panel dimensions, material needed, and cut specs), and a production-to-install status board (proofed, printed, laminated, cut, installed) so every job's current state is visible at a glance.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per shop account. Distribution: r/signmaking and r/vinylwraps threads, the International Sign Association (ISA) member directory, and local vehicle wrap installer Facebook groups. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 3. Independent College Admissions Consultant Student Timeline & Application Checklist Tracker

Independent college admissions consultants working with families need to track each student's multi-year application timeline (standardized testing dates and target scores, application deadlines by school, and a running list of target/reach/safety schools), a per-school requirements checklist since every college has its own essay prompts, supplemental questions, recommendation-letter count, and portal submission process, and session-by-session notes on essay drafts and interview prep, because a consultant working with a dozen students across overlapping junior and senior year timelines can't rely on memory for which student's Common App essay is on draft three versus which school's supplemental questions haven't been started, and missing a single school's early-decision deadline because it wasn't tracked separately from the main application timeline is exactly the kind of failure that ends a consultant's reputation with the family and the guidance counselors who refer them. Most independent consultants currently manage this across a shared Google Doc per student with no structured way to see every deadline across every student at once.

The tool maintains a per-student profile (target schools, testing dates/scores, and application timeline), a per-school requirements checklist (essays, supplements, recommendation letters, and portal deadlines) tracked against each student's actual progress, and a master deadline dashboard surfacing every upcoming due date across the consultant's full caseload so nothing slips between students.

Monetization: $29-49/mo per consultant account. Distribution: r/ApplyingToCollege and r/CollegeAdmissions threads, the Independent Educational Consultants Association (IECA) member directory, and local private school and college-prep referral networks. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 4. Art & Antique Restoration Conservator Condition Report & Treatment Tracker

Independent art and antique conservators who restore paintings, furniture, ceramics, and other client-owned pieces need to track each piece's intake condition (photographed damage, prior repairs, and materials identified) since a documented before-treatment condition report is both the conservator's liability protection and the basis for treatment planning, a treatment log (materials and techniques used, session dates, and hours worked) as the piece moves through cleaning, stabilization, and restoration stages, and a chain-of-custody record for every piece physically present in the studio, because a conservator handling several client pieces at once — often high-value heirlooms or fine art — can't rely on memory for which piece is mid-treatment versus ready for pickup, and a piece returned to a client without a documented before/after condition record leaves the conservator unable to defend against a dispute over pre-existing damage. Most independent conservators currently manage this across a phone photo album and a paper treatment log with no structured link between a piece's intake condition and its treatment history.

The tool maintains a per-piece condition report (intake photos, damage notes, and prior-repair history), a treatment log (materials, techniques, session dates, and hours) building a complete restoration record, and a studio chain-of-custody board showing every piece's current status (intake, in treatment, awaiting materials, ready for pickup) at a glance.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per conservator account. Distribution: r/artconservation and r/antiques threads, the American Institute for Conservation (AIC) member directory, and local antique dealer and auction house referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 3-4 weeks.

### 5. Holiday & Christmas Light Installer Property Design & Seasonal Takedown Tracker

Independent holiday and Christmas light installers who design, install, and later remove seasonal lighting displays need to track each property's lighting plan (roofline measurements, strand count and color scheme, and any client-specific design requests) since a compressed installation season means an installer running dozens of properties in a few weeks can't rely on memory for which house needs warm-white icicle lights on the eaves versus a specific color scheme around the windows, a bulb and strand inventory tied to what's actually loaded on the truck for the day's installs, and — the detail most installers lose track of — a mandatory takedown date per client, since an installer who doesn't schedule and complete every takedown before a client's own deadline (a rental property turnover, an HOA removal date, or simply the client's own preference) risks damaging the referral relationship that drives next season's repeat business. Most independent installers currently manage this across a paper property list from last season and a guess at what inventory is left with no structured way to see which properties still need takedown before the season fully closes.

The tool maintains a per-property lighting design record (roofline measurements, strand/color scheme, and design photos), a bulb and strand inventory loadout tied to the day's scheduled installs, and a seasonal due-list dashboard tracking every property's install-and-takedown status so no client's lights are left up past their deadline.

Monetization: $19-29/mo per installer account. Distribution: r/ChristmasLights and r/landscaping threads, the Christmas Decor and holiday lighting franchise operator Facebook groups, and local HOA and property management referral relationships. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.

### 6. Mobile Lash & Brow Technician Client Fill-Cycle & Product Tracker

Independent mobile lash and brow technicians who travel to a client's home for eyelash extension fills, lash lifts, and brow tinting need to track each client's specific service history (lash curl/length/adhesive combination used, brow tint shade, and any patch-test or allergy notes on file), a fill-cycle due-list since lash extensions typically need a fill every 2-3 weeks to stay full, and a per-appointment product and supply checklist for what needs to be packed for that day's route, because a technician running 4-8 appointments a day across a spread-out client base can't rely on memory for which client is allergic to a specific adhesive brand versus which client's fill is a week overdue, and applying the wrong adhesive to a client with a documented sensitivity is a safety failure with real liability exposure. Most independent mobile lash technicians currently manage this across a phone notes app and a paper client card with no structured way to see who's due for a fill this week.

The tool maintains a per-client service profile (lash/brow specs used, adhesive brand, and patch-test/allergy notes), a fill-cycle due-list dashboard surfacing every client approaching their 2-3 week refill window, and a per-appointment supply checklist so the right adhesive, lash trays, and tools are packed for each stop on the day's route.

Monetization: $15-25/mo per technician account. Distribution: r/Lashtechs and r/estheticians threads, local mobile beauty services Facebook groups, and PMU/lash artist trade show vendor networks. Time to first dollar: 2-3 weeks.
