# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-10 (Session 179)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session leans on cutting time delay and effort for the buyer.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Get-cash-now offers:** pull future revenue forward with prepay and gift-card campaigns.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Instant Photo-Quote Widget for Junk Removal and Landscaping Companies

**Problem:** A homeowner wants a price now. Junk-removal and landscaping companies make them fill in a form and wait a day for a quote, and by then the homeowner has booked a competitor. Quoting is pure effort and delay, the two denominators of the value equation.

**What to build:** A website widget where the customer uploads 3-5 photos and selects a few options. A vision model estimates volume or area and returns a price range in under a minute, with a "book this slot" button. The owner approves or adjusts through a queue, and the tool tracks quote-to-booking rate and estimate accuracy against the final invoice.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Instant quotes in 60 seconds or the first month is free," plus a $20 fee per job booked through the widget.

**Skill fit:** Vision model API, a pricing rules engine and a booking calendar.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through junk-removal and landscaping owner groups, including franchise-adjacent independents.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $149/mo + $20 per booked job. An owner booking 15 jobs per month pays $449/mo. 28 owners x $449 = **$12,572/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Accuracy improves with every job, so the price model becomes a data asset tuned to each company.

**Biggest risk:** Inaccurate quotes. Keep a human approval step at first and show a range instead of a single number.

**Growth path:** Junk removal and landscaping, then moving helpers, pressure washing, tree service and painting.

### 2. Slow-Night Cash Campaign Engine for Independent Restaurants

**Problem:** Independent restaurants have empty Tuesday and Wednesday nights and fixed costs every day. Hormozi's "get cash now" idea says prepaid offers pull revenue forward, but owners only do one gift-card push at Christmas.

**What to build:** A campaign tool that builds a prepaid offer ("$100 gift card for $80, valid on slow nights"), sends it to the restaurant's past guests by email and SMS, and tracks redemptions by day. It compares redemption to the slow-night baseline so the owner sees real incremental covers and not just discounting.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We run it for you, and you pay 10% of gift-card sales we generate."

**Skill fit:** Campaign automation, POS data import and redemption analytics.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through local restaurant-owner Facebook groups and direct visits.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A restaurant sells $4,000 of gift cards per campaign, so 10% is $400, with about two campaigns per quarter, roughly $270/mo, plus a $69/mo platform fee = about $340/mo. 40 restaurants x $340 = **$13,600/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** A restaurant that gets cash upfront keeps repeating campaigns, and guest lists grow with each one.

**Biggest risk:** Discount-driven guests who never return. Track a second-visit rate for gift-card guests and report it.

**Growth path:** Restaurants, then cafes, bars, salons and independent theaters.

### 3. Annual Rebooking & Prepay Engine for Window-Cleaning and Pressure-Washing Companies

**Problem:** These seasonal services are bought once or twice a year, and most customers simply forget until the next season. Companies restart marketing from zero every spring, even though they hold a list of past customers.

**What to build:** Import past customers and job dates. Send a rebooking message 11 months after the last job with a "lock in this year's slot and save 10% when you prepay" offer. Track rebooking, prepay revenue and route density by neighborhood.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We bring back lapsed customers or you pay nothing. We take $15 per rebooked job."

**Skill fit:** Simple scheduling, messaging, payment links and cohort tracking.

**MVP scope:** 2-3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days, timed to start before spring.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A company with 1,200 past customers rebooks 12% = 144 jobs x $15 = $2,160 in the season. Spread across the year with a $59/mo platform fee, about $240/mo. 50 companies x $240 = **$12,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Prepay revenue improves the owner's cash flow in the slow months, which is the main thing they would pay to fix.

**Biggest risk:** Seasonality makes revenue lumpy. Offer annual pricing and target southern states with longer seasons.

**Growth path:** Window cleaning and pressure washing, then holiday-light installers, gutter cleaners and lawn aeration.

### 4. Sales-Call Scoring & Objection Analytics for Small Home-Improvement Sales Teams

**Problem:** A 5-15 rep team selling windows, solar or roofing in homes records calls but never reviews them. The owner knows reps close at different rates and cannot see which objections lose deals or which script lines win.

**What to build:** Upload or sync recorded calls. Transcribe, tag objections (price, spouse, timing), score each call against the owner's script checklist, and show closing patterns per rep. Generate one coaching clip per rep per week. Show which rebuttals win.

**Grand Slam angle:** A 60-day pilot: "We find the three biggest leaks in your sales calls or the pilot is free," then a flat monthly fee.

**Skill fit:** Speech-to-text APIs, LLM tagging and dashboards.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through home-improvement owner communities.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $29/rep/mo with a $199 minimum. A 10-rep team pays $290/mo. 40 teams x $290 = **$11,600/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** A single rep who closes 5% more pays for the whole team's subscription, and the library of winning rebuttals improves every month.

**Biggest risk:** Call recordings may be in different systems. Start with uploads from a dialer export and one CRM.

**Growth path:** Home improvement, then real-estate teams, auto dealers, insurance agencies and any inside-sales team.

### 5. Site-Level Margin & Renewal-Pricing Engine for Commercial Cleaning Contractors

**Problem:** A cleaning contractor with 30-100 sites prices each by square footage and rarely checks whether it is profitable. Some accounts consume overtime and supplies, while others are very profitable. At renewal the contractor either leaves money on the table or loses the account by raising price blindly.

**What to build:** Import payroll hours, supply costs and contract prices per site. Compute margin per site, rank sites, flag overtime-heavy accounts and suggest a renewal price per site that reaches a target margin. Provide a one-page justification letter for the customer.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We find at least $20K a year in margin or the first quarter is free."

**Skill fit:** Analytics, simple pricing logic and document generation.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through cleaning-business owner groups and franchise-independent communities.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $249/mo. 45 contractors = **$11,205/mo**. Add a $1,000 one-time margin audit for another 3 per month.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Contract renewals recur every year, and margin data becomes the owner's key pricing tool.

**Biggest risk:** Payroll data by site is rarely tidy. Offer a simple weekly hours-by-site sheet for crews.

**Growth path:** Commercial cleaning, then landscaping contractors, security guard firms and property-maintenance companies.
