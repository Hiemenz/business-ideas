# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-11 (Session 185)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session cuts time delay (vacancy days, long sales cycles) and uses lead magnets.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Lead magnets and liquidation offers:** a useful free asset that captures a lead, and a bundled offer that turns dead stock into cash.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Event-Booking Lead Feed for Food Trucks and Mobile Caterers

**Problem:** A food truck's best days are private events, festivals and corporate lunches, but finding them means scrolling Facebook groups and emailing organizers. Event organizers, in turn, cannot easily find trucks that are free on their date.

**What to build:** A feed that collects upcoming events (festival vendor calls, brewery nights, corporate campus lunches, markets) from public listings, matched to each truck's cuisine, capacity and radius, with a one-tap application template. Track applications, bookings and revenue per event type.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We bring you three qualified event leads a month or you do not pay."

**Skill fit:** Scraping and normalizing event listings, matching and an application workflow.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends for one metro.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through food-truck owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $59/mo + $25 per event booked through the feed. A truck booking 4 per month pays $159/mo. 75 trucks x $159 = **$11,925/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Both sides of the market join over time, and the booking history becomes a reputation system for trucks.

**Biggest risk:** Event data is scattered. Start with one city and add events by partnering with breweries and business parks.

**Growth path:** One metro, then 10 metros, then add organizer-side listings and reviews.

### 2. Make-Ready Coordinator for Small Landlords and Property Managers

**Problem:** When a tenant leaves, every empty day costs rent. Cleaning, painting, repairs and re-keying run in sequence, each vendor is called by hand and the schedule slips by days. A landlord with 20-60 units may have 2-5 units vacant at any time.

**What to build:** A turnover workflow: when a move-out date is entered, the tool creates a make-ready checklist, books preferred vendors in order (clean, paint, repair, lock change) from the landlord's vendor list, tracks each step and shows days vacant per unit. Report average turnover days and the cost of vacancy.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We cut your average vacancy by 5 days or the first quarter is free."

**Skill fit:** Workflow scheduling, vendor messaging and a dashboard.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through small-landlord groups and local real-estate investor meetups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $2/unit/mo with a $79 minimum. A 40-unit landlord pays $80/mo. That is low, so target managers with 100+ units paying about $200/mo. 55 managers x $200 = **$11,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Vendor networks and turnover data are hard to rebuild elsewhere, and vacancy savings are measurable.

**Biggest risk:** Small landlords with few units pay little. Target managers with 100+ units and build vendor bookings that work for them.

**Growth path:** Small managers, then short-term-rental hosts and cleaning-turnover coordinators.

### 3. Long-Cycle Nurture Engine for Pool Builders, Kitchen Remodelers and Other High-Ticket Local Sellers

**Problem:** A $60K pool or $45K kitchen buyer takes 3-12 months to decide. Most companies follow up for 2-3 weeks and then forget the prospect, and the competitor who stays in touch wins when the buyer is ready.

**What to build:** A nurture sequence over 12 months: project inspiration, financing information, case studies, seasonal reminders ("pools booked for spring start filling in January") and a quarterly check-in text. Show the pipeline of nurtured prospects and which touches triggered replies.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We revive at least 3 dead quotes in 90 days, or you do not pay."

**Skill fit:** Lifecycle messaging, content templates and CRM analytics.

**MVP scope:** 2-3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through pool and remodeling owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $199/mo. 60 companies = **$11,940/mo**. A single revived $50K job pays for years of subscription.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The nurture list becomes a growing asset, and the engine reduces dependence on paid leads.

**Biggest risk:** Owners fail to feed in lost quotes. Import the quote history and set up automatic enrollment.

**Growth path:** Pool builders and remodelers, then solar, custom home builders, landscape architects and outdoor kitchen builders.

### 4. Dead-Stock Liquidation Bundler for Small Retailers

**Problem:** A small retailer holds 10-20% of inventory that has not sold in six months. It ties up cash and shelf space, and owners discount it randomly or leave it. Bundling dead stock with a best-seller ("buy X, get Y at 50%") can clear it at a better margin than a blanket markdown.

**What to build:** Import inventory and sales history. Identify dead stock and pair each item with a fast-selling complement. Generate bundle offers, in-store signage and an email/SMS campaign, and track sell-through and cash recovered.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We turn at least $5,000 of dead stock into cash in 60 days, or you do not pay." The fee is 10% of liquidated revenue.

**Skill fit:** Inventory analytics, association pairing and campaign tools.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through independent-retailer groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A retailer clears $6,000 in a quarter = $600 fee, about $200/mo, plus $49/mo platform. 50 retailers x $250 = **$12,500/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The inventory view is useful every month, and liquidation campaigns repeat each season.

**Biggest risk:** POS exports differ. Start with Square and Shopify POS.

**Growth path:** Boutiques and gift shops, then hobby stores, bike shops, pet stores and bookstores.

### 5. Quiz-Funnel Builder for Local Service Businesses

**Problem:** Local companies run ads to a "contact us" form that converts 2-4%. A quiz ("What would a new deck cost? Answer 5 questions to find out") works as a lead magnet: it gives the visitor something useful, qualifies them and captures contact details at a higher rate.

**What to build:** A template library of quizzes per trade (deck cost estimate, roof replacement timeline, home-energy savings). Each quiz ends with an estimated range and an email/SMS capture, scores the lead (budget, timeline) and routes hot leads to the owner immediately. Report conversion rate and cost per lead.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Double your lead capture rate in 45 days, or the second month is free."

**Skill fit:** Quiz builder, scoring and lead routing.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through home-service marketing groups and agency partners.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $129/mo. 90 companies = **$11,610/mo**. An agency plan at $299/mo for 10 client quizzes adds more.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The quiz becomes the front door for the company's ads, and conversion data improves each template.

**Biggest risk:** Crowded quiz-builder market. Differentiate with trade-specific templates and cost-estimate logic.

**Growth path:** Home services, then dental and cosmetic practices, solar, financial advisers' planning calculators and real-estate agents.
