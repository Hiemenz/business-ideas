# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-14 (Session 200)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session shortens time delay with trial offers that deliver a visible win in week one.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Trial to commitment:** Hormozi's "pay to try, then upgrade" structure. Each idea converts a trial, class or showroom visit into a committed customer.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Trial-to-Enrollment Engine for Small Language Schools and Tutoring Studios

**Problem:** A language school runs free trial lessons, but only 20-30% of trial students enroll. After the trial, the follow-up is one email, and students who enroll often drop at week 6 when progress feels slow.

**What to build:** A trial-lesson page, a post-trial "your level and 12-week plan" summary, an enrollment link with a limited-time bonus and a week-3 and week-6 progress check that flags drop-off risk. Show trial-to-enroll rate and 90-day retention.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Lift trial-to-enroll by 10 points or the first quarter is free." Fee: $99/mo.

**Skill fit:** Booking, plan generation, sequences and retention flags.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through school-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $99/mo. 125 schools = **$12,375/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Each extra enrolled student is worth hundreds, and the plan summary improves every trial.

**Biggest risk:** Small schools with low budgets. Keep onboarding light.

**Growth path:** Language schools, then test-prep, music and coding academies.

### 2. Trial-Class Conversion Engine for Boxing, Kickboxing and Fitness Gyms

**Problem:** Boutique gyms give free first classes, but most trial visitors never come back. Staff are busy coaching and don't follow up, and the gym has no plan for the first 30 days.

**What to build:** A trial sign-up page, a same-day "how it went" text, a 3-visit onboarding plan, an offer to join with a first-month incentive and a re-engage message if a member misses two weeks. Show trial-to-member rate and 90-day retention.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Convert 25% more trials into members or the first 60 days are free." Fee: $119/mo.

**Skill fit:** Check-in, messaging and member-risk flags.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through gym-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $119/mo. 105 gyms = **$12,495/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Every added member brings recurring dues, and the effect is measurable.

**Biggest risk:** Crowded gym-software market. Differentiate on the first-30-days plan.

**Growth path:** Boxing and fitness gyms, then martial arts, yoga, climbing and dance studios.

### 3. Class-Fill and Repeat-Guest Engine for Paint-and-Sip and Art Class Studios

**Problem:** An art studio hosts classes and private parties but fills only 60-70% of seats. Guests attend once, and the studio rarely invites them back or sells private events to the party-planners in the room.

**What to build:** A class calendar with seat counts, a fill-the-last-seats text to past guests, a "bring a friend" offer, a private-event inquiry flow and a 30-day "come back" prompt. Show seat fill rate and repeat-guest rate.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fill 85% of your seats or the first 60 days are free." Fee: $79/mo plus $1 per seat sold through the tool.

**Skill fit:** Ticketing, text campaigns and event inquiry forms.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 21-45 days through studio-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A studio selling 300 seats a month pays $79 + $300 = about $380. 35 studios x $380 = **$13,300/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Seats are perishable, so filling them is direct profit.

**Biggest risk:** Small niche. Add cooking classes, pottery studios and craft workshops.

**Growth path:** Art studios, then pottery, cooking, candle-making and workshop venues.

### 4. Showroom-Visit Follow-Up Engine for Independent Furniture and Mattress Stores

**Problem:** A visitor spends 40 minutes in a furniture store, says "I'll think about it" and buys elsewhere. The store captured no contact details and has no follow-up, even though the sale is worth $2-5K.

**What to build:** A salesperson tool to save the visitor's favorite pieces with photos and a quote, a follow-up sequence ("your sofa is held until Friday; delivery slots open next week"), a price-match note and a pipeline of open quotes. Show follow-up sent and visit-to-sale rate.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Win back 10% of walk-outs or the first quarter is free." Fee: $149/mo.

**Skill fit:** Mobile quote tool, sequences and pipeline view.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through store-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $149/mo. 85 stores = **$12,665/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** A single recovered sale pays for months of the tool.

**Biggest risk:** Sales staff may not use it. Keep capture to three taps.

**Growth path:** Furniture and mattress stores, then flooring, lighting, appliance and jewelry shops.

### 5. Contract Renewal and Add-On Engine for Snow-Removal and Seasonal Property Services

**Problem:** A snow-removal contractor depends on seasonal contracts, but renewals are chased by phone in October and many customers drift. Add-ons (salting, roof snow, spring cleanup) are rarely sold.

**What to build:** A renewal campaign launched in September with one-tap renewal links and early-bird pricing, add-on offers and a per-storm service log for customers. Show renewal rate and add-on revenue.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Renew 85% of your contracts before the first storm, or the first quarter is free." Fee: $79/mo plus 1% of renewed contract value.

**Skill fit:** Renewal flows, payment links and service logs.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days, timed to the fall renewal window.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A contractor with $80K of contracts pays about $79 + $67 = $146/mo. 85 contractors x $146 = **$12,410/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Contract renewal is a once-a-year, high-value moment, and the tool also serves lawn-care season.

**Biggest risk:** Seasonality. Offer a lawn-care mode for summer.

**Growth path:** Snow removal, then lawn care, landscaping, leaf removal and property maintenance.
