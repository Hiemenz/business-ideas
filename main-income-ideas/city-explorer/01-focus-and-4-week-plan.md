# City Explorer: Focus and 4-Week Plan

[Back to the City Explorer overview](../2026-11-14-city-explorer-badges-app.md)

The full concept is too big for one person. This page picks **one launch product** and a 4-week test that needs no code. Everything else in this folder waits until this test works. Numbers marked (assumption) are estimates to replace with real results.

## The one launch product

**Who:** Parents of children aged 0-8 in one neighborhood cluster, including married couples who want a regular date night.

**What they get:** A free weekly message that gives a complete weekend plan (place, time, travel, backup, what's been checked), a one-tap "lock it in" that texts the other parent, and a Friday rescue message if nothing is planned. Married couples get one optional date-night plan a month with a short conversation card.

**What it is not (yet):** No app, no badges, no photo hunts, no paid listings, no other cohorts, no older-adult or dating tracks.

**One question the test answers:** Do parents go out more when they get a complete plan than when they get an idea?

## What to build (all manual, under $100)

- A one-page sign-up form with the 10-question intake (see the overview).
- A spreadsheet with 60 hand-checked kid-friendly places and 10 date-night plans in one cluster. Check hours, stroller access, changing tables and nap-friendly timing by phone or visit.
- A text or email tool for the weekly send. Start by hand if under 30 people.
- A shared inbox or phone number for replies.

## The 4 weeks

| Week | What you do | Hours | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Pick the cluster. Build the 60-place sheet and 10 date plans. Write the intake form and the first three weekly messages. Message 40 parents you know. | 12-15 | 20 parents signed up |
| **2** | Send the first plan to everyone. Half get an idea only (Group A). Half get the full lock-in plan with a Friday rescue (Group B). Log replies and who went. | 10-12 | First results logged |
| **3** | Send the second plan, switching nothing. Call 8 parents (4 per group) and ask what got in the way. Add 10 more parents through two parent-group admins. | 10-12 | 30 parents, 8 interviews |
| **4** | Send the third plan. Offer a founding price ($19 for the first year) and 3 local businesses a free featured slot. Count the results and decide. | 8-10 | Decision made |

## What to measure (sheet columns)

Parent, group (A or B), cohort tag, week, plan sent, reply ("Going," "Maybe," "No"), locked in, went (self-report on Sunday), would repeat, notes.

## Decision rules (assumption)

| Result after week 4 | Decision |
|---|---|
| Group B goes out at least 1.5x as often as Group A, and 40%+ lock in | Build the simple web version, then start venue pilots |
| B beats A by a smaller margin | Keep the weekly send, fix the plan format, run 3 more weeks |
| No difference, or fewer than 15 active parents | Stop or change the segment or city before building anything |
| 50%+ of the 20 say they would pay $19-39/yr | Open the paid plan at week 5 |
| 3+ businesses say they would pay for visits | Run venue pilots with redeemed codes |

## Money, kept simple

- **Cost to test:** About $100 and 40-50 hours across 4 weeks.
- **First revenue goal (month 3, assumption):** 25 paying parents x $39 = about $975/yr in subscriptions, which is small. The real test of the business is 5 venues paying about $99-149/mo each ($500-745/mo).
- **Break-even (from the gaps file):** About 8 paying venues plus 100 paying users covers fixed costs. If you cannot get 5 venues to commit by month 4, the model needs rethinking.
- **Keep your day job** until the business covers half your target income for 3 months straight.

## The venue test (weeks 3-6)

- Visit or call 30 independent family-friendly places.
- Offer: "Free featured slot for 60 days. If we send fewer than 20 countable visits, you owe nothing. After that, $99/mo."
- Count visits with a code ("show this text at the counter").
- Success: 5 venues agree to a paid slot after the pilot.

## What waits until this works

| Later | Trigger to start it |
|---|---|
| Other cohorts (single parents, pet owners, foodies, visitors, older adults) | Test group retention above 50% at week 12 |
| Badges and passports | Parents ask for something to collect, or retention dips |
| Photo hunts | The place list goes stale or you need new images |
| Dating, connection cards and Date Night Club | Married-date-night plans get strong replies |
| Paid listings and badge counts | 30+ venues on the free list and 300+ users |
| Native app | The web version retains users for 3 months |

## Legal and trust basics before the first send

Get opt-in for texts and email with an easy opt-out, write a plain-language privacy policy, collect no children's names or photos, and say only what you have checked ("stroller access checked on [date]"). Have a lawyer review the terms before you take money. Details are in the gaps file.

## Founder check

- Can you give 10-15 hours a week for 4 weeks?
- Do you like talking to parents and calling venues?
- What result in week 4 would make you stop, and what would make you continue? Write it down now.

## Where this fits with your other ideas

The matchmaking business and the main-income ideas compete for the same evenings. If you can only run one, run the 4-week test here first, since it costs about $100 and gives a clear yes or no.
