# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-16 (Session 206)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session removes effort for the buyer: the organizer does the work, the family or the volunteer only taps.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Leverage other people's audiences:** Hormozi's "lead getters" idea: rely on partners who already have the audience. Several ideas sell through schools, landlords and agencies that carry the reach.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Campaign-in-a-Box Engine for School and Youth-Group Fundraisers

**Problem:** A school or club fundraiser relies on volunteers, paper order forms and a one-week push. Most families never share the link, totals are reported late and the same product sale repeats every year with a shrinking return.

**What to build:** A campaign page per student or team with a shareable link, a text and email template for relatives, progress goals and badges, a leaderboard by class and a reminder sequence for the last 48 hours. Handle product or donation checkout and a payout summary. Show money raised per participant and share rate.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Raise 25% more than last year or we waive our fee." Fee: 5% of money raised.

**Skill fit:** Campaign pages, share flows, checkout and reporting.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days, timed to the school-year fundraising calendar.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A school raising $20K pays $1,000 per campaign. 150 campaigns a year = $150K/yr, about **$12,500/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Every school and club runs repeat campaigns, and parents come back each season.

**Biggest risk:** Strong incumbents and seasonality. Differentiate on low fee and easy sharing, and sell to clubs and teams first.

**Growth path:** Schools, then sports teams, bands, scouts and small charities.

### 2. Lease-Up and Renewal Engine for Student-Housing Landlords

**Problem:** A landlord with 6-30 student rentals fills units late, with a rush in spring and empty units in August. Renewals are asked about at the last minute, and group-roommate changes cause chaos.

**What to build:** A lease-up page with virtual tours and group applications, a "lock your unit for next year" renewal campaign in October, a roommate-match tool for leftover rooms and a waitlist. Show units filled by date and renewal rate.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fill your units 30 days earlier or the first quarter is free." Fee: $99/mo plus $50 per lease signed through the tool.

**Skill fit:** Listing pages, applications and renewal flows.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through small-landlord groups near campuses.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A landlord signing 12 leases a year pays $1,188 + $600 = about $150/mo. 80 landlords x $150 = **$12,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** A vacant unit costs $700+ a month, so earlier fill pays for the tool quickly.

**Biggest risk:** Strong seasonality and a campus-by-campus sales effort. Start with two campuses.

**Growth path:** Student housing, then small multifamily, co-living and short-term corporate rentals.

### 3. Family-Match and Retention Engine for Small Nanny and Au Pair Agencies

**Problem:** A boutique nanny agency matches families with caregivers by phone and spreadsheet. Placements fall through, replacement searches start from zero and the agency has no way to stay in touch after the first 90 days.

**What to build:** A family intake with schedule and needs, a caregiver profile with availability, a ranked match list, a trial-day scheduler, a 30/60/90-day check-in and a renewal or replacement request flow. Show placements, time to fill and retention.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Cut time-to-placement in half or the first quarter is free." Fee: $129/mo.

**Skill fit:** Matching logic, scheduling and check-in flows.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through agency-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $129/mo. 95 agencies = **$12,255/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Each placement fee is large, and faster fills increase revenue.

**Biggest risk:** Sensitive trust issues. Keep human review in every match.

**Growth path:** Nanny agencies, then senior-care matching, tutors, house-sitters and pet-sitters.

### 4. Direct-to-Customer Subscription Engine for Small Seafood and Shellfish Sellers

**Problem:** A small fishing or shellfish operation sells to wholesalers at low prices. Direct sales are handled by text, and catch-day announcements go to a few people. Customers want fresh seafood but do not know when it is available.

**What to build:** A catch-day text list, a pre-order page with pickup slots or local delivery, a weekly share subscription and a recipe card with each order. Show pre-orders, subscribers and revenue per catch.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Sell out each catch before you land it, or the first quarter is free." Fee: $69/mo plus 3% of orders.

**Skill fit:** Pre-order slots, SMS and subscriptions.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through fisher and shellfish-grower groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A seller with $5K/mo in direct orders pays $69 + $150 = about $220. 55 sellers x $220 = **$12,100/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Direct sales pay much more than wholesale, and customers repeat weekly.

**Biggest risk:** Cold-chain and delivery logistics. Start with pickup only.

**Growth path:** Seafood and shellfish, then farms, butchers, cheesemakers and bakeries.

### 5. Enrollment and Waitlist Engine for Adult Day Programs and Respite Centers

**Problem:** An adult day program has capacity but families find it late. Tours are inconsistent, trial days are not tracked and caregivers drop out of the process when the paperwork starts.

**What to build:** A caregiver-friendly inquiry page, a tour and trial-day scheduler, a simple checklist for next steps, a waitlist that offers openings and a 30-day check-in to prevent drop-off. Show inquiry-to-tour, tour-to-enrollment and days with open capacity.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fill 5 more spots in 90 days or the first quarter is free." Fee: $129/mo.

**Skill fit:** Intake, scheduling, waitlists and reminders.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through program-director groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $129/mo. 95 programs = **$12,255/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Each enrolled participant brings recurring revenue, and fill rates show clearly.

**Biggest risk:** Small, sensitive market. Keep messages gentle and reviewed by staff.

**Growth path:** Adult day programs, then memory-care, respite and home-care programs.
