# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-10 (Session 177)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice).
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed, so the buyer carries no risk.
- **Money models:** retention, upsell and "get cash now" mechanics that make a customer worth more.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Cancel-Save & Freeze-Save Engine for Boutique Fitness Studios

**Problem:** A boutique studio with 300 members at $150/mo loses 5-8% of them every month, and the owner usually learns about a cancellation after it is processed. Most of those members were visibly drifting weeks earlier (attendance falling from 3x to 0.5x per week) and never got a personal nudge.

**What to build:** Connect to Mindbody, Mariana Tek or Zen Planner exports. Score members on attendance decay, flag the "drifting" cohort and send a coach-voiced text. Add a cancel-intercept flow that offers a freeze, a plan downgrade or a free PT session instead of cancelling. Report saves and the revenue they represent.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We only take 20% of the first three months of revenue we save." The owner has no downside, and the ask is only to approve the message copy.

**Skill fit:** Attendance-pattern analysis, simple churn scoring and SMS automation.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends: CSV import, decay score, message templates, saves dashboard.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days. Pilot with two local studios to produce a real saves number.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A studio saves about 8 members per month at $150, so $1,200 of retained MRR per month. A 20% share is about $240/mo in the first quarter, and fees compound as saved members stay. Add a $149/mo platform fee. 35 studios x about $390/mo = **$13,650/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Saved members keep paying month after month, so fees from earlier saves keep stacking.

**Biggest risk:** Studio software exports differ. Start with two platforms and a manual CSV upload.

**Growth path:** Studios, then martial-arts schools, yoga and dance studios, then pilates and climbing gyms.

### 2. Invoice-to-Cash Chasing Service for Small B2B Trade Contractors

**Problem:** Small subcontractors (electrical, drywall, HVAC install, paving) carry 45-90 days of unpaid invoices. The owner hates chasing money and does it in bursts, so $20-100K sits uncollected at any time.

**What to build:** Pull open invoices from QuickBooks Online. Send escalating, polite reminders with a pay link, and prompt the owner to call at set milestones with a call script. Show a collections board with each GC's average days-to-pay so the owner can see slow payers before taking the next job.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Cash in your account faster, or no fee": 3% of invoices collected after the first reminder, with nothing for invoices that pay on their own.

**Skill fit:** QuickBooks API, scheduling, simple analytics on payer behavior.

**MVP scope:** 2-3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through contractor Facebook groups and local builders' associations.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A contractor with $60K of aging invoices collecting $40K because of reminders pays 3% = $1,200 once, then roughly $400/mo ongoing. $99/mo platform fee. 30 contractors x about $500/mo = **$15,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Days-to-pay data per payer becomes a benchmark that subs would pay to see.

**Biggest risk:** Damaging the relationship with a general contractor. Mitigate with tone controls and an owner-approval step on every escalation.

**Growth path:** Trade subs, then agencies and freelancers, then a "factoring-lite" referral partnership.

### 3. Post-Purchase Upsell & Bundle Optimizer for Small Shopify Stores

**Problem:** A store doing $30-150K/mo spends heavily on getting the first order and then shows the buyer a bare thank-you page. The first order is when a customer is most willing to buy again, and the store does not test offers there.

**What to build:** A post-purchase page app that tests one-click add-ons, bundles and "complete the set" offers by cart type, with automatic winner selection. A dashboard shows incremental revenue per 100 orders.

**Grand Slam angle:** Free to install, with 10% of the upsell revenue it generates.

**Skill fit:** Shopify app API, A/B testing and basic recommendation logic.

**MVP scope:** 4 weekends, because Shopify post-purchase extensions need some setup.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through Shopify communities and DTC founder groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A store with 2,000 orders/mo and a 6% take rate on a $25 add-on yields $3,000 of upsell revenue, so a 10% share is $300/mo. 40 stores x $300 = $12,000/mo.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Revenue share scales with each client's order volume without extra work.

**Biggest risk:** Competition from established apps. Differentiate on niche (single-category stores such as supplements or coffee) with niche-specific offer templates.

**Growth path:** One-click upsells, then post-purchase surveys, then subscription-conversion offers.

### 4. Quoted-vs-Actual Margin Leak Finder for Small Custom Fabrication Shops

**Problem:** Custom shops (cabinetry, metal fabrication, signage) quote from experience and never learn which job types lose money. Hours overrun on certain jobs, material waste is unmeasured and the owner discovers the problem at year-end.

**What to build:** Import quotes and time-card/material data from QuickBooks or a spreadsheet. Compute margin by job type, customer, estimator and material. Show a "margin leak" list (for example "stair rails run 31% over on welding hours") and a pricing-adjustment suggestion for the next quote.

**Grand Slam angle:** A paid 30-day margin audit with a guarantee that it finds at least $10K of annual margin leak, or the fee is refunded.

**Skill fit:** Analytics, data cleaning and dashboards.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days. Sell the audit first, then the ongoing dashboard.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $1,200 audit x 4/mo = $4,800/mo, plus $199/mo dashboard for 35 retained shops = $6,965/mo. Total about **$11,765/mo** at 35 shops with a steady 4 audits/mo.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The audit funds early growth and the dashboard turns each audit into recurring revenue.

**Biggest risk:** Messy time tracking in small shops. Offer a simple job-timer sheet as part of onboarding.

**Growth path:** Audits, then a monthly dashboard, then benchmarks across shops by trade.

### 5. Automated Referral Engine for Pool-Service & Lawn-Care Route Businesses

**Problem:** Route businesses (pool cleaning, lawn care, pest control) grow by adding stops in the same neighborhoods. Their happy customers would refer neighbors, but nobody asks, and the owner has no system to reward or track referrals.

**What to build:** After each completed visit, send a short satisfaction text. Happy customers (4-5) get a referral link with a reward ("one free month for you and your neighbor"). Track referral status per stop and show route density gained per referral, because a new stop on an existing route is almost pure margin.

**Grand Slam angle:** "$40 per new stop we generate, nothing otherwise," billed only when the new customer completes the first service.

**Skill fit:** SMS flows, a lightweight CRM and route-density analytics.

**MVP scope:** 2 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through pool and lawn-care owner Facebook groups and trade forums.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A route with 150 stops gets 3 referrals/mo, so $120/mo performance plus a $79/mo platform fee = about $200/mo. 60 operators x $200 = **$12,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Operators measure it by stops added, and every new stop also lengthens the customer's lifetime in the tool.

**Biggest risk:** Low-volume operators produce little referral revenue. Qualify for owners with 80+ stops.

**Growth path:** Referral engine, then review requests, then seasonal upsell (pool openings, aeration, fertilization).
