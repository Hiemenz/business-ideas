# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-14 (Session 195)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice).
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed, paid from revenue the client can see.
- **Recurring revenue first:** Hormozi pushes owners to turn one-time buyers into memberships and continuity, because LTV drives how much can be spent on acquisition. This session moves one-off sales to recurring or repeat ones.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Membership Conversion Engine for Independent Car Washes

**Problem:** An independent car wash sells single washes at $12-20 while a monthly unlimited plan at $25-35 would bring steady cash. Most owners offer the plan only on a sign by the pay station, and members who stop using it cancel without a save attempt.

**What to build:** Plate-recognition or loyalty-number tracking of repeat single-wash customers, a text offer after the third wash ("unlimited for $29, first month $9"), a usage dashboard that flags members who have not visited in 3 weeks with a nudge, and a cancel-save flow. Show the owner member count, churn and revenue per car.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Add 100 members in 90 days or you do not pay." Fee is $3 per member per month for members it converted.

**Skill fit:** Customer matching, SMS flows and a retention dashboard.

**MVP scope:** 4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through car-wash owner groups and trade forums.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $99/mo plus $3 per converted member. A wash with 200 converted members pays about $700/mo. Many smaller sites convert 80, paying about $340. 35 sites x about $350 = **$12,250/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Members are the wash's most predictable income, and the owner sees the gain monthly.

**Biggest risk:** Dependence on the wash's gate or POS data. Start with sites that use loyalty numbers or a phone-number lookup.

**Growth path:** Car washes, then detailing shops, oil-change centers and laundromats with wash-and-fold plans.

### 2. Reorder Engine for Local Print and Sign Shops

**Problem:** A print shop makes business cards, banners and yard signs for small businesses that need the same thing again in 3-12 months. The shop never reminds them, so customers drift to online printers.

**What to build:** Import past orders, build a reorder date per customer and item, and send a text or email with a one-click reorder link ("Same 500 cards, ready Thursday"). Add a seasonal prompt (election, graduation, holiday) and show repeat-order revenue.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Recover $2,000 in reorders in 90 days, or the first quarter is free."

**Skill fit:** Order-history parsing, reminders and a one-click checkout.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through print-shop owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $89/mo per shop. A shop with 400 repeat-eligible customers and a 6% reorder lift of $120 average adds about $2,900/mo. 120 shops = **$10,680/mo**; a 5% revenue share on reorders would raise it.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The order history grows richer each year, and reorders carry little acquisition cost.

**Biggest risk:** Order data is messy. Offer a one-time spreadsheet import service.

**Growth path:** Print shops, then trophy shops, embroidery shops, promotional-product sellers and label printers.

### 3. Class-Pack and Membership Optimizer for Boutique Pilates and Yoga Studios

**Problem:** A boutique studio sells drop-ins, 5-packs and 10-packs. Packs expire unused, new students buy one class and never return, and nobody knows which pack type gives the best lifetime value.

**What to build:** Track each student's visits since signup, message new students after class one and three with the next-step offer, convert pack buyers to monthly memberships at the right moment and flag expiring packs with a bonus-class nudge. Report lifetime value by first purchase type.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Turn 20% more first-timers into regulars in 60 days, or you pay nothing."

**Skill fit:** Booking-system exports, cohort analytics and messaging.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through studio-owner communities.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $119/mo per studio. 90 studios = **$10,710/mo**. A studio converting 8 more members a month at $130 sees about $1,000/mo in added revenue.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** First-timer conversion is a constant need, and cohort data guides pricing.

**Biggest risk:** Strong competition from booking platforms' own marketing tools. Sell the done-for-you playbook and results guarantee.

**Growth path:** Pilates and yoga, then barre, cycling, martial-arts and climbing gyms.

### 4. Paid-Community Churn and Engagement Manager for Niche Creators

**Problem:** A creator runs a paid community on Discord, Circle or Skool for $20-99/mo. Members join with enthusiasm and fade out by month three, and the creator cannot see who is going quiet until they cancel.

**What to build:** Connect the community and payment accounts, score each member's engagement, flag fading members, draft a personal check-in or a "start here" nudge and track which saves worked. Show cohort retention and revenue at risk.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Cut monthly churn by a third or the next two months are free."

**Skill fit:** API integrations, engagement scoring and message drafts.

**MVP scope:** 4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through creator and community-builder groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $79/mo. 140 creators = **$11,060/mo**. A community with 300 members at $39 saving 15 extra members a month earns the creator about $585/mo more.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Retention compounds, and creators pay to protect recurring revenue.

**Biggest risk:** Platform API limits. Start with the platform that offers the richest data access.

**Growth path:** Paid communities, then course creators, coaching programs and newsletter publishers.

### 5. Maintenance-Plan Starter for Garage Door and Gate Service Companies

**Problem:** A garage door company earns on repairs and installs, but each customer's springs, rollers and openers need checks every year. The company has the customer's address and door age, yet it never offers a yearly tune-up plan.

**What to build:** Import past jobs, build a list by door and opener age, text a tune-up offer with a price and booking link, and sell an annual plan at checkout through a tech tablet script. Track plan members, visit schedule and repair revenue found on visits.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Sign 100 plan members in 6 months or the platform is free."

**Skill fit:** Job-history parsing, offer campaigns and a visit scheduler.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through garage-door company owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $99/mo plus $2 per plan member per month. A company with 150 members pays about $400/mo. 30 companies = **$12,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The plan base creates predictable work and repair upsells on every visit.

**Biggest risk:** Small market of companies. Expand early to gate, fence and awning services.

**Growth path:** Garage doors, then gates, water-heater, septic and appliance-repair companies.
