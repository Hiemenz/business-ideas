# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-15 (Session 204)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session reduces effort and sacrifice with done-for-you kits and subscriptions.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Make it a no-brainer:** Hormozi's advice to stack value until the price feels small. Each idea bundles what an owner already sells into a repeatable, higher-value package.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Membership and Private-Event Engine for Independent Cinemas and Drive-Ins

**Problem:** An independent cinema competes with streaming and relies on ticket sales that swing with each release. Loyal viewers have no membership, concessions are the real margin and private screenings are rarely sold.

**What to build:** A monthly membership (tickets plus concession credit), a private-screening and birthday booking page, a pre-show concession order link, a "bring a friend" pass and a showtime text for members. Show members, concession revenue per head and private-event bookings.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Add 100 members in 90 days or the first quarter is free." Fee: $119/mo plus $0.25 per member visit.

**Skill fit:** Membership billing, booking pages and SMS.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through independent-theater owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A cinema with 150 members and 600 member visits a month pays $119 + $150 = about $270/mo. 47 cinemas x $270 = **$12,690/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Memberships create recurring revenue and lift concessions.

**Biggest risk:** A small market of independent cinemas. Add live-event venues and community theaters.

**Growth path:** Cinemas, then drive-ins, community theaters, comedy clubs and music venues.

### 2. Kit-Club and Class-Fill Engine for Yarn and Fabric Shops

**Problem:** A yarn or fabric shop sells supplies at low margins and loses customers to online sellers. Classes fill unevenly, and customers buy yarn without a finished project in mind.

**What to build:** A monthly project-kit club (pattern plus materials), a class calendar with seat limits and a waitlist, a "finish your project" reminder with a discount on the next kit and a refer-a-friend bonus. Show club members, kit margin and class fill.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Add 40 kit-club members in 90 days or the first quarter is free." Fee: $79/mo plus $1 per kit shipped.

**Skill fit:** Subscription billing, class booking and reminders.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through shop-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A shop with 60 kit members pays $79 + $60 = about $140. 90 shops x $140 = **$12,600/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Kits turn one-off buyers into members, and classes build community.

**Biggest risk:** Thin margins. Keep fees small and target shops with active classes.

**Growth path:** Yarn and fabric, then craft stores, quilt shops, art-supply stores and hobby shops.

### 3. Water-Test and Seasonal Chemical-Plan Engine for Pool Supply Stores

**Problem:** A pool supply store depends on customers who bring in water samples. In spring and summer it is busy, then customers shift to buying chemicals online. The store gets no reminder channel and no recurring plan.

**What to build:** A customer pool profile (size, type), a text reminder to bring a water sample, a seasonal chemical plan with delivery or pickup, a spring opening and fall closing kit and a loyalty reward. Show repeat test visits and recurring-plan revenue.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Add 15% to chemical revenue or the first quarter is free." Fee: $99/mo plus 1% of plan sales.

**Skill fit:** Customer profiles, reminders and plan subscriptions.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through store-owner groups, timed ahead of spring.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A store with $10K/mo in plan sales in season pays about $200 averaged across the year. 60 stores x $200 = **$12,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Recurring chemical needs make plans natural, and results show in repeat visits.

**Biggest risk:** Seasonality and online competition. Add a hot-tub mode for year-round demand.

**Growth path:** Pool supply, then hot-tub dealers, aquarium stores and garden centers.

### 4. Contractor-Account and Reorder Engine for Independent Paint and Hardware Stores

**Problem:** An independent paint or hardware store depends on contractors who buy often but are courted by big-box stores. Orders come by phone, past purchases are not remembered and the store loses reorders to convenience.

**What to build:** A contractor account page with saved past orders, one-tap reorder with a will-call pickup time, a job-based quote tool, tiered pricing and a weekly "what you bought last month" text. Show contractor reorder rate and revenue per account.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Raise contractor revenue by 15% in 90 days or the first quarter is free." Fee: $149/mo.

**Skill fit:** Saved orders, reorder logic and quote tools.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through store-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $149/mo. 85 stores = **$12,665/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Contractor accounts are large and recurring, and one-tap reorder improves loyalty.

**Biggest risk:** Integration with store point-of-sale systems. Start with manual order import.

**Growth path:** Paint and hardware stores, then lumber yards, electrical supply and plumbing supply shops.

### 5. Group-Booking and Season-Pass Engine for Ropes Courses, Zip-Line and Adventure Parks

**Problem:** An adventure park depends on school groups, camps, birthdays and corporate teams, but handles group inquiries by email and phone. Weather cancellations are rescheduled by hand, and visitors rarely buy a season pass.

**What to build:** A group-booking page with deposits, weather-based rescheduling, a day-of check-in list, add-ons (lunch, photos) and a season-pass offer after the first visit. Show group revenue, reschedule rate and pass conversion.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Book 20% more groups or the first quarter is free." Fee: $119/mo plus 2% of group deposits.

**Skill fit:** Group booking, weather rules and passes.

**MVP scope:** 4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through park-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A park taking $15K/mo in group deposits pays $119 + $300 = about $420. 30 parks x $420 = **$12,600/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Group bookings are large, and passes turn visitors into repeat customers.

**Biggest risk:** Small, seasonal market. Add indoor attractions and winter programs.

**Growth path:** Adventure parks, then trampoline parks, escape rooms, mini-golf and climbing gyms.
