# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-14 (Session 197)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session attacks time delay: respond fast, book fast, pay back fast.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Leads you already have:** Hormozi's "reactivate the list" and "follow up more" advice. Each idea turns inquiries the owner already paid for into booked revenue.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Tour-to-Booking Follow-Up Engine for Small Wedding Venues

**Problem:** A barn or estate venue gets 20-40 inquiries a month, hosts a tour for some and books a few. After the tour, follow-up is one email. Couples tour 4-6 venues and book the one that stays in touch. A single booking is worth $8-15K.

**What to build:** An inquiry inbox with an instant reply and tour-booking link, a post-tour sequence (photo recap, date-hold countdown, a "dates other couples are eyeing" note, a limited-time incentive) and a pipeline board by wedding date. Show inquiry-to-tour and tour-to-booking rates.

**Grand Slam angle:** "One extra booking in 6 months, or the platform is free." Fee: $99/mo plus $150 per booking that came through the sequence.

**Skill fit:** Inbox parsing, email/SMS sequences and a pipeline view.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through venue-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A venue closing 4 extra bookings a year pays about $99 x 12 + $600 = about $1,800/yr, around $150/mo. 90 venues x about $150 = **$13,500/mo**. A higher-touch tier at $199/mo lifts it.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The value is one booking worth thousands, and the sequence runs every season.

**Biggest risk:** Small market and seasonality. Sell annual plans and add event venues and rental halls.

**Growth path:** Wedding venues, then event halls, photographers, planners and caterers.

### 2. Waitlist and Term-Renewal Engine for Swim Schools and Kids' Lesson Programs

**Problem:** A swim school has full classes and long waitlists, yet loses 25-35% of students each term because of schedule changes and no re-enrollment push. Empty spots from no-shows go unfilled while families wait.

**What to build:** A waitlist that auto-offers an open spot by text with a 2-hour claim window, a re-enrollment campaign before term end with a one-tap "same time next term" button, and a level-progress card for parents. Show spots filled from the waitlist and re-enrollment rate.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fill every open spot within 24 hours and lift re-enrollment 10 points, or you do not pay." Fee: $99/mo per location.

**Skill fit:** Waitlist logic, SMS and scheduling.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through swim-school owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A school with 300 students at $160/term gains 10 points = 30 students = $4,800/term. $99/mo x 130 schools = **$12,870/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Every term has a re-enrollment moment, and results are visible in enrollment.

**Biggest risk:** Incumbent class-management software. Differentiate on the waitlist-fill and one-tap renewal flows.

**Growth path:** Swim schools, then gymnastics, dance, martial arts and music schools.

### 3. Membership and Capacity-Fill Engine for Sauna and Cold-Plunge Studios

**Problem:** A sauna and cold-plunge studio has a fixed number of sessions and peaks on weekends. Weekday mornings sit empty, drop-in buyers never become members and members drift away after the first month.

**What to build:** Slot booking with off-peak pricing, a drop-in-to-membership offer sent right after the first visit, streak rewards, a "bring a friend" guest pass and a lapsed-member win-back. Show capacity used by hour and member retention.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fill your off-peak slots by 30% or the first 60 days are free."

**Skill fit:** Booking, pricing rules, memberships and win-back flows.

**MVP scope:** 4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through wellness-studio owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $129/mo per studio. 95 studios = **$12,255/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The studio's margin sits in filled off-peak slots, and the model repeats at every location.

**Biggest risk:** Small niche and fad risk. Sell to broader recovery and wellness studios.

**Growth path:** Sauna studios, then float spas, infrared studios, recovery clinics and contrast-therapy gyms.

### 4. Group-Booking Conversion Engine for Axe-Throwing, Escape-Room and Entertainment Venues

**Problem:** Entertainment venues earn most revenue from group events, such as birthdays, team outings and bachelorette parties. Group inquiries come by form and phone, replies take hours, and the group books the first venue to answer with a clear price.

**What to build:** An instant group quote page (headcount, date, package, add-ons) with live availability and a deposit link, an auto-reply within a minute and follow-up for unbooked quotes. Add upsells such as food packages and extra time. Show response time, quote-to-deposit rate and add-on revenue.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Answer every group lead in under a minute and book 20% more, or you do not pay." Fee: $79/mo plus 2% of deposits taken.

**Skill fit:** Quote builder, calendar availability and payments.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 21-45 days through venue-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A venue taking $8,000/mo in group deposits pays $79 + $160 = about $240/mo. 55 venues x $240 = **$13,200/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Group revenue is large per booking and the speed advantage is measurable.

**Biggest risk:** Venues already using booking tools. Position on the quote-and-follow-up flow.

**Growth path:** Entertainment venues, then party buses, breweries with event space, bowling alleys and trampoline parks.

### 5. Service-Plan and Reactivation Engine for Independent E-Bike and Bike Shops

**Problem:** A bike shop sells a bike once and sees the owner again only when something breaks. Hundreds of past buyers are on a list that is never used, and spring tune-up demand is left to chance.

**What to build:** Import past customers and bikes, send a seasonal tune-up offer by text and email, sell a prepaid annual service plan and send a reminder at the right mileage or month. Add a "trade-in your old bike" prompt on the bike's third year. Show revenue from reactivated customers.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We bring back 50 past customers this season, or the first quarter is free." Fee: $79/mo plus $10 per booked service.

**Skill fit:** List import, campaign flows and service-plan sales.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through bike-shop owner groups, timed ahead of spring.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A shop booking 40 services a season pays $79 x 3 + $400 = about $640 a season, around $210/mo. 60 shops x $210 = **$12,600/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The customer list grows with every sale, and each season has a campaign.

**Biggest risk:** Seasonality and small margins. Sell annual plans and add year-round service plans.

**Growth path:** Bike shops, then scooter, motorcycle, ski and boat shops.
