# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-12 (Session 190)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice).
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Price on value, not time:** flat-rate pricing, prepaid annual plans and customer gifting that make a customer worth more and stay longer.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Client-Anniversary Gifting Automation for Real-Estate Agents and Small Advisory Firms

**Problem:** Real-estate agents live on referrals from past clients, but most stop contacting a client within a year of closing. A gift on the home-purchase anniversary is the classic way to stay top of mind, and nobody has time to remember dates, pick gifts and place orders.

**What to build:** Import past clients with their closing dates. Schedule a gift for each anniversary (a local-bakery box, a plant, a photo of the house), let the agent approve a batch quarterly and handle ordering and delivery through local vendors. Add a handwritten-style note and a referral ask. Track gifts sent and referrals that follow.

**Grand Slam angle:** "A 90-day trial: if the program does not generate at least one referral conversation, you do not pay."

**Skill fit:** Scheduling, gift-vendor APIs and CRM import.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through real-estate agent groups and brokerage partnerships.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $29/mo + a 15% service margin on gifts. An agent sending 12 gifts a month at $45 pays about $110/mo. 100 agents x $110 = **$11,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Referrals from past clients are worth many times the gift cost, and agents repeat the program every year.

**Biggest risk:** Gift fulfillment quality. Start in a few metros with a vetted local vendor in each.

**Growth path:** Agents, then mortgage brokers, financial advisers, insurance agencies and home-service companies.

### 2. Flat-Rate Price-Book Builder for Small Repair and Trade Shops

**Problem:** Many small repair and trade shops still charge by the hour. Fast technicians earn less than slow ones, customers fear a runaway bill and quotes take time. Flat-rate pricing (a fixed price per task) makes the customer's decision easy and rewards skill.

**What to build:** Start from the shop's costs, technician rates and target margin. Generate a flat-rate price for each common task from historical jobs (average time, parts, trip cost), show the effect on revenue and let the owner adjust. Output a tablet-ready price book and printable cards. Track actual margin per task over time.

**Grand Slam angle:** "A price book that lifts your average ticket by 8% within 90 days, or you get the first quarter free."

**Skill fit:** Cost modeling, historical job analysis and a simple price-book editor.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through trade-business owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $499 one-time setup + $49/mo for updates and margin tracking. 15 setups a month in the first three months gives $7,485/mo, then 70 retained shops at $49 = $3,430/mo plus ongoing new setups. Settles around **$11,000-12,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Pricing is the highest-leverage change a shop can make, and the margin data keeps the shop subscribed.

**Biggest risk:** Owners fear customer pushback. Show a few jobs priced both ways and start with the 20 most common tasks.

**Growth path:** Repair and trade shops, then auto shops, appliance repair, plumbing and electrical.

### 3. Annual-Plan Upsell Engine for Monthly Members

**Problem:** A studio, gym or software business with 400 monthly members could pull cash forward by offering a discounted annual plan. Most never ask, and monthly members cancel at 5-8% a month, while annual members rarely do.

**What to build:** Identify monthly members who have stayed 3+ months, then offer a prepay annual plan (two months free) by text and email at the right moment, such as after a goal milestone or before a price change. Provide a one-click payment link and track conversion, cash pulled forward and the churn difference.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We pull at least $10,000 of cash forward in 60 days, or you pay nothing." The fee is 5% of annual plans sold.

**Skill fit:** Segmentation, messaging, payment links and cohort tracking.

**MVP scope:** 2-3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through studio and membership-business owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A business sells 30 annual plans at $1,000 = $30,000, so 5% = $1,500 per campaign, about two campaigns a year, about $250/mo, plus $49/mo. 40 businesses x $299 = **$11,960/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Prepaid revenue improves cash flow and retention, and the campaign repeats with every new cohort.

**Biggest risk:** Owners worry about discounting. Show that annual members stay longer and quote the break-even point.

**Growth path:** Studios and gyms, then software subscriptions, newsletters, clubs and associations.

### 4. Form-Abandonment Recovery for Local Service Websites

**Problem:** A visitor starts a quote form, enters a phone number or email and leaves. For many local sites, 30-50% of form starts never finish, and the business never hears from those people even though they were nearly ready to book.

**What to build:** A script that captures partial form entries (with consent language) and triggers an immediate text or email: "Looks like you didn't finish your request. Want us to call you?" The owner sees partial leads in a queue with the page and answers they gave. Report recovered leads and booked jobs.

**Grand Slam angle:** "We recover leads you are losing today, and you pay $15 per recovered lead that books."

**Skill fit:** Front-end script, messaging and lead analytics.

**MVP scope:** 2-3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through home-service marketing groups and agency partners.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $59/mo + $15 per recovered booked job. A company booking 10 recovered jobs pays $209/mo. 55 companies x $209 = **$11,495/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** It improves every ad dollar's return and works with any site that has a form.

**Biggest risk:** Privacy perception around partial form capture. Use clear consent text and capture only after the visitor enters a contact detail.

**Growth path:** Home services, then law offices, clinics, schools and B2B lead forms.

### 5. Crew Scoreboard and Bonus Tracker for Small Field-Service Companies

**Problem:** A cleaning, landscaping or install company pays crews hourly regardless of output, so there is no incentive to be fast or careful. Owners want to pay bonuses for quality and speed but lack a simple way to measure either.

**What to build:** A crew app: each job logs time on site, a before-and-after photo set, a customer rating and callbacks. The tool computes a weekly score per crew (jobs per day, ratings, callbacks) and a bonus pool based on the owner's rule, displayed on a shared leaderboard.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Lift jobs per crew per week by 10% in 60 days, or the first quarter is free."

**Skill fit:** Mobile job logging, scoring rules and leaderboards.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through cleaning and landscaping owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $6/crew member/mo with a $79 minimum. An 18-person company pays $108/mo. 100 companies = **$10,800/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Crew scores become a management and hiring tool, and productivity gains are visible in revenue per crew.

**Biggest risk:** Crew resistance to tracking. Present it as a bonus program and keep the logging to a few taps.

**Growth path:** Cleaning and landscaping crews, then install teams, moving companies and restoration crews.
