# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-13 (Session 193)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session works mostly on perceived likelihood: show the buyer the result in numbers before they buy.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Warm leads first:** referrals and warm introductions convert far better than cold outreach.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. ROI Calculator and Proposal Microsite Builder for Small B2B Vendors

**Problem:** A small vendor of software, equipment or services says "we'll save you money," and the buyer has no way to check it. Deals stall because the buyer cannot justify the spend to a boss. A calculator that shows "$38,400 saved per year" in the buyer's own numbers raises the perceived likelihood of success.

**What to build:** A builder where the vendor defines inputs (headcount, hours, error rate, current cost) and a formula, and gets a branded calculator page and a proposal microsite with the buyer's results, a payback period and a share link for the buyer's boss. Track which calculators were opened, completed and forwarded.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Your first calculator in 48 hours, and a 60-day trial. If it doesn't help close a deal, you pay nothing."

**Skill fit:** Formula builder, page generator and engagement tracking.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through B2B founder communities and small-vendor sales teams.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $99/mo per calculator pack of 3, with a $199/mo plan for 10 calculators. A mix averaging $150/mo x 80 vendors = **$12,000/mo**. Add a $499 done-for-you calculator build for new customers.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Each vendor needs calculators for each product and segment, and engagement data shows what buyers care about.

**Biggest risk:** Calculators with unrealistic assumptions erode trust. Build in conservative defaults and an assumptions panel.

**Growth path:** Small vendors, then agencies, installers, MSPs and consultants.

### 2. Deposit-Secured Waitlist and Booking Manager for High-Demand Solo Makers and Artists

**Problem:** A custom cake designer, portrait painter or bespoke furniture maker has more demand than capacity. Requests arrive by DM, no one pays a deposit, flaky inquirers waste the owner's time and the queue lives in a notes app.

**What to build:** A booking page with limited slots per month, a short brief form, a deposit to join the queue, auto-reminders and a status page for clients. Offer a paid "skip-the-line" option. Track slots filled, deposits and conversion from inquiry to deposit.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fewer time-wasters, full calendar. Take 3% of deposits and nothing else."

**Skill fit:** Booking pages, payment links and queue logic.

**MVP scope:** 2-3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 21-45 days through maker and artist communities.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $29/mo + 3% of deposits. An artist taking $3,000 in deposits pays about $119/mo. 100 artists = **$11,900/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Makers keep using the queue as their order system, and deposit volume grows with their reputation.

**Biggest risk:** Low willingness to pay among hobbyists. Target full-time makers who turn work away.

**Growth path:** Cake designers and painters, then photographers, tattoo artists, bespoke tailors and custom builders.

### 3. Street-Rate Optimizer for Independent Self-Storage Operators

**Problem:** An independent storage facility prices units by feel. Occupancy runs 85% while rates sit below the market, or rates are too high and units stay empty. Owners rarely test price changes by unit size and floor.

**What to build:** Import the unit roster and rental history. Compare unit rates with nearby facilities' published rates, and recommend rates per unit type to balance occupancy and revenue. Suggest promotions (first-month deals) for slow sizes, and track revenue per available square foot.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Raise revenue per square foot by 5% within 90 days, or the first quarter is free."

**Skill fit:** Price scraping, occupancy analytics and pricing recommendations.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through self-storage owner groups and trade forums.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $149/mo per facility. A 300-unit facility earning $20K/mo gains $1,000/mo from a 5% lift. 80 facilities = **$11,920/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Pricing needs updating continuously, and the owner can measure the gain in the monthly rent roll.

**Biggest risk:** Competitor price data coverage. Start in metros where facilities publish rates online.

**Growth path:** Self-storage, then RV and boat storage, parking lots and small commercial rentals.

### 4. Warm-Introduction Mapper for B2B Founders and Sales Reps

**Problem:** Warm introductions close at many times the rate of cold emails, but a rep cannot see which of their contacts know the target company's buyer. They send cold messages when a mutual contact could introduce them.

**What to build:** Connect the user's contact list and professional-network exports. For each target account, show who in the user's network has a connection to someone at the company, draft the intro request for the mutual contact and track which requests turned into meetings.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Five warm intros to your target accounts in 30 days, or you do not pay."

**Skill fit:** Contact matching, network graphs and message drafting.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through founder and sales communities.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $59/mo per user. 190 users = **$11,210/mo**. A team plan at $199 for 5 seats improves revenue per account.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The network graph grows more valuable as more users connect, and meetings booked is an easy metric.

**Biggest risk:** Data-access limits on professional networks. Build on user-provided exports and email and calendar contacts.

**Growth path:** Founders and reps, then recruiters, fundraisers and agency owners.

### 5. Virtual Walkthrough Estimator for Moving Companies

**Problem:** A moving company sends an estimator to each home or guesses by phone. Visits cost time and delay the quote, and phone guesses lead to under-quotes that turn into disputes on moving day.

**What to build:** A customer-facing page where the customer walks through the home with a phone camera or uploads photos of each room. A vision model lists furniture and boxes, estimates cubic feet and truck size and returns a price range. A coordinator reviews and sends the final quote. Track quote accuracy against actual moves.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Quotes in 10 minutes without a site visit. If your quote-to-booking rate doesn't improve, the first month is free."

**Skill fit:** Vision models, volume estimation and quote workflow.

**MVP scope:** 4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through moving-company owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $199/mo + $10 per completed estimate. A company doing 60 estimates a month pays $799/mo. Many small movers do 20, paying $399/mo. 30 companies x about $450 = **$13,500/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Faster, more accurate quotes win jobs, and accuracy improves with each completed move.

**Biggest risk:** Estimation errors. Keep a human reviewer and show ranges until accuracy is proven.

**Growth path:** Moving companies, then junk removal, storage pickup, furniture delivery and estate cleanout services.
