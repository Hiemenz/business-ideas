# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-15 (Session 201)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session focuses on bundling and anchoring: show a bigger package first so the middle option feels like the obvious choice.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Increase average order value:** Hormozi's "more people, more often, more money" growth levers. Most ideas raise order size or frequency, not just lead count.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Quote-to-Deposit and Package Upsell Engine for Tent and Party-Rental Companies

**Problem:** A rental company quotes tents, tables, chairs and dance floors by email, and customers pick the cheapest line items. Quotes sit unanswered, no deposit locks the date and add-ons (lighting, heaters, flooring) rarely get sold.

**What to build:** A quote builder with three anchored packages (Basic, Popular, Full Event), live inventory by date, a deposit link and a follow-up sequence on open quotes. Show package mix, quote-to-deposit rate and average order value.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Raise average order value by 20% in 90 days or you do not pay." Fee: $99/mo plus 1% of deposits.

**Skill fit:** Quote builder, inventory by date and payment links.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through rental-owner groups, ahead of wedding season.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A company taking $20K/mo of deposits pays $99 + $200 = about $300/mo. 45 companies x $300 = **$13,500/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The package mix lifts order value on every quote, and results are visible in revenue.

**Biggest risk:** Seasonality. Sell annual plans and add corporate events.

**Growth path:** Tent and party rental, then equipment, wedding and trade-show rentals.

### 2. Booking and Add-On Engine for U-Pick Farms and Agritourism

**Problem:** A pumpkin patch, berry farm or corn maze depends on a few weekends. Weather swings attendance, walk-ups crowd the lot and few visitors buy add-ons (hayrides, food, pre-picked boxes). School and group visits are handled by phone.

**What to build:** Timed-entry ticketing with weather-based reschedules, group and school booking pages, add-on bundles at checkout, a pre-visit text and a "see you next season" list. Show attendance by hour, add-on attach rate and group revenue.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Free to set up. We take 3% of ticket sales and 10% of add-on sales."

**Skill fit:** Timed ticketing, weather rules and checkout.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days, timed ahead of the season.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A farm with $60K of season ticket and add-on sales pays about $2,400 a season, or $200/mo averaged, plus $29/mo. 55 farms x $230 = **$12,650/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Each season repeats, and the mailing list grows every year.

**Biggest risk:** Strong seasonality. Add winter events, such as light shows and tree farms.

**Growth path:** U-pick farms, then tree farms, pumpkin patches, corn mazes and winery events.

### 3. Loyalty and Reorder Engine for Independent Tea Shops and Bubble-Tea Stores

**Problem:** A tea or bubble-tea shop has loyal regulars but no way to reach them. Punch cards get lost, slow weekday afternoons are empty and new seasonal drinks launch with no announcement.

**What to build:** A phone-number loyalty program with no app download, a "slow-hour" text offer, seasonal-drink launches by text and an order-ahead link. Show repeat-visit rate and revenue from texts.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Add 10% to weekday revenue in 60 days or the first quarter is free." Fee: $79/mo.

**Skill fit:** Phone-based loyalty, SMS campaigns and POS integrations.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 21-45 days through shop-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $79/mo. 150 shops = **$11,850/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** The customer list is the shop's asset, and campaigns repeat every season.

**Biggest risk:** Crowded loyalty-app market. Differentiate on no-app, phone-number simplicity.

**Growth path:** Tea shops, then cafes, bakeries, frozen yogurt and juice bars.

### 4. Design-Visual Quote Engine for Pergola, Deck and Outdoor-Living Builders

**Problem:** A pergola or outdoor-living builder quotes $10-40K projects from sketches and verbal descriptions. Homeowners cannot picture the result and compare on price alone.

**What to build:** A tool that takes a yard photo and the builder's catalog and generates a mock-up with tiered options (Good, Better, Best), a price range and a deposit-to-design link. Track which tier is chosen and quote-to-job rate.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Win 15% more projects or the first quarter is free." Fee: $149/mo.

**Skill fit:** Image generation, a catalog and a proposal page.

**MVP scope:** 4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through contractor groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $149/mo. 85 builders = **$12,665/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** A visual proposal improves close rates and raises the chosen tier.

**Biggest risk:** Mock-up quality. Keep the builder's review step and show "concept" labels.

**Growth path:** Pergolas and decks, then pools, patios, fences and landscaping.

### 5. Package and Maintenance-Plan Engine for Garage-Floor and Epoxy Coating Installers

**Problem:** A coating installer wins a one-time job, then never sees the customer again. Quotes are a single price with no tiers, and referral asks are inconsistent.

**What to build:** Tiered quote pages (standard flake, premium, premium with cabinets), a post-job review and referral request, a yearly "clean and reseal" plan and a before/after gallery for the next quote. Show tier mix, referral rate and plan sales.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Raise average job value by 20% or the first quarter is free." Fee: $79/mo.

**Skill fit:** Quote tiers, galleries and referral sequences.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through installer groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $79/mo. 150 installers = **$11,850/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Tiered pricing and referrals lift revenue on every job.

**Biggest risk:** Small installer base. Expand to related trades.

**Growth path:** Garage floors, then concrete staining, driveway sealing, paint and flooring.
