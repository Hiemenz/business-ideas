# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-16 (Session 210)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session sells experiences, where the dream outcome is a memorable night and the main friction is the effort of organizing it.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Premium tiers and anchoring:** Hormozi's advice to offer a high-priced option so the middle one looks reasonable. Each idea adds VIP, group or season tiers to experiences that are currently sold at one price.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Ticketing and Group-Upsell Engine for Dinner-Theater and Murder-Mystery Companies

**Problem:** A murder-mystery or dinner-theater troupe sells tickets through a basic form, with no group tier, no add-ons and no list of past guests. Empty seats on slow nights go unfilled, and private-party inquiries are handled by email.

**What to build:** A show calendar with tiered seats (standard, VIP table, private group), add-ons (drinks package, photo, souvenir), a last-seat text to past guests, a private-event inquiry flow and a gift-voucher page. Show seats filled, average ticket value and private-event revenue.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fill 85% of your seats or the first quarter is free." Fee: $79/mo plus 2% of ticket sales.

**Skill fit:** Ticketing, tiers, vouchers and SMS.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through troupe-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A troupe selling $10K/mo in tickets pays $79 + $200 = about $280. 45 troupes x $280 = **$12,600/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Seats are perishable, and groups and gift vouchers raise revenue per show.

**Biggest risk:** Small niche. Add escape rooms, comedy clubs and local theater.

**Growth path:** Dinner theater, then comedy clubs, magic shows, haunted attractions and live-event companies.

### 2. Booking and Upsell Engine for Ghost-Tour and Walking-Tour Operators

**Problem:** A local walking-tour operator sells tickets through a travel site that takes 20-30%. Private groups email in, tours run half full on weekdays and repeat locals are never invited to a new tour.

**What to build:** A direct-booking page with tour calendar, private-group quotes, tiered tickets (standard, small-group, VIP with a drink), a gift voucher and a "new tour" text to past guests. Show direct-booking share, group revenue and fill rate.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Move 30% of bookings to direct or the first quarter is free." Fee: $79/mo plus 3% of direct bookings.

**Skill fit:** Booking pages, tiers and vouchers.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through operator groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** An operator with $8K/mo of direct bookings pays $79 + $240 = about $320. 40 operators x $320 = **$12,800/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Direct bookings avoid platform fees, and private groups pay more per head.

**Biggest risk:** Dependence on travel sites for discovery. Keep both channels and show a "direct" discount.

**Growth path:** Walking tours, then food, bike, boat and bus tour operators.

### 3. Season-Ticket and Donor-Renewal Engine for Community Theaters and Orchestras

**Problem:** A community theater or orchestra depends on season subscribers and small donors, but renewals are mailed paper forms. Subscribers lapse unnoticed, single-ticket buyers are never offered a season and donor thank-yous are late.

**What to build:** A subscription page with seat selection, a renewal reminder with an early-bird deadline, a single-ticket-to-season upgrade prompt, donor tiers with automatic thank-you notes and a lapsed-subscriber win-back. Show renewal rate and average gift.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Lift renewals by 10 points or the first season is free." Fee: $99/mo.

**Skill fit:** Subscription sales, seat maps and donor sequences.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through arts-administrator groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $99/mo. 120 companies = **$11,880/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Renewals are the main revenue event of the year, and the effect is measurable.

**Biggest risk:** Small organizations with tiny budgets and incumbents like ticketing platforms. Keep pricing low.

**Growth path:** Community theater and orchestras, then choirs, dance companies, museums and festivals.

### 4. Membership and Equipment-Time Engine for Maker Spaces and Community Workshops

**Problem:** A maker space has tools and room, but membership churns after 3-4 months. Class sign-ups are managed by hand, equipment bookings conflict and new members never take the training that makes them stay.

**What to build:** Membership tiers, a new-member onboarding path with a first-month project, class and equipment booking with deposits, a "bring a guest" day pass and a win-back for members who stop. Show active members, equipment utilization and class fill.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Cut member churn by a third or the first quarter is free." Fee: $99/mo.

**Skill fit:** Membership billing, booking and onboarding flows.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-60 days through maker-space groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $99/mo. 120 spaces = **$11,880/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Membership dues are recurring, and retention drives profitability.

**Biggest risk:** Many spaces are volunteer-run with low budgets. Target paid-staff spaces.

**Growth path:** Maker spaces, then art studios, woodshops, coworking spaces and community kitchens.

### 5. Member-Renewal and Event Engine for Local Chambers of Commerce

**Problem:** A small-town chamber relies on membership dues, but renewals come by invoice and a lot of members lapse. Mixers and sponsorships are managed in spreadsheets, and members cannot see the value they receive.

**What to build:** A member directory, a renewal flow with a "your year in numbers" summary (leads, event attendance, referrals), event sign-up and sponsor packages, a new-member welcome sequence and a lapse-risk flag. Show renewal rate and event revenue.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Raise renewals by 10 points or the first quarter is free." Fee: $129/mo.

**Skill fit:** Directory, renewal flows, event sign-up and member reports.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days through chamber and economic-development groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $129/mo. 95 chambers = **$12,255/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Dues are the lifeblood of a chamber, and one lapse prevented pays for the tool.

**Biggest risk:** Slow, committee-driven buying. Offer a one-month pilot with the executive director.

**Growth path:** Chambers, then trade associations, business-improvement districts, alumni groups and clubs.
