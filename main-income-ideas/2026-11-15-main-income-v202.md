# Main Income Ideas - 2026-11-15 (Session 202)

## Who this is for
A 29-year-old software engineer with data analytics skills who wants to replace a full-time salary ($8-15K+/mo) within 12-24 months by building solo, in evenings and weekends, with no outside funding. Every idea below must have a realistic path to first revenue inside 90 days and a named, reachable distribution channel.

## Lens for this session: Alex Hormozi
Built from the $100M Offers / $100M Leads frameworks:
- **Value equation:** Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Success) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice). This session works on the dream outcome: sell identity and belonging, not just products.
- **Grand Slam Offer:** performance-priced or guarantee-backed.
- **Continuity offers:** Hormozi's recurring-revenue "continuity" model. Each idea turns one-off purchases into a club, pre-order or seasonal plan.

Per standing feedback, none of these are compliance or regulatory tools. Each solves a revenue-leakage or growth problem.

## Ideas

### 1. Wine-Club and Allocation Engine for Independent Wine Shops

**Problem:** An independent wine shop sells bottles one at a time and competes with grocery prices. Its best customers have no reason to commit, and limited bottles go to whoever walks in first.

**What to build:** A tiered monthly club (2, 4 or 6 bottles), a customer taste profile, limited-allocation drops announced by text, a skip or swap portal and a tasting-event signup. Show club members, churn and revenue per member.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Add 40 club members in 90 days or the first quarter is free." Fee: $99/mo plus $2 per shipment or pickup.

**Skill fit:** Subscription billing, taste profiles and SMS drops.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through shop-owner groups, ahead of the holidays.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A club with 60 members getting 2 shipments a quarter pays about $99 + $40 = $140/mo. 90 shops x $140 = **$12,600/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Club members are the shop's most loyal and profitable customers, and the model repeats monthly.

**Biggest risk:** Shipping and alcohol-delivery rules vary, so start with in-store pickup and local delivery.

**Growth path:** Wine shops, then craft-beer shops, cheese shops, butchers and coffee roasters.

### 2. Event-Night and Pre-Order Engine for Tabletop Game and Hobby Stores

**Problem:** A game store depends on event nights and new-release launches, but events are announced on a bulletin board, pre-orders are tracked on paper and regulars drift to online retailers.

**What to build:** An events calendar with signup and fees, a pre-order system with deposit and a pickup text on release day, a player-points loyalty program and a "bring a friend" bonus. Show event attendance, pre-order revenue and repeat players.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fill your event nights to 80% or the first 60 days are free." Fee: $79/mo.

**Skill fit:** Event signup, pre-order logic and loyalty points.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through store-owner groups.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $79/mo. 150 stores = **$11,850/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Community and pre-orders make the store sticky, and releases repeat all year.

**Biggest risk:** A niche market with tight margins. Keep pricing low and add comics and collectibles shops.

**Growth path:** Game stores, then comic shops, hobby shops, trading-card shops and anime stores.

### 3. Seasonal-Swap Reminder and Package Engine for Independent Tire and Auto Shops

**Problem:** A tire shop has a rush in the first cold snap and in spring, then sits quiet. Customers forget to swap tires, and the shop sells one set at a time with no package of alignment, rotation and storage.

**What to build:** A vehicle list with tire-change reminders timed to local weather, a seasonal package (swap, balance, storage), a text booking link and a loyalty rotation plan. Show booked swaps, package sales and revenue by month.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fill your first 2 weeks of swap season or the first quarter is free." Fee: $99/mo plus $5 per booked swap.

**Skill fit:** Reminder logic, weather triggers and booking links.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-60 days through shop-owner groups, timed to the seasons.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A shop booking 120 swaps a season pays about $99 x 3 + $600 = $900 a season, around $300/mo. 40 shops x $300 = **$12,000/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Reminders turn seasonal chaos into a booked calendar, and each customer repeats yearly.

**Biggest risk:** Strong seasonality and incumbent shop software. Sell the reminder campaign as a separate add-on.

**Growth path:** Tire shops, then oil-change, auto-repair, car-wash and body shops.

### 4. Seasonal Plan and Pre-Order Engine for Independent Garden Centers

**Problem:** A garden center earns most of its revenue in a short spring window, with bad weather weekends hurting badly. It captures few customer emails and sends seasonal reminders late, if at all.

**What to build:** A seasonal calendar of what to plant and when, a spring pre-order for plants, soil and starter kits with pickup slots, weather-triggered sales texts and a fall bulb campaign. Show pre-order revenue and repeat customers.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Fill your first two spring weekends with pre-orders or the first quarter is free." Fee: $89/mo plus 2% of pre-order sales.

**Skill fit:** Pre-order catalogs, pickup scheduling and weather texts.

**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 45-75 days, timed ahead of spring.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** A center with $30K of pre-orders pays about $600 in fees a season plus $89/mo, around $180/mo. 70 centers x $180 = **$12,600/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Pre-orders smooth the spring rush and raise baskets, and the customer list grows every year.

**Biggest risk:** Seasonality. Add holiday trees and fall mums to extend the year.

**Growth path:** Garden centers, then nurseries, farm stands, florists and landscape suppliers.

### 5. Quote-to-Deposit Engine for Party Bus and Limousine Operators

**Problem:** A party-bus or limo operator gets quote requests for proms, weddings and nights out, but replies are slow and dates are held without deposits. Customers compare 3 operators and take the fastest answer.

**What to build:** An instant quote page by date, hours and group size, a deposit link, a vehicle-availability calendar, a pre-trip text and an add-on offer (decor, extra hour, driver tip pre-pay). Show quote-to-deposit rate and add-on revenue.

**Grand Slam angle:** "Respond to every request in under a minute and book 20% more, or you do not pay." Fee: $99/mo plus 1.5% of deposits.

**Skill fit:** Quote builder, availability logic and payments.

**MVP scope:** 3 weekends.

**Time to first $:** 30-45 days through operator groups, timed to prom and wedding seasons.

**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** An operator taking $25K/mo in deposits pays $99 + $375 = about $470. 28 operators x $470 = **$13,160/mo**.

**Why this can go beyond side money:** Each booking is high value and speed wins it.

**Biggest risk:** Seasonal peaks and a small operator base. Add airport shuttles, tour vans and event transport.

**Growth path:** Party buses, then limos, shuttles, wedding transport and charter vans.
