# Main Income Ideas - 2026-07-30 (Session 5)

## Who this is for
Builder profile: 29, software engineering + data analytics skills. Same bar as Sessions 1-4: not "$500-3K/mo of side money" but a solo-buildable path with a credible route to **$8-15K+/mo** — full-time-salary-replacement income — within 12-24 months.

## Ground rules for every idea in this series
- **Solo-buildable start.** One person, evenings/weekends, no funding, no cofounder required to get the first version live.
- **Realistic path to $8-15K+/mo**, not a side-money ceiling — every idea states the math (policy/parcel/exit/subscriber count x price point) plainly, no TAM hand-waving.
- **Time to first dollar < 90 days.** Long enterprise sales cycles are disqualifying by default.
- **Named distribution channel** for the first 10 customers *and* a credible path to the next 10x.
- **Boring is fine.** Low-glamour, real-pain problems beat trendy ones.
- **No repeats.** Nothing below overlaps a concept already covered across Sessions 1-4 of this series, `side-income-ideas/`, or `discovered-problems/`. This session introduces a genuinely new mechanic — **recurring insurance-distribution commission** (a true annuity that compounds without new sales effort) — plus a per-parcel field-agent fee and two more subscription/flat-fee sectors, keeping the pattern of pricing against real dollar stakes rather than a small-business subscription budget.

## Ideas

### 1. Niche Insurance MGA for Vacant Property/Land Insurance
**Problem:** Vacant homes (between renovation/sale, inherited estates, foreclosures) and raw land parcels are difficult and expensive to insure through standard homeowners carriers, which often decline or dramatically surcharge vacant-property risk — owners (flippers, estate executors, land investors) are stuck with expensive surplus-lines coverage from whichever broker happens to know this niche, and many go without coverage at all because finding a specialist is hard.
**What to build:** An MGA-style distribution operation — a quote-and-bind web platform for vacant-property/land insurance built in partnership with an actual carrier or wholesale/surplus-lines broker willing to delegate underwriting authority or accept referred business, earning a commission (10-20% of premium) on every policy bound, with a policy-management/renewal tool keeping the book of business recurring.
**Skill fit:** Direct on the software half (quote engine, policy management, renewal tracking); the carrier/wholesaler relationship and state insurance-producer licensing (a state exam, not a rare credential) is the main non-technical lift.
**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends for a quote-intake and policy-management tool once a carrier/wholesale partnership is in place; underwriting logic itself typically comes from the carrier's own rating rules, not built from scratch.
**Time to first $:** 60-90 days, including licensing and carrier-partnership setup — distribute through real estate investor associations (house flippers, land investors) and estate attorneys/executors who routinely need vacant-property coverage for a specific, findable audience.
**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** Vacant property/land policies run $800-3,000/yr in premium; a 15% average commission is $120-450 per policy, and a growing book of 200-300 active policies (achievable over 12-18 months at 15-20 new policies/mo) generates **$8-13K/mo in recurring renewal + new-business commission**.
**Why this can go beyond side money:** Insurance commission is a recurring annuity — every bound policy pays again at renewal without new sales effort, so the book of business compounds month over month rather than resetting, unlike a one-time service fee.
**Biggest risk:** Requires an actual carrier or wholesale partner willing to work with a small, unproven distributor, and licensing requirements vary by state — start as a licensed sub-producer under an established wholesale broker (rather than seeking a direct carrier appointment from day one) to get real market access immediately while building a track record.
**Growth path:** Side project → licensed, partnered with a wholesale broker, first 20-30 policies bound through direct outreach to real estate investor associations. Replaces-part-time-income → 100-150 policies in the book, renewal commissions compounding alongside new business (~$4-6K/mo). Replaces-full-time-salary → 250-300+ policies in the book, a direct carrier appointment earned through volume/loss-ratio track record (better commission terms), renewal income alone approaching half of total revenue.

### 2. Delinquent Property Tax Lien/Deed Auction Intelligence & Bidding Assistance
**Problem:** Tax lien and tax deed investing (buying delinquent-property liens/deeds at county auctions for high fixed-rate interest or below-market property acquisition) is a real, well-established investment niche, but auction data is scattered across thousands of individual county websites and PDFs with wildly inconsistent formats, making it genuinely hard for even serious investors to research and bid across multiple counties efficiently.
**What to build:** A subscription intelligence product aggregating upcoming tax lien/deed auction listings across counties (parcel details, minimum bid, assessed value, delinquency history) into a searchable, filterable database, paired with a higher-tier "done for you" service where the builder researches and flags the most attractive lots ahead of a specific auction for investors who want hands-on support.
**Skill fit:** Direct — a data-aggregation and normalization problem (scraping/structuring inconsistent county data sources into one clean interface), the strongest fit in this session for the data-analytics half of the skillset.
**MVP scope:** 3-4 weekends to build the aggregation pipeline for a starter set of 20-30 counties (concentrated in a few states with active investor communities) plus a searchable database UI.
**Time to first $:** 45-60 days — distribute through tax lien/deed investing communities (active Facebook groups, forums, paid conferences) where investors already pay for auction lists and research help from less-systematic sources.
**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $49-99/mo subscription x 100-150 investors = $5-15K/mo, plus a "done for you" research tier at $200-500 per auction researched for 10-15 higher-touch clients/mo adding $2-7K/mo — comfortably into **$8-15K/mo** once both tiers have real subscriber bases.
**Why this can go beyond side money:** Serious tax lien/deed investors already spend real money (subscription services, paid research, conference tickets) in this niche because the returns at stake are large — this isn't a market that needs convincing software is worth paying for, only that this specific product is the best version of what they already buy.
**Biggest risk:** County data sources change format or structure without notice, breaking individual scrapers one county at a time — build a per-county freshness/health monitor so broken sources are caught and fixed quickly rather than silently serving stale data to paying subscribers.
**Growth path:** Side project → 20-30 counties covered, first 20-30 subscribers from direct outreach in tax lien investor communities. Replaces-part-time-income → 60-80 counties covered, 60-80 subscribers plus the first done-for-you research clients (~$5-8K/mo). Replaces-full-time-salary → 150+ counties across several active states, 120-150 subscribers plus a steady done-for-you client base, positioned as the standard research tool referenced in the niche's own conferences and forums.

### 3. Timeshare Exit Facilitation Service
**Problem:** Millions of timeshare owners want out of contracts they no longer use or can afford (rising maintenance fees, no resale market, contracts that pass to heirs), but the "timeshare exit" industry is notorious for scams — upfront-fee companies that take money and deliver nothing — leaving genuinely desperate owners afraid to trust anyone, even though legitimate exit paths (developer deed-back/surrender programs, and in some cases licensed resale or facilitated cancellation) exist and are underused simply because owners don't know which path applies to their specific contract and resort.
**What to build:** A legitimate exit-facilitation service that determines which real exit path applies to a specific timeshare (deed-back program eligibility, resort-specific surrender policies, or referral to licensed resale) and manages the paperwork through to completion, charging a flat fee substantially due on confirmed completion (not the all-upfront model that defines the scam segment of this industry) to directly differentiate on trust.
**Skill fit:** Direct on the software half (a database of resort-specific deed-back/surrender program rules and eligibility criteria, plus a case-tracking tool); the paperwork/negotiation work with resorts and HOAs is manual service work, learnable domain knowledge rather than a technical skill.
**MVP scope:** 2-3 weekends for a resort/program-rules database (starting with the largest timeshare brands, covering a large share of the market) and a case-intake/tracking tool.
**Time to first $:** 45-60 days — reach owners directly through high-intent search content ("how to get out of my timeshare legally," a large existing search category) and referral relationships with fee-only financial advisors and elder-law attorneys who see clients stuck with unwanted timeshares.
**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $1,500-3,000 flat completion fee per exit (well below what scam upfront-fee operators charge, priced against the real value of eliminating years of ongoing maintenance-fee obligations); 5-8 completed exits/mo once referral channels are running = **$8-24K/mo**.
**Why this can go beyond side money:** A flat fee priced against years of eliminated ongoing obligation (not a small monthly software cost) supports a high per-case value, and the trust differentiation (fee due mostly on completion, not upfront) is a genuine, defensible wedge into a market currently poisoned by bad actors.
**Biggest risk:** The timeshare exit industry's reputation is badly damaged by scam operators, so building trust is the core challenge, not a side issue — lead with the completion-based fee structure prominently in all marketing, publish a clear scope of exactly which programs are pursued for which resorts, and consider referral-only distribution (attorneys, advisors) before broad consumer marketing to build a credible track record first.
**Growth path:** Side project → first 5-10 exits completed through direct outreach and one attorney/advisor referral relationship, building a track record and testimonials. Replaces-part-time-income → 15-20 exits/mo once search-intent content ranks and 2-3 referral relationships are active (~$5-8K/mo, given real casework time per exit). Replaces-full-time-salary → resort/program-rules database expanded to cover most major timeshare brands, referral network broadened, 25-30+ exits/mo pushing past $10-15K/mo.

### 4. Patent/Trademark Maintenance & Renewal Monitoring Retainer
**Problem:** Small inventors, solo founders, and small businesses that filed their own or a cheap attorney-filed patent or trademark routinely miss USPTO maintenance fee deadlines (patents at 3.5, 7.5, and 11.5 years; trademarks on a fixed renewal schedule) — missing one deadline can mean losing the IP entirely, but most small IP owners have no systematic tracking once the initial filing is done and the attorney relationship has ended.
**What to build:** A monitoring and filing retainer service: a tool tracking a client's patent/trademark portfolio against USPTO maintenance/renewal deadlines, sending advance alerts, and handling the actual fee payment/renewal filing on the client's behalf before each deadline.
**Skill fit:** Direct — deadline tracking against a structured, fixed schedule and filing routine renewal paperwork is squarely a rules-and-scheduling problem; no patent-attorney-level expertise is required for routine maintenance filings, as distinct from prosecuting a new application.
**MVP scope:** 2-3 weekends for a portfolio-intake tool (pulling public USPTO data by patent/trademark number) and a deadline-tracking/alert system.
**Time to first $:** 30-45 days — distribute through solo-inventor and small-business-founder communities (relevant subreddits, small-business trademark/patent Facebook groups) and referral relationships with the IP attorneys who filed the original applications but don't want to manage ongoing maintenance themselves.
**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $30-60/mo retainer per IP asset tracked; an owner with a small portfolio (2-4 assets) pays $60-240/mo; 60-100 tracked assets across 30-50 clients = $3-6K/mo from tracking alone, plus a $200-500 filing fee collected at each actual renewal event across the portfolio, pushing combined monthly average toward **$8-12K/mo** once the portfolio base is large enough that renewal events happen continuously.
**Why this can go beyond side money:** The retainer is priced against the real risk of losing IP entirely (a founder's trademark or patent can be core business value), and a large enough tracked-asset base means renewal filing fees land continuously rather than needing new customer acquisition to keep growing revenue.
**Biggest risk:** Retainer-only revenue per client is modest, so this depends on real portfolio volume rather than a few high-value clients — prioritize the referral channel from IP attorneys (who see many clients at once) over one-by-one direct-to-inventor acquisition, since attorney referrals bring portfolio volume faster.
**Growth path:** Side project → 30-50 tracked assets from a handful of direct clients and one attorney referral relationship. Replaces-part-time-income → 150-200 tracked assets across a growing client base, 2-3 attorney referral relationships active, renewal filing fees landing regularly (~$5-8K/mo). Replaces-full-time-salary → 400-500+ tracked assets, a broader attorney referral network, renewal events happening continuously enough that filing-fee income alone approaches the retainer income, pushing combined revenue past $10K/mo.

### 5. Right-of-Way/Easement Acquisition Agent for Utility/Telecom Buildouts
**Problem:** Utility, telecom (especially rural broadband/fiber buildouts funded by BEAD and similar programs), and pipeline companies need to acquire right-of-way easements from hundreds or thousands of individual landowners along a planned route before construction can begin — a real, established profession (right-of-way agent), but the process is still largely paper-based and manually tracked project by project, and demand is currently surging well ahead of the supply of experienced agents given the scale of ongoing broadband buildout funding.
**What to build:** A tech-enabled right-of-way agent service — a landowner-outreach and document-tracking tool (parcel ownership lookup, negotiation status, signed easement document management) that makes an individual agent dramatically more productive across more parcels — working as a subcontracted agent for utility/telecom/pipeline buildout projects, paid per-parcel closed ($150-500/parcel depending on complexity) or via project day-rate contracts.
**Skill fit:** Direct on the software half (parcel/ownership data lookup, negotiation-status tracking, document management) — a structured-data and workflow problem; landowner negotiation is a learnable field skill, and no special license is required in most states for right-of-way acquisition work (unlike real estate brokerage).
**MVP scope:** 2-3 weekends for a parcel-tracking and document-management tool; the negotiation/outreach itself is the actual job, not something to automate.
**Time to first $:** 45-60 days — the fastest entry point is subcontracting to an existing right-of-way acquisition firm or engineering firm managing a buildout project (they're actively short on agents given current broadband funding volume), found through the International Right of Way Association (IRWA) member/project network, rather than trying to win a prime contract directly as an unknown solo operator.
**Income ceiling (realistic, with math):** $150-500 per parcel closed, with an experienced agent closing 15-30 parcels/mo once productive on a given project (the tracking tool's main value is enabling more parcels/month than a paper-based agent manages) = $4-12K/mo per active project, and running as a subcontractor on 2 concurrent projects reaches **$8-15K/mo**.
**Why this can go beyond side money:** Per-parcel fees scale with the sheer volume of parcels along a buildout route (a single rural fiber project can involve hundreds to thousands of parcels), and the current funding-driven surge in buildout projects means demand for agents, not a limited customer base, is the binding constraint.
**Biggest risk:** Right-of-way work involves real field time (landowner meetings, some travel along a project's route) that a software tool can speed up but not eliminate — treat productivity gains from the tracking tool as extending how many parcels one person can manage concurrently, not as a way to make this a purely remote, software-only business.
**Growth path:** Side project → subcontracted onto one buildout project through an IRWA connection, 10-15 parcels/mo closed while learning the field workflow. Replaces-part-time-income → productive on one project at 20-25 parcels/mo, tracking tool cutting administrative time enough to take on a second concurrent project (~$6-9K/mo). Replaces-full-time-salary → running 2 concurrent projects at full productivity, reputation with acquisition/engineering firms built through IRWA connections leading to being proactively offered new projects rather than having to seek them out, pushing past $10-15K/mo.

## This Session's Pick: Start With #5, Track #1 as the Compounding-Annuity Parallel Bet

**Fastest real entry with active demand:** Idea #5 (right-of-way agent) has no licensing barrier, a clear subcontracting entry point through an existing professional association (IRWA) rather than needing to win business as an unknown solo operator, and rides a genuine, funding-driven surge in demand right now — the most immediately actionable build this session.

**Highest-leverage long-term bet:** Idea #1 (vacant property/land insurance MGA) is slower to start (licensing, carrier partnership) but is the only idea in this session — and one of very few across the whole series — where income compounds into a recurring annuity that keeps paying without repeated new sales effort, worth building in parallel once #5's per-parcel income covers the licensing/setup runway.

Running #5 as the primary build (fast entry, no licensing wait, real current demand) while working licensing and a wholesale-broker partnership for #1 in the background continues this series' pattern of pairing a fast, cash-generating build with a slower, higher-leverage bet running alongside it — in this case, one that starts paying dividends on its own well after the initial setup work is done.
